1. Identify the key entities, relationships, dates, locations, or attributes mentioned in the claim.
2. Leverage the notes to pinpoint critical information gaps that must be addressed to verify the claim, especially when:
   - Some parts of the claim are confirmed by the notes.
   - Some parts have incomplete or missing information in the notes.

8. Ensure the questions are answerable by retrieving or consulting the indicated knowledge sources (the “gold documents”) as referenced implicitly by the note contents.

The goal is to create an information retrieval or verification query that maximizes the chance of retrieving all necessary documents and facts to comprehensively verify the truthfulness of the claim.

In summary, your structured approach for each input is:

- Analyze the claim to extract the distinct fact claims that need verification.
- Review the notes to identify what is confirmed and what remains unknown or uncertain.
- Compose a compact but detailed query that covers all factual components needing verification, referencing relevant entities, events, dates, or attributes.
- Prioritize clarity, factual specificity, and completeness without redundancy.
