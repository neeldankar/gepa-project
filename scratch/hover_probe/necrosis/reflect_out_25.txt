Task Description: 

You will be provided with three input fields:
- `claim`: a factual statement or assertion that needs to be verified or contextualized.
- `notes`: a list of extracted factual snippets or sentences relevant to the claim, summarizing and supporting various aspects of it.
- `context`: a list of detailed background documents or encyclopedia-style entries providing comprehensive information related to entities, events, or topics mentioned in the claim.

Your goal is to produce two output fields:
- `new_notes`: a concise, accurate synthesis of the key factual information from `notes` and `context` that supports or clarifies the claim. These should be phrased clearly and confirm or refine the claim’s facts, focusing on relevant names, events, relationships, dates, and other domain-specific details. Avoid ambiguity or unsupported speculation.
- `titles`: a list of specific document or entity titles from the `context` and possibly from `notes`, representing the core authoritative sources or knowledge units that substantiate the refined information in `new_notes` and relate directly to the claim.

Detailed Task Guidance and Domain-Specific Nuances:

1. **Verification and Alignment**: 
   - Carefully verify the claim against the combined evidence in `notes` and `context`.
   - Detect small factual inconsistencies (e.g., name misspellings, date mismatches) and correct them or highlight them in `new_notes`.
   - The claim may involve domain-specific entities (persons, events, organizations, titles, episodes, tournaments), so precise identification is critical.

2. **Entity Disambiguation and Specificity**:
   - Separate similarly named entities (e.g., multiple actors named Nicholas Boshier vs. different persons with similar names).
   - Distinguish different domain areas where the same term occurs (e.g., "Soul Mates" can be multiple works: TV series, film, episode titles).
   - Include disambiguated, full titles or parenthetical clarifications (e.g., “Soul Mates (TV series)”, “Jennifer Jones (curler)”).

3. **Summarization Strategy**:
   - Extract the most relevant, high-confidence facts that directly inform the claim.
   - Use `notes` as starting summarized evidence; corroborate and expand with the authoritative `context` entries.
   - Remove redundancy while preserving distinguishing details (e.g., multiple confirmations of the same fact can be consolidated).

4. **Output Structure and Coverage**:
   - `new_notes` should be factual, neutral, and explicit — synthesizing information to confirm or nuance the claim.
   - `titles` should mention key documents, biographies, episodes, events, or entity entries from `context` that provide primary evidence for `new_notes`.
   - Ensure the `titles` list helps a future retriever find the authoritative sources related to the claim’s topic.

5. **Domain Examples**:
   - Entertainment industry: Confirm actors, creators, show titles, production and broadcast details.
   - Music industry: Confirm membership and participation of artists in groups, songwriters, and song details.
   - Sports domain: Confirm athlete identities, event participation (e.g., qualifiers, championships), roles (e.g., team captain/skip), event years, and outcomes.

6. **Reasoning and Clarifications**:
   - When a claim is partially unverified, clearly state what is confirmed and what remains unknown.
   - Highlight any relevant prior achievements or associated entities that contextualize the claim (e.g., a player’s prior tournament wins).
   - When multiple similar entities or events exist, clearly differentiate which one applies to the claim.

Generalizable Approach:
- Cross-reference the claim with all `notes` and `context` entries to identify matching facts.
- Summarize supporting evidence into clean, precise statements for `new_notes`.
- Collect authoritative `titles` that cover these facts as your source references.
- Address ambiguities or gaps explicitly rather than making unsupported claims.
- Maintain consistent formatting and use formal, encyclopedic style.

This approach ensures the outputs form a reliable, evidence-backed refinement of the claim with clear source linkage, suitable for tasks requiring fact verification and knowledge grounding in variable domains.
