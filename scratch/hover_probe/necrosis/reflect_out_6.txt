   - Include important related works or topics needed for proper disambiguation or completeness (e.g., names of TV series, movie titles, composer names).
   - The goal is a comprehensive but focused set of titles to support understanding and retrieval.

General Strategy:
- Thoroughly cross-reference `claim` statements with both `notes` and `context` to identify facts that confirm or explain the claim.
- Use a reasoning approach that resolves conflicting or partial information by weighing sources, noting when a claim misattributes network affiliation or roles.
- Summarize or merge related notes to avoid duplication and enhance clarity.
- Pay special attention to domain-specific entities and relationships, such as:
    - TV series casting and network broadcasting (correct attribution of actors, roles, and channels).
    - Actor collaborations and development credits for TV shows.
    - Composer roles and teaching statuses.
    - Collaborations between artists and their known works.
- Ensure all included notes and titles contribute directly toward validating or contextualizing the claim.
- Avoid adding unsupported assertions or extraneous information not clearly relevant to the claim.

Additional Guidance:
- Maintain domain-specific terminologies where relevant (e.g., ‘Star Plus’, ‘Zee TV’, ‘ABC2’, ‘Austrian composer’, ‘negative roles’, ‘collaborations’).
- In the presence of multiple related entities, clarify distinctions by incorporating show names, roles, and affiliations.
- Remember that claim validation might hinge on subtle factual distinctions (e.g., whether a show aired on Star Plus versus Zee TV).
- Prioritize completeness of entity titles to aid subsequent retrieval or verification tasks.

By following these detailed instructions, your output should produce a clear, accurate, and comprehensive set of notes and titles that support or elucidate the input claim based on the supplied notes and context.
