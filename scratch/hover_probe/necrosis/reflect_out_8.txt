Task Objective:

You will receive three text inputs: a `claim`, a set of `notes`, and a `context`. Your goal is to produce two outputs: `new_notes` and `titles` that collectively support, clarify, or correct the factual accuracy of the `claim` by leveraging and cross-referencing information within the `notes` and the `context`.

Input Specifications:
- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion that involves one or more domain-specific entities (e.g., persons, bands, TV series, organizations, events) and contains verifiable facts.
- `notes`: A collection of short textual snippets drawn from various sources relevant to the claim and associated entities. These may include repetitive or conflicting information.
- `context`: More comprehensive, encyclopedia-style or descriptive passages providing background, history, or detailed facts about the entities, works, or topics related to the claim and notes.

Output Requirements:

1. `new_notes`:
   - Extract and synthesize a concise, accurate set of notes that directly support, explain, or clarify the factual content of the claim.
   - Prioritize notes that are fully verifiable and ideally corroborated by both `notes` and `context`.
   - Omit redundant, irrelevant, or contradictory information to maintain clarity and precision.
   - Emphasize connections involving multiple key entities or fact clusters critical to the claim, such as:
     - Specific actor/show/network relationships in entertainment claims (e.g., casting, roles, broadcasting channels).
     - Band formation details, membership, and geographic origins in music-related claims.
     - Composer, musician, or artist collaborations, teaching relations, or performance credits.
     - Release details and production specifics of albums, live events, concerts, or media.
   - Use authoritative or repeated evidence from context to resolve conflicting or ambiguous facts (e.g., discrepancies in dates, locations, or affiliations).
   - When specific domain terminology applies (such as network names, band genres, or studio labels), include it precisely.

2. `titles`:
   - Compile a comprehensive but focused list of key entities referenced in the `claim` or required to understand it, drawn from both notes and context.
   - Include all major persons (e.g., musicians, actors, creators), bands, TV shows, record labels, organizations, or works mentioned.
   - Include related entities necessary for proper disambiguation or completeness, such as network names, venues, album titles, or notable collaborators.
   - This list should aid retrieval, verification, or contextual understanding of the claim and new_notes.

General Methodology:

- Cross-reference the claim against `notes` and `context` to identify accurate, domain-specific factual support or refutations.
- Integrate information from multiple sources to build a cohesive, clear summary of key facts relevant to verifying the claim.
- Prioritize facts verified by both `notes` and `context`, particularly in cases of conflicting information.
- Avoid introducing unsupported or extraneous information unrelated to the claim.
- Address ambiguities by weighing source authority and frequency of corroboration.
- Carefully distinguish between similarly named entities or overlapping domains (e.g., two bands with similar names, differing TV channels) by including clarifying context such as dates, locations, or production details.
- Emphasize domain-relevant details such as:
  - Formation years, band origin cities, and membership histories for musical groups.
  - Specific roles actors played, series they appeared in, and broadcasting networks for TV shows.
  - Artist collaborations and production credits relevant to musical or entertainment claims.
  - Venue and release details related to live concerts or album recordings.
- Ensure factual precision in entity names (e.g., "WCJB-TV", "Gainesville, Florida", "Against Me!", "Laura Jane Grace") and domain-specific terms (e.g., “negative roles”, “ABC2”, “Star Plus”).

Examples of common scenarios include:

- Verifying the place of a band's formation or their associated city, ensuring geographic facts are correctly referenced (e.g., Gainesville, Florida in North Central Florida).
- Correcting or confirming formation years of bands, artists’ membership timelines, or release dates of albums.
- Clarifying which band or musician performed or was involved in a particular live concert or song, especially when multiple possibilities exist.
- Establishing the network that broadcast a particular TV show and verifying the correct roles of actors and show creators.
- Distinguishing between entities with overlapping or similar names by carefully referencing proven contextual facts.

By following these guidelines, your output should provide a clearly focused, accurate set of `new_notes` and `titles` that thoroughly support or elucidate the factual correctness and context of the input `claim`.
