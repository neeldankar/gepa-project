Task Objective:

You will be provided with three textual inputs: a `claim`, a collection of `notes`, and a `context`. Your task is to produce two outputs—`new_notes` and `titles`—which together verify, support, clarify, or correct the factual accuracy of the claim by synthesizing and cross-referencing information from both the notes and the context.

Input Specifications:
- `claim`: A factual assertion involving one or more domain-specific entities (e.g., persons, bands, TV shows, organizations, events) containing verifiable facts.
- `notes`: Collections of short text snippets extracted from multiple sources relevant to the claim and associated entities. These may contain repetition, overlapping facts, or conflicting information.
- `context`: More extensive, encyclopedic passages providing comprehensive background, histories, or detailed facts surrounding the entities or topics related to the claim and notes.

Output Specifications:

1. `new_notes`:
   - Extract and synthesize a concise, precise set of notes that directly support, clarify, or correct the claim’s factual content.
   - Only include information verifiable and ideally corroborated by both `notes` and `context`.
   - Avoid redundant, irrelevant, or contradictory data to maintain clarity and accuracy.
   - Prioritize fact clusters highlighting critical entity relationships central to the claim, including:
     - In entertainment claims: actor/show/network roles, casting details, broadcasting channels.
     - In music claims: band formation details, membership history, geography of origin, record label affiliations, album or concert release details.
     - Composer/musician collaborations, production or songwriting credits.
     - Specific event details (dates, venues, releases) related to the claim.
   - Use authoritative evidence from context to resolve ambiguities or discrepancies (e.g., differing dates, locations, or affiliations).
   - Include precise domain-specific terminology and naming conventions (e.g., correct spellings of bands, official network names, album titles).

2. `titles`:
   - Generate a focused but comprehensive list of all key entities referenced in the claim or needed to fully understand it.
   - Include major persons (e.g., musicians, actors), bands, TV shows, record labels, organizations, or works mentioned in the claim, notes, or context.
   - Include supporting or disambiguating entities such as network names, venues, album titles, collaborators, or production personnel relevant to the claim.
   - This list aids subsequent retrieval, verification, and contextualization of the claim and `new_notes`.

General Methodology and Domain-Specific Guidelines:

- Rigorously cross-reference facts in the claim against both notes and context to determine verification or refutation.
- Integrate data from multiple sources into a coherent, concise summary that focuses only on facts strengthening or clarifying the claim.
- Prioritize information confirmed by authoritative, detailed sources in both notes and context especially when resolving conflicting or ambiguous facts.
- Carefully distinguish similarly named entities or overlapping domains by exact disambiguation (e.g., two bands with similar names, different TV networks).
- Recognize domain-specific fact clusters, including but not limited to:
  - Bands: formation year, originating city (e.g., Gainesville, Florida), member timeline, label signings, genre classification.
  - Musicians and composers: collaborations, producer roles, songwriting credits.
  - TV and film: actor roles, show creators, networks/channels broadcasting the programs.
  - Events and releases: dates, venues, formats (e.g., live album, studio album), production details.
- Avoid introducing information not supported by the provided inputs.
- Weigh frequency and authority of corroborating evidence to resolve contradictions or unclear facts.
- Maintain factual precision in entity names, event titles, and technical terms to ensure clarity and correctness.

Examples of domain-specific considerations:

- For a claim about a curling athlete: verify competition history, skip/captain roles, Olympic participation and gold medals, team membership, provincial vs national championships.
- For rock bands or musicians: verify formation location and year, record labels they were signed to, known producers or A&R personnel, and distinct identities between similarly named entities.
- For songwriter and group membership claims: verify individual members’ associations with named girl groups or bands, songwriting credits tied to named works or groups.
- For TV series or shows: confirm actor roles, series creators, broadcasting networks, and differentiations between similarly titled shows on different networks.

By following these detailed procedures, your output should deliver a clear, concise, and factually precise synthesis (`new_notes`) and an accurate list of key entities (`titles`) that fully support or clarify the truthfulness and context of the input claim.
