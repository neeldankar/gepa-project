- `claim`: A factual assertion or statement that needs to be verified, evaluated, or clarified.

These include detailed definitions, historical context, distinctions between closely related entities, dates, names, attributions, and clarifications that can disambiguate overlapping or confusing information.

Your task is to produce two output fields:

1. `new_notes`:

   - Remove duplicate, redundant, or extraneous information, while preserving necessary nuance and detailed facts.
   - Incorporate clarifications or disambiguations by leveraging the `context` to:
        * Correctly attribute works (e.g., musical compositions to correct composers with full background: nationality, gender, historical period, cataloging).
        * Disambiguate between entities with similar or overlapping names or roles (e.g., different bands, actors with partially overlapping credits, dog breeds with similar names or origins).
        * Highlight subtle distinctions, such as different versions of works or different adaptations of novels.
        * Address common confusions or misattributions (e.g., wrong album linked to a band, incorrect film adaptation linked to a novel).
   - The summary should be concise, logically coherent, factually precise, and focused only on evidence relevant to evaluating the claim's truthfulness or falsehood.
   - Emphasize clarity in specifying correct attributions, origins, sponsorship, and affiliations, especially where ambiguity or controversy arises.

2. `titles`:
   - Identify and list the key entity titles or authoritative document names from `context` most relevant and foundational for substantiating or clarifying the claim.
   - These should be primary or definitive sources that underpin the new notes and are essential references for fact-checking and validation.
   - Include people (e.g., noted personalities, composers, authors), specific works (e.g., musical albums, films, novels), organizations or companies (e.g., dog breed registries, sponsoring companies), and other key concepts required for correct understanding.
   - Prioritize sources that address distinctions or controversies raised by the claim, such as:
       * Correct identification of breeds including recognition status and regional origin.
       * Proper attribution of roles or credits for actors or bands.
       * Accurate association between works and their creators.
       * Verification of sponsorship and event-related details.
   - The titles should serve as precise pointers for authoritative retrieval if further verification is needed.

General methodological approach:

- Compare and cross-validate the claim with the notes and context systematically.
- Identify contradictions, confirmations, or lack of evidence.
- Eliminate irrelevant or misleading information.
- Integrate context-based clarifications to resolve ambiguities or common confusions.
- Summarize findings in a succinct, factually precise manner without narrative bias or opinion.
- Select titles that represent the core authoritative sources for quick retrieval and deeper fact-checking.

Your overall goal is to synthesize a precise, clear, and concise factual summary (`new_notes`) aligned to the claim’s accuracy and a targeted list of authoritative source entities or document titles (`titles`) from the `context` supporting that summary.
