Task Description:
You are tasked with fact-checking and refining information related to a specific claim by analyzing three inputs: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your ultimate goal is to generate two clear outputs: a synthesized list of `new_notes` and a focused set of `titles`.

Inputs:
- `claim`: A concise statement or assertion involving entities, events, or factual relationships that require verification.
- `notes`: An unstructured collection of statements, evidence snippets, or observations related to the claim, which may include redundancies, contradictions, indirect references, or incomplete facts.
- `context`: A set of authoritative documents or descriptions providing background knowledge, detailed data, or corroborative facts about the entities or events involved in the claim.

Outputs:
1. `new_notes`:  
   - A refined, concise, and factually precise synthesis drawn from both the `notes` and `context`.  
   - It must directly address the truthfulness, validity, or accuracy of the claim by emphasizing key factual points.  
   - Eliminate redundancy, contradiction, or extraneous information present in the original notes.  
   - Ensure that the statements are standalone — each should be a clear, domain-relevant fact that either supports, refutes, or clarifies elements of the claim.  
   - Domain-specific facts must be accurately captured, such as:  
     * Organizational compositions (e.g., school districts and their member schools with correct geographic locations).  
     * Chronology or timeline of events (e.g., correct date and sequence of actors’ roles or institutional events).  
     * Role differentiations (e.g., distinguishing similar or related entities like characters versus actors, or related but distinct camps or series).  
     * Clarification of entity relationships, affiliations, and functions (e.g., confirming whether an individual held a specific title or performed certain roles).  
     * Correction of misattributions or conflations common in the domain (e.g., mixing up TV shows, confusing commandants with negotiators, or inaccurate actor credits).  

2. `titles`:  
   - A concise list of the most relevant document or entity titles drawn from the `context` that are essential to verify or understand the claim.  
   - Titles should reflect key entities, organizations, or subjects directly mentioned or logically implicated in the claim.  
   - Avoid including unrelated, marginally relevant, or duplicate documents. Focus on providing traceability and relevance.  
   - Ensure the titles provide comprehensive yet focused coverage supporting the synthesis.

Key Considerations and Domain-Specific Guidance:
- When chronological or temporal claims are involved, carefully reconcile dates from all inputs to establish a correct timeline.  
- Identify and differentiate entities with potentially confusingly similar names or roles to avoid conflating them (e.g., multiple TV series, different voice actors for a single character, different schools with similar names).  
- Recognize domain-specific terminologies, such as:  
  * Educational districts — correct school names and locations (e.g., Sewanhaka Central High School District schools are in Nassau County, NY).  
  * Film, TV, and voice acting credits — verifying the actual roles of actors, their nationality if relevant, and avoid presuming unsubstantiated attributions.  
  * Historical military or political positions — accurately confirming ranks, duties, and factual historical context (e.g., Nazi SS command structure, Auschwitz commandants, and operational control).  
- Cross-validate contradictory information and provide a logically consistent resolution explaining which details are supported and which are refuted.  
- Provide reasoning within your synthesis implicitly, so the `new_notes` read as a factual snapshot that helps users judge the claim’s accuracy without ambiguity.  
- Avoid speculation or unsupported conjectures; rely strictly on evidence provided.

Generalizable Strategy:
- Begin with extracting and confirming the exact entities and events in the claim.  
- Filter and consolidate evidence from notes to eliminate redundancies and conflicts.  
- Correlate this with authoritative information from the context to validate or correct claims.  
- Create a balanced summary that clearly delineates truths, falsehoods, and clarifications.  
- Select key titles that are the primary sources covering these facts, ensuring traceability and focused verification.  

Output Format:
- Provide `new_notes` as a list of concise, factually accurate statements directly relevant to the claim’s verification or falsification.  
- Provide `titles` as a list of succinct and pertinent document or entity names from the context.

By following this approach and domain-aware guidance, you will produce outputs that assist in reliable claim verification while filtering noise and ambiguities typical in diverse, multifaceted inputs.
