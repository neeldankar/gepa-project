Task Description:
You are given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your task is to analyze these inputs and generate two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

Inputs:
- `claim`: A statement or assertion involving one or more entities, events, or facts.
- `notes`: A list of statements or evidence snippets relevant to the claim, but possibly redundant, repetitive, or indirectly related.
- `context`: A list of contextual documents or descriptions providing background, detailed information, or supporting facts about the entities, events, or subjects related to the claim.

Outputs:
1. `new_notes`: A concise, refined, and factually relevant synthesis of the provided `notes` and `context` that directly address the truthfulness, validity, or accuracy of the `claim`. This should:
   - Remove redundancies and contradictions present in the original notes.
   - Emphasize key factual points derived from the `notes` and `context` that support or refute the claim.
   - Present clear, standalone statements that reflect the critical evidence about the claim.
   - Capture domain-specific factual information relevant to the claim’s topic (e.g., sports seasons and driver participation, historical dates and academic affiliations, TV series premiere dates and actor roles).

2. `titles`: A short list of the most relevant document or entity titles from the provided `context` that best capture the essential topics involved in verifying the claim. These should:
   - Include key entities, organizations, or subjects directly mentioned or implied in the claim and evidence.
   - Support traceability and understanding of the claim’s context.
   - Reflect a balance between comprehensive coverage and focus on the most pertinent sources.
   - Usually exclude unrelated or indirectly connected context items.

Approach and Domain-Specific Considerations:
- Carefully reconcile potentially conflicting dates, events, or roles mentioned in the `notes` and `context` to establish a clear timeline or factual sequence.
- Identify and differentiate distinct yet sometimes confusingly connected entities (e.g., differentiating between two TV series an actor may have been involved in).
- Recognize domain-specific terms and entities such as racing series seasons, rally vs. circuit racing distinctions, institutional founding years, or character and actor associations in TV series.
- When claims involve chronology, verify the temporal sequence with reliable data from context (e.g., institution establishment dates, series premiere years).
- Use domain knowledge to detect if a claim conflates related but separate facts (e.g., an actor’s participation in different TV series).
- Ensure that your reasoning is logically consistent and clearly explains why the claim is supported, refuted, or unsupported based on the evidence.
- Strive to generate `new_notes` that act as a refined summary of the critical evidence, while `titles` should guide the user towards the main sources underpinning this evidence.

Output Format:
- Provide the `new_notes` as a list of concise statements.
- Provide the `titles` as a list of succinct document or entity titles.

Your response should support verification and evaluation of the claim by integrating all relevant factual details while filtering out noise and ambiguities.
