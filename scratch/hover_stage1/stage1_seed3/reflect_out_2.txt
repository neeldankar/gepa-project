Task Description:

You are provided with three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- `claim` is a statement or assertion that may contain factual information or a connection between entities, events, or attributes that needs verification, clarification, or elaboration.
- `notes` is a list of extracted facts, partial evidences, or relevant informational snippets related to entities or topics mentioned in the claim. These notes may include supporting, contradictory, or neutral details.
- `context` is a list of descriptions, summaries, or excerpts of relevant documents, articles, or sources potentially related to the claim or notes, providing deeper background or authoritative information.

Your goal is to produce two fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

1. `new_notes`:
   - This should be a concise, factual, and relevant subset or refined synthesis of the input notes, possibly merged or pruned for clarity.
   - Include the most pertinent facts that address key elements of the claim: support, refute, clarify, or contextualize it.
   - Filter out repeated, irrelevant, or tangential statements.
   - Where the claim contains contradictions or inaccuracies, highlight and clarify them.
   - If certain core information is missing in the notes but available in context, incorporate it into `new_notes`.
   - Maintain a neutral, objective tone with precise references to entities, events, or roles involved.
   - Notes should be self-contained and understandable without external references.

2. `titles`:
   - This should be a list of entity or document titles (as strings) that represent the most relevant source documents or knowledge bases for verifying or understanding the claim.
   - Titles should directly correspond to entries or contexts referenced in the notes or claim.
   - Aim to capture all critical entities, events, organizations, or named concepts essential to the claim’s truth evaluation.
   - Titles should be distinct, non-redundant, and clearly indicate the topical focus of their corresponding documents.

Reasoning Strategy:

- Dissect the claim logically, checking for internal consistencies and contradictions (e.g., roles assigned to a person that conflict with known facts).
- Analyze notes and context to confirm factual components: people, roles, dates, affiliations, and relationships mentioned in the claim.
- Identify gaps or contradictions and address them explicitly in the reasoning and new_notes.
- Incorporate domain-specific knowledge: for example, in sports, "goalie leading a strike force" is often contradictory; in entertainment, clarify roles (actor vs. director), and confirm identity associations carefully.
- When background or auxiliary context provides stronger or clarifying information not present in notes, integrate that information.
- Aim to replicate or enhance retrieval of the most relevant documents or entities to fully verify the claim, minimizing omissions.
- Use a neutral, evidence-based approach without subjective assumptions.

Domain-Specific Guidance:

- For claims involving roles or titles (e.g. actor roles, musical group identities), verify consistency against known data about those entities.
- When multiple similar or related documents exist (e.g., soundtrack and film, different versions of a band name), include all relevant but non-redundant titles.
- Pay attention to temporal details (dates, periods when people played for certain teams or worked on projects).
- Detect and highlight internally contradicting claims (e.g., goalkeepers cannot lead strike forces).
- Clarify cases where no evidence exists to support a claim by explicitly stating so in new_notes.
- Avoid speculation; limit to verifiable facts given in notes or context or logically inferred from them.

Format Requirements:

- Submit your response as a JSON-like structure with two keys: `new_notes` (list of strings) and `titles` (list of strings).
- Both fields should be concise but comprehensive for the purposes of accurate fact verification and referencing.

Summary:

Given `claim`, `notes`, and `context`, produce a refined, verified, and clear set of `new_notes` that address and explain key claim elements, and a focused set of `titles` for supporting documents or entities critical to claim verification, incorporating reasoning about factual accuracy, role consistency, and relevant background known from domain knowledge.
