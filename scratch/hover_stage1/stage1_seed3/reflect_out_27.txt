Task Description:  
You will be given two inputs:  
- `claim`: A factual statement making specific assertions about entities such as films, actors, musicians, bands, historical events, or characters. Claims often include genre classifications, cast or membership details, chronological timings (e.g., release dates, formation dates), or quantitative comparisons (e.g., band member counts).  
- `notes`: A list of factual sentences or extracted information relevant to the claim. These notes provide partial or sometimes contradictory evidence about the entities mentioned in the claim, such as confirming or denying cast members, production studios, musical group affiliations, release years, or historical facts.

Your goal is to produce a single output:  
- `query`: A precise, focused, and minimal question (or a set of closely related questions) that directly targets the missing or unclear factual information needed to verify or refute the `claim` based on the `notes`.

Key Strategies and Considerations:  
1. **Analyze the Claim Carefully:**  
   - Identify all important factual points or relationships that the claim asserts (e.g., actor participation, genre classification, band membership, temporal sequences).  
   - Detect points requiring disambiguation, especially where multiple entities with similar names or overlapping roles might exist.

2. **Leverage the Notes to Focus the Query:**  
   - Use the notes to understand which aspects of the claim are already confirmed, contradicted, or missing.  
   - Avoid restating information that can already be directly verified in the notes; focus on gaps or uncertain elements.

3. **Formulate Focused, Minimal Queries:**  
   - Each question should aim to retrieve only the necessary missing factoid(s), avoiding vague or overly broad queries.  
   - Include contextual specifics such as:  
     - Full names of entities (e.g., full movie titles with years, complete band or group names).  
     - Precise event years or release dates to disambiguate between versions or iterations.  
     - Roles or relationships that need verification (e.g., confirming if a named actor played a specific role in a given movie).

4. **Domain-Specific Fact Considerations:**  
   - **Film Domain:**  
     - Movies may be classified into nuanced genres (family, comedy, adventure, action, drama). Explicitly confirm exact genre classifications when relevant.  
     - Verify precise casting details, including voice actors linked to specific characters and film releases.  
     - Confirm production studios or production companies associated with films.  
     - Temporal relations often hinge on exact release dates or event sequences within films.  
   - **Music Domain:**  
     - Group membership is critical — confirm whether a person appeared in a group/band, not just solo works.  
     - Band or group member counts matter for comparison claims.  
     - Musical genre classifications can vary; confirm or clarify exact genre labels of bands or artists.  
     - Verify existence and attribution of specific recordings such as EPs, albums, or singles to particular artists.  
   - **Historical / Military Domain:**  
     - Verify the formation or establishment dates of military units or regiments when a temporal claim is made.  
     - Confirm the specific regiment or unit featured in a film or historical depiction.  
   - **Character or Entity Disambiguation:**  
     - Verify which version, adaptation, or year a character appears in, particularly if multiple entities share the same name.  
     - Clarify roles or status if multiple similarly named entities exist (e.g., different actors playing "Gary Oldman" characters).

5. **Output Format:**  
   - Compose the `query` as one or more concise questions.  
   - Questions must be clear, unambiguous, and reference the entities with sufficient context to disambiguate them.  
   - When multiple factual points must be verified, include all relevant questions together, but keep them as minimal and precise as possible.

Example Template for Queries:  
- Did [Full Entity Name with Year] have [specific actor/character/member] involved?  
- What is the genre classification of [movie/band name with year or additional details]?  
- When was [military unit/band/movie] established or released?  
- Has [artist name] ever appeared as a member of a group band?  
- Which production company produced [movie name/year]?  
- Who is the Irish artist that released the EP titled "[EP Name]" and did they belong to a group band?

By following this detailed, domain-specific, and focused approach, the generated `query` will retrieve the necessary evidence to reliably confirm or refute the claim using the available or retrievable factual documents.
