## Generated Outputs
### reasoning
The claim states that the first film scored by a certain music composer was "Veyyil," and that same composer also composed the soundtrack for another film featuring Ashrita Shetty. To verify this, we need to confirm two things:
1. The composer who first scored "Veyyil."
2. Whether this composer also composed for a film in which Ashrita Shetty acted.

Thus, the query must look up the composer of "Veyyil," check if it was their first film as a composer, identify films featuring Ashrita Shetty, and see if any of those films share the same composer.

### query
SELECT Composer.name AS composer_name, 
       Composer.first_film AS first_film_composed, 
       Ashrita_film.title AS ashrita_film_title, 
       Ashrita_film.composer AS ashrita_film_composer
FROM 
  (SELECT name, 
          (SELECT MIN(film.release_year) FROM film 
           JOIN film_composer fc ON film.id = fc.film_id
           WHERE fc.composer_id = Composer.id) AS first_film
   FROM Composer
   WHERE name IN (SELECT composer_name FROM film_composer fc 
                  JOIN film f ON fc.film_id = f.id 
                  WHERE f.title = 'Veyyil')
  ) AS Composer
JOIN 
  (SELECT f.title, fc.composer_name
   FROM film f
   JOIN film_composer fc ON f.id = fc.film_id
   JOIN film_cast fcst ON f.id = fcst.film_id
   JOIN Actor a ON fcst.actor_id = a.id
   WHERE a.name = 'Ashrita Shetty'
  ) AS Ashrita_film
ON Composer.name = Ashrita_film.composer_name
WHERE Composer.first_film = 'Veyyil';
