Task Description:

You are tasked with generating precise, well-formed natural language search queries that enable the retrieval of authoritative documents or data to verify or refute a given factual claim. Each claim may involve multiple components including named entities (persons, works, places), dates, relationships, and contextual facts.

Input Format:

- claim: A statement that asserts one or more factual points requiring verification.
- notes: A list of text excerpts or informational snippets from various sources possibly containing supporting, contradictory, or incomplete information about the claim.

Output Requirements:

- Generate one or more detailed natural language queries designed to efficiently retrieve documents or authoritative sources needed to fully verify all elements of the claim.
- If the claim is compound or contains multiple parts, queries should either explicitly incorporate all key parts or be split into multiple clearly framed queries addressing each part.
- Queries must explicitly reference key entities, relationships, dates, titles, or contextual details present in the claim (e.g., nationalities, roles, works, collaborations).
- Queries should be comprehensive enough to cover critical verification aspects, but focused and precise to maximize retrieval relevance.
- They should aim to retrieve data on people (e.g., professions, filmography, roles), works (e.g., films, albums, documentaries; release dates; creative credits), relationships (e.g., collaborations, authorship, directing), and other relevant metadata.
- When conflicting or missing information appears in notes (e.g., ambiguous death dates or uncertain teacher-student relationships), craft queries to resolve those ambiguities by targeting all plausible possibilities.
- Queries must be structured to target authoritative sources or data repositories (e.g., reliable filmography databases, biography records, production details, casting lists).

Domain-Specific Considerations:

- For persons: Queries should consider multiple attributes—nationality, professions (e.g., actor, director), known works, voice roles, or collaborations—to confirm identities and roles.
- For artistic works: Include titles (original and translated), type of work (film, documentary, TV series, animation), release or production years, and key contributor names.
- For events or works related data: Confirm production years, collaborations between named entities, roles in creative works (e.g., co-directors, co-writers), and relationships between works.
- When verifying whether two entities share an attribute (e.g., a person starring in certain films, or two films produced in the same year), queries should reflect the intersection or comparative nature.
- For ambiguous claims involving possible errors or conflations (e.g., misattributions of roles or links between unrelated works), queries should target detailed cast/crew lists, biography details, and verified collaborations.

Generalizable Strategy:

1. Extract all named entities and relationships from the claim, including people, works, dates, and contextual identifiers.
2. Cross-reference with notes to identify which claim components are confirmed, contradicted, or lacking concrete information.
3. Focus queries on retrieving authoritative evidence that resolves uncertainties and confirms or denies each key element of the claim.
4. Deploy separate or combined queries depending on complexity: address multi-part claims thoroughly by covering each aspect and resolving overlaps.
5. Formulate queries in natural language to emulate typical, comprehensive fact-checker searches aimed at databases, knowledge bases, or search engines.
6. Use precise language targeting metadata: e.g., “voice actors for character X in film Y (released YEAR)”, “collaborations between PERSON A and PERSON B on film TITLE”, “production year of DOCUMENTARY TITLE”, or “filmography and nationality of ACTOR NAME”.

Example Query Templates:

- "Who voiced the character [Character Name] in the [Year] animated film [Film Title]?"
- "Filmography and nationality of actors starring in [Film Title] (Year)"
- "Did [Person Name], a [Nationality] actor from [Work A], voice any character in [Year] animated films?"
- "Verification of director credits for film [Title] and involvement of [Person Name]"
- "Production year of documentary [Documentary Title] and director credits"
- "Are [Film Title A] and [Film Title B] both produced in year [Year]?"
- "Co-writers and directors of TV series [Series Title] and their film directing credits"
- "Collaborations between [Person A] and [Person B] in [Film or Documentary Title]"

Summary:

You will analyze claims for key factual components and generate clear, explicit natural language queries that, when executed in authoritative knowledge sources, yield evidence to verify or refute the claims fully. Your queries must be detailed, precise, and designed to resolve ambiguities or incomplete information, especially for complex or multi-part claims involving biographical data, creative works, production details, collaborations, and temporal facts.
