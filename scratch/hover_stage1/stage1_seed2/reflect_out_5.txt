Task Description:

You will be given three fields as input:

- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion that needs to be verified or analyzed.
- `notes`: A list of notes or facts extracted from source documents related to the claim. Some notes may confirm or refute parts of the claim; others may provide contextual or background information.
- `context`: A list of source document excerpts or summary descriptions relevant to the claim, providing the supporting knowledge base.

Your job is to produce two output fields:

1. `new_notes`: A concise, coherent summary list of key fact-based notes derived from the input `notes` and `context` that:
   - Directly address the truthfulness or validity of the claim’s components,
   - Summarize evidence supporting or contradicting the claim,
   - Highlight any contradictions or confusions found in the claim based on the information,
   - Remove excessive repetition or superfluous details from the input `notes`,
   - Reflect only information supported by the given context and notes.

2. `titles`: A list of the titles (or names) of the most relevant documents or entities from the context used to verify or refute the claim. This should be a minimal but sufficient set of sources central to the reasoning.

Reasoning and Approach:

- Break down the claim into constituent factual assertions.
- Use the notes and context to verify each part, identifying supporting, contradicting, or missing evidence.
- Pay close attention to niche or domain-specific facts (e.g., sports roles and terminology, historical foundation dates, music band discographies, individual biographies, etc.).
- Carefully distinguish between names, dates, titles, roles, and technical terms relevant to the claim’s subject matter.
- Avoid assuming or introducing information outside the provided context.
- When evidence is insufficient to verify the claim, state this clearly in the new_notes.
- The selected titles should correspond to documents or entities from the context most essential to supporting your verification and reasoning.
- The output should be clear, factual, and logically consistent, aiming to support a final judgment on the claim’s validity, though you need not explicitly say whether the claim is true or false.

Additional detailed domain information to consider:

- For sports-related claims, understand specific player roles (e.g., goalkeepers vs. forwards in football), team histories, nicknames, and seasons.
- For musical groups or album release claims, understand band aliases, release counts, and record labels.
- For academic or institutional claims, pay attention to foundation dates, institutional name changes, biographical data regarding individuals’ affiliations and training.

In summary, your task is fact verification and summarization: synthesize key evidence from supplied notes and context to produce factual summaries and document titles crucial for evaluating the claim, tailored to diverse specialized domains.
