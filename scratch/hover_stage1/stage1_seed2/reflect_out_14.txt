Task Description:
You will be provided with three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your task is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`. 

Input fields explanation:
- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion that may require verification or clarification.
- `notes`: A list of preliminary facts, observations, or points relevant to the claim, drawn from preliminary data or partial evidence.
- `context`: A list of detailed factual passages, background information, or resource excerpts that elaborate on entities, events, dates, or relationships related to the claim.

Output fields to produce:
- `new_notes`: A concise, accurate, and factual set of key evidence-based notes derived from the `notes` and `context`, that directly address the validity, explanation, or correction of the `claim`. These should highlight the most relevant and verified pieces of information confirming or refuting the claim, ideally removing redundancies and contradictions, and focusing on core factual details supported by the `context`.
- `titles`: A list of relevant document, entity, or topic titles from the `context` that are critical to understanding or verifying the claim—these act as focused "retrieval targets" or factual anchors that correspond to the core knowledge needed in the claim domain.

Detailed task instructions and domain-specific guidance:
1. Analyze the `claim` carefully to understand the factual assertion(s) involved, including named entities, dates, locations, roles, relationships, and events.
2. Review the `notes` for preliminary relevant facts, but verify and refine these against the authoritative and more detailed `context`.
3. Use information in the `context` to:
   - Confirm or refute specific parts of the claim, or clarify ambiguities.
   - Identify factual corrections for inaccuracies in the claim.
   - Extract relevant and domain-specific facts such as:
      - Names, dates, roles, and affiliations of persons and organizations.
      - Specific historical events (e.g., sports events, film release years, WWII concentration camp commandants).
      - Relationships between entities (e.g., family ties, organizational relationships).
      - Geographical or venue details linked to claims (e.g., stadium locations, club home grounds).
   - Recognize distinctions between closely related factual items (e.g., differentiate between negotiation conductors vs. commandants, movie sequels unrelated to the film in question).
4. Summarize and rationalize the key points into a clear and succinct `new_notes` list that directly support or contradict the claim.
   - Remove duplicated or less relevant notes.
   - Ensure clarity in attribution of facts (e.g., who conducted negotiations, location of stadiums).
   - Focus on verifiable and documented information based on `context`.
5. For `titles`, select the minimal set of key documents or topics from the `context` that represent core knowledge sources justified in the reasoning process or necessary for fact verification.
   - Titles should reflect primary entities, key event venues, important persons, or significant thematic topics.
6. Your reasoning should be internally consistent and based on context-confirmed facts rather than assumptions.
7. Maintain a neutral, objective tone focusing on factual correctness.

Generalizable methodology:
- Treat the task as a focused evidence synthesis and fact-checking operation.
- Contrast initial claims with detailed contextual data.
- Identify and extract authoritative facts from context to support accurate knowledge notes.
- Present concise, factually-grounded notes enabling clear understanding of claim veracity.
- Select relevant titles as anchors for knowledge retrieval and validation.

Domain-specific knowledge examples to keep in mind:
- Geographical locations and club associations (e.g., Dynamo Kyiv is based in Kyiv, Ukraine not England).
- Historical film release dates and composer associations for film score attribution.
- WWII historical facts such as roles of Rudolf Höss (commandant) versus Kurt Gerstein (negotiator).
- Major sports event venues with their correct countries (e.g., UEFA Euro 2012 Final at Olympic Stadium Kyiv).
- Distinguishing between entities with similar names but different roles or timelines.

Your outputs will assist in structured verification and knowledge retrieval workflows, supporting accurate claim validation in niche factual domains like sports venues, historical biographies, and filmography.
