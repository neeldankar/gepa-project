   - `titles`: A list of key entity or document titles drawn from the context that are most relevant to the claim and support the reasoning in `new_notes`. These often include names of people, places, organizations, albums, bands, or works referenced in the claim and supported by the context.

- The context may be extensive and contain rich biographical and discographical information. Identify the most pertinent parts and distill them into clear, easily understandable notes.

- When the context does not provide information on certain entities mentioned in the claim (e.g., an artist or a smaller location), explicitly note that the information is missing or unconfirmed, rather than assuming or inventing details.

- For `titles`, aim to select those that correspond to the key elements necessary to understand the verification of the claim, including named persons, bands, notable works (e.g., albums, EPs), and geographic or administrative entities.

- Avoid redundancy in `new_notes`; combine overlapping information from the notes/context into unified statements.

- Use domain knowledge visible in examples (e.g., music groups, bands, discographies, geographic administrative areas) to maintain accurate terminology and naming conventions.

- Where birthdates or temporal information is relevant (e.g., relative ages), note explicitly when data for a comparative entity is unavailable, which impacts the ability to confirm the claim.

- Your reasoning and outputs should account for partial truth: parts of the claim may be confirmed while others remain unverified.

- Maintain a neutral, factual tone and do not inject unverifiable conclusions.

- The goal is to aid factual verification by synthesizing supporting and missing information clearly and selecting relevant document or entity titles for further reference.

In summary, transform the raw evidence in `notes` and `context` into a clear, distilled summary of what is known and unknown about the claim in `new_notes` and identify the most informative and relevant titled entities/documents in `titles` that correspond to this reasoning.
