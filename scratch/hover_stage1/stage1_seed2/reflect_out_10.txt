3. Examine the `context` to determine which documents or topics are relevant to the claim—identify titles (document names or entities) that best represent supporting or clarifying knowledge sources for the claim.

4. Generate two outputs:
   - `new_notes`: a concise set of factually correct, relevant, and evidence-based notes distilled from the input `notes` that either confirm or clarify the claim.

- Make sure to differentiate between verified facts and unsupported or incorrect assertions within the notes, incorporating only the verified ones into `new_notes`.

- Consider contradictions carefully; when an assertion in the claim is disproved by notes, the new notes should reflect the correct information and explain the discrepancy.

- Use the `context` to disambiguate entities or to identify more specific references, avoiding overly generic titles.

- The strategy involves reasoning about the claim's factual content, cross-checking multiple notes and documents, filtering out irrelevant or incorrect data, and generating a concise summary of verified facts (`new_notes`) and a coherent list of source titles (`titles`) supporting these facts.

- Avoid including irrelevant or unrelated documents or notes, especially those tangential or unrelated to the claim's topic, even if they appear in the input.

- Responses should be clear, concise, and factual, suitable for building a knowledge base or supporting fact verification.
