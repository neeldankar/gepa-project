Task Description:
You will be provided with three key inputs: 
- `claim`: a statement asserting a specific factual or historical event, often involving multiple entities (people, organizations, events, or media works) and their relationships, roles, timelines, or affiliations.
- `notes`: a list of extracted factual observations and details—some supporting, some contradicting—related to the claim, drawn from supplied background knowledge.
- `context`: detailed source excerpts or documents offering authoritative evidence, biographies, timelines, organizational memberships, media credits, and event descriptions relevant to the entities and claims.

Your objective is to rigorously analyze all inputs and produce two outputs:
1. `new_notes` — a concise, distilled set of key factual points that directly support or contradict the claim. These notes must be:
   - Precisely grounded in the given `context`, not speculative or outside information.
   - Synthesized from the original `notes` combined with corroborating `context`, with redundancy removed.
   - Focused on the critical facts relevant to validating or refuting the claim, such as verifying entity identities, relationships, timelines, roles, or event outcomes.
   - Clear about which parts of the claim are supported or disproven, especially resolving any contradictions.

2. `titles` — a list of the most relevant document or entity titles drawn from the provided `context`. Include:
   - Key persons (e.g., actors, athletes, directors, politicians) directly involved in or connected to the claim.
   - Organizations, sports clubs, or media productions that provide crucial background or evidence.
   - Events or concepts critical for understanding the claim.
   - Titles selected should optimally cover all essential evidence needed for assessment.

Domain-Specific Considerations:
- Claims typically involve complex relationships and precise chronology: for example, sports seasons and winners, film cast and crew with their nationalities and roles, or political figures and events with dates.
- Context documents include biographies, historical timelines, championship summaries, filmographies, organizational memberships, sponsorship details, and media credits.
- Discerning exact roles (such as director vs. actor), nationality, or seasons/years aligned with events is crucial.
- When multiple racing series, film productions, or events are mentioned, carefully distinguish them by name, season/year, and relevant entities.
- Identify any misattributions or common confusions (e.g., wrong nationality, wrong director) and clarify them using the evidence.
- Focus on named entities explicitly supported by the context: if an entity is missing or contradicted, state that.

Generalizable Strategy:
- Systematically cross-reference the claim against the notes and all provided context, verifying:
   - Entity identities, affiliations, and roles.
   - Chronological accuracy—dates of birth, event years, seasons.
   - Award winners or principal participants in events or media as claimed.
- Extract and integrate only the most relevant facts, avoiding repetition or irrelevant details.
- Identify and resolve contradictory points by pointing out which facts are unsupported or disproven.
- Select the minimum yet comprehensive set of context titles/entities that substantiate or contextualize the claim.

Input Format:
- `claim`: A textual factual assertion.
- `notes`: A list of strings, each a factual observation or detail related to the claim.
- `context`: A list of text excerpts or document summaries providing evidence related to the entities or events.

Output Format:
- `new_notes`: A concise, fact-based list of distilled notes directly relevant for confirming or refuting the claim.
- `titles`: A list of the most pertinent context document/entity titles necessary to understand and verify the claim.

Important:
- All outputs must be explicitly supported or directly derived from the given `context` and `notes`.
- Avoid introducing any external knowledge not present in the inputs.
- Maintain clarity, precision, and relevance to facilitate efficient fact-checking and summarization.
