You will be provided with three input fields:

- `claim`: A statement or assertion that links one or more entities, their attributes, professions, collaborations, or classifications.
- `notes`: A list of sentences or facts extracted from some knowledge source that pertain to the claim. These notes may contain overlapping, redundant, or partially contradictory information.
- `context`: A list of longer, structured summaries or encyclopedia-style entries about the entities, works, or topics mentioned in the claim.

Your task is to analyze and synthesize the provided information to produce two output fields:

1. `new_notes`: A concise, factually accurate, and relevant subset of statements distilled primarily from the given `notes` and supported or clarified by the `context`. These notes should:
   - Summarize or confirm the truthfulness of the claim by referencing verifiable information.
   - Include specific factual details such as:
     - Correct professions or roles of persons (e.g., musician, vocalist, actress).
     - Accurate genre classifications of films or music (e.g., animated adventure, family comedy action adventure).
     - Confirmed collaborations or relationships (e.g., co-writing songs, record label associations).
     - Correct dates or timelines, if specified (e.g., release years, collaboration durations).
     - Clarifications or contradictions to claims when the claim is inaccurate or partially inaccurate.
   - Avoid redundancies and contradictions.
   - Be precise about subtle distinctions, e.g., that a film is an American family comedy adventure but not strongly action-oriented, or a record label being American rather than British.

2. `titles`: A list of the most relevant document or article titles that should be associated with the claim to provide authoritative or supporting background. In selecting titles:
   - Prioritize documents that directly discuss the main entities or topics in the claim.
   - Include the names of key people, works (songs, films), organizations (bands, labels), or concepts relevant to the claim.
   - Cover all key elements mentioned in the claim to fully contextualize it.
   - Titles should be specific enough to distinguish among closely related entries (e.g., include years for multiple film versions).

Your reasoning should:

- Interpret the claim carefully, identifying its core assertions about people, professions, collaborations, or classifications.
- Cross-check the claim against the provided notes and context, noting whether facts support, partially support, or contradict it.
- Identify any gaps, inconsistencies, or ambiguities and reflect these in the new_notes through qualified language.
- Explain your rationale clearly so that the new_notes and titles logically follow from the evidence.

Special domain knowledge considerations:

- When dealing with films and characters (e.g., Disney-related), accurately differentiate between various versions of films (animated, live-action, direct-to-video sequels), their genres, and character roles.
- In music-related claims, be careful to distinguish between bands, band members, record labels (including nationality), and collaborations or co-writing credits.
- Recognize that labels, bands, or artists might share names or have multiple associations; specificity matters.
- Recognize professions such as musician, singer, songwriter, actress, and make distinctions where relevant.
- For collaboration claims, confirm specific involvement, co-writing, or tenure duration only if clearly supported.
- For genre classifications, note exact genres and whether "action," "comedy," "adventure," or other descriptors are explicitly affirmed or only implied.

General methodology:

- Use the notes to extract granular facts.
- Validate and enhance these facts by consulting the more detailed context entries.
- Summarize and refine the combined knowledge into succinct, non-redundant new_notes.
- Select titles that comprehensively cover the scope of the claim and aid later retrieval or verification.
- Highlight any misinformation and clarify the correct facts.

Output format:

- `new_notes`: A list of clear, direct factual statements relevant to the claim.
- `titles`: A list of document or article titles corresponding to these facts and the claim's topic.
- Include a reasoning field describing the analytical process and justification for outputs.

The goal is to produce a factually grounded, concise, and comprehensive synthesis that confirms, qualifies, or refutes the input claim based on the provided notes and context.
