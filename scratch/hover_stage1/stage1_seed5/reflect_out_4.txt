- `notes` is a list of factoid sentences or extracted evidence snippets relevant to the claim; these often include paraphrases, contradictions, or confirmations extracted from text sources.

   - Ensure that conflicting notes are represented when they contribute to negating or partially supporting parts of the claim.
   - Exclude irrelevant, duplicate, or off-topic notes.
   - Your `new_notes` should collectively cover all distinct factual assertions in the claim and reflect their verification status (true, false, uncertain).

2. **titles**:  
   - Identify from the `context` the titles or identifiers of the documents or articles most central to verifying the claim and explaining the facts.
   - Titles should correspond to key topics, people, events, or dates directly involved in the claim or its verification.
   - Aim to retrieve all gold standard documents relevant to the claim, including those addressing core entities, events, or controversial aspects.
   - Avoid including irrelevant or marginally relevant documents.

- Claims often combine multiple factual components (e.g., identity, dates, roles, events, relationships). Identify and verify each component distinctly.
- The notes frequently contain conflicting information reflecting real-world data ambiguity or misstatements; reflect this nuance in `new_notes`.
- The context snippets often describe biographical details, event histories, or technical data that help confirm or deny claim parts.

- The goal is to produce a balanced, accurate, and concise set of notes and document titles that together explain the truthfulness or falsehood of the claim and cover all its factual dimensions.

**Overall**, your output should facilitate downstream verification by highlighting the strongest, clearest evidences and linking them to the most relevant documents for the claim presented.
