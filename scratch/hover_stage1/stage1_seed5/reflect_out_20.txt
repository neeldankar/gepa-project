Task Description:
You will be given three input fields: `claim` (a statement that asserts a factual or historical event), `notes` (a list of extracted factual observations and supporting or contradictory details related to the claim, based on provided contextual knowledge), and `context` (background documents or excerpts that provide detailed evidence relevant to the claim and related entities or events).

Your goal is to analyze these inputs carefully and output two fields:
1. `new_notes` — a distilled, concise list of key factual points that directly support or contradict the claim, derived from the original `notes` and corroborated by `context`. Avoid repetition, and focus on the crux facts relevant to confirming or refuting the claim.
2. `titles` — a list of relevant document or entity titles from the `context` that are most pertinent to understanding the claim and its fact-checking. Include entities such as key people, organizations, events, or concepts referenced in the claim or notes.

Domain-Specific Considerations:
- The claims often involve detailed facts about sports clubs, historical figures, film and TV personalities, or political events. These claims frequently connect multiple entities, their relationships, affiliations, timelines, or narratives of dispute.
- Evidence in `context` includes biographical summaries, historical timelines, organizational memberships, sponsorship details, and media credits.
- The notes usually reflect attempts to validate or refute specific parts of the claim, including namedropping precise organizations (e.g., football leagues), dates (e.g., presidencies), or roles in media productions.

Generalizable Strategy:
- Systematically cross-check factual claims against multiple context documents focusing on verifying entity relationships, chronology, and roles.
- Extract and synthesize critical factual points that directly support or contradict the claim, pruning less relevant or redundant observations.
- Identify and include the most central documents or titles that provide authoritative evidence related to the claim.
- When contradictions arise, clarify which parts are unsupported or disproven based on reliable contextual information.
- Maintain clarity and precision in both `new_notes` and `titles` to facilitate efficient fact-checking or summarization.

Input format:
- `claim`: A textual claim making a factual assertion.
- `notes`: A list of factoids or statements derived or extracted from context relevant to the claim verification.
- `context`: A list of text snippets providing background information on entities, events, or concepts related to the claim.

Output format:
- `new_notes`: Concise, factually precise, and relevant distilled notes.
- `titles`: The most relevant document/entity titles from the context that substantiate or relate to the claim.

Ensure all outputs are grounded in evidence present in the given `context` and supported by `notes`.
