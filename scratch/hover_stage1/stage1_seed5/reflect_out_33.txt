Task Description:  
You will be given three input fields:  
- `claim`: A factual or historical statement involving multiple entities (often from domains such as sports clubs, historical figures, film and TV personalities, political events, or cultural artifacts). The claim may assert relationships, roles, timelines, affiliations, or specific event participation.  
- `notes`: A list of extracted factual observations drawn from the provided context that relate to the claim. These notes may include details that support, contradict, or fail to confirm aspects of the claim. They might contain references to persons, organizations, dates, works (films/books), awards, or locations relevant to verifying the claim.  
- `context`: Background textual material (often excerpts or summaries) providing detailed evidence on entities, events, or concepts connected to the claim. This can include biographical data, historical timelines, organizational memberships, filmographies, geographical information, award histories, or other domain-specific knowledge.

Your Goal:  
Analyze the `claim` in light of the `notes` and carefully corroborate or refute it using the `context`. Produce two outputs:

1. `new_notes`:  
- Distill and synthesize the original notes and the context-derived evidence into a concise set of factual points.  
- These should clearly focus on key evidence that either supports or contradicts the claim, pruning repetition and omitting less relevant details.  
- Highlight entity relationships, chronology, roles, and affiliations crucial to confirming/refuting the claim.  
- If contradictions or uncertainties arise, explicitly note which aspects are unsupported or disproven.  
- Maintain clarity, precision, and factual accuracy grounded strictly in the supplied context and notes.

2. `titles`:  
- Provide a list of the most pertinent document titles or entity names from the context that are central to understanding or verifying the claim.  
- Include key persons, organizations, events, awards, places, or cultural works that the claim or notes reference.  
- This helps indicate authoritative sources or primary evidence relevant to the fact-checking.

Domain-Specific Considerations:  
- Claims often intersect multiple domains (e.g., sports history, literature prizes, filmographies, architectural works, political history), requiring validation of complex inter-entity relationships.  
- Familiarity with domain-specific entities may be implicit: e.g., "poet-diplomat" referring to poets who served as diplomats, or "Nobel Prize in Literature" laureates; sports clubs affiliated with particular leagues; films tied to specific novels or actors; architectural works associated with sculptors or locations.  
- Chronological accuracy is essential, such as verifying event dates, award years, or tenure periods.  
- Some claims reference nuanced or less commonly known facts (e.g., existence of an Austrian poet-diplomat Nobel laureate, or the specific cathedral an artist contributed to), so absence or presence of mention in context is in itself significant.  
- When multiple possible entities share a name (e.g., multiple "St Paul's Cathedral"), verify that the claim references the correct one, based on geographic or contextual clues.

Generalizable Strategy:  
- Methodically cross-check each facet of the claim against multiple contextual sources focusing on:  
    • Entity relationships (e.g., person to organization, actor to role, sculptor to building).  
    • Chronology and timelines to ensure dates align or conflicts are noted.  
    • Domain-specific roles and affiliations (e.g., club membership, award recipient).  
- Synthesize notes so that only the essential, claim-relevant evidence remains, avoiding redundant or tangential facts.  
- Identify and output the core context documents or entities that form the foundational evidence base for the claim verification.  
- Explicitly clarify when evidence is absent or contradicts the claim.  
- Use precise, clear language that accurately reflects what the evidence does and does not support.

Input Format:  
- `claim`: string  
- `notes`: list of strings (factoids, observations related to claim verification)  
- `context`: list of strings (excerpts, summaries, or definitions providing evidence)

Output Format:  
- `new_notes`: list of concise, claim-relevant factual points distilled from notes and context  
- `titles`: list of document or entity titles from context critical to understanding/verifying the claim

Ensure all outputs are strictly grounded in the evidence present in the given `context` and supported by `notes`. Avoid speculation or information not supported by these inputs.

This task requires not just fact extraction but precise synthesis of multi-domain, multi-entity evidence for accurate claim verification.
