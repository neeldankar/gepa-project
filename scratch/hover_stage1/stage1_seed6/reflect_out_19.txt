- `notes` is a list of extracted supporting or contradictory pieces of information relevant to the claim, often summarizing or clarifying relevant facts.
- `context` is a list of background textual passages or document excerpts providing additional detailed information related to entities or events mentioned in the claim.

Your task is to process the inputs to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

### Detailed Task Description:

1. **Analyze the Claim:**
   - Identify all key factual assertions or components within the claim.
   - Determine the main entities, events, or concepts involved.

2. **Evaluate and Filter Notes:**
   - Review the provided `notes` to select concise, clear, and factually relevant items that confirm or refute parts of the claim.
   - Remove redundancy and misinformation.
   - Retain notes that directly support or contradict the claim, especially those grounded in authoritative or historical data.
   - Synthesize the notes to provide a coherent factual summary that addresses the claim's components, distinguishing between supported, unsupported, or contradicted elements.

3. **Generate `new_notes`:**
   - Assemble a concise list of informative notes that:
     - Confirm true assertions within the claim.
     - Refute false or unsupported assertions.
     - Provide clarifying facts for complex or compound claims.
   - Avoid extraneous or overly detailed context unrelated to verifying the claim.
   - Ensure the notes are self-contained and understandable without additional context.

4. **Select Relevant Document Titles (`titles`):**
   - Choose the document titles or entities from the `context` that are directly relevant to the claim and the supporting/refuting notes.
   - These titles should encompass:
     - Key named entities or subjects involved in the claim (e.g., people, organizations, films, seasons).
     - Documents that provide critical evidence about the claim facts.
   - Avoid including unrelated or tangential titles.
   - Prioritize documents known to be “gold” or crucial evidence for the claim verification.

### Reasoning Approach:
- Fact-check each claim part by correlating notes with detailed context.

- Summarize conclusions clearly to produce `new_notes`.
- Link these conclusions to key relevant documents by identifying their titles in the `context`.

### Output Formatting:
- `new_notes`: A list of short, factual sentences summarizing the truthfulness and evidence about the claim.
- `titles`: A list of succinct document or entity titles that directly relate to the claim and evidence.

By following these instructions, the assistant should reliably verify claims, synthesize evidence, and identify the most relevant supporting sources from extensive and detailed input data.
