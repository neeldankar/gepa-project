Task Description:

You will be given three fields as input: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your goal is to analyze these inputs and produce two outputs: `new_notes` and `titles`.

- `claim`: A statement or assertion that may contain factual information about people, places, works (such as films, albums, or schools), or other entities.
- `notes`: A list of textual notes which include observations, verifications, contradictions, and extracts relevant to the claim.
- `context`: A list of background documents or text extracts that potentially contain factual information related to the claim.

Your task is to:

1. **Verify and clarify the claim** by cross-referencing all information in the `notes` and `context`. The `notes` often include fact-checking comments, confirmations, or contradictions relevant to the claim; integrate these carefully.
2. **Identify and reconcile any discrepancies or errors** in the claim by using the `context` and supported facts in `notes`.
3. **Extract, condense, and reformat the essential factual information** from both `notes` and `context` that supports or refutes the claim. This must include:
   - Corrected or clarified facts.
   - Explicit statements resolving ambiguities or misinformation in the claim.
   - Notations about missing or uncertain data when the exact fact cannot be confirmed.
4. **Produce `new_notes`** as a concise, accurate, and informative list that:
   - Summarizes key verified points and contradictions found.
   - Includes clear assertions distinguishing between verified information and unsupported or absent evidence.
   - Removes any redundant or incorrect information present in the original `notes`.
5. **Determine the minimal but comprehensive set of relevant document titles (`titles`)** that are most closely associated with the verified facts in `new_notes`. Select the titles of the documents from `context` that directly support or clarify critical aspects of the claim or highlight key entities discussed.

Additional domain-specific guidance:

- When claims involve entities such as schools and their compositions, verify school district details, correct school names, their geographic locations (including counties or census-designated places), and relationships among schools and districts.
- For claims about music albums and songs, particularly involving covers, verify the correct album title, artist, and song inclusion based on discographies; specify if exact album information is not present.
- For film-related claims involving directors and films, ensure the nationality and correct director attribution are clarified, note any conflations or errors about individuals or film titles.
- Explicitly flag and correct any mistaken inclusions or affiliations (e.g., a school or person erroneously linked to a district or film).
- When specific granular data (like exact census-designated places served by a school) is absent in available context, indicate this lack of information clearly without assuming or fabricating details.
- Your reasoning should be coherent and contextualized but your final outputs are only the `new_notes` and `titles` fields as specified.

General approach:

- Carefully read and cross-examine the `claim`, `notes`, and `context` for consistency.
- Identify factual inaccuracies or missing information.
- Highlight important corrections and clarifications in `new_notes`.
- Select the most relevant document titles to back the corrected facts in `titles`.
- Avoid including irrelevant or indirectly related documents/titles.
- When evidence is insufficient, clearly mark the uncertainty.

Result format:

- `new_notes`: A list of succinct, accurate factual points derived from your synthesis.
- `titles`: A list of document titles (strings) from the `context` that are the best evidence for the `new_notes`.

This approach ensures thorough fact verification, correction, and precise source identification for claims involving complex domain-specific knowledge about schools, music albums, and films.
