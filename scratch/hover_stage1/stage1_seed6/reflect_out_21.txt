- `claim` is a factual statement or assertion involving people, events, works (like songs or movies), dates, or other named entities.
- `notes` is a list of textual excerpts or facts, often containing partial or relevant information related to the claim; these could include repeated or overlapping facts.
- `context` is a list of detailed background passages, each labeled with a title and containing extended information about people, works, events, or related topics that can help verify or clarify the claim and supplement the notes.

Your task is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

1. **`new_notes`**: This should be a concise, focused subset of the original notes, synthesized to support accurate verification or explanation of the claim. From the provided notes and context, select or rewrite those notes that are factually relevant, informative, and help confirm or refute the claim clearly. Avoid including redundant or unrelated statements.

2. **`titles`**: Extract a list of key document or article titles from the `context` that are essential and sufficient to support or understand the claim or to verify the new notes selected. These titles are typically names of people, works (songs, movies, albums), events (sports tournaments), or other relevant entities. Aim to retrieve all gold-standard titles that best cover the material needed for claim verification, including major contributors, main subjects, or important related entities mentioned.

- Claims often involve named entities such as artists, athletes, actors, songs, films, albums, tournaments, or specific historical events.
- Supporting or refuting claims requires identifying who did what (e.g., who wrote a song, who acted in a movie), relationships (e.g., associations between artists and works), and contextual timelines (e.g., age when starting a career, years of events).
- `notes` may contain repetitions or near-duplicate facts; your `new_notes` should consolidate these into unique, essential points.
- `context` entries are often structured as titled paragraphs summarizing relevant information; select titles that correspond to core entities or topics directly supporting the claim.
- Ensure that the selected `titles` cover all critical factual points necessary to verify the claim, including all principal actors/entities; failure to retrieve all relevant titles results in incomplete evidence.
- When the claim involves a contradiction or misattribution (e.g., incorrect songwriter, nationality, role), use both notes and context to clarify and emphasize the correct attribution.
- The goal is to strengthen claim verification by providing precise, relevant notes and comprehensive, accurate retrieval of supporting titles from the context.

**Generalizable strategy you can apply:**

- Carefully parse the claim to identify all involved entities and factual assertions.
- Cross-check the claim against provided notes; identify which notes confirm, deny, or clarify each assertion.
- Use context passages to deepen understanding, confirm identities and roles, and resolve ambiguities or controversies.
- Create `new_notes` that summarize the verified facts cleanly and accurately without redundancy.
- Pick an exhaustive list of titles from context covering all mentioned or relevant entities, ensuring the claim's fact pattern is comprehensively represented.

By following these guidelines, your outputs will help verify claims reliably and support consistent retrieval and summary of evidence in complex, multi-entity, multi-document scenarios involving factual assertions about people, works, and events.
