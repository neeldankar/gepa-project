Task Description:

You will be given three input fields:
- `claim`: A statement or assertion that needs to be evaluated or elaborated on.
- `notes`: A list of various supporting or relevant factual sentences and pieces of information related to the claim.
- `context`: A collection of extended, often encyclopedia-style article snippets or document summaries related to entities or topics mentioned in the claim and notes.

Your goal is to produce two outputs:
1. `new_notes`: A distilled, carefully selected, and factually accurate subset of the `notes` that directly support or clarify the key points of the claim. Remove contradictions, irrelevant information, or outdated facts. When necessary, refine the notes to highlight factual accuracy, correcting or clarifying any inaccuracies present in the original notes (e.g., correcting dates, full names, or numeric facts). Your new notes should provide concise, essential facts substantiating or refining the claim based on the `notes` and `context`.
2. `titles`: A list of relevant document or entity titles extracted from the `context` that correspond to the primary subjects or key entities involved in the claim and supporting notes. This list should cover the authoritative or main sources necessary to understand or verify the claim. Aim to identify all crucial titles associated with the claim, including individuals, works (e.g., films, albums), organizations, or key terms, prioritizing completeness and relevance.

Key domain-specific nuances and strategies to follow:

- When the claim involves named individuals, confirm full names, aliases, and known attributes to ensure note accuracy.
- For temporal or numeric facts (e.g., premiere years, species counts, award numbers), cross-reference notes with context to resolve inconsistencies or errors and reflect the corrected data in your `new_notes`.
- When comparing groups or entities (e.g., species counts of plant genera), identify the exact referenced scientific names and numeric details accurately and clarify ambiguous or incomplete notes.
- When music or art related claims are involved, verify the exact contributions and side projects of artists and the prominence or recognition of awards.
- Maintain fidelity to the claim’s core assertions, but do not hesitate to explicitly note and correct minor inaccuracies in dates, titles, or factual details, explaining these with direct evidence from the context.
- Use the `context` to identify the authoritative titles; these will usually be article or document headings closely matching named entities or concepts in the claim or notes.
- Aim to identify and retrieve all critical titles relevant to corroborating the claim, acknowledging if some gold-standard documents remain unretrieved.
- The goal is a concise, accurate summary of factual evidence as `new_notes` and a comprehensive list of corresponding source titles as `titles`.

General approach:

- Carefully read and reason through the `claim` in light of both `notes` and `context`.
- Identify key facts, contradictions, or errors between claim and evidence.
- Curate `new_notes` to include only relevant and accurate details supporting or refining the claim.
- Extract all appropriate article or entity titles from `context` that substantiate the claim or are crucial for understanding.
- Present outputs clearly and succinctly.

This task requires both fact-checking and summarization skills, with domain-specific expertise in interpreting entities such as films, artists, plants, or awards. Ensure all new notes and titles reflect the most reliable and precise information available in the inputs.
