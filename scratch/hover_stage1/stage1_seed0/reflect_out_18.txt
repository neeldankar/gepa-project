Task Description:

You will be provided two inputs:  
- **claim**: a single factual assertion or a compound factual statement containing multiple related factual components that require verification.  
- **notes**: a list of factual statements or pieces of context relevant to the claim, potentially containing domain-specific information, indirect evidence, dates, names, titles, taxonomic data, production credits, etc.

Your goal is to generate one concise, precise, and natural language **query** that interrogates all key factual elements and their interconnected relationships in the claim, utilizing the information in the notes. The query will serve to verify or refute the full claim efficiently by retrieving authoritative evidence.

---

Detailed Instructions:

1. **Claim Analysis:**
   - Break down the claim into all distinct factual assertions, including entities, dates, relationships, roles, and temporal elements.  
   - Detect implicit or explicit relationships between these assertions that need joint verification to determine claim truthfulness.  
   - Examples of entities include: full names of persons (with roles, e.g., writers, directors, composers, actors, producers, coaches), titles of works (films, books, awards), specific organizations/units (sports teams, military regiments), taxonomic classifications (genus and species counts), corporate entities and locations.

2. **Utilizing Notes:**
   - Review notes carefully to extract domain-specific facts, including:
     - Film details: release years, director(s), cast, composer, sequels or related works.
     - Literary elements: names of authors, literary awards, nationalities, roles.
     - Scientific/botanical data: genus names, number of species, vernacular names, taxonomic status.
     - Corporate information: company names, nationalities, headquarters.
     - Sports data: teams, roles of staff, conference names, season years.
     - Historical/military data: units’ origins, campaigns, merged units.
   - Your query should acknowledge and incorporate these details where relevant to maximize the likelihood of accurate verification.

3. **Query Formulation Principles:**
   - Formulate a **single, explicit, fact-checking question** that fully covers all key factual elements *and* their logical interconnections in one integrated statement; avoid splitting into multiple queries.  
   - Reference domain-specific entities explicitly and fully, including roles and contextual details (e.g., “the 442nd Regimental Combat Team,” “Lane Nishikawa’s 2006 film ‘Only the Brave’,” or “David Shire’s score for the 1985 film ‘Return to Oz’”).  
   - Structure the query to be precise and unambiguous, leaving no room for vagueness.  
   - Include relevant temporal information as applicable (e.g., specific years, timeframes).  
   - When a claim involves comparative or sequential events (e.g., release dates), embed this logic clearly in the question.

4. **Examples and Templates:**
   - For verifying film and director claims:  
     “Did [Director full name] direct the [Year] film titled [Film name], which features [specific entity or unit], and did [specific unit] originate or begin operations in [year]?”  
   
   - For verifying sequential film releases and composer credits:  
     “Was [Film A] released before the 1985 film [Film B], described as a sequel or partial sequel to [Reference Film], for which [Composer] composed the score?”  
   
   - For claims mixing cast and production facts:  
     “Does [Film X] star [Actor/Actress name] who won acclaim for [Role] in [Reference Film] by [Director], and did the producer of [Film X] also produce [Film Y]?”  
   
5. **General Strategy:**
   - Consolidate all factual components into one comprehensive fact-checking query.  
   - Use formal and direct language optimal for querying knowledge bases or search systems.  
   - Ensure the query covers all verification points present in the claim without redundancy or omission.

---

This task demands careful attention to domain-specific factual details and the ability to synthesize multiple connected facts into a precise, fact-verifying question. Your output query will enhance downstream retrieval of authoritative evidence that either confirms or refutes all aspects of the claim simultaneously and efficiently.
