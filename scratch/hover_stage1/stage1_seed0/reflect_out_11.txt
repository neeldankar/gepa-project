You will be given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- `claim`: A factual assertion or statement requiring verification, clarification, or correction. It may involve specific details such as names, dates, entities, affiliations, event locations, or negation/refutation.
- `notes`: A collection of textual evidences, observations, extracted facts, or prior findings that may relate to the claim. These notes may include partial, redundant, or conflicting information.
- `context`: A set of background documents or excerpts containing detailed factual information that can support or contradict aspects of the claim and notes.

Your task is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

---

### 1. new_notes

- From the input `notes`, select and consolidate a concise subset of relevant, accurate, and non-contradictory facts that directly address the components of the `claim`.
- Prioritize notes that provide direct evidence supporting or refuting specific factual elements of the claim, such as:
  - Precise names and their relationships (e.g., singer, songwriter, director, institution, ownership).
  - Dates or timeframes relevant to events or works.
  - Locations, venues, or organizational hierarchies (e.g., hotel ownership and event venues).
  - Explicit clarifications or corrections where the claim may contain errors or ambiguities.
- Exclude notes that are speculative, irrelevant, redundant, or do not contribute substantial factual clarification.
- When the claim involves multiple entities or layered affiliations (e.g., provincial vs. federal political parties, band genres, awards), differentiate these clearly and highlight correct affiliations based on context.
- If the claim is about negation or correction (e.g., disproving a genre classification or refuting an alleged connection), explicitly highlight this in the consolidated notes.
- Avoid repeating almost identical facts; merge similar information into singular, precise statements.

---

### 2. titles

- Identify and list titles, names, or identifiers of the key documents or principal entities from the `context` that are critical for evaluating the claim and underpin the facts in `new_notes`.
- The titles should represent primary factual sources that validate the selected notes or provide essential background to understanding or verifying the claim.
- Include documents/entities that cover relevant specific details such as:  
  - Official discographies, filmographies, or event histories  
  - Authoritative biographies or role descriptions (e.g., musicians, actors, directors)  
  - Venue descriptions or ownership details for places of performance or events  
  - Award or certification documents when verifying achievements  
  - Specific works or productions referenced in the claim (albums, films, shows)  
- Avoid including marginally related, tangential, or irrelevant documents.

---

### Additional domain-specific guidelines:

- Understand the hierarchical or affiliative relationships among entities (e.g., youth wings vs. main political parties; multiple albums or shows linked to an artist).
- Carefully cross-reference numeric or temporal data such as chart positions, release dates, or award years.
- When notes mention artists covering songs, confirm whether the chart achievements correspond to the original or the cover version.
- For verifying debut appearances or roles, confirm the match between the artist, work, and director, paying close attention to nationality or other qualifiers if given.
- For venues and events, verify both the specific auditorium/location and the ownership details of the venue or parent entity, as claims often link these aspects.
- When multiple similar notes exist from the input, synthesize to avoid redundancy while preserving all core factual claims.
- Treat speculative or unconfirmed information as unsupported unless firmly evidenced in the context.

---

### Reasoning approach (to apply systematically):

- Carefully parse the claim to identify all factual subcomponents for verification.
- Cross-check each fact or assertion from the `notes` against the `context` to verify its accuracy and resolve contradictions.
- Filter out notes not backed or contradicted by the `context`.
- Consolidate relevant notes into concise, factually precise statements directly addressing the claim’s components.
- Identify and list all key documents or named entities most critical to substantiating or refuting the claim.
- Ensure that the outputs allow clear traceability from claim to evidence, for confident interpretation.

By following these detailed instructions, your output will provide a focused, well-supported factual synthesis suitable for rigorous claim evaluation across varied domains involving complex entity relationships and detailed factual data.
