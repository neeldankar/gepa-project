You will be provided with two input fields: a `claim` and associated `notes`. Your task is to generate a single, concise, and precise natural language `query` that can be used to verify or fact-check the entire claim based on the details found within the notes.

**Task Objective:**  
Formulate one targeted question that, if answered by authoritative sources or from the provided notes, decisively confirms or refutes all key factual elements and their relationships presented in the claim.

---

**Detailed Task Instructions:**

1. **Analyze the Claim:**  
   - Identify and extract all distinct factual components and assertions.  
   - Detect any implied relationships or dependencies between multiple components that affect the claim’s overall truthfulness.  
   - Consider dates, roles, names, titles, nationalities, species, awards, or any domain-specific facts as key verification targets.

2. **Leverage the Notes:**  
   - Thoroughly examine the notes for relevant information, including domain-specific terminology such as:  
     - *People:* full names, birth dates, professions, awards received.  
     - *Awards:* exact award names, recipients, dates, and contexts.  
     - *Films and Screenwriting:* film titles, directors, co-directors, production years.  
     - *Botanical:* genus/species names, species counts, vernacular names, taxonomic status.  
     - *Corporate Entities:* company names, nationalities, headquarters, operational details.  
     - *Sports:* teams, individual roles (head coach, coordinator), leagues or conferences, and relevant seasons.  
   - Note overlapping, repeated, or partial information that may help integrate multiple claim components.

3. **Formulate the Query:**  
   - Create a single, clear, unambiguous verification question that covers ALL key factual elements and their interrelations without fragmenting the claim into multiple queries.  
   - Incorporate relevant domain-specific entities explicitly with full context (e.g., “Keith Moon’s induction into the Modern Drummer Hall of Fame in 1982” rather than just “Keith Moon’s induction”).  
   - Address temporal elements where relevant (e.g., “in the early 1980s”, “in 2013”, “currently”).  
   - Avoid vague, overly broad, or open-ended questions. Instead, phrase to provoke a yes/no or specific factual answer that confirms or refutes the entire claim.  
   - When the claim involves comparisons or multiple related factual assertions (e.g., “published more often than Bookmarks” and “Keith Moon’s induction”), integrate these into one coherent verification question addressing both points simultaneously.

4. **Domain-Specific Examples to Consider:**  
   - **Literature:** author full names, literary award titles (e.g., Nobel Prize in Literature), recipient nationality, specific roles such as writer, diplomat, poet.  
   - **Film:** film titles, director/co-director names, associated years or awards.  
   - **Botany:** genus/species counts, vernacular names in several languages, taxonomic rank/status.  
   - **Corporate:** exact company names, nationality, headquarters cities, field of operation.  
   - **Sports:** individual roles (head coach, offensive coordinator), team names, league or conference participation, relevant season/year.

5. **General Strategy:**  
   - Frame the query as a verification question suited for authoritative, efficient retrieval (e.g., “Was X the Y in [context or time period]?”, “Did A direct B and also co-direct C?”, “Is species Z part of genus G?”, “Is company X headquartered in city Y?”).  
   - Where a claim includes a factual inaccuracy or contradictory element (e.g., “Ukrainian director of ‘Young Doctors in Love’” where no such director exists), the query should address the actual verified facts and relationships that confirm or refute this directly and clearly.  
   - Avoid crafting multiple fragmented queries; consolidate claim components logically into a single integrated question.

---

**Why this matters:**  
Your generated query will guide retrieval of the most relevant and authoritative evidence supporting or refuting the claim. Precision and completeness in this formulation accelerate and improve fact-checking effectiveness, avoid ambiguity, and enable a single, definitive answer source.

---

**Summary:**  
Given a `claim` and `notes`, produce ONE precise, fact-oriented, fully encompassing natural language verification query that explicitly names key domain-specific entities, covers all factual assertions and their relationships, reflects any temporal details, and leads to a direct confirm/refute answer about the claim’s truthfulness.
