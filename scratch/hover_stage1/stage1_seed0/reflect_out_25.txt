Task Overview:
You will receive two inputs: a `claim` (a natural language statement containing factual assertions) and associated `notes` (a list of relevant factual data, context, and background details). Your task is to generate a **single, concise, precise, natural language query** designed specifically to verify or fact-check the entire claim using the information provided in the notes. This query is intended to be used for efficient retrieval or confirmation of authoritative evidence that supports or refutes all key facts and relationships embedded in the claim.

Detailed Task Description:

1. **Analyze the Claim for Key Factual Components:**
   - Identify all explicit and implicit factual elements in the claim that must be verified to assess its overall truthfulness.
   - Detect relationships between entities mentioned in the claim, including temporal references, hierarchical roles, or combined fact assertions that must be validated together.
   - Break down multi-part claims into logical components, ensuring that all interconnected facts are addressed cohesively.

2. **Utilize the Notes to Contextualize and Incorporate Domain-Specific Details:**
   - Thoroughly review the notes as they often contain critical domain-specific facts, e.g., names, titles, dates, organizational details, awards, species, or roles.
   - Extract relevant entities and technical terms, such as full personal names, award names, company headquarters, film titles and credited directors, species names and counts, or sports team leadership roles.
   - Use these domain-specific terms explicitly in your query to create precise, unambiguous fact verification questions.

3. **Formulate a Single, Integrated Verification Query:**
   - Compose one clear, direct question that simultaneously targets all the essential factual components and their relations as stated or implied in the claim.
   - Avoid vague or open-ended questions; the query should be answerable with authoritative facts confirming or refuting specific claim details.
   - If the claim has multiple related factual assertions, logically integrate those into a unified query—do not fragment into multiple queries.
   - Include any temporal information present in the claim (e.g., years, periods, seasonal context) to sharpen precision.
   - When multiple similar entities exist, use full identifiers (full names, titles, years, roles) to avoid ambiguity.

4. **Examples of Domain-Specific Entities and Elements to Incorporate (based on claim content):**
   - **Literary domain:** Full names of authors (e.g., Alan Sillitoe), prize titles (e.g., Nobel Prize in Literature), author nationalities, and specific roles (poet, writer, diplomat).
   - **Film and television:** Film titles (e.g., Finding Dory), director/co-director names, actor roles, years of release or broadcast.
   - **Music:** Album titles, artists’ full names, years of album release, notable songs covered, and career timeline.
   - **Botany:** Genus/species names, number of species, vernacular names in various languages, taxonomic classification.
   - **Corporate information:** Company names, nationalities, headquarters cities, product or service descriptions.
   - **Sports:** Team names, league/conference names, individual roles (head coach, offensive coordinator), seasons or years relevant.

5. **General Strategy and Best Practices:**
   - Frame queries as verification questions (e.g., "Was X the Y in [context/year]?" or "Is [person] credited with directing [film] and co-directing [another film]?").
   - Consolidate multiple factual points into one comprehensive question to maximize retrieval efficiency.
   - Ensure your query is specific enough to avoid retrieving unrelated or partially relevant documents.
   - Adapt the level of detail in the query according to the complexity of the claim.
   - Use explicit references to people, events, organizations, dates, and titles found in both the claim and notes.

Objective:
Your final output should be a precisely worded natural language question that, when answered, conclusively verifies or disproves all key factual statements and relationships present in the claim, referencing domain-specific terms and temporal context as appropriate.

Example Template for Query Construction:
- "Was [entity/individual full name] the [role/title] of [organization/team] during [time period], and did [they] also [perform related fact or role]?"
- "Did [person/author name] write/appear in [work/title] released in [year], and did that same person participate in [another work/event] at [time]?"
- "Is the [named album/film/book] by [artist/director/author] released in [year] the one that includes [specific fact]?"

By following these instructions, you will produce focused, domain-specific querying prompts that enable efficient and accurate fact verification based on the provided notes.
