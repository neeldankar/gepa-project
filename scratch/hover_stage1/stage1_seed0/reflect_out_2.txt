Given three input fields: 

- `claim` (a statement that asserts a fact or relationship between entities),  
- `notes` (a list of factual or evidential statements related to the claim and entities involved), and  
- `context` (a list of document snippets or knowledge sources containing detailed information about relevant entities or topics),

your task is to produce two output fields: 

1. `new_notes`:  
   - Select and/or synthesize a concise subset of the input notes that directly support, partially support, or refute the claim based on the provided context.  
   - Ensure the `new_notes` summarize the key factual information, clarifications, and evidential points necessary to evaluate the claim’s validity.  
   - Remove redundancy and irrelevant notes; focus only on those that contribute to the reasoning about the claim.  
   - Highlight any gaps or missing information from the context that affect the ability to fully verify the claim.

2. `titles`:  
   - Identify and list the key document titles or entity names derived from the context that are most relevant and necessary for verifying the claim.  
   - These titles typically correspond to articles, persons, works (e.g., films, books, bands), or other named entities referenced in the claim or notes.  
   - Include documents that provide evidence or context about the main entities mentioned in the claim and are essential for understanding or confirming the claim.

Additional guidance and domain-specific details:

- The `context` often contains multiple document snippets, some more relevant than others. Carefully evaluate which documents pertain directly to the claim’s entities or concepts.  
- The `claim` may involve relationships, roles, or factual assertions about individuals (authors, actors, boxers, musicians), creative works (films, books, songs), or group classifications (bands).  
- When constructing `new_notes`, reflect nuanced validations such as confirmed facts, lack of information (partial support), or contradictions.  
- Consider partial support scenarios where one part of the claim is verifiable and another is not, and summarize this clearly.  
- The `titles` should include all critical documents needed for comprehensive verification, even if some documents do not have explicit supporting details but are relevant entities in the claim.  
- Avoid including unrelated documents among the `titles`; focus on precision and relevance to best support the claim's assessment.  
- Use formal, informative language suitable for fact-checking or knowledge validation tasks.  
- The task aims to support a process of retrieving and consolidating the most pertinent evidence and source identifiers for verifying claims using evidence-rich documents.

Goal: Produce a factually grounded reasoning summary (`new_notes`) and a precise list of relevant sources (`titles`) that together allow accurate verification or falsification of the input claim based on notes and context provided.
