You will be provided with two text fields as input: a `claim` that makes one or more factual assertions, and associated `notes` that contain relevant contextual or factual information potentially validating or refuting parts of the claim. Your task is to produce a **single, concise, precise, natural language query** that is designed to verify the entire claim by retrieving authoritative evidence that confirms or disproves every key factual component and their interrelations.

---

### Detailed task instructions:

1. **Analyze the claim thoroughly:**  
   - Identify all discrete factual assertions contained in the claim.  
   - Note any relationships or dependencies between these factual components that must be verified together to assess the claim’s overall truthfulness.  
   - Capture any temporal, role-based, categorical, or attributional facts (e.g., specific people’s roles at certain times, genre classifications, award titles with recipients and dates, taxonomic counts, company headquarters, team roles in specified seasons).

2. **Leverage the notes effectively:**  
   - Examine the notes carefully—they provide critical factual data including names, titles, dates, domain-specific terminology, and contextual clarifications.  
   - Account for potential clarifications or contradictions in the notes (e.g., distinctions between original and adapted works, roles people actually had vs. roles claimed, genre labels confirmed or denied).  
   - Extract proper nouns (people’s full names, award titles, film titles along with year or format, company names and location, sports teams and leagues, botanical taxa) to incorporate into the query.  
   - Recognize when notes confirm, deny, or leave unconfirmed specific aspects of the claim.

3. **Formulate a single integrated verification query:**  
   - Compose one clear, direct, and specific question that targets **all** key facts and their relationships in the claim.  
   - Explicitly include domain-specific entities (e.g., full person names and roles, movie titles with year or description, award titles and recipients, taxonomic genus/species counts, company nationality and headquarters, sports team and roles in a given season) to avoid ambiguity.  
   - Where the claim contains multiple interconnected assertions, integrate them logically into one succinct query rather than splitting into multiple questions.  
   - Include temporal details where relevant (e.g., “in 2013,” “currently,” “in the 1996 adaptation”) to precisely situate the facts needing verification.  
   - Avoid vague or open-ended phrasing; the question should be framed to enable a clear yes/no or specific fact-based answer.

4. **Domain-specific guidance based on observed task data:**  
   - **Literary domain:** Include full names, nationality, award names (e.g., Nobel Prize in Literature), role types (e.g., poet, diplomat, writer).  
   - **Film domain:** Reference full film titles with years or formats (animated, live-action), director or co-director names, main characters, and actors’ full names along with their specific roles or positions (host, judge, star).  
   - **Botany:** Use genus names, exact counts of species, vernacular names in multiple languages, and taxonomic status.  
   - **Corporate context:** Include precise company names, nationality, headquarters city, and core business descriptions.  
   - **Sports:** Specify individual roles (e.g., head coach, offensive coordinator), team names, relevant seasons and leagues or conferences.  
   
5. **General strategy to maximize query effectiveness:**  
   - Synthesize the verification of multiple related fact components into one integrated query whenever logically coherent.  
   - Use explicit entity identifiers and context to prevent ambiguity and ensure that the query targets authoritative and precise evidence retrieval.  
   - Reference any clarifying details from notes that refine or challenge the claim's assertions (e.g., “Was X a judge on show Y in year Z?” rather than just “Was X a judge?”).  
   - Ensure the query construction aligns with the intent to fact-check the claim with authoritative sources, making the information retrieval efficient and focused.

---

### Objective:

Produce a **single, concise, and precise verification question** that confirms or refutes every factual element and relationship in the claim, using domain-specific terminology and context drawn from the notes. This query should enable clear and efficient retrieval of evidence to decisively evaluate the claim’s overall truthfulness without requiring multiple follow-ups.
