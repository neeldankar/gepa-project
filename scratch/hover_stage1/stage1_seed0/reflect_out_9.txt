You will be given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- `claim` is a factual statement or assertion that requires verification or clarification.
- `notes` is a list of textual evidences, observations, or previously extracted facts relevant to the claim and the context.
- `context` is a list of background documents or excerpts that provide detailed factual information from which relevant evidence can be sourced.

Your task is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

1. **new_notes**:  
   - Select and consolidate from the `notes` field a concise subset of relevant, accurate, and non-contradictory facts that directly address the components of the claim.  
   - The selected notes should highlight key evidence supporting or refuting the claim or clarify ambiguities in the claim, with minimal redundancy.  
   - Exclude any notes that are irrelevant, speculative, overly repetitive, or fail to contribute important factual clarification.

2. **titles**:  
   - Identify and list the titles or names of key documents or entities from the `context` most pertinent to evaluating the claim and the notes.  
   - These titles should represent the primary sources that underpin the `new_notes` or are critical for validating or understanding the claim.  
   - Aim to comprehensively cover the main relevant documents but avoid including irrelevant or marginally related titles.

**Additional Guidance (based on domain-specific examples):**  
- Claims often involve verifying detailed factual or temporal information, such as event dates, award recipients, affiliations, or membership data; carefully cross-reference dates, names, and attributes in the context.  
- Notes may contain repeated or slightly varied statements; carefully synthesize and filter these for clarity and conciseness.  
- Where claims involve multiple entities (e.g., political parties, bands, films, awards), ensure you identify the correct entities and their relationships as supported by the evidence.  
- Consider the hierarchical or affiliative relationships between entities (e.g., provincial vs. federal political parties, youth wings of parties).  
- Recognize that some claims involve negation or refutation (e.g., correcting a misclassification of a band’s genre). Highlight this clearly in the `new_notes`.

**Reasoning Approach to Apply:**  
- Analyze the claim carefully to identify all factual components needing verification.  
- Cross-reference `notes` with `context` to verify accuracy and consistency.  
- Remove any notes unsupported or contradicted by the context.  
- Choose key documents that form the factual basis for the chosen notes.  
- Be mindful to not miss relevant titles that are central to the claim’s verification.

By following these steps, your output will enable clear, concise, and well-supported evaluation of the claim with traceable evidence.
