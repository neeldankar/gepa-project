Task Description:

You will be provided two textual inputs: a `claim` and corresponding `notes`. Your task is to generate one concise, precise, and fully integrated natural language `query` that is specifically designed to verify or fact-check the entire set of factual components within the `claim` using the contextual and factual information contained in the `notes`.

Instruction Details: 

1. **Claim Analysis**:  
   - Identify all key factual elements and assertions in the claim that require validation. This includes not only explicit facts but also any implied relationships or multiple connected claims that form a complete assertion about people, places, organizations, entities, events, dates, or roles.  
   - When the claim contains multiple interrelated facts or entities, these should be combined logically and coherently in the final query to check their joint accuracy in one comprehensive question.  

2. **Use of Notes**:  
   - Carefully review the notes, as they provide relevant domain-specific knowledge, facts, and context that will guide the formulation of the query.  
   - Consider details like: full names, roles, nationalities, years/dates, hierarchical or administrative relationships, titles of work, awards, corporate or institutional affiliations, taxonomic or scientific classifications, or sports roles relevant to the claim.  
   - Explicitly incorporate any domain terminology or formal designations mentioned in the notes that correspond to the entities or facts in the claim.

3. **Formulating the Query**:  
   - Produce a single, clear, concise question that addresses all essential factual components and their interconnections from the claim in one integrated query.  
   - Reference specific and fully qualified domain entities to avoid ambiguity, for example:  
     - Full individual names and roles (e.g., "Eli Roth, known for his role as Donny 'The Bear Jew' Donowitz")  
     - Film titles along with directors or founders (e.g., "the 2012 film directed by RZA, the founder of Soul Temple Records")  
     - Geographic and administrative units with political hierarchy (e.g., "Fàn County under the jurisdiction of a city in Henan Province, China")  
     - Educational institutions alongside their locations and affiliations (e.g., "College of Southern Nevada located in Clark County, Nevada")  
     - Sports teams, coaching roles, and relevant seasons or conferences  
   - Where the claim includes temporal references, incorporate those explicitly in the query (e.g., referencing the year 2012 or a specific season).  
   - Avoid vague, broad, or open-ended questions; the query should directly seek verification of the claim's factual assertions.

4. **General Strategy**:  
   - Combine multiple related facts into one cohesive verification question rather than splitting into multiple queries.  
   - Use verification-focused phrasing such as:  
     - "Did X perform Y role in Z during [time]?"  
     - "Is it true that A is located in B, and that B and C share [relationship]?"  
     - "Did person X write film Y which was directed by person Z, who holds position P?"  
   - The query should be answerable by retrieving authoritative evidence confirming or refuting the entirety of the claim based on the facts in the notes.

5. **Domain-specific Entities and Terminology (for reference)**:  
   - **Film and Screenwriting:** film titles, directors, screenwriters, founders of record labels, acting roles  
   - **Geopolitical and Administrative:** counties, cities, prefecture-level cities, provinces, countries, jurisdictional relationships  
   - **Education and Sports:** college/university names, locations, sports teams, coaching roles, playing years/seasons  
   - **Literary awards and individuals:** full names, nationalities, awards like Nobel Prize in Literature  
   - **Botany and Taxonomy:** genus/species counts, vernacular names, taxonomic status  
   - **Corporate:** company names, national origin, headquarters city  
   
Final objective: Deliver a single, targeted, authoritative query that addresses all factual elements and relationships within the claim, using precise domain-specific terminology drawn from the notes, enabling efficient lookup to confirm or deny the claim in full.
