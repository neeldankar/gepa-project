Task Description:
You are tasked with generating a precise, comprehensive search query designed to retrieve documents that fully support or refute a given multi-faceted factual claim. The claim involves multiple interrelated entities such as people, creative works (films, operas, bands), historical events, roles, relationships, awards, and temporal elements.

Input Format:
- `claim`: A complex factual sentence combining several detailed pieces of information, including names of people, titles of creative works, roles/characters, timelines, relationships, and contrasting assertions.
- `notes`: A list of factual snippets providing detailed evidence, background, or context related to the claim. These notes commonly contain biographical details, production data, nationalities, collaborations, historical timelines, or clarifying contradictions among entities mentioned in the claim.

Goal:
Produce an explicit `query` that can be used to retrieve all relevant documents or evidences that verify or falsify every component of the claim. The query should ensure maximal recall of all relevant facts by tightly and precisely referencing every key element of the claim that the notes address.

Detailed Guidance:
1. **Entity and Fact Extraction**: Carefully parse the claim to extract all critical elements:
   - Names of individuals (e.g., artists, historical figures) and their identities (e.g., real names vs stage names).
   - Titles of works (films, operas, bands) along with their relevant metadata (release year, country, genre).
   - Roles, character identifications, and relationships among entities (e.g., band membership, side projects, political opposition).
   - Temporal information such as periods of tenure, collaborations, release dates.
   - Contradictory or supporting statements within the claim that require verification.
   
2. **Use Notes to Understand Context**:
   - Confirm or refute nationalities and origins (bands, record labels, films).
   - Confirm collaborations, membership and project durations.
   - Identify real-life identities behind stage or code names (e.g., spies vs actors).
   - Extract precise production details (director, production countries, artistic inspiration).
   - Confirm award counts and recognitions, when applicable.
   - Detect inaccuracies or contradictions (e.g., a film’s actual inspiration differing from what the claim asserts).
   - Understand domain-specific concepts such as opera structures (acts vs scenes), band configurations, or journalistic stances.
   
3. **Query Construction**: Frame the query as one tightly integrated but multi-part question (or minimal connected sub-questions) that explicitly includes all key entities, relationships, events, and contradictory elements. For example:
   - Ask for the nationalities and collaborations involved with specified bands and labels, including durations.
   - Clarify the number of acts in an opera in comparison to structural elements of a film.
   - Verify awards won by named individuals with cross-reference to known nicknames or epithets.
   - Confirm production details, directorial credits, and sources of inspiration for creative works.
   - Resolve temporal and contradictory claims with respect to dates and factual timelines (e.g., release years, terms in office).
   
4. **Query Requirements**:
   - Include all relevant named entities and their roles.
   - Target key relationships or oppositions that affect claim truthfulness.
   - Cover temporal and production contexts to differentiate similar or related entities.
   - Avoid vague or abbreviated queries; prefer fully explicit identifiers (e.g., use full official names, titles, years).
   - Optimize for retrieval of all documents linked to the notes and the claim, including any partially or indirectly relevant supporting facts.
   
5. **Incorporate Domain Knowledge**:
   - Political Context: Presidents’ terms (e.g., Cuban presidents and their dates), opposing journalists and their stances.
   - Arts and Media: Filmographies (production year, nationalities), sequels, opera compositions (acts count), band memberships and side projects.
   - Identity Verification: Real names versus code names (e.g., espionage history).
   - Awards and recognitions: Verify counts and associations where applicable.
   
Summary:
Your output must transform a complex multi-part factual claim, with potentially conflicting or detailed supporting evidence, into a comprehensive, precise, and explicit query designed to retrieve all documents necessary to fully verify or falsify the claim. The query should ensure retrieval systems can identify the full spectrum of relevant evidence by explicitly naming and relating all entities, dates, events, and claims involved.

Example Query Frames:
- "What is the nationality and collaboration duration of the Scottish punk band APB with Young American Recordings and Sleeper? Are Young American Recordings and Sleeper British? What are the exact nationalities of Sleeper and Young American Recordings?"
- "Who is 'German Frank Sinatra,' and what awards has this individual won? What awards has Nivek Ogre (Skinny Puppy member and Rx collaborator) won, and how do these compare?"
- "How many acts does the opera Il trovatore have? What are the production countries, director, and year of the film Magic Hunter? Was Magic Hunter inspired by Il trovatore or Der Freischütz? Do films like Magic Hunter have acts or just scenes? Does Il trovatore have more acts than Magic Hunter?"

By following the above guidelines, ensure your output query maximizes relevant document recall and fully addresses the claim’s verification needs.
