Task Description:  
You will be given two inputs: a `claim` and a set of `notes`. Your role is to produce a single output field called `query`.  

- The `claim` is a detailed factual assertion or set of related factual assertions involving multiple entities (people, organizations, works, locations, dates, roles, events) and their attributes or relationships. These claims often combine temporal aspects, authorship, roles, venues, or genealogical information.  
- The `notes` are curated factual fragments, verified or likely verified, providing evidential support or refutation relevant to parts of the claim. They highlight key entities, relationships, and essential facts that should guide the query formulation.  

Your objective is to formulate a **natural language information retrieval or database query** that, if executed, would retrieve all core supporting data sources, documents, or records necessary to fully verify or refute every factual component of the claim comprehensively and accurately.  

Core Principles and Methodology:  

1. **Decompose the Claim into Key Components**  
   Break down the claim to extract:  
   - **Entities:** Named individuals, organizations, creative works (songs, films, TV shows), events, venues, teams, locations, temporal points or periods, genealogical relations, or any other relevant named entities.  
   - **Relationships:** How entities are related—authorship, co-writing, directing, performing, founding, ownership, familial ties, participation in events, tenures in roles, hierarchical positions.  
   - **Attributes and Assertions:** Details such as the professions, chart positions, founding dates, specific roles held, venue locations and ownership, performance dates, original recorders, and genre classifications.  

2. **Use Notes to Prioritize and Clarify Essential Facts and Gold Documents**  
   - Identify which entities or facts the notes emphasize as critical or “gold” sources (e.g., official records, authoritative biographies, documented ownership).  
   - Understand domain-specific contexts: music authorship and chart history; film and TV credits; performance venues and ownership; organizational histories with rosters and dates; genealogical backgrounds; historical tenure and roles.  
   - Avoid broad or unnecessary queries; focus on the documented evidence the notes signal as essential.  
   - Address ambiguities illuminated by the notes (e.g., distinguishing between similarly named entities, confirming roles, clarifying if two entities are the same or different individuals).  

3. **Formulate a Comprehensive, Precise, and Exhaustive Query**  
   Your query should:  
   - Explicitly mention all key entities identified.  
   - Cover all relationships and factual assertions, including titles, roles, authorship, event dates, and locations.  
   - Explicitly incorporate timeline details where tenure, founding dates, recording or release dates, or chronological orders are relevant.  
   - Request all information needed to verify each component of the claim (e.g., precise songwriting or directing credits, chart records, original recording artists and their genre, venue ownership details, familial links).  
   - Include all gold documents highlighted by the notes to ensure completeness.  
   - Where logical, break the query into enumerated sections to separately cover different factual domains or claim facets.  

4. **Domain-Specific Query Elements and Examples**  
   Tailor queries depending on the thematic focus:  
   - **Authors & Creative Works:** Request songwriter, lyricist, director, co-writer, and source credits; filmographies; discographies; full credits for TV series or documentaries; collaborations and co-creations.  
   - **Performances & Venues:** Request histories of events, residencies, venue details (name, location, ownership), performance lineups, and dates.  
   - **Teams & Organizations:** Request founding dates, rosters by time period, management/coaching staff histories, official records of organizational changes or ownership.  
   - **Genealogical & Biographical Data:** Request family trees, genealogical connections, biographies showing familial ties or historical roles.  
   - **Historical Roles & Tenure:** Request official records for positions held, including durations, titles, and timelines relevant to historical contexts.  

5. **Avoid Ambiguity and Vagueness**  
   - Phrase queries in clear, natural language or structured enumerations, explicitly detailing each information need.  
   - Avoid overly broad or generic requests that could retrieve irrelevant data.  
   - Specify the domain or context if entities have multiple meanings or possible identities.  

6. **Output Format**  
   - Provide only one field named `query`.  
   - The field contains the fully formulated query text with no additional explanations, metadata, or formatting beyond natural language or well-structured enumerated requests.  

By following these instructions, your generated query will enable comprehensive retrieval of all essential documents and factual evidence needed to verify or falsify *every* key part of the claim effectively and efficiently.
