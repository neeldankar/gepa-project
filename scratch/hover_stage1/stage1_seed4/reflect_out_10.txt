You will receive inputs consisting of three separate fields:

- `claim`: A statement making one or several factual or historical assertions about people, events, cultural works (e.g., operas, films, characters), places, organizations, or related subjects. These claims often assert relationships such as authorship, role attribution, jurisdiction, or biographical details.

- `notes`: A list of fact-based statements—usually extracted from multiple sources—containing evidence relevant to verifying or clarifying the claim. These notes may contain details about persons’ biographies, specific roles, dates, geographic jurisdictions, or relationships, sometimes repeated or with overlapping information.

- `context`: A collection of documents, excerpts, or background information expanding on key entities mentioned in the claim or notes. This may include more detailed biographical info, descriptions of works, historical facts, or information about locations and organizations.

Your task is to produce two outputs:

1. `new_notes`: A distilled, precise, and relevant subset of the notes and/or context that explicitly addresses the truthfulness and factual components of the claim. These new notes should focus exclusively on the core points that clarify or confirm whether the claim is accurate or false, or explain the essential factual links. Avoid including any irrelevant, redundant, or tangential information. If multiple pieces of supporting evidence appear in both notes and context, combine and trim them into concise statements emphasizing:
   - Verified attributions of works, roles, authorship, or coaching/training affiliations.
   - Clear jurisdictional or geographic relationships.
   - Confirmed identities or biographies especially relevant to the claim, including ethnic identity or historical era if applicable.
   - Explicit absence of evidence when refuting a part of the claim (e.g., no records indicating a person’s association with a particular activity).

2. `titles`: A list of precise and descriptive document or topic titles representing the key subjects, entities, or sources referenced in the `new_notes`. These titles should be appropriate as search queries or references for further research. Include all crucial aspects of the claim, ensuring coverage of:
   - Individuals involved (with full names and roles).
   - Specific works or cultural artifacts mentioned.
   - Relevant places, institutions, or administrative divisions.
   - Topics related to domain-specific distinctions (e.g., voice acting practices such as dubbing versions, musical periods or composer differentiation, sports roles—player, coach, trainer).
   - Thematic contexts explaining or clarifying relationships or attributions.

Domain-specific considerations:
- Claims frequently involve distinguishing between multiple people with similar names or related roles (author vs illustrator, athlete vs coach, composer vs librettist).
- Especially for musical or operatic claims, differentiate accurately between different composers (e.g., Baroque composer Rameau vs Romantic period Puccini) and versions of works.
- In voice acting and dubbing contexts, multiple actors may voice a character in different languages or editions—only direct known attributions should be used to confirm claims.
- Sports-related claims often require disentangling team staff roles (e.g., head coach vs trainer vs player) or verifying team jurisdiction and coach appointments.
- Geographic or political claims may involve administrative hierarchies (e.g., county under city jurisdiction) and confirmation of shared country/province.
- The absence of evidence (e.g., "no records that person did X") may be used to substantiate a factual negation in the claim.

Generalizable reasoning strategy:
- Break down the claim into its key factual components.
- Search the notes and context for exact verifications or contradictions addressing those components.
- Consolidate multiple related evidences into streamlined, authoritative statements focusing on direct relevance.
- Ensure new_notes avoids repetition or over-detail.
- Make sure titles comprehensively reflect every core concept and named entity required to fully understand or investigate the claim.

Output format reminder:
- Produce two fields: `new_notes` as a list of clear, factual statements; and `titles` as a list of relevant, descriptive document or subject titles.

This approach guarantees an accurate, focused assessment of the claim’s validity and facilitates further targeted research.
