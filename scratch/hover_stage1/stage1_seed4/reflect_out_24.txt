- `context`: Background documents or excerpts providing additional information on important entities in or related to the claim. These may include biographies, work summaries, award details, filmographies, definitions, or historical data.

2. `titles`: A list of clear, specific document or topic titles representing the key subjects or sources referenced in the `new_notes`. This list should cover all important aspects of the claim to aid further research.

   - Ensure the titles comprehensively cover all critical entities and themes from the extracted notes/context related to the claim.

### Domain-Specific and Task-Specific Considerations:

- Verify the accuracy of attributions such as which individual performed a role (e.g., voiced a character, directed a film) or received an award.
- Understand distinctions between similar or related entities, such as different composers named in operatic works, different actors nominated for awards in different roles, or differences between athlete, trainer, and coach.
- Consider timelines critically — which person or work was active or awarded in which year.

### Generalizable Strategy:

- Identify the key entities and relationships present in the claim.
- Systematically compare and cross-reference the claim components against the notes and context for confirming or refuting evidence.
- Extract and condense the minimum but most decisive facts that impact claim validity.
- Avoid inclusion of extraneous details not relevant to the claim’s truth or clarification.
- Formulate `titles` that precisely reflect all critical facets of the evidence source material.

### Output Format:

Your output must include both `new_notes` and `titles`, clearly labeled. The `new_notes` should be factual, concise, and explanatory with respect to the claim. The `titles` should comprehensively represent the key relevant topics or documents that were utilized to derive the `new_notes`.

Adhering to these guidelines will ensure accurate, clear, and domain-aware verification and summarization of factual claims involving roles, attributions, awards, nationalities, and historical facts in arts, entertainment, literature, and related fields.
