Task Description:  
You will be given two input fields: `claim` and `notes`. Your task is to generate a single field called `query`. This `query` should be a precise, comprehensive, natural language information retrieval or database query designed to retrieve all necessary supporting documents or data to verify every aspect of the `claim`.

Inputs:  
- `claim`: A complex factual statement or set of statements that assert relationships, events, or facts involving multiple entities (e.g., people, films, organizations, dates, roles). The claim contains multiple components that need verification.  
- `notes`: Verified or likely verified factual information fragments, evidence, or background details relevant for assessing the claim. These provide clues on what entities, relationships, and attributes are key for verifying parts of the claim.

Output:  
- `query`: A natural language query (can be a single or multi-part request) that, if executed against an information retrieval system or database, will retrieve all relevant documents or datasets needed to fully verify or refute every factual element of the claim.

Detailed Instructions and Strategy:  

1. **Parse the Claim to Identify Key Verification Components**  
   - Extract all key entities mentioned or implied: persons (with attributes like birth dates or nationalities), films (titles, genres, release years), organizations (teams, companies, labels), events (TV episodes, campaigns), roles or occupations (actor, director, professor, CIA operative, manager), places, and dates.  
   - Identify relationships or predicates within the claim that must be validated, such as:  
     - Membership or involvement (e.g., starred in, directed, founded)  
     - Temporal aspects (e.g., birth month/year, time periods of roles)  
     - Connections between entities (e.g., co-stars, co-writers, overlaps in profession)  
     - Attributes of entities (e.g., nationality, profession, affiliations)  

2. **Leverage the Notes for Context and Coverage**  
   - Use notes as evidence of what factual pieces have been verified or refuted around the claim entities.  
   - Identify which key entities and relationships are central and thus must be covered in the query.  
   - Recognize when notes highlight gaps or missing verification to ensure the query covers that information.

3. **Formulate a Comprehensive Yet Focused Information Retrieval Query**  
   Your query must:  
   - Target all entities involved (e.g., all named persons, films, organizations).  
   - Request information sufficient to verify each critical relationship or property asserted in the claim (e.g., filmographies including cast and crew, biographical details like birthdates and professions, affiliations with organizations, roles in events or commercials).  
   - Reflect temporal components when relevant (e.g., focus on founding dates, time windows of association, episode or campaign periods).  
   - Include any indirect or implied connections needed to fully assess the claim (e.g., whether the director of a particular film is affiliated with a specific record label).  
   - Avoid extraneous information that is not related to verifying the claim.

4. **Guiding Principles for Query Construction by Claim Type:**  
   - If the claim involves **shared professions or attributes** between two or more persons, query their full professional profiles to confirm overlaps.  
   - If the claim involves **filmography or media works**, query cast lists, writers, directors, release years, and production credits for each named film or media.  
   - If the claim involves **sports teams or organizations**, query founding dates, rosters for specified seasons or years, managerial records, and individual affiliations.  
   - If the claim involves **timelines, births, or biographical data**, query full biographical details including birth months/years, nationalities, and career histories.  
   - If the claim concerns **events or campaigns (commercials, TV episodes)**, query detailed credit lists, actors involved, and character roles within those events.

5. **Examples of Key Query Elements**  
   - "Retrieve detailed biographical information about [Person], including birth date, nationality, and profession."  
   - "List all films directed by [Director] and the respective release dates."  
   - "Provide full cast, crew, and director details for film [Film Title] (year), including [Claimed Actor’s] involvement."  
   - "Retrieve founding information and roster history for sports team [Team] during [Years]."  
   - "Retrieve records of actors portraying [Role] in episode [Episode Name] of show [Show Title]."  
   - "Retrieve information on any commercial campaigns involving [Actor] for brand [Brand]."  

6. **Important Domain-Specific Details to Include**  
   - When verifying actors, consider roles played, co-stars, and any writing/directing credits.  
   - When verifying organizational affiliations, identify founders and their contributions or productions.  
   - When verifying multiple claims about overlapping careers or attributes (e.g., profession + birthdate + nationality + roles), queries must encompass all these axes in the request.  
   - When only partial or no direct matches appear in notes, queries should broaden scope to fully cover potential relevant data to avoid missing crucial documents.

7. **Output Format**  
   Output only the `query` text as a single natural language request or a clearly structured series of requests sufficient to retrieve all relevant documents and data to verify every factual aspect of the claim.

By following these instructions, you ensure that the generated query covers all entities, relationships, and details necessary for complete verification of the claim, and retrieves the entire set of relevant supporting documents indicated by the notes and feedback.
