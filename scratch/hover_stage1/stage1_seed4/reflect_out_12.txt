Task Description:
You will be provided two input fields: `claim` and `notes`. Your task is to generate a new field called `query`.

- The `claim` is a complex factual statement or series of statements. It asserts specific relationships, events, roles, or facts involving multiple entities (such as people, organizations, films, sports teams, places, or dates).
- The `notes` are fragments of factual information, background context, or evidence related to verifying or assessing the claim. These may confirm, refute, or contextualize elements of the claim.

Your objective is to produce a comprehensive, precise, and focused natural language `query` designed to retrieve all documents or data needed to verify every aspect of the claim fully and accurately.

---

Detailed Instructions and Strategy:

1. **Identify and decompose factual components of the claim:**
   - Extract all **key entities**, including but not limited to:
     - People (actors, directors, managers, players, professionals)
     - Organizations (companies, school districts, sports teams)
     - Works (films, TV shows, video games)
     - Locations (cities, counties, countries)
     - Time periods and dates (years, historical events)
     - Roles and professions (lead actor, director, player, professional role)
   - Identify **relationships and assertions** in the claim, such as:
     - Membership and composition (e.g., school district membership, team rosters)
     - Participation/connectivity (e.g., starring in a film, acting credits)
     - Founding or creation details (e.g., platform production country)
     - Overlapping careers or relationships (e.g., co-starring, co-appearances)
     - Geographic service or coverage (e.g., which school serves which area)
   - Note **attributes or predicates** that must be verified, including:
     - Whether entities are linked (e.g., if two platforms are from the same country)
     - Time-alignment of events (did an actor appear in a show in a specific year)
     - Correctness of specific membership lists or rosters

2. **Utilize the `notes` effectively:**
   - Use the `notes` to clarify key verification points, such as:
     - Which entities are confirmed or disputed to belong where
     - Gaps in available information (e.g., lack of evidence for a relationship)
     - Contradictions or confirmations about dates, roles, and memberships
   - The `notes` guide what supporting sources need to be retrieved to cover all parts of the claim, ensuring completeness.

3. **Construct the query to cover all parts of the claim, ensuring retrieval of all relevant document types:**
   - For **claims about people and their professions/roles,** query their official profiles, credited works, and timeline intersections.
   - For **claims about films or works,** query cast lists, directors, release years, and co-appearances.
   - For **claims regarding platforms or products,** query production companies, platforms’ countries of origin, and game development platforms.
   - For **claims about organizational composition,** query official member lists, boundaries, and service areas.
   - For **claims involving timelines,** query historical data validating dates, periods of involvement, and chronological overlaps.

4. **Ensure comprehensive coverage:**
   - The query must explicitly mention all key entities involved.
   - Include all relationships or properties asserted in the claim.
   - Use detailed descriptors to avoid ambiguity (e.g., specify exact years or roles).
   - The query should aim to retrieve all gold-standard supporting documents referenced or implied by the notes and feedback.

5. **Preferred Query Format:**
   - Write the query as a clear, natural language request or as a series of logically ordered requests.
   - Avoid vague or overly broad queries; be as specific as possible to cover the entire claim.
   - Structure queries to guide retrieval systems to cover each verification need, for example:
     - "Retrieve the cast and leading actors of [Film] released in [Year]."
     - "Retrieve official member lists and boundary maps of [Organization]."
     - "Retrieve countries of origin for the platforms associated with [Game]."
     - "Retrieve the filmography and TV show appearances of [Person] between [Years]."

---

Notes on Domain-Specific Nuances:

- Film/TV Claims: Identifying the lead actor is critical; verify their involvement in all claimed productions with dates.
- Platform/Technology Claims: Identify production companies and countries of origin; check for evidence linking games to platforms.
- Organizational Claims: Verify membership or composition carefully, especially when claims incorrectly include or exclude entities.
- Overlap Claims: Verify temporal overlap and joint participation in projects or organizations.
- Geographic Claims: Verify service regions or census-designated places covered by entities such as school districts or teams.

---

By following this approach, you produce queries that retrieve all necessary evidence to fully validate or refute the complex claims given.

Output:
- Only output the constructed `query` text based on the above guidelines.
