Task Description:
You will be given two input fields: `claim` and `notes`. Your task is to produce a new field `query`.

- The `claim` is a complex factual statement or set of statements that asserts certain relationships, events, or facts involving multiple entities.
- The `notes` are supporting or refuting fragments of factual information, background details, and evidence relevant to assessing or verifying the `claim`.

Your goal is to formulate an information retrieval or database query (`query`) that, if executed, would retrieve all supporting documents or data needed to verify every part of the claim accurately. The query should be specifically designed to capture all key entities, relationships, and factual aspects mentioned or implied in the given claim.

Key Points and Strategy:
1. **Identify factual components in the claim**  
   Parse the claim to identify:
   - Key entities (people, organizations, films, events, teams, places, dates, roles, etc.)
   - Relationships between entities (e.g., "starred with", "founded in", "managed", "directed by")
   - Attributes or predicates that need verification (e.g., professional overlap, filmography, team foundation dates, managerial roles)

2. **Leverage evidence in `notes`**  
   The `notes` contain verified or likely verified facts that relate to the entities and claims. Use these to guide your understanding of which pieces of information are central and what queries could retrieve documents covering those pieces.

3. **Formulate a comprehensive yet precise query**  
   The query should:  
   - Request information that covers all key entities involved  
   - Cover all relationships or assertions made  
   - Be sufficiently detailed to guide retrieval of all necessary documents, ensuring that none of the claim’s critical assertions are left unverifiable

4. **Generalizable querying approach**  
   - For claims involving relationships or overlap between two or more persons or entities, query their respective profiles, roles, and overlaps (e.g., shared professions, co-appearances)  
   - For claims involving filmography or works, query casts, co-stars, directors and release details for each relevant film  
   - For claims involving teams or organizations, query founding dates, player rosters during specified periods, and managerial histories  
   - For claims involving timelines, query historical data for proper date and role alignment  

5. **Prioritize retrieval of documents for all entities referenced**  
   The goal is to retrieve a full set of relevant documents that together confirm or refute the claim. The query should explicitly try to capture all gold documents identified in notes or feedback.

Example considerations from provided cases:
- When claim involves whether two professionals share a profession, the query should seek explicit mentions of professions for both individuals.
- When claim involves people starring together in films and their participation in other films and directors, the query should request casts of the films and director credits.
- When claim involves sports teams and individuals across specific time periods, the query should request founding dates, rosters by years, managerial records, and affiliations with any teams.

Output Format:
- Output only the `query` text, formatted as a natural language request or series of requests designed to retrieve all relevant and complete supporting information for verifying the claim.
