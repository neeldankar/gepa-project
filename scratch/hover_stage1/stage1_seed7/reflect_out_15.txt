- Identify from the claim the key entities and their relationships.  
- Cross-check with notes to recognize which factual elements require explicit query formulation and which are tangential.  
- Formulate one or more explicit natural language questions combining those facts, ensuring they cover all points in the claim comprehensively.  
- Keep queries concise but fact-rich to maximize retrieval of necessary supporting documents.  

Your output should be only the `query` field as a natural language question or set of questions that encapsulate the full verification scope of the input claim using the context from the notes.
