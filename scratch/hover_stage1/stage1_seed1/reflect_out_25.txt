Given a `claim` string and a list of `notes` strings containing relevant evidential information, generate a clear and precise `query` that effectively identifies the key factual information needed to verify the claim.

Task details:

- The `claim` is a statement or set of statements that assert certain factual information (e.g., birthplaces and their attributes, chronological birth order, roles in events or organizations).
- The `notes` are supporting or related information extracted from various sources that may partially confirm, refute, or leave uncertain aspects of the claim.
- The objective is to produce a `query` that explicitly asks for the essential pieces of information required to verify the claim's truthfulness. This includes identifying missing factual elements, core entities, and relationships between them.
- When formulating the `query`, consider these points:
  - Identify entities or concepts in the claim that need further disambiguation or fact-checking (e.g., person’s birthplaces, birthdates, roles, associations).
  - Incorporate geographical, chronological, or hierarchical relationships relevant to the claim (e.g., city ranks, event participation, organizational roles).
  - Use the information in the `notes` to determine what is known and unknown and focus the query on the unknown or uncertain facts necessary for claim verification.
  - If multiple interrelated facts need verification (e.g., location, birth order, identity), incorporate them all into a single, precise query that reflects these dependencies.
  - Ensure the query is formulated as one or more direct questions that can retrieve factual evidence to conclusively verify the claim.
- Avoid overly broad or vague queries; be as specific and targeted as possible based on the known facts and uncertainties.

The form of the output includes:
- A reasoning explanation that justifies which facts remain unverified and why the query focuses on those facts.
- The final `query` that clearly captures all these information needs in a concise, natural language question or set of questions.

This structured approach helps to retrieve exact factual documents or information needed to determine the claim's veracity efficiently and accurately.
