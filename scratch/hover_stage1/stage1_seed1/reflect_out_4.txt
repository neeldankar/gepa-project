You are given three input fields: `claim` (a factual statement or assertion), `notes` (a list of textual evidence snippets related to the claim), and `context` (a list of background or reference documents associated with the entities or topics mentioned in the claim). 

Your task is to analyze the `claim` in light of the `notes` and `context`, then produce two output fields: 

1. `new_notes`: a concise, factual summary of the evidence from `notes` that directly supports or refutes the claim. The summary should:
   - Extract and reformulate key factual points that clarify or verify specific elements of the claim.
   - Resolve ambiguities, inconsistencies, and distinguish between related but separate facts (e.g., differentiating a book from a magazine article, or clarifying nationality discrepancies).
   - Exclude redundant or contradictory snippets unless necessary to highlight conflicting evidence.
   - Emphasize factual precision and correct any inaccuracies present in the claim based on provided evidence.
   - Present information specifically relevant to the claim’s details (e.g., names, dates, titles, places, affiliations, and nationalities).

2. `titles`: a list of relevant document or entity titles (drawn from the `context`) that substantiate or are critical to the claim’s verification. The titles should:
   - Identify key people, works (books, films, magazines), places, or organizations that are directly connected to the claim.
   - Include all entities necessary to fully understand and fact-check the claim.
   - Avoid including unrelated or peripheral documents.
   - Titles should be the canonical or most recognizable form used in the `context`.

Additional task details and best practices:

- Carefully evaluate and cross-reference the `notes` and `context` to distinguish proper factual relationships (e.g., confirm that “Print” is a magazine and periodical, and not confuse a book authored by the same person as a magazine article).
- When the claim includes compound assertions involving multiple entities, verify each part separately and present evidence for each in `new_notes`.
- Correct any false or misleading components of the claim explicitly in the `new_notes`.
- Ensure `titles` cover all crucial entities mentioned in the reasoning and the summary.
- The outputs should be consistent: `new_notes` justifies your assessment of the claim, and `titles` indicate the authoritative sources backing this assessment.

Format summary:

Inputs:  
- `claim`: A factual claim to validate.  
- `notes`: List of evidence snippets pertinent to the claim.  
- `context`: List of background documents and descriptions about relevant entities.

Outputs:  
- `new_notes`: A succinct, factual synthesis of the evidence clarifying and resolving the claim’s veracity and details.  
- `titles`: Essential entity or document titles from context that underpin the assessment.

The goal is to enable precise fact-checking by clearly connecting evidence to the claim and naming the authoritative sources, facilitating easy reference and verification.
