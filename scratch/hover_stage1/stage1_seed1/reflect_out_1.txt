Task Description:
You will be given a claim and a set of notes related to that claim. Your task is to generate a precise, factually grounded search query that aims to retrieve authoritative documents supporting or refuting the claim. The query should be formulated by synthesizing key entities, attributes, relations, and relevant details from both the claim and the notes. 

Input Format:
- claim: A single sentence or a brief statement describing one or more factual assertions involving people, works (movies, songs, publications), events, roles, professions, or relationships among these entities.
- notes: A list of textual statements providing background facts, context, or partial information that may confirm, reject, or clarify aspects of the claim.

Output:
- query: A concise natural language question or search query that captures the essential verification points needed to fact-check the claim effectively. The query should focus on the core entities and their specific roles, connections, or attributes mentioned in the claim, leveraging the evidence in the notes to disambiguate and contextualize as needed.

Key Requirements and Domain-Specific Details:
1. Identify and preserve relevant named entities present in the claim (e.g., people, songs, movies, publications).
2. Use the notes to resolve ambiguity in the claim, such as confirming specific names, roles, or date ranges, and incorporate these into the query.
3. When multiple entities or relations are claimed (for example, an actor appearing in several works or collaborations between musicians), construct the query to explicitly ask about these intersections or relationships.
4. The query should clearly specify the criteria needed to evaluate the claim, e.g., "Which actor acted in both X and Y and is known for Z?" or "Are both persons X and Y musicians?"
5. Queries should target the minimal, most relevant facts needed to verify the claim, avoiding extraneous detail not warranted by the input.
6. When temporal or frequency information is involved (publication frequency, induction years), ensure these qualifiers are included in the query as precise filters.
7. The ultimate goal is to retrieve authoritative source documents supporting precise verification points derived from claim and notes, so the query should be tailored accordingly.

Generalizable Strategy:
- Parse the claim to extract key entities, roles, and relations.
- Check the notes for confirming or clarifying information about these entities or relations to refine or resolve ambiguities.
- Formulate the query as a focused question or search string that targets the intersection or relationship claimed.
- Incorporate relevant temporal, frequency, or role constraints from the notes to narrow down the query.
- Aim to maximize retrieval of gold-standard documents supporting or refuting the claim.

This approach ensures the generated queries are factually grounded, domain-specific, and designed to facilitate effective document retrieval and fact verification.
