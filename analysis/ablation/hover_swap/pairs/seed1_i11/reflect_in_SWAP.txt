# Example 2
## Inputs
### claim
The actor who starred, along with Go Ah-sung, in the South-Korean film, Office,  appeared in The Deal. The Deal was directed by Choi Woo-shik.

### notes
['The South Korean film Office (2015) starred Go Ah-sung and Park Sung-woong.', 'Park Sung-woong starred as a serial killer in The Deal (2015).', 'There is no information that Choi Woo-shik directed The Deal.']

## Generated Outputs
### reasoning
The claim states that the actor who starred with Go Ah-sung in the South Korean film "Office" also appeared in "The Deal." Additionally, it claims that "The Deal" was directed by Choi Woo-shik. To verify this, we need to:
1. Identify who starred alongside Go Ah-sung in "Office."
2. Check if that actor appeared in "The Deal."
3. Verify if "The Deal" was directed by Choi Woo-shik, who is primarily known as an actor, not a director.
The query needs to gather these data points to confirm or refute the claim.

### query
SELECT 
  office_co_star.name AS co_star_name,
  credit_mov.title AS deal_title,
  deal_director.name AS deal_director_name
FROM 
  Film office
JOIN 
  Cast office_cast ON office.id = office_cast.film_id
JOIN 
  Actor office_co_star ON office_cast.actor_id = office_co_star.id
JOIN 
  Film credit_mov ON credit_mov.title = 'The Deal'
JOIN 
  Cast deal_cast ON credit_mov.id = deal_cast.film_id
JOIN 
  Actor deal_actor ON deal_cast.actor_id = deal_actor.id
JOIN 
  Director deal_director ON credit_mov.director_id = deal_director.id
WHERE 
  office.title = 'Office'
  AND office_co_star.name != 'Go Ah-sung'
  AND office_co_star.name = deal_actor.name
LIMIT 1;

## Feedback
Correctly retrieved 2/3 gold documents: ['Office (2015 South Korean film)', 'Park Sung-woong']. Documents remaining to be retrieved: ['The Deal (2015 film)'].
