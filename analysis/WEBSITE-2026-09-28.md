cite-safe until it is committed.

- **CITE-SAFE**: committed source, reproduced on bytes, not superseded.

- **BANNED**: excluded from external use by Neel's rule.
- **PENDING**: later work contradicts or supersedes the ledger's wording or number. Needs Neel's decision (see §8).
