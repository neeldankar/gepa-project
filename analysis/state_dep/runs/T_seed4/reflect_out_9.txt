Task Description:

You will be given three input fields:
- `claim`: a statement or assertion that links one or more entities or facts.
- `notes`: a list of extracted sentences or statements, often partial and noisy, related to the entities or topics in the claim.
- `context`: a list of document snippets or extracts, each representing a potential source document containing factual information related to the claim and notes.

Your objective is to produce two output fields:
- `new_notes`: a refined subset of `notes` that directly support, clarify, or refute the claim, removing redundant or irrelevant sentences. The selected notes should be factual and relevant to validating or invalidating the claim.
- `titles`: a list of document titles corresponding to the key documents from `context` that contain the critical information used to support or refute the claim. These titles should represent distinct, authoritative, and relevant sources backing the notes.

Detailed Instructions and Strategy:

1. **Analyze the Claim**  
   Carefully parse the claim to identify the entities involved (e.g., people, organizations, events, buildings, films) and the specific factual relationships or assertions made about them (e.g., locations, dates, sponsorships, roles).

2. **Review Notes**  
   Notes are often repetitive or partially overlapping, and can contain contradictory or irrelevant information. Your job is to synthesize them and select only those notes that:
   - Provide clear, verifiable information directly related to the key aspects of the claim.
   - Help confirm or reject the details in the claim.
   Avoid redundant notes and those unrelated to the key claim components.

3. **Cross-reference With Context**  
   The `context` field contains document snippets from authoritative sources. Identify which documents:
   - Contain detailed, factual information relevant to the claim’s key elements.
   - Help validate the accuracy of the notes you select.
   - May provide important clarifications that refute or nuance the claim.

4. **Titles Selection**  
   For each critical note selected, find the corresponding document(s) from `context` that substantiate it. Include their titles in the `titles` list. The set of titles should cover all vital aspects of the claim and the associated notes.

5. **Factual Domain-Specific Knowledge**  
   - Recognize that entities (e.g., football clubs, films, actors, buildings) have precise relationships and attributes such as location, sponsorship, participation in leagues or films, release years, and roles.
   - Understand common domain distinctions, e.g.:
     - Football clubs and leagues have specific divisions and sponsors.
     - Films have release years, cast lists, and production credits.
     - Public buildings may have sculptors credited or contextual historical details.
   - Distinguish similarly named entities carefully (e.g., 'Ford Motors F.C.' vs 'Vauxhall Motors F.C.', or multiple cathedrals named 'St Paul's').

6. **Generalizable Approach**  
   - Use critical evaluation to distinguish factual support from conjecture or ambiguity.
   - Consolidate information logically, summarizing notes and selecting titles that clearly align with factual validation.
   - Address contradictions explicitly by excluding unsupported claims or clarifying errors.
   - Be mindful of completeness (selecting all needed documents) and precision (excluding irrelevant or off-topic data).

In summary, your task is to produce a concise, evidence-based validation summary by selecting and organizing relevant notes and document titles from the provided inputs to accurately assess the truth of the given claim.
