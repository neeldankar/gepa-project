Given inputs with fields `claim` and `notes`, your task is to generate a precise and effective `query` that will help retrieve relevant authoritative documents or evidence to verify or refute the claim. 

The `claim` is a statement involving specific factual assertions which often include entities (e.g., people, places, organizations), their relationships, attributes (such as birth year, roles, locations), and connections between them. The `notes` field contains factual data points, supporting details, or partial evidence related to the entities and assertions in the claim.

Your goal is to:
1. Understand the claim fully, identifying its key factual components—entities, relationships, attributes, and context.
2. Analyze the `notes` for relevant content that confirms, contradicts, or relates to these components.
3. Construct a comprehensive, direct, and unambiguous query that:
   - Reflects the essential factual elements of the claim;
   - Uses terminology aligned with the claim and notes to maximize retrieval accuracy;
   - Balances specificity and generality to accurately target relevant documents without being too narrow;
   - Includes relevant conjunctions and logical operators implicitly or explicitly to cover all parts of the claim (e.g., multiple entities, geographic relations, roles, birth years);
   - References relationships between multiple entities or aspects when the claim involves comparison or linkage;
   - Avoids redundancy or unrelated information that may dilute the query.

Additional domain-specific details and strategies evidenced in prior examples include:
- When the claim involves identifying a person by nationality, birth year, roles in TV shows or commercials, your query should combine these distinct identifiers.
- When the claim asserts geographic or institutional relationships (e.g., a county containing cities and colleges), specify the named locations and institutions in relation to each other.
- When a claim states hierarchical administrative control (e.g., a county under a city), plus co-location of separate cities within one country, your query should verify governance and shared national location.
- Use precise entity names and descriptive attributes from `notes` to frame the query.
- Include all parts of the claim logically to ensure comprehensive document retrieval for verification.
  
In summary, your query should function as a focused information retrieval request that covers all factual components of the claim in tandem, leveraging knowledge excavated from the `notes` to guide its construction. The objective is to enable identification and retrieval of all gold standard supporting documents necessary to validate or invalidate the claim.
