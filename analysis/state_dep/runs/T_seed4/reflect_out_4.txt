Task Description:
You will be given three input fields: 
- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion that needs to be verified or analyzed.
- `notes`: A list of notes or initial factoids extracted or inferred related to the claim.
- `context`: A list of documents or passages providing background information relevant to the claim.

Your goal is to produce two output fields:
- `new_notes`: A concise summarization and updated list of notes that clarify, refine, or correct the original notes based on the given context and the verification of the claim.
- `titles`: A list of relevant document or entity titles from the context that are pertinent to understanding, verifying, or refuting the claim.

Detailed Instructions and Strategy:

1. **Claim Analysis:**
   - Carefully examine the claim to identify factual components (persons, places, events, institutions, dates, professions, etc.).
   - Understand implicit and explicit relationships and assertions in the claim (e.g., relationships between individuals and organizations, chronological order, place affiliations).

2. **Contextual Verification:**
   - Use the context documents to verify each factual component of the claim.
   - Identify any contradictions, corrections, or confirmations from the context.
   - Note cases where the claim confuses similar or related entities (e.g., similarly named cemeteries, different sports or professions, institutions with overlapping or close names, or chronological ordering).
   - Pay attention to domain-specific factual details: 
     - Historical timelines (founding dates of institutions, event dates)
     - Geographic affiliations (parks, cities, counties)
     - Biographical details (professions, notable achievements, affiliations)
     - Specific roles or titles (military ranks, sports positions)
     - Common confusions due to naming or geography

3. **Notes Refinement:**
   - Summarize or consolidate key clarifications derived from the context.
   - Remove redundancies or contradictory notes.
   - Clearly highlight errors in the claim as well as what parts (if any) are accurate.
   - Maintain clear and factual language.
   - The new notes should form a coherent, logically consistent set of facts explaining the ground truth or status of the claim.

4. **Title Selection:**
   - Select the entity or document titles from the context that are essential to justify your reasoning.
   - Titles should include individuals, places, institutions, events, or items referenced explicitly or implicitly in the reasoning.
   - Ensure titles cover all essential topics needed to evaluate the claim.

5. **General Reasoning Practices:**
   - Disentangle similar but distinct entities or topics to avoid confusion.
   - Distinguish between different domains or disciplines when relevant (e.g., American football vs. international soccer).
   - Use explicit dates or timelines to verify chronological claims.
   - Check the provenance and exact naming of institutions or locations to clarify ambiguous statements.
   - Document any corrections to common misconceptions contained in the claim.

6. **Output Format:**
   - Provide detailed reasoning (optional in your internal process, but helpful for your accuracy).
   - Provide the `new_notes` as a list of clear, factual, non-redundant points.
   - Provide the `titles` as a list of relevant document/entity names referenced from the context.

This approach ensures precise fact-checking and factual correction of claims, leveraging detailed domain knowledge and cross-verification from provided context, while addressing ambiguities and common confusions caused by naming similarities or profession mismatches.
