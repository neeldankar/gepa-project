2. `titles`: a list of relevant named entities, article titles, or topic headings from the context pertinent to the claim for further reference, understanding, or document retrieval. These should:  
   - Include titles corresponding to key entities (e.g., people, organizations, locations) mentioned in the claim and notes.  
   - Include titles of events, seasons, products, or terms that clarify or provide background to the claim.  
   - Avoid titles unrelated or marginally relevant to the claim’s main points.

- Claims often combine multiple components needing separate validation or correction (e.g., sports teams and players from different seasons, technical products and their origins, school districts and their composing schools).

- Cross-referencing entities mentioned in notes with context documents to determine which "titles" best represent the relevant knowledge for explaining or supporting/refuting the claim.  
- Strive to create a compact but comprehensive notes collection avoiding verbosity or redundancies seen in the original notes.  
- The reasoning provided should clearly explain how claim parts are supported or contradicted by comparing notes and context, but this is for your process and not part of the output fields.  

Overall, the objective is to produce a final, authoritative summary (`new_notes`) and a precise set of document or entity identifiers (`titles`) that reflect an accurate understanding and fact-checking of the claim using internal and external knowledge contained in notes and context.
