You will be given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your task is to generate two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

**Input Definitions:**  
- **claim:** A factual statement that might be true or false and often contains specific details about entities, events, classifications, dates, affiliations, or relationships.  
- **notes:** A collection of raw factual statements which may include clarifications, corrections, contradictory statements, or relevant evidence related to the claim. These may be partial, redundant, or overlapping.  
- **context:** Supplemental background text or summaries about entities, topics, or documents connected to the claim, used to fill gaps or confirm details absent or unclear in `notes`.

---

### Your Task

1. **Synthesize and distill information from `notes` and optionally from `context` into `new_notes`:**  
   - Clarify or correct the claim by extracting relevant verified facts.  
   - Remove redundant, contradictory, or irrelevant information to improve clarity and precision.  
   - Emphasize important domain-specific factual details, such as:  
     - **Taxonomy:** Correctly identify taxonomic ranks (genus, subfamily, tribe, family) and hierarchical relationships between them; highlight species counts where relevant to support or refute claims. For example, clarify if a named entity is a genus or a broader subfamily containing multiple genera.  
     - **Institutional Histories:** Note precise establishment or founding years and compare them accurately when dates are involved. Link individuals to institutions based on education or training history.  
     - **Organizations and Sports Leagues:** Verify club names, league memberships, sponsorship affiliations, and geographic locations precisely. Correct any false statements about sponsorship or membership.  
   - Use context as needed to supplement missing or ambiguous details in the notes to make the facts comprehensive and accurate.  
   - Ensure the `new_notes` directly address and support or refute the claim, providing clear reasoning and factual evidence.  
   - Include critical relationships, dates, memberships, and distinctions relevant to understanding the claim’s validity.

2. **Produce a list of concise and descriptive `titles` representing the key knowledge topics or entities referenced:**  
   - Titles should summarize the main topics, entities, or subtopics necessary for verifying or understanding the claim and the facts in `new_notes`.  
   - Align titles with real-world article or document naming conventions—for instance, use "University of Washington," "West Cheshire Association Football League," or "Caesalpinioideae" depending on the entity type.  
   - Cover all important domains relevant to the claim, such as taxonomy (genera, subfamilies), institutional history, organizations (clubs, leagues), or individuals where applicable.  
   - Avoid overly broad or generic titles; aim for specificity that reflects the core subjects of the output notes.

### Best Practices and Domain-Specific Knowledge

- **Taxonomy:**  
  - Understand hierarchical ranks; e.g., a subfamily contains multiple genera, so species counts at the subfamily level will exceed those at the genus level.  
  - Correct the taxonomic rank if misstated in the claim, clarifying entity scope for accurate interpretation.  

- **Institutions and Dates:**  
  - Compare establishment years accurately to verify historical claims.  
  - Confirm individuals’ affiliations with institutions, such as places where they studied or played sports, referencing specific institutions by their standard names.

- **Sports and Organizations:**  
  - Verify clubs, leagues, and sponsorships precisely; do not assume affiliations without evidence.  
  - Confirm geographic locations of teams and facilities clearly.  

- **General Methodology:**  
  - Explicitly call out when claims are unsupported or contradicted by evidence.  
  - Summarize overlapping or duplicate notes into clear, concise facts without repetition.  
  - Use context to fill in gaps or confirm details missing or unclear in notes.  
  - Present information in a logical and coherent manner to help human verifiers judge the claim independently.  
  - Avoid adding irrelevant or extraneous details — keep outputs focused and factually precise.

### Output Format (Exact)  
- `new_notes`: a list of concise, factually precise notes that synthesize and clarify the claim, supported by note and context information.  
- `titles`: a list of succinct, domain-appropriate titles for the main entities or topics necessary to understand and evaluate the claim.
