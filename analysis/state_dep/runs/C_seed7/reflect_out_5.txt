Task Description:
You will be given two input fields:
- `claim`: a factual statement (often involving comparisons, negations, or multiple entities) that needs to be verified or explored through querying.
- `notes`: a list of statements containing factual information relevant to the claim.

Your goal is to generate an output field:
- `query`: a concise, clear, and structured set of questions that, when answered or retrieved, will allow verification or validation of the `claim`.

Detailed Instructions:

1. Understand the claim fully, identifying all key entities, attributes, and relationships to be verified.

2. Use the provided `notes` to form precise, fact-checking questions that directly address the important components of the claim, such as:
   - Identity or membership (e.g., "Is Bruce Guthro a member of Radiohead?")
   - Relationships between entities (e.g., "Who sings on the Radiohead song 'All I Need'?")
   - Properties or attributes (e.g., gender, nationality, or origin of a person; geographical sizes of countries)
   - Temporal or hierarchical facts (e.g., final film directed by a director)

3. Structure your `query` as one or more short questions that specifically target retrieval of missing or confirmatory facts that will either support or disprove the claim.

4. When the claim involves multiple entities, comparative statements, or negations, ensure your `query` checks each critical aspect individually to guide full verification.

5. Avoid overly broad or ambiguous questions; keep queries focused on:
   - Verifiable facts about people, works (songs, films, compositions), places, or historical details.
   - Directly relevant information that arises in the claim or notes.
   
6. Maintain clarity and completeness to support retrieval of all relevant gold documents or sources indicated by the feedback, ideally covering all entities and key facts mentioned in the claim.

7. The reasoning (though not asked to output) should mentally connect note facts to the claim and help generate the focused queries, since many claims involve reconciling multiple pieces of information, such as:
   - Author/composer identification and associated attributes (gender, nationality)
   - Group membership status of individuals
   - Geographical size comparisons of countries
   - Attribution of works (songs, films) and their creators

This approach ensures that the queries facilitate comprehensive retrieval of all facts necessary to verify complex claims through multiple linked documents.

Example query formats:
- "Who composed [Work]? What is the nationality and gender of the composer?"
- "Is [Person A] a member of [Group]? Who sings on the song [Song Name]?"
- "Which country is geographically smaller: [Country A] or [Country B]? Did [Director] direct [Film]?"

By consistently producing clear, fact-targeted queries that cover all entities and claim components, you will maximize the retrieval effectiveness and support accurate claim validation.
