Task Description: 

You will be provided with three fields as input:

- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion to be evaluated or analyzed.
- `notes`: A list of textual evidences, facts, or statements relevant to the claim.
- `context`: A list of background knowledge excerpts or document summaries that provide domain-specific and detailed information relevant to the claim and notes.

Your goal is to produce two outputs:

1. `new_notes`: A concise, curated subset of the original `notes` that are directly relevant, supportive, or informative in relation to the claim based on the given context. These notes should effectively capture key facts and evidence that help assess the validity or support for the claim. Filter out redundant or unrelated information when possible.

2. `titles`: A list of well-chosen, descriptive titles or document names that reflect important topics, entities, or key concepts emerging from the `context` and `notes`, which are relevant to understanding, verifying, or explaining the claim. These titles often correspond to Wikipedia (or similar) document titles or entities and should cover the main components or subjects involved in the claim.

Key Guidance and Domain-Specific Considerations:

- The claim often involves verification or comparison of specific facts tied to named entities (e.g., people, events, games, films, teams) that require domain knowledge from specialized contexts such as sports events, board games, films, or industry figures.

- The `notes` may contain overlapping information, often repeating or reinforcing the same points with slight variations; your `new_notes` should condense these for clarity and relevance.

- The `context` includes detailed domain-specific factual data such as:
  - Biographies and works of individuals (e.g., film directors, game designers, athletes),
  - Historical or event summaries (e.g., FIFA tournament finals, film release descriptions),
  - Specific organizational or rating systems (e.g., FIDE, TrueSkill),
  - Details on game mechanics or popularity,
  - Connections between entities (e.g., co-directors, team rosters).

- Use reasoning that synthesizes `notes` and `context` to accurately identify whether the claim is supported or contradicted and to extract the most pertinent supporting information.

- When creating `titles`, aim to cover:
  - Key persons involved in the claim,
  - Relevant organizations or events mentioned,
  - Specific works or artifacts related to the claim (e.g., films, board games, teams).

Generalizable Strategy to Solve the Task:

1. Analyze the claim carefully to identify the main entities and relationships involved.
2. Review the `notes` and `context` to find factual evidence and background knowledge related to those entities and claims.
3. Determine which notes contain information directly relevant or evidential to the claim’s truthfulness or explanation.
4. Extract and consolidate these relevant notes into `new_notes`.
5. Identify key documents or entities that best represent the main subjects of the claim as potential titles.
6. Output the `new_notes` and `titles` in a clear and concise manner, facilitating fact-checking or summary understanding.

This task requires a blend of fact verification, summarization, entity recognition, and selection of representative topics reflecting the claim’s domain context.
