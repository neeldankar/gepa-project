You are given three input fields: `claim` (a factual statement or assertion), `notes` (a list of textual observations, findings, or facts related to the claim), and `context` (a list of documents or excerpts relevant to the claim). Your task is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`. 

The detailed task is as follows:

1. **Analyze the `claim`:** Understand the factual assertion made.

2. **Review the `notes`:** These are preliminary findings or evidence related to the claim. They may include supporting facts, contradictions, or acknowledgments of missing information.

3. **Examine the `context`:** This is a set of documents or text excerpts relevant to the claim, which may contain supporting or contradicting information. The context provides factual details that should be used to verify the claim and the notes.

4. **Reasoning:**  
   - Use the `context` to confirm, refute, or qualify the details in the `claim` and `notes`.  
   - Identify what parts of the claim are supported by evidence, which parts are unsupported or contradicted, and note gaps where the evidence is insufficient.  
   - Incorporate domain-specific knowledge inferred from the context (e.g., geopolitical, biographical, entertainment, or technical details) to assess correctness and relevance.  
   - When multiple facts or entities are mentioned (such as place names, people, or organizations), verify these against the context.  
   - If some entities or facts lack mention or verification in the given context, explicitly mark them as "unverified" or note their absence.

5. **Generate `new_notes`:**  
   - Synthesize the verified, relevant, and clear factual findings about the claim, based on your reasoning.  
   - Discard redundant or unsupported notes.  
   - Explicitly indicate uncertainties or lack of evidence where appropriate.  
   - Strive for brevity and clarity, highlighting key confirmed facts and any disputations or clarifications relevant to the claim.

6. **Generate `titles`:**  
   - Identify appropriate document titles or named entities from the `context` that directly relate to the claim and are useful for further reference.  
   - Include titles corresponding to key subjects, persons, places, organizations, or concepts mentioned in the verified facts.  
   - For any unverified or disputed entities, include a title with an annotation (e.g., "(unverified)") to signal lack of confirmation.

Additional guidance:

- Ensure thorough factual verification by cross-referencing `notes` and `context`.  
- The task requires precise, nuanced understanding of domain-specific information, such as geographic governance, corporate headquarters and location relationships, or actor filmographies and nationalities.  
- Avoid inferring or asserting facts beyond what the `context` and `notes` support.  
- Maintain a consistent structure and logical flow between your reasoning, the updated notes, and the associated titles.  
- The goal is to produce an accurate, concise, and transparently supported factual summary (`new_notes`) and relevant reference points (`titles`) that clarify the validity of the `claim`.  
- When applicable, include titles of documents even if the information is negative or their absence informs the verification.

This approach generalizes to any factual claim verification task involving multi-document evidence synthesis, requiring careful correlation between claims, evidence notes, and relevant documented contexts.
