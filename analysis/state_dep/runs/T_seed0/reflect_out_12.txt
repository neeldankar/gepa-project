Task Description:
You will be provided with three input fields:
- `claim`: a statement or set of statements asserting facts to be verified or elaborated.
- `notes`: a list of factual statements or summaries extracted from various sources relevant to the claim.
- `context`: a list of detailed passages or document excerpts related to entities, events, or topics mentioned in the claim and notes.

Your goal is to produce two output fields:
- `new_notes`: a concise, accurate, and focused set of factual notes distilled from the input `notes` and `context`, emphasizing information that directly confirms, refutes, or provides key background for the claim.
- `titles`: a list of document or entity titles (from the `context` or relevant to it) that are most pertinent and useful for verifying or understanding the claim.

Instructions & Domain-Specific Details:
1. **Thorough Verification and Disambiguation**  
   - Claims often involve complex factual components: entities (e.g., schools, athletes, teams), locations, time frames, or specific events (e.g., seasons, championship titles). Carefully disambiguate similarly named entities (e.g., different schools or sports clubs with overlapping names) using detail from context.
   - Verify the claim by cross-referencing the relevant notes and detailed context to determine whether it is supported, contradicted, or unsubstantiated.

2. **Selective Consolidation of Notes**  
   - Select only the most relevant and precise statements from `notes` that clarify or confirm/refute the claim.
   - Remove contradictory, irrelevant, or redundant information.
   - Synthesize insight where multiple notes or context passages reinforce a fact (e.g., confirming a school’s membership in a district or a player’s participation in a specific season).
   - If the claim includes multiple components, address each part in new_notes where possible.

3. **Context Titles Selection**  
   - Identify the minimal but sufficient set of document or entity titles in `context` that covers all aspects necessary to understand or verify the claim.
   - Prioritize titles that directly document crucial facts within the claim (e.g., official team and season pages, biographies of individuals named, relevant events or competition series).
   - Avoid overly broad or unrelated titles.
   - Include any titles of key individuals, organizations, or events mentioned in claim or notes that clarify or provide authoritative supporting evidence.

4. **Domain-Specific Knowledge Considerations**  
   - Educational institutions often have overlapping or similar names and jurisdictions (e.g., multiple high schools in one district) — pay attention to geographic and administrative details.
   - Sports contexts include detailed historical season data, roles (e.g., goalkeeper vs striker), player affiliations and timelines. Recognize real distinctions such as player positions, competition formats, and timelines.
   - Motorsport claims may require understanding of event numbering (e.g., “28th season”) and driver participation periods.
   - Always distinguish between confirming the nickname or identity (e.g., "Red Devils" for Manchester United) and verifying specific claims about personnel or events.

5. **Reasoning Strategy**  
   - Start by extracting the factual elements in the claim.
   - Map each element to supporting or contradicting notes/context.
   - Identify any unsupported or erroneous claim parts based on the evidence.
   - Prepare a clear and focused summary in `new_notes` that could stand alone as a factual summary of claim verification.
   - Select titles that would provide a future reader or verifier access to necessary information.

Summary:
The task is essentially a structured fact-checking and evidence synthesis exercise combining multiple sources of information. The assistant must carefully analyze multiple domain-specific nuances—such as administrative school structures, sports positions and seasons, driver participation years—to produce concise, accurate, and relevant notes and titles for each claim presented.
