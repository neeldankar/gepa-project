Task Description:

Given three inputs — a textual claim, a list of supporting or related textual notes, and a list of relevant background documents or contexts — your goal is to:

1. Analyze the claim carefully.
2. Review the notes that provide factual information, details, or evidential statements related to the claim.
3. Cross-reference and integrate relevant background context information to ensure completeness and accuracy.

Based on this analysis:

- Generate a concise but comprehensive set of **new_notes**: distilled, factual, and relevant statements derived from and supported by the provided notes and context that directly support, clarify, or refute the claim. The new_notes should be informative and factually accurate, avoiding repetition and irrelevant details, and should provide precise references to key entities, dates, events, or relationships that underpin the claim's validity or invalidity.

- Identify and provide a list of **titles**, i.e., the most relevant document or entity titles from the context that correspond to the key facts, persons, events, or concepts central to understanding or verifying the claim. These titles typically correspond to Wikipedia article titles or authoritative source names that best align with the claim content and the supporting information.

Key considerations & domain-specific knowledge:

- The claims often relate to named events (e.g., college football bowl games), teams, companies, TV shows, musical works (albums, songs), people (e.g., filmmakers, players), or documentary films.
- Dates and timing (e.g., football seasons, release years, broadcast dates) are crucial for verifying temporal aspects of claims.
- Sponsorship and organizational details often involve companies related to geographic locations (e.g., AutoZone as Memphis-based sponsor).
- For entertainment facts, correctly matching episodes, song titles, and programming blocks to correct TV shows or albums is important.
- Differentiating between similarly named entities (e.g., an album called "La La Land" vs. an American Dad episode "Lost In Space") is essential.
- Confirm if the claim is supported, contradicted, or not verifiable based on evidence.
- If the claim references participation (e.g., a sports team in a bowl game), verify exact bowl game and season alignment.
- When the claim involves comparisons (e.g., two films not made in the same year), ensure the temporal facts are verified precisely.
- The notes and context often contain overlapping or repetitive information; prioritize unique, direct, and relevant facts.
- Titles should be drawn from the provided context, reflecting the most directly related authoritative documents or entities.

Generalizable Strategy:

- Carefully extract core factual information from notes.
- Check notes against context documents to confirm or expand facts.
- Summarize essential facts in new_notes, making the reasoning traceable.
- Select titles of documents that best represent the core entities or events discussed.
- Avoid extraneous or unrelated content.

Output Format:

- new_notes: List of concise factual statements directly relevant to the claim.
- titles: List of entity/document titles from context that most closely align with claim-relevant facts.

This task supports claim verification and fact extraction across domains such as sports, entertainment, and documentary films by synthesizing diverse information sources into precise, connected knowledge points.
