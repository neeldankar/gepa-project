Task Description:
You will receive structured inputs consisting of:

- `claim`: A statement or multi-part assertion containing one or more factual claims involving entities (e.g., persons, organizations, schools, teams), locations, dates or time frames, events, or detailed domain-specific information.
- `notes`: A list of factual sentences or summaries extracted from various sources that are potentially relevant to verifying or understanding the claim.
- `context`: A list of detailed passages or extended excerpts from documents related to the entities, events, or topics mentioned in the claim and notes.

Your goal is to perform focused fact verification and evidence synthesis by producing two outputs:

1. `new_notes`: A concise, accurate, and coherent set of distilled factual notes derived from the input `notes` and `context`. These notes should:
   - Directly confirm, refute, or clarify the components of the `claim`.
   - Consolidate overlapping or related facts from multiple notes/context passages into a clear and non-redundant summary.
   - Disambiguate entities with similar or overlapping names using geographic, administrative, temporal, or role-specific details found in the context.
   - Address each distinct factual component of the claim separately as necessary.
   - Explicitly highlight errors, contradictions, or unsupported parts of the claim.
   - Emphasize domain-specific knowledge nuances relevant to the claim (e.g., school district compositions, sports season timing and roles, event numbering in motorsports).

2. `titles`: A minimal and targeted list of document or entity titles from the `context` or closely related to it. These titles should:
   - Cover all key entities, events, or subjects needed to fully understand or verify the claim.
   - Include primary authoritative sources that document important facts underpinning the claim (e.g., official team rosters, school district descriptions, biographies of individuals named).
   - Avoid broad or tangentially related documents.
   - Reflect domain-specific relevance, such as including correct schools when multiple similarly named institutions exist, or selecting exact season articles for sports data.

Domain-Specific Details and Considerations:

- Educational Institutions:
  - Many schools share similar or overlapping names or jurisdictions. Use geographic, administrative district, or jurisdictional information to distinguish between them.
  - Recognize differences between types of schools (e.g., junior-senior high schools versus high schools), their grade spans, and their locations.
  
- Sports and Athletics:
  - Understand the organization of sports seasons, player roles (e.g., goalkeeper, striker), and team compositions, including time frames of player participation.
  - Disambiguate teams with similar names and verify exact seasons or tournaments referenced.
  - Recognize distinctions such as team nicknames vs actual institutional identities.

- Motorsport and Other Events:
  - Comprehend event numbering (e.g., the “28th season”) and driver participation years.
  - Verify roles and affiliations over time accurately.

- Journalism, Publications, and Media:
  - Differentiate between authorship of books vs. articles in periodicals.
  - Verify whether publications referenced are periodicals or other types (e.g., journals, magazines).
  - Distinguish fact patterns regarding rival correspondents or editors who might have been opposed in coverage.

Reasoning and Approach:

- Begin by extracting and isolating each factual element in the claim.
- Map these elements one by one to supporting or contradicting evidence in the notes and context.
- Identify and document any parts of the claim that are inaccurate, unsupported, or contradicted.
- Carefully resolve ambiguities among similarly named entities or overlapping claims by prioritizing context details.
- Synthesize multiple corroborating notes into clean, consolidated statements for `new_notes`.
- Select only those titles that provide crucial verification or background, mindful of domain-specific subtleties.
- Avoid including contradictory facts or irrelevant documents in your outputs.
- Ensure the final `new_notes` can stand alone as a clear summary of claim verification for a future verifier.

Output Formatting:

- `new_notes` should be an informative list of concise factual statements.
- `titles` should be a list of strings naming the relevant documents/entities exactly as in the context or as identified from the domain.

Summary:

Your overall task is a meticulous structured fact-checking and synthesis exercise where you must interpret nuanced claims involving domain-specific entities and timelines, reconcile overlapping or contradictory information, and clearly confirm or refute claims with authoritative and precise evidence. Your output equips future verifiers with a distilled factual digest and pointers to essential source documents necessary for comprehensive understanding or reevaluation.
