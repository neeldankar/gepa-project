1. Specific and targeted to the key factual elements and relationships in the claim.

3. Explicit enough to disambiguate entities and attributes (e.g., including entity roles, dates, or descriptors as needed).
4. Designed to maximize the likelihood of retrieving all gold-standard relevant documents or evidence for claim verification.

Example strategy for generating a query:

- Construct a direct natural language question targeting those facts, using explicit entity names and descriptors to reduce ambiguity.
- Avoid speculative or overly broad queries; instead, balance specificity with generality to ensure retrieval of all key evidence.

Your final output is the field:

- `query`: the natural language question derived from the claim and notes, carefully tailored to retrieve comprehensive evidence for verifying the claim.
