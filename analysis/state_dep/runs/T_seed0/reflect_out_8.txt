You will be provided with three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- The `claim` is a factual statement or set of statements that need verification or support.
- The `notes` are a list of extracted sentences or short passages that may contain relevant factual information related to the claim.
- The `context` is a list of longer documents or passages that provide background and detailed information about entities mentioned in the claim or notes.

Your task is to:

1. **Analyze the claim** carefully to understand the entities, relationships, and facts involved.  
2. **Verify the claim by cross-referencing it with the given `notes` and `context`.** Extract and confirm relevant facts from the notes and context that support or relate directly to the content of the claim.
3. **Generate two output fields:**
    - `new_notes`: A deduplicated, concise list of the key factual notes that directly support or explain the claim. These should be phrased as clear, standalone factual statements distilled from the input `notes` and any necessary information confirmed from the context. Avoid vague or irrelevant details.
    - `titles`: A list of relevant document or entity titles (derived from the provided context and notes) that correspond to the key factual points needed to support the claim. These titles should identify the primary sources or subjects (e.g., names of people, organizations, locations, institutions) which one would use to further inspect or verify the claim.

Additional guidance and domain-specific details based on prior examples:

- Focus on precise administrative/geopolitical relationships for geography-related claims (e.g., county-city relationships, jurisdiction under provinces, country memberships).
- For claims involving people and their roles or associations (e.g., who held a title or position, sports affiliations), identify the exact institution or entity involved and the individual's connection to it.
- For university-related facts, distinguish between public/private designations, historical naming, and key personnel roles.
- If multiple notes are repetitive or near-duplicates, consolidate them into a single clear fact in `new_notes`.
- Ensure `new_notes` covers all key factual components of the claim, and the `titles` list includes all relevant authoritative sources or entities corresponding to those notes.
- Avoid including unrelated items in `new_notes` or `titles`.
- When the claim involves comparisons or relations between two or more entities (e.g., two cities or universities), explicitly mention both entities and their relevant shared attributes (such as both being in the same country, both being public universities, etc.).
- Make sure the `titles` reflect the documents needed for validating all claim components, matching the gold standard documents as closely as possible.

Use logical reasoning steps to interpret the claim, identify supporting evidence, and refine input notes accordingly. The goal is to generate an accurate, informative, and compact summary of evidentiary notes and their corresponding authoritative titles that would verify the claim in an information retrieval or fact-verification context.
