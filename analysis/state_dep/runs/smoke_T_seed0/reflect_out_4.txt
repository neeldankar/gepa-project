- **claim**: a factual or comparative statement involving entities, events, or items that requires verification or elaboration.
- **notes**: a collection of sentences related to the claim, containing factual information, partial confirmations, or contextual details.
- **context**: detailed source-like descriptions or encyclopedic text snippets that provide background information about entities or events mentioned in the claim and notes.

2. **Creating `new_notes`**  
   Synthesize concise, factual, and directly relevant statements that support, clarify, or correct the claim based on your analysis. Remove redundant or irrelevant sentences found in the original `notes`.

   - Capture important contextual facts such as distinctions between similar entities, clarifications on naming or categorization, or corrections of inaccuracies in the claim.  
   - When dealing with entities from different domains (e.g., opera vs. film) or structural differences (e.g., acts in operas vs. films), note these distinctions explicitly if they affect claim validation.

3. **Creating `titles`**  
   Extract or generate a list of concise document or topic titles from the `context` that are the most relevant and necessary to support or verify the claim.  
   - Include titles of key people, works, or items involved in the claim.  
   - Include titles of significant documents or concepts that clarify necessary distinctions or provide authoritative facts.  
   - Titles should be specific and broadly reflect source content (e.g., "Il trovatore", "Magic Hunter", "Travis Oates").

5. **Generalizable Strategy**:  
   - Disambiguate entities and correct inaccuracies in claims based on evidence.  
   - Use explicit confirmation, contradiction, or clarification to resolve claim components.  
   - Prioritize authoritative and comprehensive sources from `context` for title extraction.  
   - Remove extraneous or redundant information in `new_notes` to produce concise, high-value content for claim verification.

Your outputs should focus on clarity, relevance, and precision to assist in determining claim veracity and link to pertinent source documents.
