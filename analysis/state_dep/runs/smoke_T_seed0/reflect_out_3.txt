Task Description:
You are provided with an input claim (a factual statement or assertion) and a list of notes that contain related information extracted from various sources. Your objective is to produce a precise and focused search query (or set of queries) that can effectively verify or refute the claim by retrieving relevant documents or sources not currently included in the notes.

Input Format:
- claim: a single sentence or phrase representing a factual claim that requires verification.
- notes: a list of strings containing evidence, facts, or information snippets related to the claim. These notes may confirm, deny, or be insufficient to fully verify the claim.

Output Requirements:
- Produce only the field "query" containing one or more natural language questions or keyword phrases tailored to retrieving authoritative and specific documents that would help conclusively verify the claim.
- Focus on queries that directly address any gaps or unresolved elements indicated by the notes.
- Queries should target definitive facts such as identities, roles, relationships, attributes, specific dates, affiliations, or key details mentioned or alluded to by the claim.
- If the claim combines multiple sub-claims or elements, break down the query into parts that can independently verify each critical component.
- Write queries in clear, concise, grammatically correct natural language that can be used as search inputs to retrieve relevant documents or passages.

Reasoning Strategy (implicit in query construction):
- Compare the claim’s components with the notes to identify which parts are confirmed and which are uncertain or lack sufficient evidence.
- Identify key entities, events, dates, or relationships referenced in the claim and absent or ambiguous in the notes.
- Formulate queries explicitly designed to find authoritative sources or documents that confirm these missing or ambiguous pieces.
- Avoid irrelevant or overly broad queries that do not focus on the claim verification.
- When relevant, specify precise time periods, persons' full names, event names, titles, and identifiers to ensure retrieval of correct documents.
- If a claim involves matching multiple attributes (e.g., a person’s role at multiple points in time), frame queries that separate these attributes clearly.

Additional Domain-Specific Details:
- For claims involving people (e.g., directors, coaches), queries should include full names, roles, and time frames.
- For claims involving products, brands, or media (e.g., drinks, films), queries should clarify geography, versions, or related media (e.g., songs, co-directors).
- For claims related to organizations or events, specify seasons, conferences, or group memberships precisely to avoid ambiguity.
- Incorporate knowledge from notes about what is missing, e.g., official titles, historical leadership in a season, or flavor profiles not documented.

Overall, the task is to create targeted and effective search queries that help complete the verification process of a factual claim by retrieving missing source documents, based on the discrepancy or absence of evidence in the given notes.
