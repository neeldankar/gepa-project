Task Description:

You will receive three inputs:

- **claim**: A factual or partially factual statement involving specific entities (people, organizations, locations, events, films, sports matches, etc.), roles, dates, or outcomes that requires verification or detailed analysis.
- **notes**: A collection (list) of statements relevant to verifying or refuting the claim. These notes may repeat or partially overlap and vary in clarity and completeness.
- **context**: A list of detailed contextual document excerpts or summaries containing authoritative and structured information about the core entities, events, dates, or topics referenced in the claim.

Your objective is to integrate and synthesize information from *all* three inputs to produce:

1. **new_notes**: A concise, refined subset of the original notes that most directly supports, clarifies, or refutes the claim with factual accuracy and clarity. These notes should:
   - Eliminate redundancy and irrelevance.
   - Highlight key facts such as exact dates, precise roles or attributions, event outcomes, entity identifications.
   - Correctly reflect temporal order, geography, attributions, or domain-specific subtleties.
   - Avoid speculation or unsupported assertions.
   - Summarize or rephrase text for clarity when beneficial, preserving factual correctness.

2. **titles**: A list of informative, accurate document or topic titles derived from the context that are essential to understanding or verifying the claim. Requirements:
   - Titles should correspond to the main documents or topics containing relevant information about the claim’s key entities/events.
   - Use official or commonly accepted names, including relevant qualifiers such as year, version, or season (e.g., "Wonder Woman (2017 film)", "Fargo TV series (Season 3)").
   - Include multiple titles if multiple contextual sources jointly contribute to verifying the claim.
   - Ensure titles are concise but sufficiently specific to identify the core source.

---

Guidance and Domain-Specific Details:

- **Core entities include:**  
  - Named people (potentially multiple with similar names; disambiguate carefully).  
  - Organizations and companies.  
  - Geographic locations (be mindful of geopolitical boundaries and distinctions, e.g., country vs. state).  
  - Films, TV series (including seasons, episodes), books, and other works, with precise titles and release years.  
  - Sports events (including leagues, tournaments like FIFA Confederations Cup, individual matches) with accurate participant names and match outcomes.  
  - Historical military units, events, and timelines.

- **Temporal reasoning is often critical:**  
  - Confirm event or release dates (e.g., film release year, TV season premiere dates).  
  - Confirm sequence of events or performances to validate temporal claims (e.g., a performance occurred before/after another event).

- **Role and attribution accuracy:**  
  - Confirm who performed (e.g., who acted in a film, who scored in a match, who voiced a character).  
  - Verify specific roles or titles (e.g., poet-diplomat laureates, type of aircraft manufacturer headquarters).

- **Geographical clarifications:**  
  - Be precise about locations and political boundaries (e.g., both entities located in the same country, not just the same state; understanding U.S. states as subdivisions of one country).

- **Avoid contradictions and unsupported claims:**  
  - Cross-check notes and context to ensure no contradictory facts are included unless highlighting and explaining the dispute explicitly.  
  - If the claim is invalid, clearly point out the fact-based reasons.

---

Methodological Strategy:

1. **Parse the claim** to identify key entities, dates, actions, or relationships requiring verification.  
2. **Search the context** documents thoroughly to extract authoritative facts about these entities, including background, dates, roles, and outcomes. Context is typically the most reliable source.  
3. **Match and verify facts from notes** with context: select notes that are supported and relevant, discard or correct notes that contradict or are irrelevant.  
4. **Summarize and refine notes** to create a coherent, precise subset that directly addresses the claim’s validity and components.  
5. **Determine the key context documents/topics needed** to understand or verify the claim, naming them clearly and accurately as titles.  
6. **Present outputs succinctly** so they can be used directly for downstream verification or further analysis.
