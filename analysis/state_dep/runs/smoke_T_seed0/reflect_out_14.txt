Task Description:

You will be given three inputs to analyze and verify a factual claim:

- `claim`: a statement that makes assertions involving specific entities, dates, events, roles, or categorizations, which may be factually true or false.
- `notes`: a list of supporting or refuting statements, facts, or evidence snippets relevant to the claim.
- `context`: a list of detailed document excerpts or summaries containing authoritative information about the entities, events, or topics mentioned in the claim.

Your goal is to process these inputs deeply and produce two outputs:

1. **new_notes**: a concise, carefully curated and refined subset of the original `notes` that directly supports or refutes the claim based on the context. These notes should emphasize factual accuracy, clarity, and relevant details crucial for verification.

2. **titles**: a list of clear, precise, and informative titles corresponding to the main documents or core topics from the `context` essential to understanding and verifying the claim. Titles should accurately capture key entities, events, or themes.

---

Detailed Instructions and Guidance:

1. **Claim Verification Approach**

   - Parse the claim carefully, extracting key entities, dates, roles, events, or facts asserted.
   - Search the `context` to find authoritative information on those entities or events, paying particular attention to:
     - Proper names, roles, titles (e.g., names of bands, composers, football clubs).
     - Specific dates or timelines mentioned (e.g., founding years, release years, event dates).
     - Precise attribution of actions or outcomes (e.g., who scored in a match, who voiced a character).
     - Domain-specific details (e.g., league tiers, subscriptions, musical catalog numbers).
   - Cross-check the `notes` against the `context` to confirm or reject the claim, excluding irrelevant, redundant, or unsupported notes.
   - When the claim compares quantities (e.g., number of albums), verify the data and note any missing or incomplete information that prevents verification.

2. **Refining `new_notes`**

   - Include only statements from the original `notes` that are most relevant and factually precise for confirming or denying the claim.
   - Summarize or rephrase notes to improve clarity and factual precision where appropriate.
   - Avoid speculation or unsupported inferences.
   - Emphasize key facts such as exact dates, correct attribution of roles/actors/participants, clear outcomes, and accurate entity identification.
   - Remove duplicates and irrelevant statements.

3. **Generating Accurate and Informative `titles`**

   - Titles should represent the main documents or topic headings in the `context` that contain essential information needed to understand or verify the claim.
   - Use official, commonly recognized, or full names of entities, works, events, organizations, teams, or persons.
   - When multiple documents cover different necessary aspects, include multiple titles.
   - Keep titles concise yet descriptive enough to clearly relate to the core claim elements.
  
4. **Domain-Specific Considerations**

   - **Sports Domain:**
     - Distinguish types of "football" explicitly: American football (NFL conferences like NFC and AFC) vs. association football (soccer leagues, club divisions).
     - Note proper league names, tiers, and sponsors.
     - Correctly attribute statistics such as who scored, league memberships, and event outcomes.
     - Clarify any common confusions such as club naming similarities or league sponsorship.

   - **Entertainment / Music Domain:**
     - Carefully distinguish between artists and bands with similar or related names.
     - Confirm exact release counts (e.g., albums, EPs) based on context.
     - Distinguish between entities that "perform as" others or have alternate names.
     - Use catalog numbers, opus numbers, or composition details to disambiguate musical works.
     - Verify composer identities, nationalities, and compositions' dating, particularly when multiple composers with similar or overlapping names appear.

   - **Historical / Military Domain:**
     - Confirm regiment or battle dates, formation histories, and participating entities precisely.
     - Validate roles and titles of individuals involved, ensuring no confusion among similarly named persons.

5. **Generalizable Problem-Solving Strategy**

   - Break down the claim into core factual assertions.
   - Thoroughly scan the `context` for authoritative, detailed coverage on these assertions.
   - Identify and select the subset of `notes` that directly corroborate or contradict the claim using `context`.
   - Summarize and prioritize facts for clarity and brevity.
   - Extract or compose clear titles of the key source documents essential to understanding the claim and the verification process.
   - Generate outputs that are concise, unambiguous, and easy to interpret by downstream users or systems.

---

Following these instructions will enable accurate, domain-aware verification of factual claims, precise note refinement, and reliable contextual document identification across diverse knowledge areas such as sports, music, and history.
