Task Description:

You will be given three inputs:
- `claim`: a specific statement or assertion involving named entities, events, dates, roles, or factual information that may need verification or nuanced clarification.
- `notes`: a list of statements or evidences that relate to the claim, potentially supporting or refuting it.
- `context`: a list of detailed textual summaries, document excerpts, or authoritative descriptions pertaining to the entities, events, and subjects mentioned in the claim.

Your objective is to perform a precise, factually grounded vetting and synthesis of the claim using the provided notes and context, producing two outputs:

1. `new_notes`: a carefully curated and refined subset of `notes` that best supports or refutes the claim, emphasizing clear, concise, and accurate factual information drawn from `context`. This should exclude irrelevant, redundant, contradictory, or speculative information, aiming for maximal clarity, factual precision, and relevance.

2. `titles`: a list of concise, informative titles that describe the main topical documents or core contextual themes essential to fully understanding, analyzing, or verifying the claim. The titles should refer clearly and specifically to the key entities, events, topics, or sources found within the `context`.

Detailed Instructions and Domain-Specific Guidance:

1. **Claim Verification Methodology:**
   - Parse the claim thoroughly, identifying:
     - Named entities (people, teams, organizations, films, locations).
     - Key events or roles described (e.g., scoring in a match, award nominations, team membership).
     - Temporal details (dates, seasons, periods).
     - Domain or event-specific terminology (e.g., league names, film titles, award show specifics).
   - Scrutinize the context to locate direct, authoritative facts related to these elements.
   - Correlate the notes with context facts to confirm, partially confirm, or contradict the claim.
   - Consider nuances such as different people with similar names, different forms of football (American vs. association football), distinctions between actors’ roles and award nominations, or changes in league sponsorships/structures.
   - Identify any missing but essential elements in notes or context for claim verification.

2. **Refining `new_notes`:**
   - Select notes that are directly relevant and factually supported by the context.
   - Summarize or synthesize multiple related notes into compact, clear statements if needed.
   - Remove duplicate or off-topic notes that do not aid verification.
   - Maintain strict accuracy—avoid speculation or inference beyond what the context allows.
   - Highlight exact details such as:
     - Correct names and spellings of entities.
     - Specific dates, awards, match results, or roles.
     - Clear attributions of actions to correct actors, players, producers, or other entities.
   - When an element in the claim is unsupported or contradicted by context, include notes that clarify this absence or contradiction explicitly.

3. **Generating Precise `titles`:**
   - Titles serve as a thematic summary of the context documents critical to the claim.
   - Use well-recognized, official, or widely accepted names of films, sports teams, leagues, people, or events.
   - Titles should avoid vague or overly generic phrasing; they must specifically reflect key subjects (e.g., "Ellesmere Port Town F.C. Overview" instead of generic "Football Club").
   - Generate multiple titles if multiple distinct entities or cluster topics in context are involved.
   - Titles assist downstream users in swiftly identifying relevant source material for the claim.

4. **Domain-Specific Considerations:**
   - **Entertainment domain:** 
     - Distinguish carefully between actors with similar or related names.
     - Confirm specifics like award nominations and roles per film or show.
     - Separate lead roles from supporting or cameo appearances.
     - Confirm film titles fully (including year or subtitle if needed to disambiguate).
     - Recognize the difference between starring in a production and producing or directing it.
   - **Sports domain:** 
     - Differentiate between kinds of football (American football vs. soccer/association football).
     - Verify league memberships carefully, as multiple leagues and sponsorships exist with similar names.
     - Get correct team names, and ascertain whether leagues are sponsored by certain organizations.
     - Distinguish among different teams with similar location-based names.
     - Include specific seasons or divisions when possible.
   - **Biographical or historical domain:** 
     - Verify dates of formation, tenure, or participation.
     - Confirm correct roles and titles held, historical events, or recognitions.
   - Cross-check claim components, notes, and context carefully for internal consistency.

5. **Generalizable Strategy:**
   - Parse claim to extract entities, roles, and assertions.
   - Search context for definitive fact patterns tied to these entities.
   - Map notes content to context facts, verifying or refuting the claim systematically.
   - Refine notes to those that best encapsulate verifiable truths or contradictions.
   - Generate clear, precise document or thematic titles that map to the core context relevant to the claim.
   - Ensure outputs are succinct, unambiguous, and aligned with verifiable knowledge.

This multi-faceted, domain-aware approach will maximize the factual accuracy, relevance, and clarity of your verification output, supporting effective assessment across diverse knowledge areas.
