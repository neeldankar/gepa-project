You will be given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- `claim`: A factual or informational statement that may need to be verified or explained.
- `notes`: A list of extracted or summarized statements derived from various documents related to the claim and context.
- `context`: A list of longer document excerpts or topic descriptions that provide detailed background information relevant to the claim.

Your task is to produce two output fields:

1. `new_notes`: A concise, coherent summary that synthesizes key factual information from the `notes` and `context` which supports, refutes, or clarifies the `claim`. It should:
    - Extract the core, relevant facts that directly address the claim.
    - Remove redundant or irrelevant details.
    - Explicitly connect entities (people, bands, organizations, events) and facts as needed to verify or explain the claim.
    - Correctly reflect any nuances or limitations in the evidence (e.g., if some parts of the claim are unsupported or contradicted).
    - Organize information logically for clarity and factual accuracy.

2. `titles`: A list of document or topic titles that are essential for understanding and verifying the claim. These should ideally correspond to the key entities, events, or subjects identified in the `context` and `notes` that underpin the reasoning.

Additional considerations and domain-specific nuances to incorporate:

- When dealing with music-related claims, pay attention to:
  - Band names, formation history, genres (e.g., "progressive rock", "electronic", "alternative rock").
  - Collaborations between artists and associated bands.
  - Judge panels for music competition shows, including individual judges and their roles.
  - Distinguishing between similarly named entities (e.g., bands, artists, awards).
- For political claims:
  - Note birth dates, leadership succession dates, and the connection between different party levels (federal, provincial, youth wings).
  - Demonstrate understanding of party structures and affiliations (e.g., "Ontario New Democratic Youth" as affiliated to both provincial and federal parties).
- Recognize when claims are only partially supported or not supported by the available notes and context, and explicitly mention this.
- Use precise, evidence-based language—avoid making unsupported assertions.
- Include a brief step-by-step reasoning summary in your own internal process to identify the key facts from notes and context before producing `new_notes`, but do not output reasoning to the user.
- Titles should be comprehensive but relevant: include all major documents or entity titles that provide evidence directly related to the claim.
- Avoid including extraneous context titles unrelated to the claim.

In summary, your goal is to:

- Verify the accuracy and completeness of the claim by synthesizing evidence.
- Summarize findings into concise, clear `new_notes`.
- Identify and list the relevant document titles (`titles`) that are crucial for verification and context.
