Task Description:

You will be given two fields, `claim` and `notes`. The `claim` is a factual or knowledge-based statement that requires verification or fact-checking. The `notes` field contains a list of sentences or bullet points extracted or summarized from various documents or context relevant to the claim, including potential evidence that supports, contradicts, or is neutral towards the claim.

Your goal is to produce a clear, focused, and efficient *search query* (output field `query`) that can be used to retrieve evidence documents or data to verify the truthfulness of the `claim`. The query should be designed to maximize the retrieval of relevant documents supporting or refuting the claim and minimize retrieval of irrelevant documents. 

Instructions for generating the query:

1. Analyze the `claim` carefully to understand the key entities, relationships, dates, and facts involved. Identify the main factual assertions in the claim that require verification.

2. Review the `notes` to gather background knowledge and domain-specific information that may help refine or contextualize the claim for the search. The `notes` often contain critical domain-specific temporal facts (e.g., founding dates, term years), geographic details (e.g., jurisdictions, provinces, cities), biographical details (e.g., players, managers, film stars, familial relationships), and precise named entities (e.g., sports teams, cities, films, persons).

3. From this analysis, compose a concise but comprehensive query that:
   - Explicitly targets the key parts of the claim that must be verified.
   - Includes relevant named entities (e.g., proper nouns, dates, official names).
   - Uses natural language questions or keyword phrases that align with typical knowledge sources.
   - Avoids unnecessary ambiguity or overly broad terms, focusing on search terms that can retrieve authoritative evidence documents.

4. When formulating the query, account for domain-specific nuances extracted from the notes such as:
   - Baseball franchises’ founding years and managerial history.
   - Administrative divisions and geographic hierarchies in China.
   - Filmographies and familial relations between actors in cinema.
   
5. The goal of the query is to guide a retrieval system toward documents that can confirm or reject the claim with relevant factual evidence.

Example approach from the illustrations:

- Identify the precise claim elements (e.g., “Canadian baseball team founded in 1969,” “manager of Tampa Bay Devil Rays,” “player tenure 1973-1987”).
- Note corrections or clarifications in the notes (e.g., real founding dates).
- Formulate direct fact-check queries that ask about these components explicitly for verification.

Your final output should only contain the field `query` with the refined question or search phrase best suited to retrieve documents relevant to verifying the claim.
