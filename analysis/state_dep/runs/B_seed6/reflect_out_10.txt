- `context` is a list of full documents, article excerpts, or authoritative sources from which the `notes` were derived, containing substantive background information.

Your task is to produce two precise output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

1. **new_notes**:
   - Extract and consolidate the most relevant, accurate, and non-redundant notes from the provided `notes` that are essential for evaluating or verifying the `claim`.
   - Retain unique, critical facts or explicit data points such as dates, names, locations, roles, relationships, and ownership that directly support or refute the claim.
   - Remove duplicate, redundant, or overly similar notes while preserving a comprehensive overview of relevant evidence.
   - Include clarifications regarding missing or unavailable key information when verification cannot be completed due to lack of data (for example, unavailability of birthdate information to confirm age).
   - Ensure `new_notes` are concise but detailed enough to allow an informed assessment of the claim.

2. **titles**:
   - Identify and list the titles (article titles, well-known entity names, or key topic names) from the `context` that are explicitly referenced or crucial to verify or understand the `claim`.
   - Include all principal entities mentioned in the claim (e.g., persons, organizations, works, locations).
   - Include related entities or concepts that provide important background or essential clarifying information.
   - Avoid including unrelated or tangential document titles.
   - Titles should reflect the recognized Wikipedia-style or canonical naming as seen in the provided `context`.

**Additional domain-specific guidance:**

- Claims often involve verifying factual data about persons (e.g., birthdates, nationalities), works (e.g., films, songs, albums), organizational relationships (ownership, location, affiliations), or historical facts.
- Name variations or partial references require careful linkage to the correct entities using context clues.
- Ownership and location relations frequently involve layered corporate or property details; note these relationships clearly when relevant.
- The presence of duplicated or slightly variant notes is common; synthesize these into a streamlined, comprehensive fact set.
- The goal is to provide a minimal yet comprehensive subset of notes and document/entity titles sufficient for evidence-based evaluation or refutation of the claim.
- If the claim cannot be fully verified or refuted based on the available data, explicitly acknowledge which information is missing or inconclusive.

**Recommended reasoning approach (not to be included in output):**

- Internally analyze the claim against notes to identify strongest relevant facts.
- Resolve ambiguities in names or data by cross-referencing context.
- Summarize supporting or contradictory facts.
- Identify gaps or missing data that prevent conclusive verification.
- Produce concise, factual, and well-structured outputs.

**Output format:**

- `new_notes`: a list of concise, non-redundant, claim-relevant factual notes synthesized from `notes`.
- `titles`: a list of key document or entity titles from `context` vital for understanding or verifying the claim.

Adhere strictly to this output format and focus outputs on evidence useful for factual claim assessment grounded in the linked contextual documents.
