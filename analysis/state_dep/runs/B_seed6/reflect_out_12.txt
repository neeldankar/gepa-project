Task Description:

You are given three input fields:  
- claim: A factual statement or assertion, potentially involving relationships or attributes of entities.  
- notes: A list of extracted evidentiary statements summarizing information relevant to the claim. These often repeat key facts or indicate presence or absence of supporting evidence. They sometimes include mentions of collaborations, species habitat, lifespan overlaps, or origin information depending on the domain.  
- context: A list of reference documents or excerpts providing additional background knowledge relevant to the claim and notes. These may contain encyclopedic descriptions, biographical data, taxonomic classifications, collaborative histories, dates, or definitions.

Your goal is to produce the two output fields:  
- new_notes: A concise, synthesized set of statements derived from notes and context that summarizes the core supporting and opposing evidence about the claim's correctness. The new_notes should:  
  * Clearly highlight which elements of the claim are supported by evidence and which are not.  
  * Reference temporal overlaps, habitat distributions, provenance, collaborations etc., as relevant.  
  * Avoid redundant or excessive detail; focus on key factual points that directly address the claim.  
  * Remove speculative, irrelevant or unsupported details from the original notes.

- titles: A concise list of relevant entities/concepts/document titles drawn from the context that pertain directly to the claim and the evidence. These likely correspond to Wikipedia or authoritative document titles of people, species, organizations, events, or cultural works mentioned in the notes and context.

Key Domain-Specific Points and Strategies:  
- When the claim references species and their habitat, gather taxonomic and geographic habitat information. Note differences between closely related species and highlight missing evidence if a habitat attribution is unsupported.  
- For claims about collaborations or organizational affiliations, establish the nationality, duration, or nature of these relationships explicitly; point out any mismatches or lack of evidence.  
- When lifespan or temporal coverage overlaps are involved, confirm dates of birth, death, event production, and note overlaps explicitly.  
- Recognize names of bands, species, films, and persons carefully and disambiguate among similarly named entities using context.  
- Extract and prioritize direct evidence from notes and context that supports or contradicts the claim rather than restating the claim itself.  
- Organize reasoning logically and clearly to convey which parts of the claim are true or false based on evidence.  
- Titles should be specific and relevant to the core entities discussed in notes/context (e.g., "Basil Dean", "The Godfather Part II", not general terms).  
- Avoid introducing information that is not supported by input notes or context.

Overall, your output should reflect a high-quality factual verification synthesis, demonstrating your ability to:  
* integrate evidence from disparate notes and context,

* select relevant entity/document titles for focused topical extraction.

Use clear, precise language suitable for knowledge verification, disambiguation of entities, and summarization of factual evidence.
