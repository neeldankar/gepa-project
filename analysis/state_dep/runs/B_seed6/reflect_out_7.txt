Task Description:
You are given three inputs: `claim` (a statement about a factual or conceptual matter), `notes` (a collection of extracted pieces of factual or evidential information related to the claim), and `context` (a set of documents or passages providing background knowledge and authoritative information relevant to the claim).

Your goal is to analyze these inputs and produce two outputs:  
1. `new_notes`: a refined list of concise, relevant, and accurate notes directly supporting or clarifying the claim, distilled from and consistent with the given `notes` and `context`.  
2. `titles`: a list of document or article titles from the `context` that are essential for understanding and verifying the claim. These should correspond to authoritative and specific sources in the context that address key entities, events, or concepts mentioned in the claim and notes.

Detailed Guidance:
- Carefully verify the factual claim against the `context` and the provided `notes`. Identify and reconcile any contradictions or inaccuracies.  
- Retain only the key points in `new_notes` that are directly relevant and factually correct with respect to the claim and context, avoiding repetition.  
- The `new_notes` should reflect the verified truth or corrected position about the claim, summarizing critical evidence or reasoning as derived from the notes and context.  
- The `titles` should represent specific Wikipedia or encyclopedic entry titles (or similarly authoritative document headings) corresponding to the main subjects, entities, or core concepts linked to the claim and notes.  
- The correctness of `titles` is essential to ensure the sources cover all major relevant aspects of the claim (e.g., people named, specific breeds/species, films, military units, historical periods).  
- Domain-specific entities frequently encountered may include: dog breeds and their origin/breeding information; film directors, films, TV series, and their creators; military units, regiments, battles, and historical timelines; and biographical details.  
- Disambiguate names and terms if the context contains multiple related or similarly named entities to ensure precision.  
- When the claim contains a compound or multi-part statement, each part should be evaluated separately, and the notes/titles should adequately represent the nuanced verification or refutation.  
- Where a confusion or false association is present in the claim (e.g., mixing different individuals or entities), clarify this explicitly in the `new_notes`.  
- Summarize reasoning that links notes to the verification of the claim, implicitly or explicitly, to guide selection of notes and titles.

Generalizable Strategy:
- Parse the claim into key factual assertions.  
- Cross-reference these assertions with the `notes` and `context` to check for confirmation or refutation.  
- Distill and prune the `notes` into a minimal, non-redundant set of accurate points directly addressing the claim’s facts.  
- Identify the most authoritative and relevant context documents based on named entities and subjects critical to understanding the claim and its validation.  
- Present these sources as the `titles` to support future fact-checking or explanation.  

Output format:  
- `new_notes`: list of concise, factually accurate statements derived from notes/context, avoiding redundancy.  
- `titles`: list of document titles precisely matching authoritative entities or topics relevant to the claim.

This approach ensures factual accuracy, relevance, and traceability of information for claims involving specialized, domain-specific knowledge.
