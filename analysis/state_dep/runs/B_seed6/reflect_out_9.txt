You are provided with three input fields: 
- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion that may be true or false.
- `notes`: A list of factual sentences or extracted statements relevant to the claim.
- `context`: A list of document or knowledge snippets related to entities or topics mentioned in the claim.

Your task is to produce two output fields:
1. `new_notes`: A distilled, concise, and relevant subset of the provided `notes` that best support or refute the claim, focusing on accuracy, direct relevance, and key facts needed to verify the claim.
2. `titles`: A list of document or article titles that correspond closely to the most pertinent knowledge sources (from `context`) supporting your `new_notes`. The titles should correspond to authoritative or primary documents relevant to the claim and should ideally cover all essential aspects needed for verification.

Detailed instructions and domain knowledge:
- The claim often involves entities like companies, airports, actors, films, characters, and their relationships such as locations, roles, timelines, nationalities, and specific details about media productions.
- The `notes` may contain redundancy, contradictions, or extraneous details. Your job is to extract the core facts that directly pertain to validating the truthfulness of the claim.
- The `context` may contain many document summaries or excerpts. Titles to be retrieved should correspond to documents strongly connected to the distinct key facts in your `new_notes`. Avoid unrelated or only tangentially connected documents.
- Pay special attention to temporal sequences, geographic hierarchies (e.g., company headquarters, state vs. country), nationality details, cast membership, character-actor relationships, and release dates or timelines, as these often underlie the claim verification.
- If the claim involves contradictions (e.g., a stated nationality that does not match data), your `new_notes` should clearly reflect the discrepancy.
- Summarize or consolidate multiple similar notes into clear, authoritative statements, avoiding duplication.
- Your output should allow a human or system reviewer to confidently assess the claim’s validity based on facts cited in `new_notes` and referenced by the `titles` of authoritative sources.
- Where possible, ensure the `titles` collectively cover all key referenced facts in your `new_notes` so the underlying knowledge sources can be reviewed.

Generalizable strategy:  
- Parse the claim to identify key entities and relationships.
- Select notes that clearly confirm or disprove each key element of the claim.
- Identify which documents from `context` hold the strongest factual support for these notes, and include their titles.
- Discard irrelevant or repeated notes and extraneous documents.
- Present a clear chain of evidence enabling straightforward claim verification.

Deliver the output strictly in the specified format:
- `new_notes` as a concise list of factual statements relevant to the claim.
- `titles` as a list of document/article titles that map directly to the key evidence in `new_notes`.
