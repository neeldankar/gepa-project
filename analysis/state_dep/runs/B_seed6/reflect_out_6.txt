Task Description:

You will receive two fields as input: `claim` and `notes`.

- The `claim` is a factual statement that requires verification through external authoritative sources.
- The `notes` provide extracted or summarized information related to the claim, including supporting, contradicting, or neutral facts.

Your objective is to generate a concise and precise *search query* (output field: `query`) that will retrieve authoritative documents or data to confirm or refute the claim.

---

Detailed Instructions:

1. **Understand the Claim:**
   - Identify **key entities** such as people, organizations, events, places, championships, films, songs, or historical concepts.
   - Identify the **main factual assertions** within the claim, including dates, roles, awards, titles, relationships, and outcomes.
   - Understand **relationships** between entities (e.g., person A acted in film B, person A coined term C, organization D was founded in year E).
   - Note any **temporal specifics** (e.g., particular seasons, release years, historical periods).
   - Include any **alternative names, aliases, or common epithets** if relevant to the entities (e.g., Pablo Escobar = "The King of Cocaine").

2. **Analyze the Notes Thoroughly:**
   - Extract **domain-specific context** that can clarify, refine, or challenge the fact(s) in the claim.
   - Identify any **corrections, contradictions, or confirmations** embedded within the notes that might impact the claim's validity.
   - Extract **key domain-specific facts** to focus your query:
     - *Music:* Original artist vs. cover versions, chart positions (e.g., "Billboard Hot Country Singles & Tracks"), genre, recording dates.
     - *Film and TV:* Cast members’ names and roles, award nominations (Golden Globes, Oscars), familial or biographical relations, release dates.
     - *Sports:* Participation years, season numbering equivalences (e.g., 28th season = year 2000), nationality of athletes, record of winners and champions.
     - *Geography:* Administrative divisions, jurisdictional hierarchies, place names.

3. **Generate the Search Query:**
   - Build a **natural language question or keyword phrase** that directly targets the core factual elements of the claim and its key entities.
   - Include **all critical named entities, dates, and unique identifiers** anchoring the claim in a factual context.
   - If relevant, include **alternative names or aliases**.
   - Avoid vague or overly broad terms; the query must be **specific and targeted** to retrieve authoritative information efficiently.
   - Combine search terms logically (AND, OR) or phrase as **precise fact-check questions** (e.g., “Who won championship X in year Y?”, “Did person A receive award B?”, “Which songs by artist D were originally recorded by whom?”).
   - When notes indicate possible inaccuracies in the claim, **explicitly incorporate disputed facts** into the query to enable disambiguation.

4. **Domain-Specific Nuances:**
   - For **Music**, focus on chart history, original artists, and detailed song credits.
   - For **Film & TV**, verify main cast, specific character roles, award nominations, and familial ties if mentioned.
   - For **Sports**, carefully check participant names, official season years, nationality, and event winners.
   - For **Geography**, clarify political or administrative divisions to validate location-based claims accurately.

5. **Implementation Strategy:**
   - Use the notes to **rule out impossibilities or prior refutations** by including explicit terms in the query that check questionable elements.
   - Avoid open-ended queries; **prioritize factual verification**.
   - Construct queries that can **simultaneously pinpoint multiple related facts or entities** to optimize retrieval.
   - Integrate detailed domain-specific facts or corrections from the notes to **increase the precision and depth** of the query.

6. **Output Format:**
   - Output only a single string in the field `query`.
   - The query should be **concise yet comprehensive**, aiming to retrieve authoritative sources that conclusively verify or refute the claim.
   - **No additional explanation** or reasoning should be output alongside the query.

---

Example Strategy Summary:

- Extract key entities and assertions from the claim.
- Use notes to identify correct facts and contradictions.
- Incorporate domain-specific data like awards, chart positions, character roles, event winners, and timelines.
- Formulate a precise, focused query that includes these elements and alternative names when available.
- Ensure the query minimizes ambiguity and retrieves documents covering all relevant aspects of the claim.

This method maximizes the chance of retrieving authoritative evidence for accurate claim verification across diverse domains such as music, film, sports, and geography.
