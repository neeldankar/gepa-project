Task Description:

You will be given two inputs: `claim` and `notes`.

- `claim`: a factual statement or set of assertions claiming truth about entities, events, dates, roles, awards, or other verifiable facts.
- `notes`: extracted or summarized relevant information related to the claim, containing potential evidence that supports, contradicts, or is neutral toward the claim.

Your goal is to generate a concise, targeted *search query* (`query`) designed specifically to retrieve authoritative and relevant documents or data that will enable verifying the truthfulness of the claim.

---

Detailed Instructions:

1. **Analyze the Claim Thoroughly:**

   - Identify **key entities** such as people (actors, athletes, musicians), organizations (bands, teams, studios), events (matches, openings, award ceremonies), works (films, songs, seasons), places (cities, administrative divisions).
   - Pinpoint **main factual assertions**: who did what, when, and where; including awards, competition results, roles joined, founding dates, original recordings, chart positions, nationality, or start/end years.
   - Understand **entity relationships** (e.g., actor X starred in film Y, driver Z competed in season N, person A won award B in year C).
   - Note any **temporal constraints** (specific years, seasons, decades).
   - Include **aliases, nicknames, or alternate names** if relevant to broaden or focus the search (e.g., a song's writer known by pen name, or "The King of Cocaine" for Pablo Escobar).

2. **Leverage the Notes to Refine the Query:**

   - Extract domain-specific contextual facts that clarify, confirm, or refute aspects of the claim.
   - Identify **contradictions, confirmations, or gaps** uncovered in the notes.
   - Use domain knowledge in your interpretation:
     - **Music**: focus on chart types (e.g., Billboard Hot Country Singles & Tracks), original artist versus cover versions, genre classifications, and songwriting credits.
     - **Film & TV**: verify main and supporting cast roles, familial relations, awards and nominations (e.g., Golden Globes), sequels, release years.
     - **Sports** (Motorsports, Tennis, Baseball): confirm competition years, season numbering, nationality of players or drivers, winners, team relocations, player tenures, and start ages.
     - **Geography**: administrative hierarchies of place names, city and county relations, jurisdiction boundaries.
   - Recognize if any claim elements are likely false or incomplete per the notes; ensure your query explicitly tests those elements.

3. **Compose a Precise, Effective Search Query:**

   - Use a **natural language question** or a targeted **keyword phrase** combining key entities, dates, and unique fact identifiers needed to validate the claim.
   - Include all **critical entities and temporal markers** that anchor the fact-check.
   - Avoid vague or overly broad terms; ensure the query is **specific enough to retrieve authoritative sources** about the claim's core elements.
   - Use logical connectors (AND, OR) or phrasing to refine (e.g., “Who won [championship] in [year]?”, “Did [person] receive [award] for [role]?”, “Which songs by [artist] reached [chart]?”).
   - Where aliases or alternative names exist, include them in the query.
   - If notes point out likely falsehoods or partial truths, explicitly include the contested elements to enable contradiction testing.

4. **Domain-Specific Nuances to Remember:**

   - **Music**: Chart positions and original recording artists are pivotal. Distinguish between cover versions and first recordings. Use exact chart names.
   - **Film/TV**: Cross-check cast membership with defined roles, awards and nominations, sequel relationships, and actor nationalities.
   - **Sports**:
     - Use the correct season-year mappings (e.g., "28th season = year 2000").
     - Clarify drivers' periods of participation, team changes, winners of specific seasons or events.
     - Verify sportsmen's career start ages when claimed.
   - **Geography**: Clarify names and administrative divisions, especially when claims involve places with overlapping or nested jurisdictions.

5. **Implementation Strategies:**

   - Use the notes to weed out impossible claims by incorporating those points into your query.
   - Formulate **fact-check style queries focusing on verifying key parts** rather than open-ended search strings.
   - Design the query to retrieve multiple relevant documents in one go—cover all core claim aspects.
   - Use facts or names known from the notes that challenge the claim to improve precision.

6. **Output Format:**

   - Output only the field `query` with a **single, concise** search query string.
   - The query should be **comprehensive enough** to yield authoritative evidence for or against the claim.
   - Do **not** include explanatory text, reasoning, or analysis—only the final query string.

---

Summary:

By careful claim and notes analysis, and exploiting domain-specific details and common pitfalls, compose a single, precise, and comprehensive query tailored to retrieve authoritative evidence to verify the claim. The focus is on maximizing precision and recall of relevant documents related to core factual assertions within the claim.
