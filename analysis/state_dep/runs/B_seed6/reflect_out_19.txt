Task Description:

You will be given two input fields: `claim` and `notes`. The `claim` is a factual statement that needs verification through fact-checking. The `notes` contain extracted, summarized, or contextual information related to the claim. These notes may include evidence supporting, contradicting, or neutral toward the claim. Your ultimate goal is to generate an effective, concise, and specific *search query* (field `query`) that can be used to retrieve authoritative information to verify the claim.

---

Detailed Instructions for Generating the Search Query:

1. **Analyze the Claim:**
   - Identify and extract all key entities: people, organizations, events, places, titles, championships, films, songs, or other domain-specific objects.
   - Pinpoint the core factual assertions: dates, roles, titles, awards, career milestones, geographical classifications, or historical facts.
   - Understand relationships among entities (e.g., actor-to-film, player-to-team, chairman-to-university).
   - Note temporal constraints such as specific years, seasons, decades, or event editions.
   - Consider aliases, nicknames, or alternate names to ensure comprehensive coverage (e.g., “Red Devils” for Manchester United).

2. **Review the Notes Thoroughly:**
   - Extract domain-specific facts that clarify, confirm, or contradict aspects of the claim.
   - Identify and note any partial truths, corrections, or refutations embedded in the notes.
   - Use the notes to identify context-sensitive domain details that refine the search:
     - *Sports*: season numbers and their corresponding years, player career timelines, franchise relocations, team nicknames, nationality of athletes.
     - *Film and TV*: precise film release dates, verified filmographies of actors, award wins/nominations, familial or professional relations.
     - *Music*: chart names and positions (e.g., Billboard Hot Country Singles & Tracks), original vs cover recording artists, genre specifics.
     - *Geography*: political or administrative divisions, official classifications of institutions.
   - Detect common pitfalls or erroneous elements in the claim as exposed by the notes and incorporate these into the query for explicit checking.
  
3. **Formulate a Targeted, Fact-Verification Search Query:**
   - Compose the query as either a natural language question or a precise keyword phrase that directly targets the claim's verification.
   - Incorporate all critical named entities, temporal markers, and domain-specific qualifiers.
   - Use logical operators (AND, OR) or quotation marks to emphasize key phrases and anchor the retrieval context.
   - Avoid vague, broad, or open-ended terms—prioritize specificity and clarity.
   - When appropriate, include alternative names or nicknames to cover variant references.
   - Aim for queries that can retrieve multiple, authoritative documents relevant to verifying all core components of the claim.
   - Phrase queries to check for contradictions or missing elements noted in the evidence, e.g., "Did X occur before Y?" or "Is person A associated with organization B in year C?"
   
4. **Domain-Specific Nuances to Keep in Mind:**
   - *Sports (especially football/soccer, motorsports, baseball)*:
     - Verify exact season/year equivalences (e.g., “28th season = year 2000”).
     - Confirm player transfers, positions played, and team nicknames.
     - Include nationality or other unique identifiers where relevant.
   - *Film and TV*:
     - Validate cast members' roles, award nominations, and release chronology.
     - Confirm familial or professional relationships if mentioned.
   - *Music*:
     - Focus on chart history, original vs cover versions of songs.
     - Use official chart names and peak positions where relevant.
   - *Geography and Institutions*:
     - Verify official status and classifications (e.g., public vs private university).
     - Clarify hierarchical or administrative relationships.
   
5. **Implementation Strategies:**
   - Use the notes to exclude known falsehoods by incorporating their elements explicitly.
   - Craft queries as fact-check questions rather than open-ended searches to improve retrieval relevance.
   - Include all key facts necessary to verify multiple aspects of the claim in a single query.
   - Prioritize coverage of any identified contradictions or incomplete information highlighted in the notes.
   - Avoid extraneous text or explanations in the output; output only the search query itself.

---

Output Format:

- Output only a single field `query`.
- This field’s value should be a single, concise, targeted search query string.
- The query should be comprehensive enough to retrieve authoritative documents that provide evidence for or against the claim’s factual assertions.

---

By following these detailed instructions with a focus on domain-specific factual accuracy and specificity, you will generate highly effective search queries that enable precise and productive fact verification across diverse domains such as sports, film, music, and geography.
