You are given two fields as input: `claim` (a factual statement to be verified) and `notes` (a list of evidential sentences or context extracted from relevant documents related to the claim). Your task is to produce a `query` field that formulates precise, focused, and verifiable questions aimed at fact-checking or verifying the key factual elements and assertions made in the `claim`. 

Detailed instructions to create the `query`:

1. **Analyze the claim thoroughly:** Identify the distinct factual components or assertions in the claim that require verification. This may include:
   - Names of people, places, organizations, or works (films, events, units, etc.)
   - Dates or time periods
   - Relationships or associations between entities (e.g., actors starring alongside each other, production companies involved, event locations)
   - Specific factual details about the entities (e.g., formation date of military units, location of sporting venues)

2. **Use the provided `notes` to guide query formulation:** The notes represent the currently retrieved evidential snippets. They often confirm some parts of the claim and refute or lack evidence for other parts. Your queries should focus primarily on:
   - Points of uncertainty or missing evidence highlighted in the notes with respect to the claim
   - Key entities or facts mentioned in the claim for which the notes do not provide sufficient or consistent evidence
   - Any contradictions or missing corroboration exposed by the notes

3. **Formulate queries that can be answered with factual or documentary evidence:** The queries should be:
   - Specific and unambiguous
   - Include all entities and attributes needed for disambiguation (for example, full names, years, or context such as "the 2006 film directed by Lane Nishikawa")
   - Compound queries if multiple fact checks are needed to verify the entire claim, but keep them concise and focused

4. **Domain-specific knowledge inferred from examples:** 
   - Film-related claims often involve correctness of cast, production companies, release years, and film titles.
   - Military-related claims often refer to formation dates, unit names, and historical events.
   - Sports-related claims involve locations of events, home venues, and club bases.
   - Historical or event timelines (dates of inception, when events took place) are critical.
   - Verification often contrasts entities' associations, geographic locations, or timeframes.

5. **Overall approach to the task:** 
   - Extract factual sub-claims from the overall claim.
   - Compare these sub-claims against the evidence or highlight where evidence is missing or contradictory.
   - Frame queries targeting these gaps or inconsistencies so that additional document retrieval or fact-checking can confirm or refute the claim.

By following these principles, your generated queries will guide efficient document retrieval and verification of factual claims, ensuring that all critical aspects of the claim are scrutinized with minimal ambiguity.
