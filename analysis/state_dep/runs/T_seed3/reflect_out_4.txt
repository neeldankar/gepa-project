Task Description:

You will be provided with three input fields:

- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion involving one or more entities, events, or relationships.
- `notes`: A list of extracted relevant factual statements or snippets that pertain to the claim, often summarizing or supporting/refuting parts of the claim.
- `context`: A list of documents or document excerpts (titles and text) that provide detailed background information about the entities, events, or concepts mentioned in the claim.

Your goal is to produce two output fields:

- `new_notes`: A concise, accurate subset of the given `notes` that best supports or disputes the claim based on the provided context. These notes should be directly relevant and factually correct according to the context, avoiding redundant or irrelevant information.
- `titles`: A list of document titles from the `context` that contain information necessary to verify the claim and support the chosen `new_notes`. This list should include all key documents referenced in `new_notes`, reflect comprehensive coverage of crucial facts, and omit irrelevant or unrelated documents.

Detailed requirements and domain-specific knowledge:

1. **Entity and Relationship Identification**:  
   The `claim` will often involve multiple entities (people, organizations, events, publications) and relationships between them (e.g., coining a term, professional affiliation, authorship, competition results). The `notes` and `context` will contain overlapping and sometimes repeated factual assertions regarding these entities and their connections.

2. **Cross-verification of Facts**:  
   The `notes` serve as candidate fact claims to be supported or refuted against the `context`. Your output should consolidate relevant facts, validated and confirmed by the `context`, while excluding unsupported, incorrect, or ambiguous notes.

3. **Precision in Retrieval**:  
   The `titles` in your output represent the exact documents from the `context` that allow verification of the facts in `new_notes`. Titles should correspond exactly to the relevant context entries and cover all evidence for the facts in `new_notes`. Partial or incomplete document retrieval reduces accuracy.

4. **Handling Redundancies and Contradictions in Notes**:  
   Duplicate facts or contradictory notes may appear. Select the most accurate, concise, and context-supported notes. Remove duplicates and clearly represent the verified facts.

5. **Summarizing Complex Claims**:  
   Claims may involve compound facts (e.g., two entities sharing a profession, one entity having a specific characteristic). Your reasoning should dissect the claim into factual components, verify each component, and retain notes and documents relevant to all parts of the claim.

6. **Domain-Specific Contextual Knowledge**:  
   - Professionals referenced may include scientists (e.g., paleontologists such as Elisabeth Vrba and Stephen Jay Gould), writers, athletes (professional tennis players with tournament results), designers, and journalists.  
   - Publications and artistic works (books, magazines, journals, essays) appear frequently; it is essential to distinguish authorship, publication venue (book vs. magazine article), and the nature of periodicals.  
   - Events like sports tournaments, professional achievements, and historical occurrences frequently provide verification points.  
   - Namesake ambiguity is possible (e.g., different individuals named Gould); confirm identity using contextual career and biographical details.

7. **Reasoning and Fact-Checking Strategy (Generalizable)**:  
   - Map entities and relationships mentioned in the claim to those referenced in `notes` and `context`.  
   - Crosscheck each factual assertion in `notes` against the detailed information in `context`.  
   - Discard or modify notes unsupported by context or contradicted by it.  
   - Identify and include all documents whose content is essential to verify or refute the claim, ensuring completeness.  
   - Produce a concise set of newly filtered notes (`new_notes`) that reflect the validated facts.  
   - Provide the precise titles of relevant context documents that underpin these notes.

Your final outputs must be factually accurate, focused, and traceable to the cited documents in the `context`.

Format:
- `new_notes`: List of succinct, verified factual notes derived from the input `notes` and supported by the `context`.
- `titles`: List of exact document titles from the `context` that contain the evidence for these notes.

Use clear reasoning to cover all components of the claim and disambiguate entities where necessary.
