Task Description and Input Format:

You are given three inputs:

- `claim`: A factual or factual-assertion statement that may or may not be accurate, involving people, works, events, or entities, often with temporal or role-based components.
- `notes`: A list of textual excerpts or evidence pieces that relate to verifying or refuting the claim. These notes may include dates, biographies, release info, affiliations, and other relevant facts.
- `context`: A list of detailed background document summaries, biographical entries, or entity descriptions that provide richer domain information on the entities, works, or concepts referenced in the claim and notes.

Your goal is to produce two outputs:

1. `new_notes`: A concise, distilled, and de-duplicated set of fact-based notes synthesized from the `notes` and `context`. These notes should focus on essential, directly relevant facts needed to evaluate the claim’s accuracy, especially emphasizing:

  - Chronology and temporal order (e.g., birth and death dates, release or premiere dates, leadership election dates).
  - Clear identification and differentiation between multiple entities or persons when involved (e.g., actors in different roles, distinct composers, political parties and their affiliations).
  - Domain-specific nuances, such as:

    * In film/TV/media: Distinguishing acting roles, voice work, premiere vs release dates, director vs writer credits.
    * In music: Composer nationality, opus numbers, composition and publication dates.
    * In board games: Designer styles (e.g., German-style games), game types (e.g., chess variants).
    * In politics: Party affiliations, provincial vs federal party distinctions, youth wing affiliations, leadership election specifics and dates.

  The `new_notes` should remove redundant or irrelevant information, clarify causal or temporal relationships, and implicitly or explicitly show reasoning that supports validating or refuting the claim. The notes should be efficient and focused on key verifying facts.

2. `titles`: A list of relevant entity, work, or document titles from the `context` critical for understanding and verifying the claim. Include all major entities or concepts mentioned in `new_notes` to maximize coverage for future retrieval tasks. Titles should correspond to:

  - Primary persons (e.g., key political leaders, composers, actors).
  - Works or productions central to the claim (e.g., films, musical pieces, board games).
  - Organizations or political parties involved.
  - Foundational or background reference documents that clarify core claim elements.

Domain-specific and methodological considerations:

- Use temporal data (birth, death, release, election dates) to infer or confirm sequences of events relevant to the claim.
- Differentiate clearly between people sharing names or roles in different works.
- For political claims, carefully distinguish between provincial and federal institutions and their leadership histories.
- When multiple composers, actors, or designers are involved, clearly link names to compositions or roles and confirm nationalities or styles as relevant.
- Avoid mixing works or individuals from different contexts unless explicitly connected by the evidence.
- When evidence duplicates or slightly varies, synthesize the most precise and accurate version.
- Ensure that evidence reflects verified factual content, not speculation.
- Presentation of the final output should be concise but sufficiently detailed to stand alone as a verification summary.

Overall, the task is a fact-checking synthesis that balances completeness, relevance, and clarity, integrating temporal, biographical, and domain-specific knowledge to produce a high-quality evidence summary (`new_notes`) and an inclusive set of core reference titles (`titles`).

Instructions to the assistant:

- Carefully analyze temporal sequences and cross-validate date-related claims.
- Prevent conflation of multiple persons, works, or roles—keep entities distinct and explicitly linked to their specific claims.
- Emphasize domain-specific facts (e.g., difference between voice acting and acting, federal vs provincial party affiliation, composer nationalities).
- Remove redundant notes and combine facts to form clear chains of evidence.
- Provide a final `new_notes` list that would allow an independent reader to understand and verify the claim’s truthfulness through factual evidence alone.
- For `titles`, include all core entities and works mentioned in the distilled notes and any foundational documents essential for understanding the claim fully.
- Keep language factual and neutral, avoiding interpretation beyond the evidence.
  
Following these guidelines ensures outputs support robust, context-aware fact-checking and facilitate future retrieval or auditing processes.
