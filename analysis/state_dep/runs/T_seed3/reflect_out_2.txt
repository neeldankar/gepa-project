Task Description:

You are given three inputs: a `claim` (a factual statement to verify or elaborate), a set of `notes` (textual extracts relevant to the claim, often including clarifications, corrections, and partial evidence), and a `context` (background knowledge and document summaries related to the entities and topics in the claim). Using these inputs, you must produce two outputs:

1. `new_notes`: a distilled and refined subset of the original notes that best supports analysis, fact-checking, or clarification of the claim. The new notes should synthesize key evidential points, emphasize precise factual details or corrections (e.g., actor nationalities, film sequels, locations of cemeteries), and remove redundancy or speculative statements.

2. `titles`: a list of relevant document titles or entities referenced in the context or notes that are essential to understanding the claim and its validity. These titles should uniquely identify key concepts, people, places, or works of art associated with the claim, such as movie titles, actor names, cemeteries, geographic locations, or other named entities implied by the claim.

Detailed instructions and domain-specific considerations:

- Carefully analyze the `claim` to identify all critical factual elements such as:
  - Named entities (persons, places, works, objects)
  - Temporal or sequential relationships (e.g., movie sequels, historical timelines)
  - Attributes or qualifiers (nationalities, roles, relationships)

- Use the `notes` to:
  - Confirm, refute, or clarify these elements with specific evidence or corrections (e.g., clarifying Jeremy Renner’s nationality as American, not Welsh)
  - Highlight specific supporting facts (e.g., "28 Weeks Later" is the correct sequel to "28 Days Later"; Solo is orange-flavored; Golden Gate National Cemetery’s location relative to Golden Gate National Recreation Area)
  - Discard contradictory, irrelevant, or unsupported claims in the notes
  - Synthesize multiple notes addressing the same fact into clear, non-redundant statements

- Use the `context` to:
  - Identify and confirm relevant entities aligned with the claim and notes
  - Retrieve titles that uniquely refer to important people, places, films, songs, cemeteries, or organizations mentioned or implied
  - Acknowledge partial or uncertain data when applicable (e.g., no evidence linking George W. Wallace’s burial place to GGNRA)

- The `new_notes` output should be:
  - Concise and sufficiently informative to understand the factual status of the claim
  - Reflective of verified facts, correcting common misconceptions 
  - Free from speculation that can’t be supported by notes or context

- The `titles` output should:
  - Represent critical references (including entities missing from your initial notes if they are relevant and present in context)
  - Strive to fully capture all gold-standard relevant documents or entities associated with the claim
  - Avoid overly broad or generic titles unless they directly connect to key claim elements

- If the claim involves:
  - Filmography: carefully distinguish sequels, release years, cast details, and actor nationalities.
  - Soft drinks or products: specify flavor profiles, brand origins, and advertising contexts.
  - Geographic or burial-related claims: identify cemeteries, parks, or locations with respect to official boundaries or associations; confirm or disclaim inclusions in larger areas.

Strategy Summary:

- Parse the claim into its constituent factual components.
- Cross-validate these using the provided notes, selecting the most precise, directly relevant, and authoritative statements.
- Use the context to complement your notes by naming the key entities to include in `titles`.
- Address contradictions proactively by clarifying them explicitly in `new_notes`.
- Prioritize inclusion of all known pertinent entities even if partial evidence exists.
- Ensure the outputs enable a reader to understand the claim's context, evidential support, and factual accuracy at a glance.

Output format:

- `new_notes`: A list of non-redundant, concise fact-focused strings aligned strictly to claim-relevant evidence.
- `titles`: A list of document or entity titles extracted or inferred from context and notes, covering all core claim elements.

This task supports fact-checking, claim verification, and evidence summarization by integrating multi-source textual evidence with associated metadata and background knowledge.
