Task Description:

You will be given three input fields:

- `claim`: A factual assertion involving one or more entities (such as people, organizations, events, publications) and their relationships or characteristics.
- `notes`: A list of extracted factual statements or snippets that relate to the claim, possibly supporting or refuting it. These may contain redundancies or partial information.
- `context`: A collection of documents or document excerpts (including titles and associated text) that provide detailed, authoritative background information on the entities, events, or concepts mentioned in the claim.

Your objective is to produce two output fields:

- `new_notes`: A precise, concise, and verified subset of the `notes` that directly support or dispute the claim based strictly on the provided `context`. The `new_notes` should:
  - Include only facts fully supported by the context.
  - Exclude duplicates, irrelevant, inaccurate, or unsupported information.
  - If the claim is compound (involving multiple discrete facts), address each component comprehensively.
  - Represent any contradictions found by excluding unsupported notes and selecting the most accurate and relevant.

- `titles`: A list of exact document titles from the `context` that contain the information essential to verify or refute the facts included in `new_notes`.
  - This list must comprehensively cover all evidence underlying the selected notes.
  - Titles must match context entries exactly.
  - The set of titles should exclude unrelated or unnecessary documents.

Detailed Requirements and Domain-Specific Knowledge:

1. **Entity and Relationship Disambiguation**  
   - Claims frequently involve multiple entities (people, organizations, events, publications) and complex relationships (e.g., authorship, professional affiliations, event results).  
   - Entities may share identical or similar names (e.g., multiple persons named "Gould"). Disambiguate identities through contextual biographical or career information.

2. **Cross-Verification of Notes Against Context**  
   - All notes must be cross-checked with the context for factual accuracy and relevance before inclusion.  
   - Unsupported or contradictory notes must be excluded.  
   - Avoid including redundant statements; select the most concise, direct note supported by context.

3. **Precision and Completeness in Document Retrieval**  
   - Extract exact document titles from the context that fully support the facts reflected in `new_notes`.  
   - Partial or incomplete source retrieval impairs verification accuracy.  
   - Include all key documents, especially when claims involve multiple components or topics spanning different sources.

4. **Handling Redundancies, Ambiguities, and Contradictions in Notes**  
   - When multiple notes express the same fact, keep only the best-supported, clearest version.  
   - Discard any note contradicted by the context even if repeated multiple times.

5. **Compound Claims Decomposition**  
   - Break down claims into separate factual components.  
   - Verify each factual element independently.  
   - Ensure that the `new_notes` and `titles` collectively cover all parts of the claim.

6. **Domain-Specific Contextual Knowledge**  
   Your reasoning will benefit from understanding domain-specific nuances as found in the context, including:  
   - **Professional fields:** Paleontology, tennis, music residencies, academia (universities, administrators), horse breeding, hospitality, and other specialized domains.  
   - **Publications:** Distinguish between books, academic journals, magazine articles, and other media types. Confirm authorship, publication venue, and nature of periodicals.  
   - **Events:** Sports tournaments, residencies, organizational boards, historical timelines—validate event participation and outcomes carefully.  
   - **Identities and Titles:** Recognize correct affiliations and titles, for instance, knowing that "Morgan State University" was originally "Centenary Biblical Institute," or that "The AXIS" auditorium is located inside Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino in Las Vegas.  
   - **Geographical and Organizational Details:** Pay attention to location details, ownership (e.g., Caesars Entertainment owning a specific hotel and casino), and historically accurate facts.

7. **Generalizable Reasoning and Fact-Checking Strategy**  
   - Map all referenced entities and relations from the claim into the notes and then into the context.  
   - Verify each note’s accuracy by aligning with contextual evidence.  
   - Evaluate the completeness and trustworthiness of the notes relative to the context before inclusion.  
   - Exclude any note not fully supported by the context or contradicted within it.  
   - Identify and list all context documents providing evidence for all accepted notes.  
   - Strive for clarity, conciseness, and traceability of facts to sources.

Output Format:  
- `new_notes`: A list of concise, accurate, verified notes supporting or disputing the claim.  
- `titles`: A list of exact document titles from the `context` providing evidence for these notes.

Your final response must be factually accurate, clear, and fully traceable to the supporting documents provided in the `context`.
