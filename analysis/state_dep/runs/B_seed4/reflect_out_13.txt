- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion involving people, films, dates, places, and related entities.
- `notes`: A list of sentences extracted from various sources containing facts, data points, and partial evidence relevant to the claim.
- `context`: A list of document summaries or excerpts from articles that contain additional background information and factual details related to the entities mentioned in the claim and notes.

Your task is to process all of this information and produce two output fields:

1. `new_notes`: A concise, non-redundant, and relevant subset of the `notes` that directly support or clarify the claim. These should be factual statements citing key relationships, dates, entities, or verification of the claim’s components. Remove duplicates and irrelevant information, and keep each note clear and focused.

2. `titles`: A list of document titles representing the key knowledge sources (from the `context` or implied by the notes) necessary to verify, support, or clarify the claim. These titles should cover:
   - The principal persons or entities mentioned (e.g., actors, directors, authors);
   - Films or works involved (e.g., movies, TV series);
   - Important related topics such as geographical information or release dates if relevant;
   - Any other evidently important source documents needed to fact-check or understand the claim fully.

Guidance and domain-specific insights derived from examples:

- Claims often involve connecting multiple pieces of information: for instance, identifying that a director from a smaller country directed a film whose last work was identified by another director from a larger country.
- The geographical size comparison should be supported by explicitly citing country areas.
- Dates of film releases and roles voiced by actors should be cross-verified between notes and context.
- Recognize and disambiguate name variants (e.g., "Shia Saide LaBeouf" and "Shia LaBeouf") as referring to the same entity.
- Confirm the relationship between entities (e.g., an actor known for a TV show and their starring role in a specific movie from a given year).
- Use context document titles exactly as provided or as closely as possible; these form the 'knowledge anchors' for the claim verification.
- Refrain from adding new information not present in notes or context.

Overall, your approach should combine precise extraction and synthesis of fact-bearing sentences from notes and accurate selection of supporting documents' titles from the context to clearly validate or explain the claim.

Your output format should be:

```
### new_notes
['fact 1', 'fact 2', ...]

### titles
['Document Title 1', 'Document Title 2', ...]
```

Make sure your reasoning considers factual consistency, relevance, and completeness in supporting the claim, while the titles provide comprehensive coverage of essential knowledge sources.
