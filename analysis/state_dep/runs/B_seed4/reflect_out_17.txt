Task Description:  
You will be provided with three input fields — `claim`, `notes`, and `context` — related to a factual statement or assertion. Your task is to critically analyze these inputs and produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

Input Fields Explanation:  
- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion that can be verified or refuted using evidence.  
- `notes`: A list of sentence-level observations or facts relevant to the claim, extracted from various sources or reasoning, that may support, contradict, or provide context to the claim. These notes can contain direct factual statements, clarifications, corrections, or details about named entities, dates, locations, or related subjects.  
- `context`: A list of text snippets or document descriptions containing factual information, often about named entities (for example, people, films, buildings), dates, places, and other relevant domain-specific knowledge that can be used to validate or investigate the claim.

Outputs Required:  
- `new_notes`: A concise, relevant subset or synthesis of the original notes that directly address the claim's validity, focusing on facts that confirm, counter, or clarify the claim based on the provided context. These new notes should:  
  - Emphasize key factual findings from both notes and context that pertain to the claim.  
  - Filter out irrelevant or repetitive information.  
  - Clearly state contradictions or confirmations regarding the claim.  
- `titles`: A list of document or entity titles extracted or inspired from the context that are most relevant to the claim and the verification process. The titles should:  
  - Include all relevant named entities, such as people, films, buildings, or organizations, explicitly referenced in both the claim and the useful notes/context.  
  - Favor titles of documents or entities directly related to the claim components (e.g., if the claim references a film, include the film’s title present in the context).  
  - Avoid generic or unrelated titles.

Detailed Domain-Specific Considerations:  
- Carefully verify dates, personal relationships, and geographical locations mentioned in claims against the facts provided in notes and context.  
- Identify the correct identities and roles of people referenced (e.g., clarify relationships like “father of the star” or confirm professions).  
- Distinguish between similarly named entities or works (e.g., different films or cathedrals that share names but differ in geographical location or production era).  
- Context may include historical details, filmographies, biographical data, or architectural information; use them to corroborate claims.  
- The task involves verifying the factual accuracy of claims, assessing contradictions, and clarifying possible confusions due to ambiguous naming or incomplete information.

Recommended General Approach:  
1. Understand the claim’s factual components (names, dates, titles, relationships, locations).  
2. Review notes and context to find directly relevant facts supporting or contradicting these components.  
3. Summarize these relevant facts in `new_notes` focusing on claim validation.  
4. Identify and list in `titles` key context documents or entities that are integral to understanding or verifying the claim.  
5. Avoid including irrelevant or peripheral information in outputs.  
6. Maintain clarity and focus in reasoning and outputs, highlighting contradictions or confirmations explicitly.  

This detailed and domain-aware approach enhances the factual correctness and relevance of outputs.
