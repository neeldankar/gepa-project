Task Overview:  
You will receive two inputs: a `claim` (a factual statement involving people, events, works, or relationships) and `notes` (extracted factual or contextual information relevant to the claim but often incomplete or insufficient for full verification). Your goal is to produce a clear, concise, and effective `query` that requests additional authoritative documents or knowledge to fully verify or falsify the claim.

Key Domain-Specific Details and Context:  
- Claims often involve entities such as people (actors, athletes, artists), works (films, albums, sports events), events (matches, historical milestones), and relationships (collaborations, memberships, burial locations, timelines).  
- Notes may provide partial confirmations, disconfirmations, uncertainties, or ambiguous references, including partial filmographies, biographical details, event outcomes, or geographic facts.  
- Verification frequently requires detailed and specific factual data points including:  
  • Precise dates (birth, death, event dates, production/release years).  
  • Confirmation of roles/participation (e.g., an actor’s involvement in multiple productions, a player’s participation or scoring in a sporting event).  
  • Exact names and any known aliases or colloquial references (e.g., identifying an artist by EP title or a player’s nickname).  
  • Relationship and overlap analysis (e.g., co-occurring timelines, geographic overlaps of cemeteries and national park areas).  
- Ambiguities and possible conflations in claims should be explicitly addressed by querying for clarifications or disambiguations (e.g., distinct identities vs. conflated roles or events).  
- When the notes or claim includes indirect identifiers (like “the Irish artist with EP X”), attempt to identify the entity by name with brief justification to enable precise querying.

Generalizable Strategy for Query Generation:  
1. **Decompose the claim into its core assertions and involved entities.** Identify what facts need full confirmation or disconfirmation.  
2. **Analyze notes for already established facts and gaps**—what is missing or uncertain that the query must target?  
3. **Formulate targeted, factual questions** directed towards authoritative sources that can supply missing details, such as:  
   - Actor or player participation in specific works, roles, or events.  
   - Exact production or event dates.  
   - Membership or collaboration history.  
   - Geographic and institutional affiliations (e.g., cemetery location within national park boundaries).  
   - Confirmations/disambiguations of names, nicknames, and colloquial references.  
4. **Consolidate the questions into a clear, concise query** that simultaneously covers all facets needed to fully adjudicate the claim.  
5. **Use explicit entity names, alternative identifications, and key dates/titles** within the query to improve retrieval precision.  
6. **Address potential conflations or contradictions** in the claim by requesting verification of overlapping or exclusive facts.

Expected Output Format:  
- Provide a well-reasoned explanation summarizing what key facts or gaps must be addressed (optional if instructed).  
- Produce a single, composed `query` consisting of one or multiple direct questions that collectively request the critical missing factual evidence needed to confirm or refute the claim.

Examples of domain-nuanced queries include but are not limited to:  
- “Who starred in [film A]? Did any of those actors also appear in [film B]? Of these, who is best known for [TV show]?”  
- “Where is [person X] buried? Is this cemetery located within [specific national park or area]? How do the geographic boundaries of the cemetery and park relate?”  
- “Was there any player named [name or nickname] who scored in [specific sports final]? What teams have colloquial names matching [given nickname]?”  
- “What are the birth and death dates of [person]? Was this person a member of [group] during [years]?”  
- “What are the release years and production details of [work]? Was [person] involved as [role]?”

By adhering to these domain-specific details and strategies, your generated queries will effectively guide retrieval systems to locate comprehensive, authoritative documents necessary for full claim verification.
