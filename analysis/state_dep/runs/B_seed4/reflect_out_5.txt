Task Description:  
You will receive an input object containing two fields:  
- `claim`: A statement or set of statements asserting facts or relationships involving specific entities, events, roles, dates, or achievements.  
- `notes`: A list of sentences or short paragraphs providing factual information, context, or supporting evidence related to the claim. These notes can include overlapping, repeated, incomplete, or partially contradictory information.

Your task is to generate a single, factually precise, concise, and clear **search query** or **information request** based on these inputs. This query should be designed to retrieve missing critical information, resolve contradictions, or clarify ambiguities necessary to fully verify or refute the claim.

---

Detailed Task Requirements and Domain-Specific Knowledge:  
  
1. **Identify Key Entities and Facts in the Claim:**  
   - Extract the main elements from the claim including:  
     - Names of people (e.g., scientists, actors, athletes, directors), including full names or pseudonyms.  
     - Titles of works (e.g., films, TV series, tournaments) with release or event years if present.  
     - Dates or date ranges relevant to events, careers, premieres, or achievements.  
     - Relationships and roles (e.g., co-creator, character played, manager of a sports team).  
     - Specific factual assertions (e.g., debut dates, coining of a term, participation in an event).  
  
2. **Analyze the Notes to Detect:**  
   - Information that confirms parts of the claim.  
   - Missing details or data gaps relevant to the claim’s key components.  
   - Any contradictory or ambiguous information about the claim’s entities or facts (e.g., conflicting dates, unclear professional roles).  
   - Whether some entities or facts remain unverified or lack supporting evidence based on the notes.  
  
3. **Formulate the Query:**  
   - Focus the query on missing or ambiguous critical fact(s) identified in the notes relative to the claim.  
   - Aim to retrieve verified, entity-level information that will support or refute the claim, such as full names, roles, event titles, premiere dates, exact relationships, or career details.  
   - Combine multiple related unresolved sub-claims into a single well-structured query, if they are tightly linked and essential for verifying the claim comprehensively.  
   - Avoid redundancy and unnecessary breadth; do not ask for information that is already verifiably present or clearly contradicted unless clarification is warranted.  
   - The query should be formulated as a natural language question or factual request explicitly targeting verification or missing details (e.g., “Who was the actor...?”, “What year was the team founded?”, “Who directed…?”, “Did this person manage…”).  
  
4. **Generalizable Strategy for Query Construction:**  
   - Break down the claim into discrete factual components.  
   - Cross-check each against the notes to find which are well supported, ambiguous, or unsupported.  
   - For unsupported or ambiguously supported components, frame questions that aim to:  
     - Confirm disputed facts (e.g., dates, names, roles)  
     - Identify missing key details (e.g., a missing profession, director name, or opponent)  
     - Resolve contradictory data encountered in the notes  
   - Integrate closely related sub-queries into a single clear, efficient query where appropriate.  
   - Always maintain clarity, focus on fact verification, and keep the query relevant to the claim verification.  
  
---

**Examples of domain-specific considerations:**  
  
- When the claim refers to collaborative or co-creation relationships (e.g., coining a term), identify both collaborators and their professions to check commonalities.  
- If the claim refers to film or TV projects, verify cast lists, director(s), and premiere dates. Confirm if individuals are actors or directors as roles may be confused in the notes.  
- For historical events such as sports team foundations and season participation, verify exact founding year, player career spans, and managerial appointments, including corresponding seasons.  
- In cases of conflicting date information (e.g., premiere or founding years), generate queries specifically asking for definitive dates and role overlaps.  
- When the claim involves biographical overlap or comparisons (e.g., shared professions), confirm all individuals’ professions separately to establish agreement or disagreement.  

---

**Summary:**  
Given the `claim` and associated `notes`, your output is a focused, succinct, natural language query that requests essential missing information or critical clarifications required to fully fact-check the claim. The query should be narrowly targeted, resolving disputed or missing facts, and optimized to retrieve precise evidence or documents needed for claim verification.
