You are given two input fields: 
- `claim`: A factual or informational statement that needs to be verified or explored by retrieving supporting evidence from external sources.
- `notes`: A list of relevant factual snippets, pieces of evidence, or background knowledge related to the claim.

Your task is to produce the field `query`, which should be a concise, clear, and targeted natural language search or information retrieval query. This query will be used to retrieve additional documents that can verify, support, or refute the claim. The key points for creating the `query` are:

1. **Focus on Key Entities and Relationships:** Identify the main subjects, events, or concepts in the claim that require verification. Use the notes to pinpoint relevant entities, dates, roles, or facts crucial to the claim.

2. **Disambiguate Ambiguous Terms:** When the claim references specific people, events, seasons, or works (e.g., "the 28th season," "Rhapsody, Op. 1"), normalize or clarify these terms if possible using information from `notes` (e.g., "28th season" → "year 2000 World Rally Championship").

3. **Incorporate Verification Constraints:** If the claim includes negations or exclusions (e.g., “not Johann Christoph Pepusch,” “Harri Rovanperä did not compete”), ensure the query checks for those conditions explicitly.

4. **Combine Multiple Verification Elements:** When the claim involves comparative or multiple facts (e.g., number of albums, nationality, gender), the query should ask about all required components that jointly verify the claim.

5. **Use Natural, Fact-Seeking Questions:** Frame the query as a clear question or set of questions that directly seeks the truth-value of the claim by requesting the key facts to be extracted from documents.

6. **Leverage Information in Notes to Improve Precision:** Since notes contain partial or full evidence, incorporate critical names, dates, and attributes from notes to craft focused queries that can retrieve documents missing from the current evidence set.

7. **Aim for Sufficient Coverage to Support Fact-checking:** The query should aim to retrieve all necessary documents to support the factual verification and address all angles of the claim.

Example Patterns:
- "Did [entity] win [event] in [time period], in which [person] did/did not participate?"
- "What are the total albums released by [band1] and [band2], and is [band2] also known as [alternate name]?"
- "Who composed [musical piece], and what is their nationality and gender? Was [other composer] associated with this piece?"

Remember, the goal of the query is not to answer the claim yourself but to write a targeted question that will retrieve documents sufficient to verify the claim fully.
