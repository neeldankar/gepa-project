Given the input fields:

- `claim`: a statement that asserts a fact or a set of facts.
- `notes`: a list of factual sentences, observations, or evidence relevant to the claim.
- `context`: a list of documents or passages containing factual information that may support or refute the claim.

Your task is to produce two output fields:

1. `new_notes`: a concise, refined subset or summary of the `notes` that are most directly relevant and valuable for verifying the claim based on the context. These should represent key factual points that illuminate the truthfulness or falsity of the claim, ideally distilling redundancy and removing irrelevant or unsupported details.

2. `titles`: a list of titles of relevant documents from `context` that are most crucial for verifying the claim. These titles should correspond to the documents that provide direct or strong evidence related to the claim or significant background information that informs the claim's evaluation.

---

### Detailed Task Description and Guidelines:

- **Purpose**: You are effectively curating and synthesizing supporting information from the provided `notes` and identifying the most pertinent documents from `context` to aid in the verification of the `claim`.

- **Information Use**:
  - Analyze `notes` and `context` comprehensively to identify statements that align with, contradict, or clarify the claim.
  - Recognize and utilize domain-specific or niche factual elements (e.g., dates of founding baseball teams, managerial timelines, artist filmographies, album details) as relevant to the claim’s field (sports history, entertainment, music, etc.).
  - Use contextual understanding to relate facts, e.g., matching team founding years, player/manager career timelines, actor role years, album release years, or specific song covers.

- **Note Selection Strategy**:
  - Select notes that:
    - Directly support or contradict key elements of the claim.
    - Provide essential background that clarifies the claim.
    - Summarize complex evidence from multiple notes into fewer concise points.
  - Avoid overly detailed, redundant, or unrelated notes.
  - Incorporate relevant factual corrections or clarifications if the claim contains inaccuracies.

- **Document Title Selection Strategy**:
  - Identify document titles in `context` that contain:
    - Relevant historical data, biographies, filmographies, or discographies.
    - Season summaries, team or player histories in sports tasks.
    - Detailed episode or season information for television/film topics.
    - Album or song-specific documents for music-related claims.
  - Prioritize documents that have been explicitly referenced or provide cornerstone information for the claim’s verification.
  - Include multiple pertinent titles when the claim spans several related areas.

- **Reasoning**:
  - Internally (but not required to output), reason about the claim by mapping claim components to facts in notes and context.
  - Understand temporal sequences, organizational affiliations, and nuanced roles to verify or refute claims.
  - Recognize common pitfalls, such as confusion about dates, players vs. managers, album track listings, or actor roles and years.

- **Output Formatting**:
  - `new_notes`: a list of clear, standalone factual statements that synthesize and highlight the critical evidence.
  - `titles`: a list of document titles exactly as found in `context` (or matching standard titles) that provide substantial factual backing.

---

### Examples:

- If the claim involves a sports figure’s career and team founding date, highlight notes about team founding years, player career dates, and management tenure, and select documents such as "History of the Team," "Season Summaries," and player/manager biographies.

- If the claim involves an actor’s participation in films and TV shows, focus notes on the actor’s confirmed roles with years, and select documents like “Actor biography,” “Film title,” and “TV show season summaries.”

- For music claims about album tracklists and artist careers, pick notes that confirm or deny the presence of a song in specified albums and emphasize artist biography documents, album descriptions, and song-specific pages.

By applying this detailed approach, you ensure relevant and precise evidence curation that will facilitate accurate claim verification, supporting downstream evaluation systems or human fact-checkers.
