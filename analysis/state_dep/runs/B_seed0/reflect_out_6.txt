Task Description:

You will be given three inputs: 
- `claim`: a statement asserting a fact or set of facts. 
- `notes`: a list of candidate factual sentences or assertions related to the claim, extracted from various source documents, that may support or contradict the claim. 
- `context`: a list of document or topic snippets (usually Wikipedia-style summaries or descriptions) relevant to entities or concepts within the claim and notes.

Your goal is to analyze these inputs to:

1. Produce `new_notes`:  
   - A concise, non-redundant subset of the `notes` that are most relevant to verifying or explaining the claim.  
   - These should be factually correct, directly supporting or clarifying key aspects of the claim.  
   - Avoid including duplicated, irrelevant, or unsupported note sentences.  
   - If possible, prefer notes that provide unique, corroborated details confirming or partially confirming the claim.

2. Produce `titles`:  
   - A list of document titles or topics from the `context` that are most essential or authoritative for understanding or verifying the claim.  
   - Ideally, include all gold-standard relevant documents (key pages or topics) that directly relate to entities or factual assertions in the claim.  
   - Titles should reflect the main subjects or persons involved, their roles, or key events referenced within the claim.

Additional domain-specific guidance:  
- In many cases, the claim involves verifying relationships about people (e.g., their professions, roles, affiliations), historical events (e.g., sports seasons, film productions), or awards (e.g., Nobel Prizes).  
- Proper disambiguation of entities (e.g., ensuring that similarly named persons are correctly identified) is crucial. Notes and context often include variant names or detailed biographical info to confirm identity and titles.  
- Verify temporal aspects (e.g., years of service, season years) carefully, especially in sports or film credits contexts.  
- For claims involving collaborations (e.g., co-directors), note the presence or absence of co-credit for each individual carefully.

Generalizable strategy for answering:  
- Cross-reference notes with context documents to confirm consistency and factual accuracy.  
- Select notes that best consolidate evidence while avoiding redundant repetitions.  
- From the context, infer which documents best represent the key topics or entities involved in the claim.  
- Summarize the confirmed factual support for or against the claim in the `new_notes`.  
- List the most relevant document titles that provide authoritative background or verification in `titles`.

Output format:  
- `new_notes`: a list of curated, relevant note sentences supporting or clarifying the claim.  
- `titles`: a list of document or topic titles from the context that should be retrieved for comprehensive verification of the claim.

This procedure supports fact-checking or claim verification tasks where accurate, focused evidence extraction and source identification are required.
