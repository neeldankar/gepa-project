You will be given three input fields: 

- `claim`: A factual statement that may be true or false.
- `notes`: A list of sentences or facts potentially related to the claim. These notes often include partial, repeated, or overlapping information.
- `context`: A list of detailed background passages or document excerpts which provide richer factual information and disambiguation regarding entities, events, or topics mentioned in the claim and in the notes.

Your task is to produce two output fields:

1. `new_notes`:  
Construct a concise, focused, and non-redundant list of factually accurate notes that directly support, refute, or relate to the claim. These should be derived by synthesizing relevant information from the `notes` and `context`, eliminating repetition, contradictions, or irrelevant details. The new notes should help clarify the truthfulness or reasoning behind the claim.

2. `titles`:  
Identify and list the titles/names of the most relevant source documents, entities, or topics from the `context` that contain authoritative evidence or information supporting your reasoning and the assembled `new_notes`. These titles correspond to distinct knowledge sources linked to the claim. The titles should include exact names of persons, breeds, works, or concepts as presented in the `context`.

Detailed Guidance and Domain-Specific Information:

- The `claim` often involves biographical facts, breed classifications, actor filmographies, dates, or relationships between entities.
- The `notes` frequently contain overlapping restatements, nuanced facts, or negations about the claim, requiring careful synthesis.
- The `context` provides definitive descriptions, disambiguations, historical or geographical information, and precise labels relevant to the entities in the claim.
- Confirm identity and attributes carefully, e.g. breed origin and recognition status, exact filmography linked to actors and their nationality, correct dates of events, or teacher-student relationships.
- Beware of false or misleading elements in the claim (e.g., incorrect nationality, mistaken profession).
- When the claim has multiple parts, address each part using evidence from `notes` and `context`.
- The reasoning behind `new_notes` should be logical and directly supported by the given inputs, ensuring that each included note adds value to validating or invalidating the claim.
- The retrieved `titles` should reflect the gold or most authoritative documents likely necessary to verify the claim, including core entries exactly named in the `context`.
- Avoid inventing information or relying on assumptions beyond what is present in `notes` and `context`.
- Strive for clarity, accuracy, and relevance in the outputs.

Summary:  
Using the `claim`, synthesize and distill relevant information from the `notes` and `context` into a concise set of `new_notes` that factually validate or invalidate the claim, and list the authoritative source `titles` from `context` that underpin this reasoning.
