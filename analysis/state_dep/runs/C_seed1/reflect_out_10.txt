Task Description:

You will be provided with three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- The `claim` field contains a factual assertion or statement.
- The `notes` field is a list of distilled, often contradictory or clarifying sentences related to the claim, summarizing evidence from various sources, highlighting confirmations or rebuttals of the claim.
- The `context` field is a list of document excerpts relevant to the claim, providing background information and details that may support or refute the claim.

Your objective is to produce two output fields:
1. `new_notes` — a concise, refined, and coherent summary of factually verified information about the claim, based on synthesis and critical evaluation of the given notes and context. This summary should:
   - Correct inaccuracies or contradictions found in the claim.
   - Include relevant domain-specific factual information that clarifies or rejects the claim.
   - Remove redundancy and distill the key findings from the notes.
   - Explicitly note the absence of evidence where applicable.
   - Reflect a neutral, authoritative tone appropriate for knowledge verification or fact-checking.

2. `titles` — a prioritized list of document or topic titles drawn from or inspired by the context documents that are most relevant for understanding the claim and verifying its accuracy. The titles should:
   - Identify key source documents referenced in the context.
   - Include related entities, persons, organizations, events, or concepts critical to evaluating the claim’s validity.
   - Assist in retrieval of further evidence if needed.
   - Be a mix of broad and specific topics that reflect the information used in the reasoning.

General Strategy and Domain-Specific Details:

- The claims frequently relate to specialized domains such as entertainment (film casting and voice acting), sports teams and personnel histories, or football (soccer) clubs and league memberships.
- The task requires understanding of domain-specific facts such as:
   - Voice actors and their casting roles in films, along with distinctions between speaking and singing voices.
   - Founding dates and histories of sports teams, their affiliations, and managerial careers.
   - Naming conventions and sponsorships of football leagues and clubs, including distinctions between leagues and their sponsors.
- When handling contradictory information, the assistant must weigh evidence presented in notes and context, dismiss inaccurate claims, and support conclusions with explicit references to authoritative data.
- The assistant should explicitly highlight when no supporting evidence exists for a claim or when the claim contradicts verified data.
- Titles should reflect relevant entities or documents to aid transparency and further reference.
- Redundancies should be removed from `new_notes` to produce a coherent summary rather than a raw aggregation of facts.

Use this approach to consistently produce high-quality, fact-checked summaries and context titles that could aid in fact verification, research, or knowledge enrichment.
