Given two input fields: `claim` (a statement potentially containing multiple factual assertions) and `notes` (a list of fact-based observations extracted from relevant documents that pertain to the claim), your task is to generate a `query` that will be used to retrieve supporting or refuting documents for claim verification.

The steps and important considerations for producing the `query` are as follows:

1. **Analyze the claim carefully:**  
   - Identify all distinct factual assertions or components embedded in the claim.  
   - Break down complex claims into simpler sub-claims or points that can be independently verified.

2. **Examine the provided notes:**  
   - Analyze the notes for factual information that supports or contradicts parts of the claim.  
   - Detect contradictions or confirmatory evidence within the notes.  
   - Understand which entities, events, dates, and relations are mentioned and relevant to the claim.

3. **Focus of the query:**
   - Formulate explicit, targeted questions (queries) that directly address the key points of verification.  
   - Include questions that clarify ambiguous or potentially inaccurate parts of the claim.  
   - Ask about relevant entities, relationships, roles, dates, and events that are necessary to verify the claim’s accuracy.  
   - When there are multiple related assertions, create separate but concise questions for each.  
   - Ensure the query is comprehensive enough to retrieve all essential documents that would confirm or refute each sub-claim.

4. **Domain-specific contextual considerations learned from examples:**
   - When the claim involves people’s roles, affiliations, or collaborations (e.g., actors in films, captains of planes), ask explicitly who did what and when.  
   - For claims involving geographic or company headquarters, ask about the existence and location of the entities.  
   - For chronological claims, ask about release dates or event sequences to establish timelines.  
   - When directors or creators are named, verify their exact roles and the correctness of those attributions.  
   - When the claim references known named entities (films, persons, companies), structure the query to retrieve documents about those entities individually as well as their relationships.

5. **General strategy:**  
   - The goal is to produce queries that will retrieve the minimal but complete set of documents to verify the claim fully.  
   - The queries should reflect a comprehensive verification approach, explicitly covering all non-trivial claim components.  
   - The queries should avoid extraneous or vague formulations and instead be precise and fact-focused.

By following these guidelines, your generated `query` will effectively direct the retrieval of gold-standard documents needed to verify the claim accurately.
