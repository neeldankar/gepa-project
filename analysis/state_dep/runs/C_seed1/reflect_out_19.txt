Task Description:
You will be given two input fields: a `claim` (a factual statement or set of statements) and `notes` (a list of context sentences or evidences related to the claim). Your task is to generate an effective `query` that can be used to verify the truthfulness or accuracy of the `claim`.

Key Details and Domain-Specific Considerations:
1. The `claim` may mention multiple entities (e.g., people, bands, institutions, locations), events (e.g., formations, releases, training), or relationships (e.g., "X was formed before Y", "X is administered by Y").
2. The `notes` provide supporting or contradicting information about the claim and often mention relevant dates, entities, or attributes that help fact-check the claim.
3. The generated `query` should explicitly target the key elements of the claim that require verification, such as:
   - The identity and attributes of named entities (e.g., formation year of a band, founding date of an institution).
   - The relationship or comparison between multiple entities mentioned in the claim (e.g., was the formation year of band A the same as band B?).
   - Verification of assertions about locations, administrative control, or proximity.
4. Your queries should be phrased as clearly and specifically as possible to ensure that subsequent factual retrieval or checking systems can accurately and efficiently confirm or refute the claim.
5. If the claim contains multiple assertions, ensure the query includes all necessary sub-questions for full verification.
6. Leverage the information given in `notes` to understand what facts are already confirmed, which remain unconfirmed, and which parts of the claim need further checking.

Generalizable Strategy:
- Identify the core factual entities and attributes in the claim.
- Use the `notes` to pinpoint relevant evidences, including dates, names, and relationships.
- Formulate a comprehensive but concise query that encapsulates all the key points necessary to verify the claim's correctness.
- When multiple entities or facts are involved, ask for each element's verification and how they relate to each other.

Example:
If the claim is: 
"Band A and the band who released Album X were both formed in the year YYYY."

And the notes indicate that Band A was formed earlier and Album X was by a different band formed in YYYY, the query should be:

"When was Band A formed, and which band released the album Album X? Additionally, when was that band formed?"

This query aims to gather precise data points needed to verify if both bands were indeed formed in the same year as claimed.

Your output should include only the `query` field based on the reasoning and synthesis described above.
