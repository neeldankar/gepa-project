Task Description:

You will be given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- The `claim` is a factual statement or assertion, which may be true, false, or partially true.
- The `notes` field is a list of distilled sentences that summarize supporting, contradictory, or clarifying information regarding the claim, often extracted from multiple sources. These notes may include redundancies, contradictions, or inaccuracies.
- The `context` field contains excerpts and documents relevant to the claim, providing authoritative background, evidence, or detailed information that can confirm or refute parts or all of the claim.

Your objective:

Produce two output fields:

1. `new_notes` — A concise, coherent, and factually verified summary synthesizing the notes and context with the following criteria:
   - Correct any inaccuracies or contradictions in the claim and notes.
   - Incorporate all relevant domain-specific factual information that clarifies, substantiates, or invalidates the claim.
   - Remove redundancies, contradictions, and irrelevant or unsupported statements.
   - Explicitly mention if there is no evidence supporting any part of the claim.
   - Ensure the tone is neutral, authoritative, and suitable for fact-checking, knowledge verification, or research.
   
2. `titles` — A prioritized list of key titles inspired by or drawn from the context, which should:
   - Include names of documents, articles, or sources referenced in the context.
   - Highlight relevant entities (people, organizations), events, concepts, or terms crucial to understanding and evaluating the claim.
   - Feature a combination of broad and specific topics to facilitate further evidence retrieval or study.
   - Aid transparency and provide a clear mapping of sources used for verification.

Domain-Specific Details and Strategies:

These claims often relate to specialized domains such as:

- Entertainment (film casting, voice actors, music authorship, band members, roles of speaking vs singing voices).
- Sports (football/soccer teams, league memberships, founding dates, coaching careers, team histories).
- League and club naming conventions, including distinctions between different league sponsors and competition levels.
- Historical and biographical information related to athletes, musicians, actors, or academics.
- Geographic and demographic facts (e.g., cities, rankings by size or population).

When assessing contradictions:

- Carefully weigh the evidence in the `notes` against the authoritative `context`.
- Give priority to contextual data derived from credible, detailed, domain-expert sources.
- Discard unsupported, duplicated, or erroneous information.
- Make explicit when contradictory claims arise and clarify the verified truth with references.

General Strategy:

- Synthesize a distilled and balanced factual summary that does not merely aggregate but rationalizes and filters input information.
- Correct and clarify the claim, avoiding unchecked repetition.
- Preserve relevant domain-specific terminology and details for precision.
- Construct the `titles` to clearly map to sources that underpin the summary and that facilitate future evidence retrieval.

Ensure all outputs reflect thorough verification, clarity, precision, and are trustworthy for knowledge enrichment or fact-checking purposes.
