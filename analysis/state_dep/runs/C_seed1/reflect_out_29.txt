1. `new_notes`: a refined, concise, and accurate subset or synthesis of the input `notes` that best clarifies, corrects, or verifies the claim based on the `context`. The `new_notes` should:
   - Eliminate redundancies and repeated information.
   - Focus on confirming or refuting key factual elements of the claim.
   - Incorporate relevant facts from the provided context to support or negate the claim.
   - Summarize the essential points that would help a reader understand the accuracy of the claim.
   - Only include factual, evidence-based information grounded in the notes and context provided.

2. `titles`: a list of relevant document or topic titles that correspond to the key entities, events, or concepts necessary to understand or verify the claim. The titles typically correspond to the names or identities of:
   - Persons (e.g., actors, players, directors).
   - Events or works (e.g., films, tennis tournaments, albums).
   - Organizations or entities connected to the claim (e.g., record labels, sports teams).
   - Specific thematic distinctions relevant to the claim (e.g., distinctions between men’s and women’s tennis events).

Additional important details and domain-specific considerations:

- Claims often involve cross-linking multiple entities or domain-specific knowledge (e.g., sports tournaments and players, film actors and directors, historical events).
- It is crucial to recognize and separate distinct roles or categories (e.g., men’s doubles tennis vs. women’s singles tennis, actors’ birthdays and professions, film writing vs. directing).
- Context data frequently cites detailed factual corrections and clarifications (e.g., who partnered with whom in a doubles event, which film a person wrote or directed, the founding person of a record label).
- When the claim is ambiguous or partially unsupported, reflect that explicitly in the `new_notes` by highlighting the lack of supporting evidence.
- Titles serve as proxies for source documents or focal points for further retrieval; ensure all principal relevant topics/entities are listed.
- Focus on clarity, conciseness, and factual correctness rather than excessive detail in `new_notes`.
- Avoid conjecture or unsubstantiated claims; only work with the information provided in `notes` and `context`.
- Reasoning or explanation is not explicitly requested in the output but was modeled in examples for internal understanding and can inform the selection of `new_notes` and `titles`.

By following these guidelines, your output will help verify or refute the claim accurately and guide effective document retrieval or summarization related to the claim.
