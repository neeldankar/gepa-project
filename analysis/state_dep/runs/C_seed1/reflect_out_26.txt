Task Description:

You will be provided with three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- The `claim` is a factual statement or assertion that may be partially or wholly inaccurate.
- The `notes` field contains a list of distilled sentences derived from various sources. These notes often include contradictory information, clarifications, and evidence supporting or refuting the claim.
- The `context` field provides relevant document excerpts, background, or source material connected to the claim. These may be topic summaries, biographies, discographies, filmographies, synopses, or other domain-specific texts that offer authoritative information for verification.

Your objective is to synthesize these inputs into two outputs:

1. `new_notes`: 

   Produce a concise, factually accurate, and coherent summary that critically evaluates and integrates information from the `notes` and `context` related to the claim. This summary should:

   - Correct errors or contradictions found in the claim and notes.
   - Clarify the factual status of the claim, explicitly rejecting or revising incorrect assertions.
   - Incorporate relevant domain-specific factual data that aids understanding, such as:

     - Film and TV domains: details on casting, distinctions between speaking vs. singing roles, film nationality/co-production particulars, director credits, and narrative structure comparisons (e.g., acts in operas vs. segments in films).
     - Music domains: album types (studio/live), recording timeline, original artist and cover versions, stylistic phase shifts in musicians’ careers, and release years.
     - Sports/football domains: team founding years, affiliations, league names and sponsorships, managerial career timelines.

   - Exclude redundant or repetitive statements, creating a distilled and fluid narrative.
   - Explicitly note when there is no supporting evidence for a component of the claim.
   - Use a neutral, authoritative tone appropriate for fact-checking or knowledge verification.

2. `titles`:

   Provide a prioritized list of document or topic titles that are most relevant to verifying the claim and understanding its context. The titles should:

   - Include key source documents referenced within the context.
   - Represent important entities, persons, organizations, events, and concepts related to the claim.
   - Enable targeted follow-up research or evidence retrieval.
   - Combine broad overview topics with specific source documents or related entities.
   - Reflect precise naming conventions and distinctions (e.g., correct film co-production countries, official album titles, opera acts vs. film segments, league sponsors).

General Reasoning Strategy and Domain-Specific Details:

- When confronted with contradictory information, carefully weigh evidence from the notes and context, privileging explicit, authoritative data from reliable documents over unsourced or inaccurate claims.
- Recognize domain-specific content structures and conventions:

  - Operas have formal acts and scenes, whereas films do not typically have comparable divisions. Hence, direct numeric comparisons between opera acts and film segments often lack meaningful basis.
  - Films may be international co-productions; correct identification of the producing countries is crucial for accurate claims.
  - Music covers are attributed to specific albums, which should be classified (studio/live, solo/group) and dated appropriately.
  - Labelling of leagues and clubs, and their sponsors, have precise official titles that matter for fact-checking.
  - Actors’ roles (lead, supporting), film and TV appearances by year, and distinguishing between similarly titled productions are essential details.

- Explicitly call out when no evidence exists to verify a claim component or when the claim is contradicted by known facts.
- Redundancy elimination and synthesis are critical—rather than aggregating all notes, integrate them into a coherent account.
- Preserve a professional, neutral tone avoiding bias or unsupported speculation.

Format Requirements:

- Output `new_notes` as a list of refined, synthesized factual statements summarizing the verified information relevant to the claim.
- Output `titles` as a list of prioritized document or topic titles drawn directly or inspired by the provided context, aiding transparency and further evidence retrieval.

Use this comprehensive, domain-aware approach to consistently produce authoritative fact-checking summaries and context titles that facilitate high-quality verification, research, and knowledge enrichment.
