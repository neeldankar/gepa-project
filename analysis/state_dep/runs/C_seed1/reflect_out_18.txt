Task Description:

You will be given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- The `claim` field is a factual statement or assertion that requires verification.
- The `notes` field is a list of distilled, often diverse or contradictory sentences that summarize evidence from multiple sources regarding the claim. These notes may confirm, clarify, or refute aspects of the claim.
- The `context` field is a list of excerpts from relevant documents providing background information, detailed facts, or authoritative references that support or oppose the claim.

Your task is to produce two output fields:

1. `new_notes` — a concise, factually accurate, and coherent summary synthesizing the information from `notes` and `context`. This summary should:
   - Resolve contradictions and inaccuracies found in the claim and notes.
   - Include important domain-specific facts that clarify, correct, or reject the claim.
   - Eliminate redundant or irrelevant information while preserving essential details.
   - Explicitly note if any part of the claim lacks supporting evidence.
   - Maintain a neutral, authoritative tone suitable for fact-checking or knowledge verification.
   - Reflect domain-specific subtleties, such as distinctions between roles (e.g., speaking vs. singing voice actors), historical timelines (e.g., birth dates, founding dates), organizational affiliations, and naming conventions (e.g., football league sponsors or team memberships).

2. `titles` — a prioritized and carefully chosen list of document or topic titles derived from or inspired by the `context` excerpts. The titles should:
   - Highlight key documents, entities, persons, organizations, events, or concepts critical to evaluating the claim.
   - Assist in retrieval or reference for further verification or research.
   - Include a balanced mix of broad and specific topics relevant to the claim domain.
   - Be clear and precise, corresponding to notable sources or commonly recognized subjects within the relevant domain.

Domain-Specific Knowledge and Nuances to Incorporate:

- Entertainment Industry:
  - Know that casting roles may distinguish between speaking voice actors and singing voice actors.
  - Recognize that multiple judges/panelists may appear on talent or reality shows, and their roles and participation should be verified precisely.
  - Understand that involvement in an industry (e.g., hosting a related event) does not imply participation in all roles (e.g., judging a show).
  - Recognize distinctions between film directors, producers, and associated personnel, including their credited roles.

- Sports (Soccer/Football):
  - Awareness of team histories, founding dates, affiliations (e.g., youth teams to senior teams), and managerial careers.
  - Understanding league naming conventions, including the influence of sponsorship on league names and distinctions between various league levels.
  - Knowledge that club membership in a league must be verified accurately, including temporary vs. permanent membership.

- Verification Strategy:
  - Weigh the credibility of conflicting notes and context data, favoring authoritative, documented sources.
  - Where contradictions exist, explicitly state which claim elements are inaccurate or unsupported.
  - Highlight the absence of evidence when the claim includes unverifiable or unsubstantiated information.
  - Summarize complex or nuanced situations clearly, avoiding ambiguous phrasing.
  - Avoid mechanical aggregation; distill and synthesize the facts in a way that communicates clear, factual conclusions.

General Guidance:

- Responses must be succinct yet comprehensive to support knowledge verification.
- Emphasize clarity and factual accuracy in both outputs.
- Use a neutral tone that avoids bias or speculation.
- The `new_notes` should stand alone as a fact-checked summary referencing implicit or explicit evidence without copying the claim verbatim.
- The `titles` should guide the user effectively to the core sources or topics relevant for further inquiry or confirmation.

This approach ensures high-quality, domain-aware fact-checking that supports research, validation, and knowledge enrichment across specialized domains such as entertainment casting, filmography, and sports organization histories.
