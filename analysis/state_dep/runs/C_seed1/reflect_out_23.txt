Task Description:

You will be given three inputs: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- The `claim` is a factual statement asserting information, often involving specialized knowledge in domains such as entertainment (film roles and voice actors), sports (team histories, player or coach careers), or football/soccer (club affiliations, league history, naming/sponsor conventions).
- The `notes` consist of a list of sentences that summarize evidence relevant to the claim. These notes often include confirmations, contradictions, clarifications, or additional details derived from multiple sources. They may present conflicting information or partial insights.
- The `context` contains excerpts from documents or articles that provide background data, detailed facts, or authoritative information that support the evaluation of the claim.

Your objective is to produce two outputs:

1. `new_notes` — a concise, well-structured summary synthesizing the factual information from both `notes` and `context` with these goals:
   - Correct or clarify the claim by resolving contradictions and verifying facts.
   - Incorporate important domain-specific facts critical to understanding or debunking the claim.
   - Remove redundancy and avoid mere aggregation; distill the core verified knowledge.
   - Explicitly state when evidence is lacking, or when certain aspects are not supported.
   - Maintain a neutral and authoritative tone suitable for fact-checking or knowledge verification.
   - Ensure clarity for readers who may not have domain expertise but need reliable factual insights.

2. `titles` — a prioritized list of document or topic titles inspired by or drawn from the `context` that:
   - Include key documents, sources, or articles most relevant to the claim.
   - Reference related people, organizations, events, or concepts vital for verifying the claim's accuracy.
   - Cover a spectrum from broad categories (e.g., "World Rally Championship Seasons" or "Echeveria Genus and Habitat") to specific entities (e.g., individual drivers, specific films, or football clubs).
   - Aid potential retrieval of further evidence if needed.
   - Reflect the exact or close-to-exact document titles or well-recognized topics present in the context.

General Strategy and Domain-Specific Knowledge:

- When evaluating claims about film actors and characters:
  - Distinguish between films, roles, and different actor attributes such as speaking vs. singing voices.
  - Check actor filmographies carefully for role confirmation.
  - Flag when a film or production title does not exist or is confused with others.
  - Confirm if characters mentioned correspond historically or fictitiously to real people.

- When verifying sports claims:
  - Understand timelines of player or coaching careers and team histories.
  - Know founding dates and affiliations of clubs.
  - Distinguish between similar or changing names of leagues due to sponsorships.
  - Verify players’ participation or absence in particular seasons or tournaments with accurate dates.

- When dealing with botanical or biological claims:
  - Confirm native habitats and distributions of species.
  - Differentiate between genera and species with known range and ecological preferences.
  - Highlight absence of evidence where a species is incorrectly claimed native to habitats outside its documented zones.

- Handle contradictory information by weighing evidence quality and direct support:
  - Dismiss claims unsupported by authoritative evidence from the context.
  - Reject may-be-confused or conflated assertions when data contradicts.

- Explicitly note missing evidence when the claim involves obscure or unverified facts.

- Avoid repeating similar statements in `new_notes`; instead, integrate and summarize concisely.

- Use a neutral, scholarly prose style appropriate for fact-checking and educational contexts.

Formatting and Output:

- `new_notes` should be presented as a list of factual, standalone sentences for clarity and directness.
- `titles` should be presented as a list of strings of document or topic titles, ordered by relevance and priority for the claim verification.

By following these instructions, the assistant will generate factual, concise, and domain-aware summaries and relevant topic/document titles that help verify claims accurately and transparently, supporting detailed research or fact-checking workflows.
