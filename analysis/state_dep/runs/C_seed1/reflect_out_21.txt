Task Description:
You will be given two input fields:
- `claim`: A factual statement potentially involving people, places, events, or relationships.
- `notes`: A list of supporting or contradictory textual evidence snippets containing domain-specific factual information related to entities mentioned in the claim.

Your goal is to generate a single output field:
- `query`: Formulate a precise, fact-checking question or clarification query directly derived from the `claim` and based on the evidence in `notes`. This query should focus on verifying or interrogating the key factual components present in the claim, often involving temporal overlaps, identification of people or entities, locations, roles, or relationships.

Detailed Guidelines and Domain-Specific Knowledge:
1. Entities and Context: 
   - The claims often involve named people (e.g., Basil Dean, Francis Ford Coppola, Kevin Reece Wilson, Craig Heyer), and their roles, dates (birth, death, career timeframes), or associations with events, institutions, or works (e.g., The Godfather Part II, 2013 Big Ten Conference football season, Clark County Nevada).
   - Notes provide domain-specific factual data such as birth/death years, job titles and periods, team affiliations, geographical locations, and institutional memberships, often used to confirm or refute timelines or relationships stated in the claim.

2. Temporal Overlaps: 
   - If the claim involves timing or contemporaneous existence (e.g., "both were alive at some point in time"), your query should verify these overlaps by explicitly focusing on the time intervals and the entities involved.

3. Role and Position Verification:
   - Claims mentioning roles or positions (e.g., "offensive coordinator at Ohio State") require verifying if the person held that role at the specified time period. The query should clearly ask about the correctness of that role assignment during the stated timeframe.

4. Geographical and Institutional Relationships:
   - When the claim involves geographic entities and institutions within them (e.g., colleges located in Clark County), the query should focus on confirming the presence of the said colleges in the location and the association of people with those institutions.

5. Evidence Coverage:
   - The query should be comprehensive enough to cover the critical elements needed to check the claim but concise and specific enough to facilitate targeted document or fact retrieval.
   - If multiple entities or facts are involved, include them explicitly in the query to encourage retrieval of all relevant documents.

6. Query Style:
   - Use a clear, natural language question phrasing that is directly answerable by the evidence, such as:
     - "Were X and Y both alive during [specific years]?"
     - "Was X the [role] at [institution] during [time period]?"
     - "Does [location] include [institution(s)] where [person] played [sport/activity]?"

By following the above considerations, your generated query will best serve downstream retrieval and verification tasks, ensuring the correct gold evidence documents can be identified and retrieved.

Summary:
Given `claim` and `notes`, generate a direct fact-checking query focusing on verification of key entities, timeframes or relationships mentioned, framed as a precise question that encapsulates the core factual assertion from the claim, using domain-specific knowledge drawn from the notes.
