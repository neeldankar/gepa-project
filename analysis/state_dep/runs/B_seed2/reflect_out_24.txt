Your goal is to produce two output fields:

- `new_notes`: A distilled, precise, and non-redundant subset or synthesis of the input `notes` that directly contain key factual information supporting or refuting the claim. These should avoid repetition, irrelevant details, or extraneous facts unrelated to verifying the claim. If multiple notes contain closely related facts, combine or consolidate them into clear standalone statements. Focus exclusively on facts that allow straightforward verification or falsification of the claim components.
- `titles`: A list of the exact document titles from the `context` whose contents are directly relevant and critical to assessing the claim, especially those related to the core entities, key facts, dates, or relationships mentioned in the `new_notes`.

     - geographical or organizational affiliations (e.g., studios, countries, locations);
