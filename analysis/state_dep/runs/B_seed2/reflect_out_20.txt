Task Description:
You will be given two input fields: `claim` and `notes`. The `claim` is a complex factual statement or set of related statements that may connect multiple entities, events, or attributes. The `notes` field contains various extracted factual snippets, potentially repeated, which provide relevant information drawn from multiple documents or knowledge sources.

Your task is to generate a single `query` that is designed to help verify or fact-check the `claim` by retrieving missing or supporting evidence from knowledge sources. This `query` should be:
- Specific and focused on key entities, events, or relationships in the `claim`.
- Structured to retrieve documents or facts that can collectively confirm or refute the claim.
- Designed to cover all essential components of the claim, including implicit or explicit relationships (e.g., who did what, awards won or not won, dates, titles, roles, etc.).
- Incorporate relevant domain-specific terminology and facts gleaned from the `notes` to refine the query (e.g., precise titles, names, dates, relationships).
- Aim to retrieve documents or facts that fill gaps compared to the provided `notes` (documents or facets referenced in feedback as "remaining to be retrieved") to improve coverage.

Additional Guidelines:
- Analyze the `claim` carefully, identifying all distinct factual elements and their relationships.
- Use the `notes` to identify correct and canonical names, titles, and facts (e.g., corrected film titles, event dates, award names).
- When multiple related entities are involved (e.g., multiple people related to an award), structure the query to explicitly ask about these connections jointly or comparatively.
- Ask compound queries if necessary, but keep them concise, ensuring they are clearly connected to verifying the claim.
- The query should enable retrieval of the largest relevant subset of documents related to key claim components, including any important supporting entities referenced or missing in the `notes`.
- Avoid vague or overly broad questions; be precise but include all relevant aspects to ensure coverage.
- Use terminology from the relevant domain (film, literature, music, festivals, military service, TV programming blocks, albums) as appropriate, based on the `claim` context.

In summary, the goal is to formulate a precise, detailed, and well-informed verification query that complements the `notes` to fully fact-check the `claim`. This query will guide retrieval or checking of additional supporting information covering all factual aspects of the `claim`.

Input:
- `claim`: A complex fact or set of facts to be verified.
- `notes`: An array of text snippets giving partial relevant information from existing knowledge sources.

Output:
- `query`: A natural language question designed to retrieve or confirm detailed, domain-specific facts necessary to completely verify the `claim`.
