You will be given three input fields: 
- `claim`: a factual statement or assertion whose truthfulness needs to be evaluated.
- `notes`: a list of textual snippets extracted from various documents that are relevant to the claim. These notes may contain overlapping information and varying levels of detail.
- `context`: a list of concise summaries or descriptions of source documents that may provide additional information related to the claim.

Your task is to produce two output fields: 
- `new_notes`: a refined, concise, and non-redundant subset or synthesis of the input `notes` that directly address key facts needed to evaluate the claim. These should focus on relevant factual information supporting verification or falsification of the claim, removing repetition and irrelevant details.
- `titles`: a list of the most relevant document titles (from the given `context`) whose content contains essential information that supports verification of the claim. Titles should be directly tied to entities or facts critical to assessing the claim’s truth.

Detailed task description and guidelines:

1. **Understand the claim carefully:** Identify what factual relationships or attributes the claim asserts. Common relationships involve dates (e.g., birthdates), locations, sponsorships, team participation in events, or organizational affiliations.

2. **Analyze the `notes`:** 
   - Identify the core facts within the notes that relate to the claim components.
   - Remove duplicate or near-duplicate statements.
   - Remove notes that do not contribute meaningful information for verifying the claim.
   - Combine closely related facts into a single coherent note if appropriate.

3. **Use the `context` documents to identify relevant titles:**
   - Match core entities or specific facts mentioned in new_notes to the titles in the context.
   - Select document titles that contain background, biographical, event-related, or organizational information directly related to the claim or the facts used in new_notes.
   - Include all titles necessary to cover the key factual points supporting or refuting the claim.
   
4. **Focus on comprehensive and precise fact coverage:**
   - For claims involving dates or comparisons (e.g., birth dates before/after), emphasize the presence or absence of those dates in the new_notes.
   - For claims involving events and participants (e.g., bowl games, sponsorships), include notes identifying the event, sponsor, location, and participating entities.
   - For claims about geographical relations (e.g., entities located in the same country), use notes emphasizing the locations and their geopolitical hierarchy.

5. **Avoid fabricating information or adding facts not supported by the notes or context.** Work only with provided inputs.

6. **The reasoning behind new_notes should be implicitly reflected in the selection and refinement of notes and titles.** While no explicit reasoning output is required, your outputs should facilitate straightforward verification or rebuttal of the claim.

7. **Maintain clarity and specificity:**
   - Notes should stand alone as clear, factual statements extracted from the input.
   - Titles should be the exact document titles from context, indicating the sources of key facts.

The goal is to distill, from possibly redundant and partially overlapping information, the most relevant and precise facts and corresponding source titles that directly support verification or falsification of the claim.
