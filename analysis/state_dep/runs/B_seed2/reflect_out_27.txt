You will be given three inputs:

- `claim`: a factual statement or assertion whose truth value must be assessed.
- `notes`: a list of textual snippets extracted from various documents relevant to the claim. These snippets may contain overlapping information, duplications, and varying precision or detail levels.
- `context`: a list of concise summaries or descriptions of source documents, each including a title and a brief description. These documents are potential sources containing information that may help verify or refute the claim.

Your task is to produce two outputs:

1. `new_notes`: a refined set of factual statements synthesized from the original `notes`, focused on the essential facts needed to verify or falsify the claim. This set should:

   - Remove duplicate or near-duplicate statements.
   - Exclude irrelevant or extraneous information not contributing toward verification.
   - Combine closely related facts into concise, coherent statements if appropriate.
   - Emphasize factual precision, especially on key claim components such as dates, locations, entities, affiliations, roles, or events.
   - Stand alone as clear, factual assertions, without introducing any information not supported by the inputs.
   - Be comprehensive enough to enable straightforward verification or rebuttal of the claim when combined with the listed documents.

2. `titles`: a list of exact document titles from the `context` input that cover essential facts or entities critical to assessing the claim. Include all documents necessary to support the core facts identified in your `new_notes`. To determine relevance:

   - Match the key entities, places, events, dates, or roles mentioned in `new_notes` to titles in `context`.
   - Include documents containing biographical details, event descriptions, organizational affiliations, or geographical data fundamental to the claim.
   - Ensure all titles collectively provide sufficient coverage of the critical factual points supporting verification or falsification.
   - Do not fabricate titles or include ones unrelated to the key facts.

Detailed Task Guidelines:

1. **Understand the claim completely**: Identify the factual relationships, entities, dates, locations, or attributes it asserts. The claim may involve comparisons (e.g., birthplace equivalency, country of origin differences), affiliations, event participation, or other verifiable facts.

2. **Analyze and synthesize the `notes`:**

   - Extract core factual content that directly pertains to the claim.
   - Remove redundant or repeated statements derived from overlapping notes.
   - Remove notes that do not contribute meaningful verification information.
   - Merge multiple facts tightly related to the claim into singular, clear statements where appropriate, ensuring clarity and avoiding ambiguity.
   
3. **Leverage the `context` to select relevant titles**:

   - Select titles that correspond directly to the entities, events, or facts in your `new_notes`.
   - Prioritize documents that provide background or detailed factual support essential to verify or falsify the claim.
   - If multiple documents provide overlapping information about a key fact, include those most central or informative.
   - The goal is to list all documents needed to cover the factual support for your extracted notes, enabling easy cross-referencing and validation.

4. **Maintain rigorous factual accuracy**:

   - Do not invent or infer facts not present in `notes` or `context`.
   - Focus on the claim’s key assertions, such as exact birthplaces, dates, affiliations, or event attributes.
   - Precision over verbosity: concise yet comprehensive notes are preferred.
   - Capture distinctions critical to the claim, such as differentiating roles (e.g., singer vs. lyricist), countries of origin, hierarchical geographic information (city, state, country), or event details.

5. **Ensure clarity and usefulness for verification**:

   - Each note should be independently understandable and factually informative.
   - The note set combined with the title list should uniquely enable verification of the claim without requiring additional external information.

6. **Structure output precisely**:

   - `new_notes`: A list of concise, non-redundant factual statements addressing the claim.
   - `titles`: A list of exact document titles (from `context`) that substantiate your new_notes.

Use this process and criteria consistently to transform an unstructured and potentially repetitive set of notes and a broad document context into a focused, precise, and verifiably supportive set of facts and document sources. This will aid efficient verification or falsification of the original claim.
