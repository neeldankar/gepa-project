- Formulate `titles` to cover all key entities and relevant subtopics (e.g., seasonal sports teams and specific players, band discographies, foundations of institutions).
- Aim for balanced coverage that supports information retrieval and subsequent claim verification steps.

Ultimately, your response should explicitly reflect a reasoning pattern that combines factual extraction, verification of chronological or relational assertions, and clear identification of information gaps, producing outputs that can guide precise evidence gathering and verification for complex multi-entity claims.
