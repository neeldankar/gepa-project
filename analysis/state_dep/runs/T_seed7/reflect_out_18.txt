Task Objective:
Given three input fields — `claim`, `notes`, and `context` — your task is to produce two outputs: `new_notes` and `titles`. The goal is to synthesize, filter, and refine the input data to assess, clarify, or validate the `claim` based on verifiable and relevant facts.

Input Format:
- `claim`: A declarative statement asserting a factual relationship involving people, places, organizations, or events. Claims often rely on domain-specific information (e.g., film casting and production details, sports statistics, music band line-ups, geographic affiliations).
- `notes`: A varied collection of factual excerpts supporting, contradicting, or related to the claim. Notes may overlap, contradict, or partially address different parts of the claim.
- `context`: Longer passages or articles providing detailed domain-specific information to disambiguate, verify, or contextualize elements within the claim and notes.

Output Requirements:
1. `new_notes`: 
   - Provide a concise and precise subset of the input `notes` that directly contributes to verifying or refuting the claim.
   - Remove irrelevant, redundant, or misleading information.
   - Where appropriate, merge multiple notes to improve clarity, completeness, or accuracy.
   - Highlight critical domain-specific facts necessary for fact-checking the claim.
   - Avoid repetition and maintain factual accuracy.
   - Ensure that contradictory or unsupported parts of the claim are addressed or noted in `new_notes`.

2. `titles`:
   - Provide a clearly curated list of Wikipedia-style article titles or named entities directly relevant to the claim, notes, or context.
   - Include articles explicitly referenced or clearly alluded to in the input (e.g., movies, persons, bands, organizations).
   - Add related or background titles that fill knowledge gaps or clarify ambiguous points (e.g., production companies, team nicknames, band members).
   - Use canonical Wikipedia naming conventions (e.g., "The Scarlet Letter (1995 film)", "John Hill (musician)").
   - Prioritize “gold-standard” documents—i.e., authoritative sources directly connected to verifying the claim or understanding its context.

Domain-Specific Considerations:
- **Film and entertainment claims:** 
  - Confirm correct identification of films by title and year.
  - Verify accurate attribution of actors to films, noting that well-known actors may star in distinct films of the same genre/year.
  - Clarify production company involvement if mentioned.
  - Distinguish primary cast from supporting or unassociated actors.
  
- **Music-related claims:**
  - Clearly identify bands and members, especially distinguishing lead roles (e.g., lead guitarist, lead singer).
  - Confirm which band recorded which work or album.
  - Disambiguate similarly named bands, albums, or songs.
  - Note known birth dates or identifying information if relevant to the claim.
  
- **Sports or other specialized domains:**
  - Understand sport-specific terminology, roles, match outcomes, or historical details.
  - Recognize geographic or organizational structures relevant to the claim (e.g., administrative subdivisions, company ownership).

- **Brand or product claims:**
  - Confirm flavor profiles, origin countries, advertisement associations.
  - Disambiguate products with similar names or overlapping categories.

General Reasoning Approach:
- Cross-reference claim elements meticulously with notes and context to identify contradictions, confirmations, or unresolved points.
- Exclude notes that do not directly relate to verifying the claim or that introduce unrelated or conflicting information without evidence.
- Use context to resolve ambiguity, especially when multiple entities share names or similar attributes.
- When information is missing or ambiguous (e.g., lack of data about a certain person’s birthdate or unclear band membership), explicitly note this uncertainty in `new_notes`.
- Summarize the most relevant facts to help a user verify the claim, avoiding overly verbose or tangential details.

Formatting and Clarity:
- Write outputs in clear, succinct text.
- Use quotation marks around person nicknames or specific official names for clarity.
- Ensure `new_notes` contain only factual, focused content relevant to the claim verification.
- Provide `titles` as a simple list of canonical Wikipedia-style article names.
- Avoid including URLs, unnecessary explanations, or verbose summaries beyond the key facts.
