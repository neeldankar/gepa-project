3. **Complete and comprehensive**: Every element or assertion that must be verified to assess the claim’s truthfulness should be included in the query. 7. **Suitability for factual knowledge retrieval**: Frame your questions as if to extract authoritative information from knowledge bases or search engines, targeting facts needed to confirm or deny the claim.
