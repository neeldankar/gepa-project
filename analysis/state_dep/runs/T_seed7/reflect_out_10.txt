Task Description:
Given a `claim` consisting of one or more factual statements and a collection of related `notes` (which provide context, facts, dates, names, and relevant entities), your goal is to produce a concise, targeted `query` that can be used to verify or fact-check the claim. The `query` should be focused on retrieving the most critical missing pieces of information or clarifications needed to validate the claim against reliable sources or documents.

Input Format:
- `claim`: A short paragraph or set of sentences that assert one or more factual statements, often involving dates, names, events, roles, or relationships.
- `notes`: A list of strings that include specific factual data points, dates, names, clarifications, or explanations related to the claim. These notes may contain contradictions or detailed domain-specific information that helps contextualize the claim.

Output Requirement:
- Produce a `query` string that encapsulates the key factual questions or points of verification necessary to determine the claim’s truthfulness.
- The query should ideally mention the core entities (people, TV series, movies, teams), relevant dates, and any relationships or causal claims made.
- The query should be phrased naturally as a question or set of questions that target the missing or conflicting information highlighted in the claim and notes.
- The query should be precise enough to maximize retrieving gold-standard documents or authoritative sources relevant to verifying the claim.

Domain-Specific Considerations:
- TV/Film domain: Queries should specify actors, characters, exact premiere or release dates, and distinguish between similarly named entities (e.g., “Battlestar Galactica” vs. “Eureka”).
- Voice acting and animation: Queries should clarify specific roles, films, and release years to confirm chronological sequences.
- Sports domain: Queries should reference team founding years, player tenures (with years), management history, and franchise name changes accurately to disambiguate related entities.
- When multiple entities or timelines are involved, queries may combine multiple related questions to cover all critical points of the claim.

Generalizable Strategy:
- Identify contradictory or ambiguous information in the claim.
- Use the notes to clarify which entities, dates, and events are relevant or conflicting.
- Focus the query on verifying the core factual assertions by explicitly referencing key persons, dates, teams, or titles.
- Include as much relevant detail as necessary to retrieve all related authoritative documents (aiming to maximize gold document recall for fact-checking).

Summary Instruction:
Given a claim and related factual notes, generate a concise, specific, and comprehensive query question (or questions) that directly addresses the claim’s key factual assertions and ambiguities. The query should be carefully targeted to retrieve supporting verification documents, referencing precise entities, dates, relationships, and events mentioned in both the claim and notes.
