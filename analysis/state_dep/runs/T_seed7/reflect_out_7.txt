Task Objective:
Given three input fields — `claim`, `notes`, and `context` — generate two outputs: `new_notes` and `titles`. The goal is to produce a refined, concise, and factually accurate subset of the notes directly relevant to verifying or clarifying the claim, and to identify a targeted list of Wikipedia-style article titles or named entities that are strongly pertinent to understanding or confirming the claim.

Input Breakdown:
- `claim`: A definitive statement asserting a fact, relationship, or attribute involving people, places, organizations, or events. Claims often relate to specific knowledge domains such as sports, geography, academic/professional roles, or cultural references and may require domain expertise, timeline considerations, or disambiguation.
- `notes`: Factually relevant data drawn from varied sources, possibly overlapping or contradictory, that relate to the claim. Notes may contain extraneous or duplicated information and must be filtered for relevance and accuracy.
- `context`: Extended background information or article excerpts related to entities or concepts mentioned in the claim or notes. Context aids in addressing ambiguities, disambiguating similarly named entities, understanding domain-specific nuances, and verifying factual relationships.

Output Requirements:
1. `new_notes`:
   - Deliver a condensed, precise subset of the input notes, keeping only those facts that directly support or refute the claim.
   - Merge or synthesize overlapping information for clarity and comprehensiveness without redundancy.
   - Focus on domain-specific details crucial to claim validation (e.g., player roles and outcomes in sports, administrative hierarchy in geography, timeline of professional appointments).
   - Exclude irrelevant, contradictory, or duplicated information.
   - Retain clear and exact factual content necessary to evaluate the claim.

2. `titles`:
   - Provide a curated list of Wikipedia-style article titles or named entities highly relevant to the claim, notes, and context.
   - Include titles explicitly referenced or implicitly alluded to in the notes or context that contain evidence pertinent to verifying the claim.
   - Add related articles that help fill knowledge gaps or offer essential context (e.g., biographies of people involved, event names, locations, organizations).
   - Use canonical, standardized article naming conventions, ensuring clarity and disambiguation (e.g., specify seasons in sports series, suffixes for similarly titled events).
   - Prioritize authoritative or “gold-standard” knowledge sources connected to the claim domain.

Domain-Specific Considerations:
- Sports Domain:
  - Distinguish between similarly named sports or competitions (e.g., American football vs. association football).
  - Verify player identities, match outcomes, tournament titles, and coaching or player roles within precise seasons or events.
  - Note team nicknames carefully, ensuring no conflation with player names without strong evidence.
  - Track timelines of player careers or event occurrences, ensuring temporal accuracy.

- Academic and Professional Roles:
  - Pay attention to positions held, tenure periods, and official titles at exact times.
  - Consider temporal nuances when verifying claims about appointments or roles.

- Geographic and Organizational Claims:
  - Confirm administrative hierarchies (e.g., counties under cities, cities within provinces or states, states within countries).
  - Differentiate place types (e.g., city, county, province).
  - Avoid conflating colloquial or nickname references unless clearly supported.
  - Verify multi-layered jurisdictional relationships.

Generalizable Reasoning Strategy:
- Carefully cross-reference all elements of the claim against facts in notes and context.
- Identify contradictions and exclude unrelated or misleading data.
- Use context passages to disambiguate identical or similarly named entities.
- Summarize supporting or negating information succinctly in `new_notes`.
- Select article titles that a knowledgeable user would consult to validate, contextualize, or deepen understanding of the claim.
- Preserve clarity by using quotation marks for nicknames or special official names.
- Avoid verbosity; prioritize precision and factual relevance.

Formatting and Clarity:
- Respond with clear, concise text.
- Use consistent canonical naming conventions for titles.
- Avoid repeated facts but ensure all critical points are covered.
- Present `new_notes` as a list of factual statements or synthesized facts.
- Present `titles` as a list of article or entity names without extraneous explanations.
