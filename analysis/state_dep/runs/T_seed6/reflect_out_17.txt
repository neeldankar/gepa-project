Task Instruction:

You will be given two fields as input: `claim` and `notes`. Your task is to produce a `query` field that formulates a precise, entity- and relationship-centric question or set of questions designed to retrieve or verify the specific evidence needed to validate or refute the claim.

Input Format:
- `claim`: A factual or verification statement involving one or more entities (such as people, places, dates, works, relationships, roles, events, teams, or hierarchical geopolitical locations) whose truthfulness must be verified.
- `notes`: A collection of factually relevant information snippets ("sources" or "documents") related to the entities, events, or related claims in the `claim`. These notes provide partial or complete candidate evidence but may not be comprehensive.

Goal:
Your goal is to synthesize the essential verification needs by linking relevant entities, roles, events, timeframes, or relationships expressed or implied in the claim into a well-crafted, focused query or queries. The queries should direct retrieval efforts to the exact authoritative or “gold standard” documents needed to verify or disconfirm the claim in its entirety.

Key Requirements and Guidelines:

1. Entity and Relationship Explicitness:
   - Explicitly mention all relevant entities from the claim (e.g., names of people, teams, movies, locations) using their canonical, domain-specific spellings.
   - Explicitly encode relationships and qualifiers appearing or implied in the claim, such as family ties, professional roles (e.g., director, coach, judge), hierarchical jurisdiction (e.g., county within city within province within country), or event participation.
   - When multiple entities appear with specific interconnections (e.g., family relationships between actors, co-directors, or a trainer-coach-player relationship), explicitly include these to disambiguate or clarify the fact patterns.

2. Domain-Specific Knowledge to Integrate:
   - Geopolitical entities often reflect hierarchical administrative geography (e.g., counties under cities within a province and country). Queries should clarify these hierarchies when relevant.
   - Entertainment industry relations include cast/crew roles in films or shows, family relationships among actors, and relationships between producers, singers, and shows.
   - Sports and biographical domains may require verifying titles, teams coached, quotes attributed, ethnicity or nationality (e.g., Native American status), and athletic achievements (e.g., medals by sport and event).
   - Use official titles, production years, and other qualifiers in film/TV/music to ensure precise targeting of evidence (e.g., "Bombay Talkie (1970)" not just "Bombay Talkie").

3. Query Construction Principles:
   - Formulate queries as clear, focused, and unambiguous questions that explicitly seek to confirm or deny key claims.
   - If the notes lack direct evidence, queries should be designed to retrieve missing key information such as official credits lists, verified quotes, identity of individuals (e.g., naming the co-director), role assignments (e.g., judges on a show), and familial or ethnic relationships.
   - Queries may be composed of multiple related questions that collectively cover the claim’s verification needs.
   - Avoid vague, overly broad, or generic queries that do not connect or disambiguate entities and relationships explicitly.
   - Where potential for confusion exists (e.g., multiple persons with similar names or ambiguous place names), queries must include disambiguating qualifiers (like locations, dates, or roles).

4. Examples of Query Quality:
   - Correctly includes key entities and their relevant roles.
   - Explicitly asks for relational or hierarchical facts when needed.
   - Separates or links multiple verification angles in distinct, precise sub-questions when the claim is compound.
   - Uses domain-specific terminology and canonical spellings as provided.

5. Additional Task Context Observations:
   - When verifying claims related to film/TV casts and crews, always ask for complete or official cast/crew lists for the named productions with roles identified.
   - For familial relationship claims in entertainment, verify both individuals' identities and their claimed relationships explicitly.
   - For sports-related claims involving awards/medals, specify the athlete, event, sport, nationality/ethnicity, and exact accolade to allow precise verification.
   - For geopolitical claims, ask about the administrative hierarchy and whether two or more locations are in the same or different jurisdictions, specifying country and province/state.

In summary, your output `query` must synthesize a focused, comprehensive information request embodying all essential elements—entities, roles, relationships, temporal or jurisdictional qualifiers—needed to retrieve the authoritative evidence to fully confirm or refute the claim. The queries you produce will be used to identify gold-standard documents, so precision, explicitness, and completeness are crucial.
