Task Description:

You will be provided with three inputs: a `claim`, a set of `notes`, and a `context`. Your goal is to generate two outputs: a refined list of `new_notes` and a set of relevant `titles`. Your outputs should directly address the factual accuracy and verification of the claim based on the notes and context.

1. Understand and Analyze the Claim:
   - Carefully parse the claim to identify key entities (people, organizations, events), their relationships, facts stated, and any associated temporal or contextual details.
   - Detect the domain and specificity of the claim (e.g., film industry facts, sports seasons and player participation, historical roles, scientific affiliations).

2. Integrate and Synthesize Notes:
   - Review all given `notes`, which are extracted facts or commentary related to the claim.
   - Synthesize these notes into a clear, concise, and factual summary (`new_notes`) that either confirms, refutes, or qualifies the claim.
   - Avoid redundancy and irrelevant detail; focus on the strongest, most pertinent evidence.
   - Explicitly state if evidence is missing or incomplete for verifying the claim.

3. Validate and Expand Using the Context:
   - Use the provided `context` documents or snippets to check the factual accuracy of details in the notes.
   - Confirm or disconfirm details such as:
     * Names, dates, and roles (e.g., actors, directors, football teams, company headquarters, champions in sports seasons).
     * Chronological order and season numbering (e.g., 28th season corresponds to a specific year or event).
     * Affiliations and memberships (e.g., a company sponsoring a bowl game, band genre and membership counts).
     * Biographical and domain-specific details about individuals (e.g., postdoctoral institutions, familial relationships, commandant positions).
   - Identify and name the most relevant documents or passages aligned with the claim’s subject matter.

4. Generate Representative Titles:
   - Create a list of concise, descriptive `titles` that represent the key documents, entities, or concepts referenced in your refined notes.
   - Titles should help identify the sources or evidence crucial to verifying the claim.
   - Where applicable, include titles of missing but necessary documents or evidence inferred from feedback or incompleteness.

5. Address Gaps and Limitations:
   - Explicitly mention if the evidence is insufficient or if certain aspects of the claim cannot be fully verified based on the current notes and context.
   - Note which documents or facts would be necessary for comprehensive verification but are currently lacking.

6. Domain-Specific Knowledge Considerations:
   When relevant, incorporate domain-specific factual knowledge to enhance accuracy:
   - Film Industry: Confirm actor-director roles, film release years, accurate casting information, and directorial credits.
   - Sports: Verify season numbering, year-season mappings, team participation and invitations, sponsor companies and their headquarters, event dates.

   - History/Biography: Confirm roles such as concentration camp commandant, institutions attended, postdoctoral training locations, familial relations.
   - Academia/Science: Verify training institutions, research affiliations, precise years of establishment for institutions or organizations.

7. Generalizable Verification Strategy:
   - Cross-check chronological consistency between claim and evidence.
   - Confirm alignment of named entities and their roles.
   - Carefully synthesize multiple notes and contextual facts into logically coherent summaries.
   - Explicitly identify missing evidence or inaccuracies.
   - Use the context to fill gaps or spot contradictions from the notes.
   - Verify domain-specific details with precision, demonstrating awareness of field-specific norms (e.g., sports season dating, film release cycles).

Output Format:

- `new_notes`: A list of factual, clearly phrased sentences providing a concise summary that confirms, refutes, qualifies, or notes gaps concerning the claim, drawing from the strongest available evidence.
- `titles`: A list of succinct document or concept titles most relevant to the claim and its verification; indicate missing but needed documents if applicable.

Additional Notes:

- Avoid speculation beyond provided notes and context.
- Use clear, domain-appropriate terminology.
- Maintain clarity and brevity while ensuring factual completeness.
- Explicitly indicate lack of evidence or missing documents when encountered.

This comprehensive and domain-aware approach ensures reliable verification and summary generation aligned with the claim and evidence provided.
