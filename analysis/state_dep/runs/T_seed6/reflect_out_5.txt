Your task is to produce two output fields: 
- `new_notes`: a concise, informative subset of the `notes` that directly support or verify the claim, avoiding redundancy and irrelevant details;
- `titles`: a list of document or entity titles that correspond to key sources or knowledge bases needed to verify or understand the claim. These should reflect the primary topics or entities central to the claim and relevant evidence.

1. **Understand the claim at a factual and semantic level.**

2. **Filter and select notes that directly support or negate the claim.**  
   - From the given `notes`, select those that contain explicit facts relevant to the claim.
   - Remove duplicates or paraphrases that do not add new evidence.
   - Emphasize notes that confirm the factual criteria in the claim, e.g., geographic presence, timing sequence, membership in categories, or participation in events.
   - Disregard notes that are irrelevant or contradictory without further context.

3. **Derive titles that correspond to the core factual sources relevant to claim validation.**  
   - Titles should represent key entities, concepts, or documents related to the claim (e.g., company names, airport names, film titles, TV series, locations, people, institutions).
   - Select titles that align with the primary facts extracted as relevant in the `new_notes`.
   - Titles should enable future retrieval of evidence to verify the claim.

4. **Incorporate domain and task-specific knowledge:**
   - Geographic claims often require confirming entities are located in the same or different geographic areas (countries, states, cities).
   - Temporal claims involve date references for premieres, events, or occurrences, ensuring ordering or overlap as claimed.
   - Actor/film/TV relationships involve cross-referencing roles, appearances, and associated timelines.
   - Multiple paraphrased or synonymous notes about the same fact should be unified or represented by the clearest statement.
   - Confirm the claim’s truthfulness based on the evidence selected but do not explicitly assert claim validation — only provide supporting notes and titles.

5. **Produce outputs that are concise, factual, and explicitly relevant.**
   - `new_notes` should be a distilled set of factual snippets that together fully cover the claim’s conditions without unnecessary repetition.
   - `titles` should cover all core entities referenced or required to understand or verify the claim.

Summary: Your goal is to filter and summarize the evidence to tightly map the input claim to a minimal but sufficient set of supporting factual data (`new_notes`), along with a corresponding set of knowledge source titles (`titles`) that represent the main knowledge bases or documents necessary for verification or retrieval. Use detailed domain knowledge about places, people, filmography, and chronology as needed to correctly interpret and filter the data.
