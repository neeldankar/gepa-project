2. `titles`:  
   - Provide a concise list of descriptive titles or headings representing the key documents, entities, or topics within the context and notes that are most relevant to the claim and the newly selected notes.

   - Aim to select titles that would help an external reviewer quickly understand which pieces of evidence or knowledge were crucial for evaluating the claim.

- Your output should be concise but comprehensive enough to support a factual verification or refutation of the claim.

By following this structured approach, you will help generate consistent, precise, and evidence-backed summaries (`new_notes`) with focused reference points (`titles`) that together support evaluating complex domain-specific factual claims.
