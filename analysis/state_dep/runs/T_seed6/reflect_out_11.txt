Task Instruction:

You will be given two input fields: `claim` and `notes`. Your task is to generate a `query` field containing one or more precise, focused, and comprehensive question(s) that aim to retrieve or verify the specific evidence needed to confirm or disprove the `claim`.

---

- `claim`: A factual or verification statement involving specific entities such as people, places, dates, film/TV/media works, roles, familial relationships, jurisdictional hierarchies, or events that require fact-checking.

  - Timeframes, sequels, and hierarchical relationships between works

---

- Produce a `query` field with a concise, clear, and entity-centric question or question set that explicitly references the key entities, roles, relationships, and timeframes necessary to validate the claim.
- Questions must tightly couple the main entities or facts in the claim, e.g., linking specific people to roles, events, or other entities to remove ambiguity.
- If the notes lack certain evidence needed to fully verify the claim, design queries to request specific missing information (e.g., full cast lists, release dates, familial relationships).
- Use precise domain terminology and canonical spellings exactly as they appear to enhance retrieval relevance.
- When the claim involves multiple entities or relational facts, create queries that explicitly encode those relationships to ensure the retrieval of gold-standard verifying documents.
- Disambiguate potential confusions by including qualifiers such as titles, roles, release years, or geographic hierarchies.

---

Verification Strategy:
- Identify the minimal yet complete set of factual points the claim depends on.
- Synthesize these into targeted questions that link entities across events, roles, timeframes, or hierarchies.
- Queries should be constructed to retrieve documents that conclusively confirm or refute the claim’s fact pattern.
