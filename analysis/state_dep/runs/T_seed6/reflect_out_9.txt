Task Description:

You will be provided with three inputs:  
- **claim**: A factual statement or assertion that needs to be verified, analyzed, or clarified. This claim might contain biographical details, historical facts, relationships among entities, event details, and temporal information.  
- **notes**: A list of pre-extracted text snippets or statements from documents or sources that relate to the claim. These notes may support, refute, or qualify parts of the claim. They can contain both direct evidence and contextual clues.  
- **context**: A collection of background information, detailed documents, or excerpts about the entities, events, organizations, persons, or concepts referred to in the claim. This offers a broader fact base to validate or expand on information in the notes.

Your goal is to synthesize these inputs into two outputs:  
- **new_notes**: A concise, coherent, and factually accurate summary that addresses the claim based on the strongest available evidence. This summary should:  
  * Confirm, refute, or qualify the claim clearly.  
  * Integrate and synthesize relevant information from both the provided notes and context.  
  * Include domain-specific factual details such as establishment dates, biographical and role data, event chronologies, genre classifications, and affiliations as relevant.  
  * Avoid unnecessary repetition and verbosity, focusing on clarity and precise relevance.  
  * Explicitly note where evidence is insufficient or missing to fully verify or refute the claim.  
- **titles**: A list of clear, descriptive, and domain-relevant titles representing key documents, entities, or concepts that were most critical to forming the new_notes. These should:  
  * Cover all main entities or facts addressed or missing in the notes and context.  
  * Reflect document or concept focus, e.g., individual biographies, specific events, organizational histories, or thematic subjects essential to claim verification.  
  * Help identify the strongest sources supporting or contesting the claim.  

Detailed Instructions and Strategies:

1. **Analyze the Claim Thoroughly**  
   - Identify all key entities, dates, relationships, roles, and factual assertions.  
   - Take note of any temporal sequences or domain-specific categorizations (e.g., film genres, historical roles, sports seasons, music band membership).  

2. **Examine and Synthesize the Notes**  
   - Assess each note for evidence that supports, contradicts, or qualifies elements in the claim.  
   - Synthesize multiple notes into a single cohesive summary addressing the claim’s key points.  
   - Ensure factual accuracy, referencing details such as person’s nationality, organization founding years, specific event dates, or participation in certain events.  

3. **Use the Context as a Verification and Expansion Resource**  
   - Cross-validate information from the notes using the context documents.  
   - Confirm precise names, dates, and roles with context data to avoid inaccuracies.  
   - Identify any missing but relevant facts not evident in the notes but crucial for complete verification.  
   - Pay attention to domain-specific facts such as titles of films, names of tournament champions, specific affiliations, geographic origins, or biographical timelines.  

4. **Address Incompleteness Explicitly**  
   - When crucial supporting information is unavailable in both notes and context, state this clearly in new_notes.  
   - Indicate what documents or data points would be necessary to fully substantiate or disprove the claim.  

5. **Domain-Specific Knowledge to Include Where Applicable**  
   - Historical roles or biographies (e.g., commandants of historical sites, training institutions).  
   - Establishment years of organizations or institutions relevant to the claim.  
   - Sports team seasonal details, tournament participation, and sponsorship chronicles.  
   - Filmographies, actor nationalities, and associated works or genres.  
   - Musical group membership counts, genres, and notable songs or albums.  

6. **Generate a Compact and Relevant Titles List**  
   - Titles should be specific and descriptive, enabling quick identification of the source or subject matter supporting or refuting the claim.  
   - Include all main documents related to the claim’s key facts, including missing but necessary documents identified during synthesis.  

7. **Generalizable Verification Strategy**  
   - Systematically verify chronological and biographical details from context against notes and claim.  
   - Integrate contradictory notes carefully, balancing evidence to produce a qualified or refuted stance if needed.  
   - Maintain consistency and factual rigor, correcting errors or misconceptions in the claim clearly.  
   - Avoid making unsupported assumptions—only state what is confirmed or explicitly indicate missing evidence.  

Output Format:  
- **new_notes**: A list of one or more well-formed, precise sentences summarizing the evidence related to the claim.  
- **titles**: A list of concise document or concept titles representing the key supporting or conflicting sources.

By adhering to these instructions, your output will provide clear, evidence-based verification and insightful summaries supported by properly highlighted source titles, facilitating deeper understanding and precise fact-checking of the claims presented.
