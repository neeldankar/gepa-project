Task Description:

You are given three inputs: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion to be verified or analyzed.
- `notes`: A list of statements extracted from various sources, potentially containing information that supports, contradicts, or is unrelated to the claim.
- `context`: A list of background or reference documents providing additional relevant information tied to the claim’s topic.

Your goal is to:
1. Analyze the relationship between the claim and the information found within `notes` and `context`.
2. Extract and synthesize key factual points from the notes that directly pertain to verifying or clarifying aspects of the claim.
3. Produce two outputs:
   - `new_notes`: A concise, relevant summary list of the essential factual points that clarify, support, contradict, or explain the claim. This should filter out irrelevant or redundant information.
   - `titles`: A list of document or subject titles representing the most pertinent entities, topics, or works identified in notes and context that are helpful to understanding or verifying the claim.

Guidelines & Domain-Specific Considerations:

- Claims may concern niche or domain-specific information such as entertainment (music groups, films, DVDs), actors, athletes, sporting events, or other specialized subjects. Domain knowledge includes:
  - Music groups and artists, related releases, membership and departure dates (e.g., Jay Park's membership in 2PM and release dates of group DVDs).
  - Film and television production details including actors, co-stars, directors, release years, and roles (e.g., actors co-starring in South Korean films, director attribution).
  - Sports events, player nationalities, tournament participation, event year details, defending champions, and distinctions between singles and doubles play (e.g., Angelique Kerber’s nationality, participation, and defending champions in tennis tournaments).
- Carefully distinguish identities and roles to avoid confusing similarly named persons or groups.
- Explicitly note any contradictions or lack of evidence supporting parts of the claim.
- Emphasize temporal relations and event sequences where relevant (e.g., an artist leaving a group before or after a DVD release date).
- When available, cite precise data such as event dates, release years, and participant roles.
  
Recommended approach:

- Read and understand the claim thoroughly.
- Cross-reference the note entries with the context documents to confirm factual details and timeline consistency.
- Exclude unrelated or tangential notes.
- Summarize key factual info in `new_notes` that directly impact the truthfulness or clarity of the claim.
- List clear and objective titles denoting the subjects or documents crucial to understanding or investigating the claim, including names of persons, groups, films, albums, or events identified within the notes/context.
- Maintain clarity and factual accuracy; do not infer beyond available evidence.
- If some claim components are partially true or false, reflect that nuance in your reasoning and note selection.

Your output will help identify, in a structured manner, the essential knowledge and relevant documents necessary to verify or refute the given claim comprehensively.
