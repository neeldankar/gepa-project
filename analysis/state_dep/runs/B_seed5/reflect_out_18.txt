Task Description:

You will be given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your goal is to generate two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`. 

Inputs:
- `claim`: A factual or semi-factual statement (usually about a specific place, person, event, or entity) that requires verification or elaboration.
- `notes`: A list of pre-extracted relevant facts, statements, or partial evidences that relate to the claim.
- `context`: A list of document excerpts or summaries that provide detailed background information on entities related to the claim.

Outputs:
- `new_notes`: A concise, coherent, and focused subset of notes that directly supports, clarifies, or refines the claim based on the information found in both the provided `notes` and `context`. This should also include critical observations when parts of the claim are not supported or are contradicted by the evidence. Avoid redundancy and emphasize distinct, relevant facts that help verify or refute the claim.
- `titles`: A list of short, descriptive headings that summarize key aspects or topics covered by the `new_notes`. Each title should clearly represent a distinct information theme or fact cluster extracted from the `new_notes`, facilitating quick thematic categorization.

Guidelines and domain-specific considerations:

1. Verification and Evidence Synthesis:
   - Carefully cross-check the `claim` against both `notes` and `context` data.
   - Where the claim is verified, include supporting remarks emphasizing the confirmation.
   - Where the claim or parts of it cannot be verified or are contradicted, explicitly note the lack of evidence or the presence of conflicting information.
   - If a claim mentions entities or facts not found or supported in context or notes, include statements acknowledging that absence.
   
2. Context Utilization:
   - Use the context documents to enrich or confirm the details found in notes.
   - Extract only those facts from the context that directly relate to the claim or provide valuable clarification.
   - Avoid introducing unrelated or tangential details from the context.

3. Note Consolidation:
   - The `new_notes` must be concise and non-repetitive, merging similar facts or statements found repeatedly in `notes`.
   - Highlight authoritative or confirmatory facts.
   - Include information about entities, locations, dates, events, ownership, administrative facts, or relationships relevant to the claim.
   - When dates or timeline facts are involved, specify the correct timeline if the claim gets it wrong.
   - If ownership, location, or administrative details are claimed, verify exact names and corporate/entity structures from the context.

4. Titles Creation:
   - Titles should be brief (a few words to a sentence) summarizing major themes such as "Ownership of X", "Premiere Date of Y", "Location of Z", or "Lack of Evidence for A".
   - Titles provide a thematic overview that helps classifying the key facts or gaps identified in `new_notes`.
   
5. Common Task Domains and Examples:
   - Verification of administrative or geographic claims about towns, parks, or other places.
   - Clarifying actor roles, TV series premiere dates, and associated series or characters.
   - Linking residence shows, auditoriums, venues, and hotel ownership information.
   - Distinguishing between similarly named entities or events with similar dates.
   - Identifying missing or unverified entities mentioned in claims.

6. Reasoning Step:
   - Although not explicitly requested as output, incorporate clear internal reasoning by comparing claim details against notes and context to justify your selections for `new_notes` and `titles`.
   - Your final answer should reflect this reasoning robustly to ensure the claim is properly validated or qualified.

Summary: The task is essentially one of focused fact verification and summarization. You must synthesize multiple evidence sources into a clear, precise, and thematically organized set of notes that directly confirm, deny, or qualify the input claim, along with titles that encapsulate these synthesized insights.
