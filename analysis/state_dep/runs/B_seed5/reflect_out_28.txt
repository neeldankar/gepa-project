Task Description:

You will be provided with three input fields: `claim` (a statement or assertion that may require verification or elaboration), `notes` (a list of textual snippets, observations, or preliminary evidence related to the claim), and `context` (a list of longer, encyclopedic or detailed textual documents or entries relevant to the claim).

Your task is to analyze these inputs comprehensively and produce two output fields:

1. `new_notes`: A concise, relevant, and factual subset of notes that directly support, refute, or clarify the claim. These should be distilled, de-duplicated, and focused on key pieces of evidence. Avoid adding irrelevant or unsupported details. The new notes should clearly capture any confirmations, contradictions, or important nuances about the claim.

2. `titles`: A list of distinct, descriptive document titles or topic headings extracted from the context or inferred from the content that best summarize the important information sources needed to evaluate or understand the claim. These titles serve as a focused bibliography or reference set supporting the claim or its analysis.

Detailed Task Instructions:

- Carefully read and comprehend the claim, identifying all key entities, events, titles, or character attributes mentioned.

- Review all notes and context documents to find direct evidence or authoritative information about the claim. Use notes to identify preliminary evidence and context to obtain detailed factual data.

- Resolve any contradictions or ambiguous statements by cross-checking notes against the context. For example, check geography-related facts (e.g., cities and their rankings or states), film casting and familial relationships, historical sports facts including team rosters and player roles.

- The domain may include biographical data, geographical facts, historical sports seasons and player positions, film industry relationships and productions, and nicknames or terminologies relevant to organizations or clubs.

- When constructing `new_notes`, focus on:
  - Accurate restatements of key verified facts.
  - Explicit confirmation or denial of the claim's components.
  - Removal of duplicated or tangential notes.
  - Clarification of domain-specific roles or facts (e.g., strike force led by forwards, not goalies).

- When generating `titles`, choose:
  - Specific named entities or documents cited in the context that are instrumental to understanding or verifying the claim.
  - Titles that cover biographical information, filmographies, historical sports seasons, or organizational histories related to the claim.

- Your reasoning should integrate multiple sources and reconcile discrepancies to arrive at a supported conclusion about the claim.

- Precision and domain-accuracy are essential: for example, knowledge that "Frankfurt is the largest city in Hesse and the fifth-largest city in Germany" or that "goalkeepers do not lead strike forces in football teams" should be respected.

- Avoid fabricating information or making unsupported inferences beyond the given notes and context.

- The output should be clean, focused, and formatted exactly as two fields: `new_notes` (a list of strings) and `titles` (a list of strings).

Generalizable Strategy:

1. Parse the claim and identify the entities and facts to verify.
2. Use notes as initial evidence and filter redundant or irrelevant statements.
3. Use context documents to find authoritative or detailed information verifying or refuting the claim.
4. Extract or synthesize clear factual notes capturing this verification.
5. Select or infer precise and descriptive titles reflecting the key sources.
6. Ensure outputs are coherent, non-redundant, and domain-appropriate.

By following this approach, you will generate outputs that are factually sound, appropriately referenced, and helpful to users assessing the veracity or details of a claim.
