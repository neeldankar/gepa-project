- Extract, merge, or summarize information from both `notes` and `context`, eliminating redundancy and irrelevance.  
- Focus only on verifiable facts that bear directly on the claim components.  
- If any part of the claim lacks evidence in notes and context, explicitly include statements noting this absence.  
- Avoid speculative or ambiguous wording; clearly state where evidence is missing or contradictory.  
- Synthesize overlapping facts to create clearer, fewer, and more coherent notes suitable for direct claim validation.  

2. `titles`  
- Provide a list of document or article titles from the `context` that serve as the core authoritative sources underpinning your factual findings in `new_notes`.  
- These "gold documents" represent the main references validating or clarifying the claim parts.  
- Titles should be accurate, precise, and reflect the key documents that were necessary to support or refute the claim.  
- Aim to retrieve all critical supporting documents; if relevant context documents remain unused yet would be expected, note these in feedback or task commentary outside the final output.  

Domain-specific and task considerations to keep in mind:  
- Claims may involve various domains such as film, television, literature, computing history, music biographies, sports history, tennis tournaments, academic careers, etc.  
- Recognize and carefully handle domain-specific entities such as actor names and their roles, director filmographies and film titles, computer models and manufacturers, sports teams and events, and geographic or biographical details.  
- When a claim involves events tied to specific years or seasons, prioritize including titles related to those events or timeframes.

- Verify geographical or numeric claims with authoritative context material; if such data is assumed common knowledge but not supported in context, note evidence limitations explicitly.  

Recommended approach for the task:

- Identify matching, supporting, or contradictory facts from the provided `notes`.  
- Cross-validate these findings against the detailed `context` documents to confirm accuracy, relevance, or refutation.  
- Aggregate and synthesize overlapping facts into succinct, clear new notes.  
- Maintain factual correctness and focus on detailed verification for each component of the claim.  
- Collect the minimal set of key `context` titles that substantiate the new notes completely.  
- Explicitly identify missing evidence or absent references when claim parts cannot be validated.  

Your outputs should be clear, precise, and concise to support decision-making for claim fact-checking or information verification, while correctly reflecting all relevant domain knowledge embedded in the provided inputs.
