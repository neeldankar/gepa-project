Given a `claim` (a factual or multi-part statement) and a set of `notes` (supporting or refuting evidence statements), your task is to produce a precise and targeted `query` that seeks to retrieve or verify the missing or uncertain pieces of factual information necessary to fully evaluate the truthfulness of the claim.

Specifically:

1. Analyze the claim carefully to identify all key entities, relationships, and factual assertions it contains.

2. Review the provided `notes` for:
   - Relevant facts that support or contradict parts of the claim.
   - Gaps where essential information is missing (e.g., unknown birth dates, unclear relationships, disputed events).
   - Misattributions or conflations of different entities or facts that undermine claim validity.

3. From this combined reasoning, produce a concise, multi-part question or set of questions (the `query`) that directly targets the unresolved information needed to confirm, refute, or clarify the claim. The query should:
   - Reference the key entities by name or role as in the claim.
   - Ask clearly about the specific facts that remain unknown or disputed (e.g., birth dates, coaching positions, film director nationality).
   - Where appropriate, break down compound claims into multiple related verification questions.

4. Avoid general or vague queries. The query should enable retrieval of the exact missing evidence or authoritative records crucial for verifying the claim.

5. The goal is to facilitate retrieval of 100% of the gold-standard supporting or refuting evidence documents by precisely identifying what is missing or unclear, to fully assess the claim.

Summary of domain-specific considerations based on the examples:

- Birth date or age is essential for age comparison claims.
- Distinct individuals should not be conflated. Queries should distinguish persons and their roles carefully.
- Verification of specific titles, nicknames, or attributions (e.g., who named someone “the greatest football player”) requires direct questions about those attributions.
- Nationality coupled with profession or notable works should be explicitly verified, especially when disproving erroneous nationality or role claims.
- Multi-part claims benefit from multi-part queries that address each factual component separately but cohesively.

This approach ensures clear, targeted queries that support efficient retrieval of all necessary documents to evaluate the claim’s accuracy.
