Task Description:

You are given three inputs related to a factual claim that may contain multiple components or subclaims:

- **claim**: One or more factual assertions that need to be verified or discussed.
- **notes**: A collection of factual snippets or extracted data points that may support, refute, or be unrelated to the claim. These notes often contain redundancies, overlaps, partial information, and domain-specific details (e.g., sports statistics, biographical facts, event sponsorships, venues).
- **context**: A set of longer, more detailed documents or entries (e.g., Wikipedia-style articles, event reports, biographies) relevant to the claim. These provide authoritative background and fuller evidence.

Your goal is to produce two outputs:

1. **new_notes**  
   - A distilled, concise, and non-redundant synthesis of facts extracted and merged from the input `notes` and `context`.
   - These facts should directly support or contradict individual parts of the claim.
   - Exclude irrelevant or tangential details.
   - Clearly indicate when particular claim components lack supporting evidence or when evidence is contradictory or ambiguous.
   - When multiple notes overlap on the same fact, summarize them into a single, clear statement.
   - Facts may relate to domain-specific knowledge such as sports seasons, tournament dates, participant roles, sponsorships, biographical timelines, venue ownership, or specific event details.
   - Maintain strict factual correctness and avoid speculation beyond source evidence.

2. **titles**  
   - A list of authoritative document or article titles from the `context` that underpin the factual basis of the claim or clarify missing evidence.
   - Titles should specifically correspond to key documents that verify or explain each claim subcomponent.
   - Include multiple titles if different parts of the claim are supported by different context documents.
   - If some likely relevant context documents remain unused or unreferenced, note this to indicate incomplete retrieval.

---

Detailed Task and Domain Considerations:

- **Parsing the claim**: Break down compound claims into discrete assertions to verify each part distinctly.
- **Cross-referencing facts**:  
  - Identify matching or opposing evidence from the notes.  
  - Validate or refine these with detailed information from the context documents.  
  - Use domain knowledge to interpret entities, dates, venues, relationships, roles, or historical events.
- **Domain-specific facts**:  
  - Sports domain: seasonal records, bowl game invitation and outcome details, sponsorships, event dates and locations, ownership of venues/hotels/casinos.  
  - Entertainment domain: residency show venues, hotel associations, owner corporations, event dates.  
  - Biographical domain: individuals’ careers, accomplishments, relationships, teacher affiliations, and death notices.  
  - Others as appearing in claim (e.g., music releases, novels, political offices).  
- **Evidence gaps**:  
  - Explicitly note when no evidence is found for a claim component, neither in notes nor context.  
  - Mention if evidence contradicts the claim or is ambiguous.
- **Redundancy and synthesis**:  
  - Multiple notes often repeat the same fact in various wording; synthesize into one succinct new_note.  
  - Avoid listing duplicate or trivial restatements.
- **Titles selection**:  
  - The titles chosen should relate specifically to extracted facts.  
  - Ideal is “gold documents,” i.e., those that would be considered authoritative references supporting the claim.  
  - If the context includes related but unused documents, acknowledge their absence from final references.

---

Generalizable Strategy for Solving the Task:

1. **Decompose the claim** into individual factual assertions or subclaims.
2. **Inspect notes** for direct factual statements that address the claim’s parts.
3. **Cross-validate notes with context**, extracting precise facts that confirm, refine, or refute the claim parts.
4. **Synthesize overlapping or duplicate notes** into concise, clear factual statements for `new_notes`.
5. **Explicitly state evidence absence or contradiction** when no conclusive support is found.
6. **Compile a list of `titles`** from the context documents that contain the main supporting information.
7. **Flag when some expected authoritative documents remain unreferenced** as an indicator of missing evidence or incomplete retrieval.

---

Outcome Expectations:

- `new_notes` should be factually accurate, precise, and non-redundant, directly supporting or disputing claim parts.
- `titles` should represent key context documents used to derive `new_notes` and verify the claim.
- The output demonstrates detailed validation of all claim components, with explicit notes on evidentiary gaps.
- The response should be domain-aware and sensitive to nuanced factual details in diverse knowledge areas such as sports, entertainment, biography, and events.
