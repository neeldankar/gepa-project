Task Overview:

You will receive three input fields:

- `claim`: A factual statement involving one or more entities and their relationships (e.g., persons, works of art, creative projects, organizations, etc.). The claim may assert attributions such as who wrote, directed, sang, or produced something, or clarify relationships between people and works.
  
- `notes`: A list of textual snippets about the entities or topics relevant to the claim. These notes often contain overlapping, redundant, or incomplete information, and not all are pertinent or supportive of the claim.

- `context`: A collection of short, encyclopedia-style or document excerpts. Each snippet typically centers on a single entity (a person, film, song, band, or other named subject) and contains factual background, attributions, timelines, roles, and contextual information that can verify or clarify the claim's statement.


Your Objective:

1. Identify the key entities and relationships described in the `claim`. These generally include:
   - Named persons (e.g., composers, lyricists, directors, actors)
   - Creative works (e.g., films, songs, albums)
   - Groups or organizations (e.g., bands, record labels)
   - Roles or attributions (e.g., writer of a song, director of a film)

2. Sift through the `notes` to extract a **concise, factually accurate, and non-redundant** selection of statements that directly support, confirm, or clarify these entities and relationships. Filter out irrelevant, tangential, or duplicate notes. Prioritize:
   - Clear attributions of roles (e.g., who wrote, directed, or performed a work)
   - Facts establishing relationships between entities (e.g., who founded a record label, who co-directed a film)
   - Details that specifically address the claim's central assertion or components

3. From the `context`, identify and list only those document snippets whose titles correspond to entities or works that directly:
   - Substantiate the key facts included in the filtered `new_notes`
   - Provide important background or confirm identity, timeline, or roles relevant to the claim
   - Help disambiguate entities if necessary (e.g., differentiating multiple persons with similar names)
   
   Avoid including extraneous or only loosely related documents, even if they share keywords.

Domain-Specific and Niche Considerations:

- Film-related claims may focus on attributions such as director, co-directors, screenwriters, or actor roles. Often, verifying the exact credit (e.g., co-director or sole director) is important.

- Music or song-related claims can hinge upon specifying lyricists, composers, band members, or vocalists accurately. Multiple writers or collaborators frequently co-author songs, so precise attribution matters.

- Names of entities or projects may appear in different variants (e.g., film titles with years, band names with slight variations). Use context to confirm identity and relationships.

- Some claims involve entities like record labels or organizations linked with persons (e.g., a film directed by the founder of a label). Confirm both the person’s identity and their founding role from context.

Generalizable Strategy to Apply:

- Parse and extract key entities and relationships from the `claim`.

- Cross-reference these entities with `notes` to identify relevant, factually supportive data. Remove duplication and irrelevance.

- Use the `context` excerpts to rigorously confirm the notes' accuracy and provide the minimal document set that fully supports the claim.

- Produce `new_notes` as a succinct, de-duplicated set of claim-relevant facts.

- Provide `titles` as a list of minimal yet sufficient `context` document titles that substantiate the `new_notes`.

Output Format:

- `new_notes`: List of concise, directly relevant notes supporting or clarifying the claim's central entities and relationships.

- `titles`: List of context document titles most pertinent for verifying the claim, corresponding exactly to the included `new_notes` entities.

Your answers should be precise, focused, and conducive to straightforward fact verification.
