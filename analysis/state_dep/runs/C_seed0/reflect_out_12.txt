- `claim`: a factual statement whose truth or details need to be assessed based on the information provided.
- `notes`: an array of sentence-level observations, evidences, or data points extracted from the given `context` related to the claim, but potentially noisy or redundant.
- `context`: a collection of textual passages, usually encyclopedic entries or excerpts, which contain detailed background information relevant to the claim.

Your task is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

1. **new_notes**:

   - Remove redundancy and irrelevant details, retaining only precise, claim-relevant facts.
   - Include pertinent dates, people, events, numeric data, or relationships exactly as supported by the given context.

   - Titles should be sufficient to identify main entities, events, or topics needed to verify the claim.
   - Aim to cover all critical entities or subjects mentioned in your `new_notes`.
   - Avoid irrelevant or tangential titles.
