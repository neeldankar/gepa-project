Given the input fields `claim`, `notes`, and `context`, your task is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

Detailed task description:

1. The `claim` is a factual statement that needs to be verified or supported by evidence from the `notes` and `context`.

2. The `notes` field is a list of textual snippets relevant to the claim. These notes may be redundant, incomplete, or partially relevant.

3. The `context` field contains multiple short encyclopedia-style articles or document snippets, each usually centered on a subject (e.g., a person, a creative work, or an entity). These provide deeper or broader background knowledge that may help verify or elaborate on the claim.

Your objective:

- Produce `new_notes` as a concise, de-duplicated subset of the original `notes`. These should be directly relevant, factually supporting (or useful for evaluating) the claim. Remove any redundant or irrelevant entries. The `new_notes` should focus especially on facts that confirm or clarify the relationships, attributions, or details referenced in the claim.

- Produce `titles` as a list of the names or titles of the documents from the `context` that are most relevant to the claim and that support the extracted `new_notes`. Each title should correspond to a context snippet that contains important factual information specifically related to the claim or its key entities, such as:
  - Persons (e.g., composers, actors)
  - Creative works (e.g., films, albums, compositions)
  - Musical groups or bands
  - Any other entities explicitly involved in the claim

Important notes and domain-specific details:

- Identify entities and relationships carefully to connect the claim with the correct supporting evidence in the notes and context. For example, if the claim involves a composer and a film soundtrack, confirm that the composer’s identity and the film titles match across notes and context.

- The context snippets often contain disambiguation or background that can establish timelines, roles, or attributes (e.g., "X was the first film scored by Y," "Composer A is Hungarian," etc.). Use this to rigorously confirm or disprove the claim.

- When filtering `notes` for `new_notes`, prioritize concise, factual, and non-redundant statements that directly address key entities and the relationships or attributions in the claim.

- In `titles`, aim to include the minimal set of context documents that together provide sufficient factual support to understand and verify the claim, avoiding unrelated context documents even if they mention some of the same keywords.

Generalizable strategy:

- First, parse and identify the key entities and relationships in the claim.

- Then, cross-reference these key points with the `notes` for relevant, supportive evidence.

- Further validate and enrich this evidence by selecting pertinent `context` snippets that directly relate to these entities and their roles as described in the claim.

- Finally, produce a succinct `new_notes` that distills this evidence, paired with a list of `titles` corresponding to the most relevant context documents.

The outputs should be precise and focused, enabling easy verification of the claim with clear source attribution.
