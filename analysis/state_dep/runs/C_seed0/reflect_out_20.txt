Given the input fields `claim`, `notes`, and `context`, your task is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

Detailed task description:

1. **Purpose:**  
   The `claim` is a factual statement about entities and their relationships that needs verification or support by evidence drawn from the `notes` and `context`.

2. **Input fields explained:**  
   - `claim`: A statement involving key entities (such as people, organizations, creative works, locations) and their attributes or relations.  
   - `notes`: A list of text snippets relevant to the claim but may contain redundant, incomplete, or only partially relevant information.  
   - `context`: A collection of encyclopedia-style articles or document snippets, each focused on specific entities mentioned (persons, schools, organizations, creative works, sports teams, etc.). These provide detailed, authoritative background to help confirm or refute the claim.

3. **Your objectives:**  
   - Extract and output a **concise, de-duplicated subset of the original `notes`**, called `new_notes`, which:  
     - Directly supports, clarifies, or helps verify the claim.  
     - Focuses on crucial facts establishing correct entities, relationships, attributions, and relevant timelines.  
     - Removes irrelevant or redundant information not useful for assessing the claim.  
   - Identify and output a list of **`titles`**, which are the names of the documents from the `context` most relevant to the claim and that underpin the selected `new_notes`. These titles should:  
     - Correspond to context snippets with critical factual info about the claim's key entities or relationships.  
     - Include only the minimal set necessary to understand and verify the claim.  
     - Avoid unrelated or only tangentially connected documents, even if they mention common keywords.

4. **Domain-specific factual considerations:**  
   - Recognize and confirm **entity identities** precisely (e.g., a composer, a football club, a high school) to correctly attribute facts.  
   - Confirm **relationships** carefully (e.g., whether a song was originally recorded by a certain singer, whether a school is part of a certain district, whether a football club plays in a particular league or division).  
   - Analyze **chart rankings, league structures, or organizational memberships** as stated, verifying they align factually across notes and context.  
   - Consider **geographic or administrative details** such as census-designated places served by a school, county information, or league sponsorship status when relevant to the claim.  
   - Account for **timeline or status updates** when present, such as current membership or sponsorship status rather than outdated info.

5. **Generalizable strategy for producing outputs:**  
   - First, **extract key entities and relationships** from the claim.  
   - Second, **review notes** carefully to identify all factual snippets relating to these entities and relationships, selecting those which support or help evaluate the claim.  
   - Third, **review the context snippets**; select documents whose titles best cover the key entities and provide authoritative background or confirmatory information.  
   - Fourth, **de-duplicate and condense the selected notes** into a precise, direct set of supporting facts.  
   - Finally, produce the `new_notes` and the list of `titles` reflecting the minimal sufficient context coverage.

6. **Output formatting:**  
   - `new_notes`: A list of concise, factually relevant notes directly supporting the claim and clarifying or confirming its correctness.  
   - `titles`: A list of minimal, well-targeted context document titles that align with and substantiate the `new_notes`.

7. **Additional instructions based on observed task nuances:**  
   - When multiple versions or recordings of a creative work are mentioned, clearly distinguish original artists vs. later covers, especially when chart rankings are referenced.  
   - When organizational or league structures are described, check for exact league or division names, membership, and sponsorship to avoid mixing distinct entities or statuses.  
   - For educational institutions, clearly identify school district membership and geographic servicing to confirm claims about schools serving specific census-designated places or locations.  
   - Avoid including notes that merely repeat identical or highly similar facts unless they add meaningful variation or clarification.  
   - If the claim contains inaccuracies or incomplete info, the new_notes and titles should reflect the precise verified facts rather than the incorrect claim content.

By rigorously adhering to these detailed steps, your outputs will enable straightforward verification of the claim with clear, pertinent source attribution.
