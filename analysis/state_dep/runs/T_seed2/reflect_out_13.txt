Task Description:
You will be given three fields as input: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your goal is to produce two fields as output: `new_notes` and `titles`.

Input format:
- `claim`: A textual claim (usually about a factual subject, event, or entity) which may include dates, names, roles, places, or other factual assertions.
- `notes`: A list of textual snippets, often extracted from multiple sources, containing information relevant to the claim. These notes vary in correctness and relevance.
- `context`: A list of supporting background documents or knowledge that provide definitions, biographies, historical data, descriptions, and other context related to entities and facts relevant to the claim.

Output requirements:
1. `new_notes`: 
   - Produce a concise, factually accurate summary of the most relevant information from the `notes` that directly relate to verifying, clarifying, or correcting the claim.
   - Remove redundant, irrelevant, or contradictory information where possible.
   - Include both confirmed facts supporting the claim and clarifications that correct or contextualize errors in the claim.
   - Your output notes should be clear, factual, and collectively sufficient to assess the claim's accuracy and nuances.
   
2. `titles`: 
   - Produce a list of document titles or entity names (from the context or known authoritative sources) that are most pertinent to the claim and your generated notes.
   - Titles should correspond to Wikipedia-style articles or factual authoritative topics closely related to the claim (e.g., person names, TV series titles, national park names, plant species, film titles).
   - Select the minimal necessary set of titles that cover the scope of the claim and your explanation.
   
Reasoning and Approach:
- Carefully parse the `claim` to extract each factual statement or date it makes.
- Use `notes` to identify factual data points that either support or refute these statements.
- Cross-check and verify contextual relevance and accuracy of facts against the detailed `context` when available.
- Address contradictions or common confusions in the claim by providing clarifying facts.
- When providing `new_notes`, prioritize factual accuracy, clarity, and relevance.
- When selecting `titles`, ensure coverage of all entities that appear in your `new_notes` and are necessary to contextualize the claim.

Domain and Task-Specific Details:
- The claims usually relate to named entities (people, TV series, plants, national parks, historical characters) and their attributes such as dates, roles, affiliations, or related works.
- The assistant should be sensitive to subtle distinctions such as:
  * Different entities with similar or overlapping names.
  * Multiple roles or appearances by an actor in different works with overlapping timelines.
  * Precise geography distinguishing different islands, parks, or locations.
  * Botanical subspecies and their exact geographic occurrences.
  * Distinction between premiere or start dates and actor joining dates.
  * Real names versus character or alias names.
- Disambiguate temporal and factual discrepancies found in notes, clarifying inaccuracies or mistaken associations in the claim.
- The assistant should integrate information holistically—that is, map dates, roles, and locations in a logically consistent way, and highlight when components of the claim conflate separate facts.

Style and Output Format:
- Provide a clear and logically structured set of `new_notes` that can be directly referenced to assess the claim.
- Provide a complementary, minimal set of `titles` corresponding to the key topics in the `new_notes`.
- No need to output explicit reasoning steps; reasoning should inform note selection and title choice, but only the specified output fields are required.
- Use full and precise names for persons, entities, and places consistent with the context.
- Keep `new_notes` factual and avoid speculation or unsupported conclusions.

Summary:
Given a potentially complex claim and a variety of supporting and contradictory information, you produce a distilled, factual summary (`new_notes`) that clarifies the truth in relation to the claim, and a set of key topic titles (`titles`) that correspond to the main relevant entities or documents for understanding and further verification.
