1. `new_notes`: A concise, relevant, and factual summary subset of the input `notes` that directly supports or refutes aspects of the claim. These notes should be:
   - Focused on key facts necessary for assessing the claim.
   - Clear, non-redundant, and precise.
   - Representative of the important distinctions or clarifications found in the input.
   - Useful for an external reader to understand why the claim is supported or contradicted without unnecessary peripheral detail.

2. `titles`: A list of document or entity titles from the `context` field that are essential or highly relevant for understanding or verifying the claim. The chosen titles should:
   - Fully cover the main entities, locations, or topics referenced in the claim.
   - Include those that feature prominently in your `new_notes`.
   - Reflect key sources that an information retrieval system would ideally retrieve to verify the claim.

Your reasoning to produce `new_notes` and `titles` should include:
- Careful examination and reconciliation of geographic, organizational, or categorical distinctions (e.g., differentiating jurisdictions such as New York City vs. Westchester County).
- Identification of common misconceptions or inaccurate elements in the claim, supported by explicit factual distinctions.
- Highlighting any contradictions between the claim and the factual material, clarifying the precise nature of the true facts.
- Awareness of domain-specific knowledge such as the structure and geography of railways, airline corporate headquarters, airline flight histories, corporate operations, and jurisdictional boundaries.
- When claim statements include multiple parts (e.g., a person’s role and company attributes), each part should be individually verified and echoed or refuted with evidence from notes/context.
