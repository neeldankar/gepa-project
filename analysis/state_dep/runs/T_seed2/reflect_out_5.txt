Given two input fields, `claim` (a sentence or statement containing facts that need verification or deeper exploration) and `notes` (a list of factual statements, context, or evidence relevant to aspects of the claim), your task is to generate an appropriate `query` string.

This `query` should be a clear, focused research question or information request designed to retrieve documents or evidence that help verify the claim or explore key facts in it. The `query` should:

- Address ambiguities, inconsistencies, or points of confusion in the claim by leveraging insights from the `notes`.
- Target the key entities, attributes, and relationships identified within both the `claim` and the `notes`.
- Explicitly connect or contrast different pieces of information when the claim contains conflicting or inaccurate assertions.
- Aim to retrieve supporting documents related to important named entities (people, organizations, places, events, works of art or media, etc.) referenced or implicated in the claim and notes.
- Focus on the most critical factual components needed to verify or refute the claim, rather than summarizing or restating the entire claim.
- Use natural, clear, and concise language, phrasing the `query` as an information seeking question or query statement.

Additional domain-specific guidance based on examples and feedback:

- When the claim incorrectly associates attributes (e.g., nationality, membership, sponsorship) to entities, generate queries that clarify the correct attributes and identify the true entities involved.
- When multiple related entities are mentioned (e.g., bands and their members, football clubs and their leagues), queries should ask specifically about these relationships or memberships.
- When sponsors, event participants, or locations are part of the claim, queries should ask directly about these associations to aid document retrieval.
- Use context clues from `notes` to guide the query towards uncovering information on missing or incorrectly stated entities or facts, aiming to retrieve remaining key documents.
- If the claim mixes multiple facts or entities, formulate the query to disambiguate and verify them individually or comparatively, improving retrieval coverage.

Overall, generate queries that help a document retrieval system locate authoritative, relevant information to confirm, disambiguate, or correct the claim by focusing on the precise factual elements and their relationships, as informed by the factual notes provided.
