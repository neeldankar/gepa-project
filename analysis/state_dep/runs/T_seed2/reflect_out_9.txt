You will be given three input fields:
- `claim`: a statement or assertion that may reference entities, events, or facts.
- `notes`: a list of textual notes that provide preliminary information, observations, or analysis related to the claim.
- `context`: a list of paragraphs or text excerpts that contain detailed factual data, background information, and descriptions related to entities or topics mentioned in the claim and notes.

Your task is to process these inputs and produce two output fields:
- `new_notes`: a concise set of refined, relevant, and informative notes that:
    - Summarize and clarify the factual support or refutation of the claim based on `notes` and `context`.
    - Highlight pertinent connections or lack thereof between entities referenced in the claim.
    - Include any clarifying details, temporal or locational context, or factual corrections derived from the inputs.
    - Remove redundancy and focus on evidence that helps verify the claim’s truthfulness or falsity.
- `titles`: a list of concise, descriptive titles representing the main entities, topics, or documents that are most relevant to understanding or verifying the claim. Titles should be drawn from entities, events, organizations, locations, or topics mentioned in the notes and context. They serve to identify key authoritative reference points related to the claim.

To perform this task:
1. Carefully analyze the claim to understand what factual assertions or relationships it is making.
2. Examine all notes and context paragraphs to find evidence supporting, refuting, or clarifying the claim.
3. Identify and extract key entities (people, places, organizations, events, etc.) from the notes and context that relate directly to the claim.
4. Retain or synthesize notes that explicitly address the claim, including any critical data about the time frame, geographic location, or specific involvement of entities relevant to verifying the claim.
5. Compose the `new_notes` to serve as an evidence-based narrative or set of facts that support proper judgment on the claim.
6. Select or generate `titles` to encapsulate the key subjects or sources necessary to understand the claim’s context and evidentiary support.

Domain- and task-specific details to keep in mind:
- For claims involving actors and entertainment media, carefully track actors’ roles and linkages to shows and films for identity matching.
- For motorsport or sports-related claims, pay attention to season numbering, timelines of participation, nationality of drivers, and championship results.
- For geographic or administrative claims, scrutinize governing bodies, proximities of towns or landmarks, and administrative jurisdictions.
- Acknowledge when direct evidence for a claim element (e.g., Higgin’s Creek) is missing but infer geographical or contextual likelihood where appropriate.
- The aim is not to generate a final verdict on truthfulness but to provide clear, distilled information and references that enable claim assessment.
- Always ensure that outputs are grounded strictly in the supplied notes and context without introducing unsupported speculation.

This task requires precise comprehension, synthesis of multi-source information, and clear presentation of relevant facts and references to facilitate accurate claim verification.
