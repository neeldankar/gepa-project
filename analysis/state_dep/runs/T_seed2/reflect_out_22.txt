Task Description:

You will be given a factual claim involving one or more named entities such as people, organizations, events, works, films, places, or concepts. The claim may include details like dates, nationalities, roles, relationships, career milestones, affiliations, or temporal sequences.

Your objective is to analyze the claim thoroughly and to produce two essential outputs:

1. **new_notes**: A refined, minimal, and highly relevant subset of the provided `notes` that either directly supports, contradicts, or crucially contextualizes the claim.

2. **titles**: A concise list of document titles or identifiers from the supplied `context` which are authoritative sources underpinning the selected evidence and directly verifying or illuminating the claim’s key elements.

---

Detailed Reasoning and Analysis Guidelines:

- **Entity and Claim Decomposition**  
  Identify key components of the claim including:  
  - Named entities (persons, organizations, works, places)  
  - Roles or titles claimed  
  - Dates or chronological information  
  - Relationships or affiliations  
  - Specific events or career milestones  

- **Cross-Referencing Evidence**  
  Use `notes` and `context` to:  
  - Verify presence or absence of facts matching claim specifics  
  - Detect contradictions, weak confirmations, or missing evidence  
  - Differentiate between direct support and peripheral mentions  
  - Assess nuances such as nationality, gender, exact role names, or hierarchical place relationships  
  - Confirm timing and sequence details precisely, avoiding assumptions unsupported by evidence

- **Domain-Specific Attention**  
  Pay special attention to domain-specific factual elements depending on claim type:  
  - **Political Claims**: Political parties, leadership contests, affiliations, timelines of offices held  
  - **Biographical Details**: Birthdates, debut events, career spans, correct person identity, nationality, gender  
  - **Geographical Claims**: Precise containment and hierarchical relations (e.g., cemetery inside park inside a national recreation area)  
  - **Film & Media**: Correct matching of actor names to character names and media titles, verified release years, sequence of media appearances  
  - **Events & Timing**: Accurately confirm the earliest or precise dates; beware of retrospective assumptions  
   
- **Evidence Handling**  
  - Select only a minimal but value-rich subset of `notes` that either support or contradict the claim or provide critical context  
  - Remove duplicates and near duplicates to avoid redundancy  
  - Exclude tangential, general knowledge or unrelated facts  
  - Preserve exact factual details that clearly pertain to the claim’s components  
  - Avoid adding information not directly supported by `notes` or `context`  

- **Document Title Selection**  
  - Include only those documents from `context` that contain authoritative or direct evidence for the claim’s key facts  
  - Prioritize core entity biographies, official event pages, named works or productions explicitly referenced by the claim or `new_notes`  
  - Avoid guessing or adding documents not present in `context` even if logically relevant  
  - Aim to recover all known gold-standard or essential documents supporting the claim  

---

Output Formatting:

- **new_notes**: A clean list (array) of selected fact snippets from `notes` refined as above.  
- **titles**: A list (array) of context document titles that comprise authoritative sources underlying the selected evidence.

---

Summary:

Your response must precisely verify or contextualize the claim by delivering:  
- A factually accurate, minimal, and non-redundant set of evidence snippets (`new_notes`) that clearly support or contradict the claim.  
- A carefully chosen set of source document titles (`titles`) from the provided context uniquely relevant to those evidence snippets.

Maintain strict factual accuracy, domain and claim-specific nuance recognition, and avoid unsupported inferences or data. This approach ensures concise, high-quality verification aligned to the claim’s distinct factual demands.

---

This task requires blending entity recognition, fact cross-validation, domain-aware reasoning, evidence pruning, and source prioritization to produce precise, minimal yet sufficient verification output sets.
