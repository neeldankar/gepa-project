- `context`: A list of documents or document extracts giving more extensive background knowledge related to entities and topics mentioned in the claim.

Perform the following:

1. Reason about the claim by cross-referencing the information in `notes` and `context`, identifying the key entities, events, dates, and relationships relevant to the claim. Analyze whether the claim is supported, contradicted, or cannot be verified with the available information.

2. Generate `new_notes` by:
   - Selecting and consolidating a minimal, high-value subset of fact snippets from the original `notes` that directly support or contextualize the claim.
   - Removing duplicates or near-duplicates.
   - Including important factual details such as dates, identities, affiliations, or event specifics that pertain clearly to the claim.
   - Omitting peripheral or unrelated information.

3. Generate `titles` by:
   - Extracting the titles or identifiers of the documents from the `context` that best correspond to the chosen `new_notes`.
   - These document titles should represent the key sources that provide authoritative or direct support for the claim or its key elements.
   - Aim to retrieve all gold-standard documents relevant to the claim, prioritizing exact matches to major entities/events (e.g., person names, political parties, movies, cemeteries).

Additional domain-specific details and best practices to incorporate:

- Political leadership claims should reference documents on politicians, political parties, leadership elections, and their affiliations.

- For film or media-related claims involving actors, roles, and release dates, emphasize correct association between actor names, character names, and movie titles with verified release years.
- When claim components mention the timing or sequence of events (e.g., "released before"), use the earliest accurate release dates or historical facts from the context.
- Handle duplication and redundant notes carefully to produce a concise and precise `new_notes`.
- If some expected documents are missing from the retrieved titles (based on gold documents or claim relevance), strive to include them when possible.
- Clearly capture factual precision rather than assumptions when context is insufficient.

In summary, the task is to produce an informed, consolidated evidence subset (`new_notes`) and a precise selection of document titles (`titles`) that collectively verify or contextualize the given claim, leveraging both snippet-level notes and broader document context with domain-aware reasoning. The output should be succinct, accurate, and focused on the claim-specific factual support.
