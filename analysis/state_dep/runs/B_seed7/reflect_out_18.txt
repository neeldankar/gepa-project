Task Overview:

You will be given multiple inputs related to verifying and refining a factual claim. The claim pertains to topics involving people, places, institutions, films, songs, or other entertainment or academic subjects. Your goal is to evaluate the factual accuracy of the claim and its accompanying notes, using the provided authoritative context, and then produce a high-quality, factually precise summary (new_notes) plus a list of exact titles (titles) from the context that fully and authoritatively support the verified facts.

Input Components:

1. claim: A statement that may contain multiple factual subclaims or assertions about entities such as actors, films, TV shows, institutions, or other cultural or academic subjects.

2. notes: A list of extracted statements related to the claim. These may include duplicates, partial truths, inaccuracies, different details, or redundancies.

3. context: A set of encyclopedic paragraphs or document snippets with authoritative, well-structured background information about the entities or topics found in the claim and notes. This authoritative material is the primary source for validating or correcting claim subclaims.

Expected Outputs:

- new_notes: A synthesized, cleaned, factually accurate, and concise list of statements that:
   * Directly support or refine the claim’s assertions.
   * Integrate relevant information from both notes and context.
   * Remove redundancies, outdated or inaccurate facts.
   * Correct any factual errors found in the claim or notes based on the context.
   * Emphasize distinct and verified facts pertinent to the claim.
   * Explicitly identify and rectify any incorrect subclaims.
   * Avoid extraneous or unrelated information.
   * Are factually precise, standalone, and suitable for independent verification.

- titles: A list of exact document or entity titles from the context that provide authoritative support or essential background for the consolidated new_notes. Requirements for titles:
   * Mirror the exact titles or entity names present in the input context.
   * Fully cover all verified elements of the claim.
   * Exclude any unrelated or unsubstantiated titles.
   * Reflect what would be considered gold standard references for claim validation.

Detailed Task Instructions and Domain-Specific Considerations:

1. Decompose the claim into discrete factual subclaims. Examples include:
   - Identification of an actor with a specific role or nomination.
   - Accurate dates for premieres, releases, or appointments.
   - Correct institutional type, founding dates, or renaming histories.
   - Precise geographic or taxonomic distinctions (e.g., specific islands where a plant is native).
   - Proper attribution of roles (lead actor, director, character names).

2. Cross-check each subclaim thoroughly against:
   - The notes: Identify relevant supporting, conflicting, or redundant statements.
   - The authoritative context: Confirm true facts, detect inaccuracies, and clarify ambiguities. In cases of conflicts, prioritize the richer, more authoritative context material.

3. Be vigilant about common pitfalls and niche issues such as:
   - Mistaken nationality or origin of persons.
   - Incorrect or multiple premiere or release dates (use authoritative date).
   - Misattribution of awards, nominations, or role credits.
   - Conflating unrelated individuals or roles.
   - Confusing similarly titled works, sequels, or adaptations.
   - Clarifying character roles, e.g., supporting vs. lead, or character aliases.
   - Institutional classifications (private vs. public universities, renaming events).
   - Correct handling of scientific names or geographic distributions for biology-related claims.

4. When notes or claim parts are inaccurate or unverifiable, explicitly correct or flag these errors in the new_notes by restating the accurate facts or noting the lack of corroboration.

5. Consolidate synonymous or duplicated notes into a single precise fact.

6. Avoid speculation or assumption beyond the provided evidence.

7. Maintain clarity, brevity, and logical flow in new_notes.

8. Titles should be precise and comprehensive without over-inclusion; each title should correspond to an authoritative reference that substantiates at least one key aspect of the refined notes.

General Strategy to Apply:

- Carefully parse the claim into subclaims.
- Align each subclaim with all notes that discuss it.
- Use the context to confirm, reject, or modify these subclaims.
- Formulate a distilled set of new_notes reflecting only the verified, corrected facts.
- Collect all context titles required to support these validated notes.
- Be explicit about corrections to the claim or notes, clarifying any errors found.
- Remove any redundant or irrelevant information.

Formatting:

- Output exactly two fields: new_notes and titles.
- Both must be lists of strings.
- Each element of new_notes must be a concise, self-contained, factually accurate statement.
- Each title must match exactly an entry from the given context.
- Avoid empty or unsupported outputs unless no claim facts can be verified.

By rigorously applying this methodology, you will generate a factually sound, concise summary and a precise reference list for claims drawn from complex, multi-source information involving entertainment, academic, or geographic topics.
