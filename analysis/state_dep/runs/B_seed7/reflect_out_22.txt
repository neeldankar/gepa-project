Task Description:

You will be provided with three types of inputs relating to a factual claim about people, places, institutions, films, songs, or other entertainment or academic topics:

1. `claim`: A statement containing one or more factual subclaims or assertions about entities such as actors, institutions, species, films, or geographic places.
2. `notes`: A list of extracted sentences or statements related to the claim. These may be partially overlapping, redundant, contain minor inaccuracies, or vary in detail.
3. `context`: A list of encyclopedic-style paragraphs or document snippets that serve as authoritative sources about the entities or topics mentioned in the claim and notes, furnishing evidence for verification and refinement.

Your goal is to produce two outputs:
- `new_notes`: A cleaned, concise list of factually accurate, distinct notes that:
  - Directly support, refine, clarify, or correct the factual elements of the claim.
  - Integrate information from both the `notes` and the authoritative `context`.
  - Remove redundancies, contradictions, outdated facts, or unsupported assertions.
  - Correct any factual errors or imprecise phrasing found in the original claim or notes using the context.
  - Emphasize verified, distinct facts relevant to the claim.
  - Explicitly identify and rectify inaccuracies in the claim’s subclaims when they cannot be substantiated.
  - Summarize evidence clearly and precisely, excluding irrelevant or speculative content.
  - Present each new note as a standalone, verifiable factual statement.

- `titles`: A list of exact titles or entity names from the provided `context` that:
  - Fully substantiate or provide essential background for all validated aspects of the claim and `new_notes`.
  - Reflect gold standard authoritative references expected for thorough fact-checking.
  - Match exactly (including spelling and formatting) the corresponding entries or document titles in the `context`.
  - Exclude unrelated or unsubstantiated sources.

Detailed Guidelines and Domain-Specific Considerations:

- Decompose the `claim` into discrete factual subclaims for granular verification.
- Cross-verify each subclaim with both `notes` and richer `context` sources.
- Give priority to authoritative context over conflicting or incomplete notes.
- Correct specifics with precision:
  - Use exact names and spellings for persons, institutions, species, films, songs, geographic locations, and other entities.
  - Validate dates, such as film release years, institutional founding or renaming years, or career tenure.
  - Verify correct attribution of roles, credits, positions, and titles (e.g., lead actor, chairman, species native range).
  - Distinguish geographic specificity, e.g., island-specific plant species or regional variances in native habitats.
  - Avoid unsupported assumptions or conflation of unrelated entities (e.g., different actors with similar names).
- In entertainment domain:
  - Confirm exact character names, lead actors, and roles.
  - Confirm film genre categories based on authoritative context.
  - Clarify if a character or actor is from a live-action versus animated film and note variations accordingly.
  - If details about a subclaim cannot be verified from the `context`, note explicitly that it is unsubstantiated.
- In academic or biological domain:
  - Confirm taxonomic classifications, habitat ranges, and native areas precisely.
  - Verify distinctions among species or subspecies distributions.
- When multiple contexts appear contradictory (e.g., differing premiere years), rely on the authoritative context documents for final validation.
- Eliminate all redundant, duplicate, or slightly varying duplicate notes—only distinct facts should remain.
- If a claim is incomplete, ambiguous, or truncated, clarify the extent to which it can be verified and explicitly note missing or unsupported information.
- Reflect the original meaning faithfully but correct errors without unjustified reinterpretation.

General Reasoning Strategy:

- Break the claim into precise subclaims and fact-check each against both notes and authoritative context.
- Identify and remove unsupported or erroneous assertions.
- Synthesize and consolidate information into a definitive, non-redundant, and accurate factual summary in `new_notes`.
- Select all relevant authoritative titles from the context that cover the validated facts comprehensively.
- Document explicitly any subclaim corrections or unverifiable aspects.
- Maintain factual accuracy, clarity, and conciseness throughout.

Formatting Requirements:

- Output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.
- Both must be lists (arrays) of strings.
- Each element in `new_notes` must be a clear, standalone factual statement suitable for independent verification.
- Each title in `titles` must exactly match a context title or entity name.
- Avoid speculation, irrelevance, or unsupported content.

By following these instructions, you will transform noisy, partial, or inaccurate initial notes into a precise, authoritative factual summary that validates or corrects the original claim, together with an exact list of supporting references. This approach ensures clearly verified and fully referenced factual outputs suitable for trusted knowledge use.
