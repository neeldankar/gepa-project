Given the input fields:

- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion that often involves multiple entities, events, or attributes and their relationships.
- `notes`: A list of factoids, document snippets, or knowledge points relevant to verifying or investigating parts of the claim.

Your task is to produce the field:

- `query`: A carefully constructed set of one or more fact-seeking questions designed to retrieve the key information needed to verify the claim or to explore the claim’s components thoroughly.

Instructions and guidance:

1. **Purpose of the query**: The query should seek to extract all critical information necessary to support or refute the claim by retrieving the relevant documents or evidence. It should include all relevant sub-questions implied by the claim’s components.

2. **Domain specificity**: Claims often involve specific entities such as musical bands/albums, operas and their composers, films, actors, directors, roles, and release/formation dates.  
   - For music-related claims, query about band formation dates, album release information, and identifying bands/artists responsible for particular albums.

   - For film-related claims, query about film cast lists, relationships between actors and films, directors, and any connecting facts between the named persons and works.

3. **Multiple components**: If the claim compares or connects multiple entities or events (e.g., two operas and their composers, two films and their casts/directors), queries should reflect this by including multiple distinct but related questions, ensuring coverage of all aspects.

4. **Clarity and detail**: The query should be explicit about what is being asked, including entity names, works (albums, operas, films), roles, dates, and relationships to be clarified or confirmed.

5. **Utilizing the notes**: Use the notes to observe what factual details are relevant or missing. Formulate queries aimed at retrieving the gold documents implied by or referenced in the notes to verify all relevant parts of the claim.

6. **General strategy**:  
   - Analyze the claim to identify all entities, events, and claims of relationships or dates.  
   - Parse these components to form distinct factual questions.  
   - Combine these into a concise but complete query that aims to retrieve all necessary facts in one or more questions.

_"Sunrise Avenue and the band who released the album ‘You Could Have It So Much Better’ were both formed in 2002,"_

"Who released the album ‘You Could Have It So Much Better’? When was that band formed? When was Sunrise Avenue formed?"

- Produce the `query` to reflect the structure noted above.
