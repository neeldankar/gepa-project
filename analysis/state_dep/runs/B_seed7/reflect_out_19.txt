Task Description:

You will be given three inputs related to a factual claim about entities such as people, places, institutions, films, songs, or other entertainment or academic domains:

1. `claim`: A potentially complex factual statement that may involve multiple factual subclaims or assertions about characters, dates, events, attributions, or relationships.
2. `notes`: A collection of extracted sentences related to the claim. These may be redundant, overlapping, partially inaccurate, out-of-date, or varying in detail or specificity.
3. `context`: A set of authoritative, encyclopedic-style documents or paragraphs grounded in reliable, well-sourced background information corresponding to entities or topics in the claim and notes. These are the primary evidentiary basis for verification or correction.

Your goal is to produce two outputs:

- `new_notes`: A cleaned, concise, and strictly factual set of notes that:

  - Incorporate verified information from both the supplied `notes` and the authoritative `context`.
  - Remove any redundancy, speculation, or inaccuracies found in the input notes.
  - Correct factual errors or misleading implications in the original claim or notes based on contextual evidence.
  - Break down compound claims into discrete verified facts, highlighting each supported subclaim.
  - Explicitly identify and correct any false or unverifiable subclaims otherwise stated in the claim.
  - Emphasize domain-specific precision such as correct spelling of names, accurate dates, correct attribution of roles or titles, proper distinctions between related but separate entities, and geographically accurate statements.
  - Avoid incorporating unsupported assumptions, vague generalizations, or unverified links.
  - Ensure each note is clear, standalone, factually accurate, and relevant to the core assertions.

- `titles`: A list of exact names of context documents or entities that:

  - Fully cover and substantiate the verified elements of the claim confirmed in `new_notes`.
  - Use exact titles as they appear in the provided context.
  - Include all necessary references crucial to validate each factual element.
  - Exclude unrelated or unsupported context entries.

General Approach and Domain-Specific Considerations:

- Decompose the claim into granular factual subclaims (e.g., correct year of event, exact role of a person, relationships between entities, specific versions or editions of media).
- Cross-validate each subclaim against both the `notes` and the richer `context` to detect and resolve discrepancies.
- When there is conflicting or missing information, prioritize facts supported by authoritative context over the original notes.
- Pay special attention to domain-specific knowledge such as:

  - Entertainment: Accurate film titles, release years, voice actor credits, character origins, version distinctions, and franchise chronology.
  - Academic/Institutional: Correct founding years, renaming histories, public vs. private status, leadership tenures.
  - Biological/Geographical: Accurate species or subspecies distribution, island-specific flora or fauna, geographic differentiations.
  - Cultural references: Exact names, spelling, and historical timelines for songs, shows, and advertisements.

- Highlight when a subclaim is unsupported by evidence or contradicted by authoritative sources.
- Integrate overlapping or redundant notes into one clear, unambiguous fact.
- Avoid speculation or extraneous information unrelated to the claim’s core content.
- Preserve the original intent of the claim where accurate, but transparently flag and correct any errors or unverifiable assertions.

Formatting Requirements:

- Output must be two lists named exactly `new_notes` and `titles`.
- Both must be lists of strings.
- Each entry in `new_notes` should be a independently verifiable, concise fact.
- Entries in `titles` must exactly match the corresponding context document or entity names.
- All outputs must be clearly factual, succinct, and focused solely on the claim’s validation and context.
- Do not include speculative, unrelated, or unsupported data points.

By following these instructions, you will provide a well-reasoned, domain-aware, coherent, and factually grounded refinement of the claim notes, together with precise citations that reflect gold standard verification sources.
