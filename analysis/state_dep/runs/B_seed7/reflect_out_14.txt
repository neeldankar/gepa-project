Task Description:

You will be provided with three types of input fields related to a factual claim about people, places, institutions, films, songs, or other entertainment or academic topics:

1. `claim`: A factual statement that may include multiple subclaims or assertions.
2. `notes`: A list of extracted sentences or statements relating to the claim. These may be overlapping, redundant, contain minor inaccuracies, or offer different levels of detail.
3. `context`: A list of encyclopedic-style paragraphs or document snippets providing authoritative background information pertaining to entities or topics mentioned in the claim and notes. These provide the evidence needed to verify, correct, or refine the claim and notes.

Your goal is to produce two outputs:
- `new_notes`: A cleaned, concise, and factually accurate subset of notes that directly support, refine, correct, or clarify the claim. This requires:
  - Integrating information from both `notes` and authoritative `context`.
  - Removing redundancies, inaccuracies, or outdated facts found in the original notes.
  - Correcting any factual errors detected in the claim or notes based on the context.
  - Emphasizing distinct, verified facts relevant to the core assertions of the claim.
  - Explicitly identifying and rectifying any inaccuracies or errors in the claim's subclaims.
  - Summarizing evidence precisely and clearly, avoiding superfluous or unrelated content.

- `titles`: A list containing exact titles or names of the documents or entities from the provided context that substantiate or provide essential background for the claim and your chosen new_notes. The list should be:
  - Precise in naming, mirroring titles or entity names in the context exactly.
  - Comprehensive enough to cover all validated elements of the claim.
  - Reflective of gold standard references expected for thorough claim validation.
  - Excluding any unrelated or unsubstantiated titles.

General Strategy:

- Break down the claim into discrete factual subclaims for granular verification.
- Cross-check each subclaim with both the extracted notes and the richer, authoritative contextual documents.
- Identify inconsistencies, outdated information, or outright factual errors; then correct or clarify them as needed.
- Consolidate supported facts into a definitive, non-redundant `new_notes` list.
- Select all relevant authoritative titles that cover the core validated facts comprehensively.
- Be mindful of domain-specific nuances such as:
  - Correct names and precise spelling of persons, institutions, films, etc.
  - Accurate dates (release years, founding or renaming years, role tenures).
  - Proper attribution of roles, credits, or positions (e.g., first chairmen, starring roles, lead actors).
  - Institutional types (public/private universities), relationships, or renaming history.
  - Geographic distinctions when plants, locations, or entities are confined to specific islands or regions.
  - Correct identification of species subspecies or variant distributions.
  - Avoid assuming unsubstantiated connections or conflating unrelated individuals or entities.

Additional Domain-Specific Considerations:

- Claims often involve entertainment industry elements (films, TV shows, actors), academic institutions, plants, and geographic entities. Verifying credits, timelines, affiliations, and terminologies is critical.
- If multiple sources conflict (e.g., different documented premiere years or place names), prioritize authoritative context.
- Notes may contain duplicates or slightly varying statements; synthesize carefully to produce a precise factual summary.
- Clarify when evidence is lacking to substantiate a claim fully; avoid unsupported assumptions.
- Explicitly note when a subclaim in the original claim is incorrect or cannot be verified from the evidence.
- Where possible, interpret and reflect the claim’s meaning faithfully but correct errors without altering the intended meaning unjustifiably.

Formatting Requirements:

- Output fields must be named exactly `new_notes` and `titles`.
- Both `new_notes` and `titles` should be lists (array format) of strings.
- Each new_note should be a concise, standalone, factually accurate statement suitable for independent validation.
- Titles should exactly match context entries and be concise entity/document names.
- Maintain clarity, factual accuracy, and conciseness in all outputs.
- Avoid including irrelevant, speculative, or unsupported notes or titles.

By following these instructions, the assistant should deliver a refined, accurate, non-redundant factual summary of the claim and its evidence base, along with precise references ensuring easy verification.
