Task Description:

You will be provided with three fields as input: 
1. `claim` - a factual statement that may contain multiple subclaims or assertions. 
2. `notes` - a list of extracted sentences or statements related to the claim, possibly overlapping or redundant and at varying levels of detail or correctness.
3. `context` - a list of encyclopedic-style paragraphs or document snippets relevant to entities or topics mentioned in the claim and notes. These contain detailed background information and help verify or refute subclaims in the claim.

Your output should contain two fields:
- `new_notes`: a cleaned, concise, and factual subset list of notes that directly support, refine, correct, or clarify the original claim. This should:
  - Include information drawn from both the `notes` and `context`.
  - Remove redundancies and outdated or inaccurate information found among the notes.
  - Incorporate corrections or clarifications based on authoritative contextual information.
  - Explicitly identify and rectify any factual errors or inaccuracies in the claim based on the evidence.
  - Focus on distinct, verified facts that are relevant to the claim’s core assertions.

- `titles`: a list of the names or titles of relevant documents or entities from the context that substantiate or provide essential background for the claim and your chosen notes. These should be:
  - Specific and correctly formatted, usually matching the titles or entities as referenced in the context.
  - Comprehensive enough to cover all crucial elements of the claim.
  - Reflective of gold standard references typically expected for complete claim validation.

General strategy to solve task:
- Break down the claim into constituent factual subclaims.
- Cross-check each subclaim with both the provided notes and the richer contextual documents.
- Identify inconsistencies, correct erroneous details (e.g., date errors or misattributed roles), and confirm facts that are fully supported.
- Aggregate the validated facts into new_notes, summarizing the relevant evidence.
- Select titles of the most relevant, authoritative documents from the context that cover the validated evidence.
- Avoid including irrelevant or unsupported notes or titles.
- Your response should be formatted cleanly and be concise while comprehensive, reflecting critical thinking on the veracity and completeness of the claim.

Domain-specific considerations:
- Claims often involve people, films, songs, or other entertainment industry entities; verifying authorship, starring roles, production details, and timelines is crucial.
- Precise name spellings, dates, and relationships (e.g., familial, professional) are often critical for verification.
- Claims about membership in bands or contributions to songs require identifying accurate credits and affiliations.
- When multiple sources conflict on details (such as premiere years), preference should be given to authoritative contextual evidence.
- Notes may contain duplicates, minor variations, and explicit corrections; synthesize these carefully.
- Titles should reflect actual documents or knowledge base entries referenced in the context, not invented or generalized terms.

Formatting:
- Use the exact field names `new_notes` and `titles` for outputs.
- Provide the output as lists (array format), with each item a string concise enough to be a standalone fact or title.
- Ensure factual accuracy and clarity in all provided notes and titles.
