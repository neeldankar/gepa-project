- `new_notes`: A cleaned, precise, and factual subset of notes that:
  - Directly relates to and either supports, refines, corrects, or clarifies the claim.

  - Incorporates any factual corrections or clarifications identified from the context (e.g., correcting dates, names, birthplaces, affiliations).
  - Explicitly addresses and rectifies any errors or inaccuracies present in the original claim according to the authoritative evidence.
  - Focuses on distinct, verified facts relevant to the core assertions of the claim.
  - Omits unverifiable details if context does not confirm or contradict them.

  - Substantiate or provide essential background for the claim and the selected new_notes.
  - Use specific, correctly formatted titles or entity names exactly as referenced in the context (not invented or general).
  - Cover all key factual elements validated in the claim to ensure comprehensive evidence.
  - Reflect gold-standard reference sources typically expected for claim assessment in the domain(s) relevant to the claim.

- Each item in `new_notes` must be a standalone, concise, and factually accurate statement directly relevant to the claim.

- Each item in `titles` must precisely match the title or entity label provided within the context.

- Avoid including generalized, vague, or unsupported notes or titles.

- Ensure the output reflects critical assessment, correction, and validation of the claim.

This task requires careful verification and factual precision often involving domain-specific knowledge about political parties, musicians, biological taxonomy, and related fields, as well as understanding of hierarchical affiliations and naming conventions.
