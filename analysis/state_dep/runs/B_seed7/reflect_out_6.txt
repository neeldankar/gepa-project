You are given three fields as input:
- `claim`: a factual statement or assertion that needs to be evaluated or explained,
- `notes`: a list of extracted evidential points, supporting facts, observations, or contradictions relevant to the claim,
- `context`: a list of related reference documents or document summaries that contain background information, facts, or data relevant to the claim.

Your task is to produce two output fields:
1. `new_notes`: a concise, focused summary or refined subset of the `notes` that directly address the truthfulness, accuracy, or validity of the claim based on the context, removing redundant or irrelevant information and emphasizing key evidential points.
2. `titles`: a list of the most relevant document or entity titles from the `context` that best support or clarify the evaluation of the claim. These should include key names, entities, events, or locations crucial to understanding or verifying the claim.

---

Guidelines and domain-specific details:

- The claim involves domain-specific factual assertions, such as historical details, sports statistics, music bands’ origins, or similar specialized knowledge. Understanding these domain-specific entities and relations is critical for accurate evaluation.
- The `context` contains detailed domain-specific descriptions (e.g., biographies, event summaries, historical accounts, or series seasons) and may have overlapping or nested information about entities referenced in the claim.
- The `notes` usually contain many observations, some redundant or repetitive. Your role is to distill these to the key points that are directly relevant to verifying or refuting the claim.
- Confirm or refute the claim by cross-referencing the `notes` and `context`. Indicate if the claim is supported, contradicted, or unverified based on evidence.
- When selecting `titles`, prioritize those that:
  - Are explicitly mentioned or described in the `context`.
  - Represent primary entities or concepts central to the claim.
  - Would serve as useful references for further fact-checking or exploring the claim.
- If the claim involves persons, places, events, organizations, or entities, include titles that reflect these accurately.
- Ensure the reasoning behind the claim’s evaluation is clearly reflected in the selection of `new_notes` and `titles`.
- Avoid including irrelevant, redundant, or ambiguous information in `new_notes` or unrelated titles in `titles`.
- The goal is to facilitate efficient verification or refutation of the claim using a curated and relevant set of notes and references.

---

In sum, your output should deliver a focused overview of the evidence from the original notes aligned with authoritative context entries, succinctly supporting or challenging the claim, alongside a precise selection of document or entity titles that are vital to understanding or validating the claim.
