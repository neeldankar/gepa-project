Task Description:
You will be given two fields: `claim` and `notes`. Your goal is to produce a `query` that can be used to retrieve relevant documents or facts needed to verify or assess the `claim`. The `notes` provide supporting facts and contextual information related to the claim. 

Detailed Guidelines:

1. Understand the Claim:
   - Analyze the claim to identify all named entities (people, places, institutions, events, dates, etc.).
   - Determine the core assertions or relations in the claim that require fact-checking.
   - Break down complex claims into their key components, especially when multiple facts or comparisons are involved.

2. Use the Notes to Ground the Query:
   - The `notes` field includes partial information, such as biographical details, establishment dates, event locations, and associations.
   - Identify from the notes which entities and facts appear repeatedly or are emphasized.
   - Use information in notes to clarify ambiguous references in the claim (e.g., linking a book to its author).

3. Constructing the Query:
   - The query should explicitly ask about all core facts necessary to confirm or refute the entire claim.
   - When the claim compares two or more entities (e.g., their hobbies, establishment dates, or event venues), the query should ask about these entities and the specific comparisons or relationships.
   - Queries should be formulated as natural language questions that cover all elements needed for verification.
   - Include relevant disambiguations or clarifications gleaned from the notes (e.g., full names, titles, locations).

4. Key Domain-Specific Elements:
   - Biographical facts: authorship, hobbies, training institutions, etc.
   - Temporal facts: establishment/founding dates of institutions.
   - Locational facts: stadium locations, club bases, event venues.
   - Relationships between entities: whether an author wrote a certain book, whether a sports team is based somewhere, etc.

5. Purpose and Utility:
   - The query should be optimized to retrieve documents that collectively cover all pieces of the claim.
   - Avoid overly broad queries that fetch unrelated data; focus on key elements.
   - The generated query facilitates gathering the exact supporting or contradictory information needed for claim verification.

Summary:
Given a claim and a set of notes containing partial factual snippets, your task is to produce one or more comprehensive natural language question(s) as a `query` that will retrieve authoritative documents or information sources necessary to verify the claim. The query should reflect careful parsing of the claim's components, the relevant entities involved, their relationships, and temporal or locational specifics revealed or hinted by the notes.
