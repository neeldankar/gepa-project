Task Description:  
You will be given three distinct inputs:  
- `claim`: A statement or assertion typically involving relationships, memberships, attributes, or comparisons related to specific entities. Entities may include niche domain-specific subjects such as geographic locations (counties, colleges), sports entities (players, teams, seasons), musical acts (bands, albums, members), movies (films, characters, production details), and institutions. Claims often require factual verification or clarification of relationships like membership, roles, founding details, sponsorship, or discography counts.  

- `notes`: A list of candidate sentences extracted from various sources that may partially or fully support, refute, or relate to the claim. These notes can contain overlapping or redundant information and may vary in clarity and accuracy. They often provide concise factual data points or excerpts useful to validate the claim.

- `context`: A larger set of document descriptions or entity summaries providing comprehensive background information about the key entities mentioned in the claim and notes. These documents are usually well-structured, detailed, and reflect authoritative content such as encyclopedia entries or well-curated metadata. Examples: full bios of sports teams or players, histories of leagues, band discographies, or institutional overviews, including dates, memberships, sponsorship, and performance statistics.  

Your task is to synthesize a clear, concise, and domain-accurate response shaped around the claim, by performing the following:

   - Remove repetitions, contradictions, or ambiguous notes.  
   - When necessary, paraphrase or combine note sentences to improve clarity and precision, always preserving factual accuracy.  
   - Include notes that explicitly confirm or contradict key aspects of the claim (such as memberships, founding dates, managerial roles, sponsorships, album counts, etc.).  
   - Where information about an entity or detail is missing or explicitly stated as unavailable, include notes reflecting that fact to address potential knowledge gaps.  

2. **Select and produce `titles`:**
