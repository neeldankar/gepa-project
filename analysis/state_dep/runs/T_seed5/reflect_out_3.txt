You will be given three fields as input: `claim` (a statement involving entities, events, or relationships), `notes` (a list of factual assertions or evidence sentences relevant to the claim), and `context` (a list of background informational snippets or descriptions, often about people, places, organizations, or works).

Your task is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

1. `new_notes`: Generate a concise, coherent set of factual notes derived from the input notes and context that collectively support a clear, factually accurate evaluation or analysis of the claim. These notes should:
   - Include information that confirms or refutes parts of the claim based on the evidence.
   - Correct any factual inaccuracies in the claim.
   - Focus on relevant factual details specific to the entities, events, or relationships mentioned.
   - Avoid redundancy; consolidate similar facts into unified statements.
   - Capture critical domain-specific details such as:
       * Accurate identities and roles of persons (e.g., actor vs. director, professional titles).
       * Correct names, years, and relationships between films, locations, organizations.
       * Geopolitical or jurisdictional clarifications (e.g., burial site and park boundaries, state vs. country, entity affiliations).
       * Any disputed or ambiguous status with clarifying context.
   - Use exact or close paraphrases from the notes and context but ensure factual accuracy and clarity.

2. `titles`: Provide a list of relevant document or entity titles (e.g., article titles, page names) that correspond to key pieces of evidence drawn upon in `new_notes`. These should:
   - Include all entities and locations explicitly relevant to the claim and the factual assessment.
   - Cover specific films, people, cemeteries, parks, companies, airports, or legal statuses as appropriate.
   - Generally correspond to documents referenced either directly or indirectly in the notes and context that support your factual breakdown.

Additional guidance:

- Carefully cross-reference the claim with the provided notes and context to verify each part.
- Identify and correct any mistaken attributions in the claim (e.g., mistaking an actor for a director, incorrect burial location or identity).
- When multiple entities share similar or overlapping names, disambiguate them clearly in your notes and titles.
- When relevant, mention any historical disputes, clarifications of jurisdiction, or nuanced statuses affecting the claim.
- Aim to produce a logically consistent, internally coherent factual summary that could aid a fact-checker or knowledge base.

This task requires domain-aware factual reasoning and careful synthesis of partially overlapping or contradictory data points to form a precise, truth-aligned set of notes and document references.
