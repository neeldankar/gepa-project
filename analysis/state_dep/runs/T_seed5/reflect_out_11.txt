Task Description:

You will be given three inputs: 
- `claim`: a statement or assertion typically linking entities, events, or attributes that needs to be verified or contextualized.
- `notes`: a list of relevant facts, snippets, observations, or evidential extracts related to the claim and its domain, often including both supporting and contradictory information.
- `context`: a list of documents or summaries (titles and brief descriptions) providing background knowledge and domain-specific details relevant to the entities or events referenced within the claim.

Your task is to analyze and synthesize these inputs, then produce two outputs:

1. `new_notes`:
   - A concise and focused subset selection of the original `notes` that directly contribute to verifying, disproving, or clarifying the claim.
   - These selected notes should present the key factual points, contradictions, clarifications, or relevant contextual evidence essential to understanding the claim's validity or its semantic meaning.
   - Avoid redundancy; only include notes that add unique and relevant information toward supporting the reasoning.

2. `titles`:
   - A list of the most relevant document titles from the `context` that best support the reasoning behind the claim evaluation.
   - These titles generally correspond to authoritative, central, or highly pertinent topics, entities, persons, or events that ground the response.
   - Provide titles that collectively cover the main factual or conceptual dimensions required to assess the claim, reflecting a comprehensive retrieval of relevant background.

Detailed domain knowledge to incorporate (based on examples):

- Recognize domain-specific entities such as:
  - Football/soccer teams, players, tournaments, and matches (e.g., FIFA Confederations Cup Final, Brazilian players like Neymar and Fred, club teams).
  - World Rally Championship (WRC) seasons, driver nationalities, championship winners and their participation records, and year-season numbering mappings.
  - Film industry knowledge including movie titles, sequels, actor nationalities, filmographies, and ensemble casts.

- Understand temporal alignment and event sequencing:
  - Match season numbers to specific years (e.g., WRC 28th season corresponds to year 2000).
  - Identify which players or actors participated in which events or movies and the associated time frames.

- Discriminate between correct and incorrect or fabricated information within claims, clarifying misattributions (e.g., no "Nathaniel 'Nate' Jacks" football team or player, actor nationality mismatch).

- Leveraging external context documents to validate or refute claims, ensuring information aligns with official and authoritative records or commonly accepted facts.

- Strategy:
  - Carefully cross-reference the claim against the `notes` and `context`.
  - Extract from `notes` only those statements that decisively confirm or negate the claim or provide essential contextual insight.
  - Identify the most salient context documents whose titles are directly relevant and supportive of the key facts in `new_notes`.
  - Make logical inferences explicit in your reasoning for claim verification.

Formatting requirements:

- Provide the outputs in two fields: `new_notes` as a list of strings, and `titles` as a list of strings.
- Preserve factual accuracy and specificity.
- Ensure the selected notes and titles comprehensively address the claim’s claim and are rooted in domain-appropriate knowledge.

By following these instructions, your output will assist in fact-checking, contextualizing, or verifying knowledge claims across diverse structured domains, leveraging precise evidence extraction and document relevance identification.
