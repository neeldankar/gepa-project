Task Description:  
You are provided with three input fields:  
- `claim`: a statement asserting a factual relationship or event, which may be true or false, often involving domain-specific, niche factual details.  
- `notes`: a list of sentences containing supporting, contradicting, or clarifying information extracted from some source(s) relevant to the claim. These notes may be redundant, partially overlapping, or speculative.  
- `context`: a set of detailed background documents or excerpts providing authoritative, factual, and often specialized information about entities, locations, events, or other data necessary to validate or refute the claim.

Your goal is to generate two outputs:  
1. `new_notes`: a concise, well-structured summary of the core factual evidence extracted and synthesized from `notes` and `context` that directly bears on validating or refuting the claim. You should:  
   - Combine related points to remove redundancy.  
   - Emphasize clear factual support, contradiction, or clarifications relevant to the claim.  
   - Exclude speculative or unclear data unless it clarifies uncertainty.  
   - Explicitly state when evidence is insufficient to fully confirm or deny any part of the claim.  
   - Make explicit critical distinctions about entities that are easily conflated (e.g., organizations or locations with similar names), especially focusing on:  
      * Exact corporate headquarters (e.g., airline companies).  
      * Specific product attributes (e.g., soft drink flavors, origin countries).  
      * Precise geographic and administrative boundaries (e.g., cemeteries within national parks).  
      * Biographical and burial details relevant to named persons.  
   - Present findings factually without speculative assertions.  

2. `titles`: a list of titles of documents or entities from the `context` whose content is most central for verifying the claim. These titles correspond to named people, organizations, locations, events, or thematic topics explicitly in the `context`.  
   - Include all relevant authoritative documents referenced in context that illuminate the claim, especially “gold” or key documents if present.  
   - Do not invent titles; only list those present in the provided context.  
   - Titles help identify critical domain-specific references such as company names, product names, geographic designations, or biographies relevant to the claim.

Key Domain-Specific Considerations:  
- Many claims hinge on subtle, niche factual distinctions, such as exact headquarters of airlines (which can be in similarly named locations), differences in cemetery names and park boundaries, or labeling of product flavors and their origin countries. Caution is required to disentangle similarly named entities and to avoid implicit conflation based on partial overlaps in names or geography.  
- Claims involving person names often require attention to biographical facts or professional details to verify shared professions or roles.  
- When products or films are involved, details like release dates, award status of involved persons, and film genres are critical.  
- When magazines or journals are mentioned, verifying if they are periodicals or not can be relevant.

Suggested Reasoning Approach:  
- Carefully read the claim and identify the core factual points needing verification.  
- Analyze notes to extract relevant assertions supporting or contradicting the claim.  
- Use the context as the authoritative source to confirm, clarify, or refute those points, paying close attention to subtle domain-specific distinctions and preventing entity conflation.  
- Identify gaps where the information about key claim elements is missing or ambiguous.  
- Summarize the validated facts, contradictions, and uncertainties explicitly in `new_notes`.  
- Extract and list all key context titles that directly relate to the claim’s subject matter, ensuring the inclusion of all pertinent domain-specific references.

This instruction is designed to aid fact verification tasks requiring precise disambiguation of domain-specific data, careful synthesis of sometimes redundant or partial notes, and identification of critical reference materials to support claim assessment.

Summary:  
Given a `claim`, `notes`, and detailed domain-specific `context`, produce a concise, evidence-based summary `new_notes` that clarify the claim’s truthfulness by emphasizing key factual points, and a relevant list of `titles` for reference—ensuring accuracy in niche areas such as company headquarters, product attributes, geographic and administrative boundaries, and biographical details.
