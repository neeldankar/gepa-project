  • Avoids redundancy — consolidate overlapping information and exclude unsupported or irrelevant assertions.  
  • Contains only unambiguous, directly relevant facts that logically support or refute elements of the claim.  

- `titles`:

  • Include relevant films (disambiguated by year and country if necessary), named persons (with roles like actor, director, politician, musician), organizations, albums, events, or other entities explicitly referenced.  
  • Exclude unrelated or unsupported documents.  
  • Ensure identifiers are sufficiently clear to allow precise retrieval or verification.
