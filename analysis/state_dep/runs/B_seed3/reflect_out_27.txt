- `claim`: a factual statement or assertion that may be true or false, involving named entities such as people, films, music artists, military units, or other specific domain knowledge.

- `context`: a list of text passages or document summaries providing background, detailed descriptions, and authoritative information about entities or topics related to the claim and notes.

Your task is to process these inputs and produce two outputs:

1. `new_notes`: A concise, cleaned, and coherent list of factual statements that accurately summarize and resolve the claim using the information from the notes and context. New notes should:
   - Correct or clarify inaccuracies found in the claim.
   - Retain only relevant, verifiable facts directly supporting or refuting the claim.
   - Consolidate overlapping information when possible.
   - Exclude uncertainties or unsupported assertions unless explicitly necessary for clarity.
   - Include key details such as dates, names, titles, roles, affiliations, production companies, and other domain-specific facts.
   
2. `titles`: A list of document or entity titles from the context that best correspond to the essential facts used in `new_notes`. These are typically proper names of people, films, organizations, or other notable entities that serve as authoritative sources or categories relevant to the claim and supporting information.

Key domain-specific knowledge to apply when reviewing and synthesizing information:
- For films, include correct titles (often with release year), directors, main cast, production companies, and genre classification.
- For artists and musicians, identify group affiliations, solo works such as EPs or albums, and distinguishing nationalities or genres.
- For military units, note exact unit designations, formation years, ethnic or historical compositions, and events they were involved in.
- Verify and distinguish individuals with similar or related names based on provided data.
- Recognize the correct chronological and factual context relating to events or releases.
- Correct false claims by referring to specific details confirmed in notes and context.

Generalizable method:
- Carefully cross-check the claim against all notes, identifying accurate, partially true, or false components.
- Use context information to validate or supplement notes, especially for clarifying entities not fully described in notes.
- Filter and curate a new set of notes that convey a factual and coherent response to the claim.
- Extract and list key titles that correspond to the authoritative documents or entities referenced in the new notes, aiming to capture all relevant gold documents.

Your outputs should enable a clear understanding of what is factually true or false about the claim, supported by precise references to entities and documents, facilitating transparency and traceability.
