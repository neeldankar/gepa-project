Task Description:  
You will be given three inputs: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your goal is to produce two outputs: `new_notes` and `titles`. Your outputs must enable clear, precise, and verifiable factual assessment of the claim based on authoritative contextual information.

Inputs:  
- `claim`: A complex multi-entity factual assertion involving details such as roles, relationships, timelines, or event participation. Claims often span domains including cinema (films, actors, directors, composers), politics (people, parties, leadership roles), music (bands, musicians, songs, albums), and sports (teams, players, managers). Claims may be ambiguous or partially inaccurate and require fact-checking and clarification.  
- `notes`: A raw, unfiltered set of factual statements drawn from various sources related to the claim. Notes can be redundant, noisy, contradictory, or partially accurate. Your role is to filter and refine them into a precise factual summary.  
- `context`: Authoritative, detailed source excerpts that disambiguate entities (such as films with identical titles by year or country, musicians versus collaborators, political party branches and leadership timelines, sports teams and player/manager tenures). Context is the definitive factual reference.

Outputs:  
- `new_notes`:  
  - A concise, clear, and non-redundant subset of the `notes`.  
  - Captures all verified factual elements necessary to understand, analyze, and assess the claim, including entity identities, roles, timelines, relationships, event details, and corrections of any inaccuracies in the claim.  
  - Explicitly clarifies ambiguous or disputed claim components by relying on context.  
  - Discards unsupported, irrelevant, or contradictory information.  
  - Consolidates overlapping notes into a single precise statement where applicable.

- `titles`:  
  - A list of named source documents or entities from the `context` that substantiate the facts in `new_notes`.  
  - Includes all relevant films (disambiguated by year and country as necessary), named persons (actors, directors, politicians, musicians), organizations (films, bands, teams, parties), or events explicitly referenced in the `new_notes`.  
  - Excludes tangential, unrelated, or unsupported sources.  
  - Provides clear disambiguators for each listed title to allow unambiguous tracing and verification.

Domain-Specific and Niche Considerations:  

1. Film Domain:  
   - Many films share identical or similar titles across different years, countries, or studios (e.g., "Bombay Talkie" (1970, India) vs. a studio named Bombay Talkies).  
   - Verify and clearly state actors, directors, and composers linked to a particular film version by cross-referencing contextual identifiers.  
   - Avoid mixing credits or cast from different films with the same title.

2. Music Domain:  
   - Distinguish between actual band members and outside collaborators or producers.  
   - Accurately connect songs and albums to their rightful performers and writers.  
   - Validate timelines of releases and memberships where pertinent.

3. Political Domain:  
   - Distinguish between federal, provincial, and youth party wings and their leadership timelines.  
   - Verify party affiliations and leadership dates explicitly, including birthdates and changes.  
   - Disambiguate persons with similar names by role or date to avoid confusion.

4. Sports Domain:  
   - Accurately verify founding dates of teams, player careers, and managerial tenures.  
   - Confirm if players or managers have careers with specific teams, especially when claims link these roles.  
   - Clarify team name changes or relocations where relevant.

Reasoning and Methodology:  
1. Parse every factual assertion and entity in the claim.  
2. Cross-reference each assertion against the authoritative context to confirm identities, roles, timelines, and relationships. Disambiguate ambiguous or similarly named entities by using contextual details (e.g., dates, locations, roles).  
3. Evaluate each note for factual correctness, relevance, and support from context. Reject notes that contradict context or are irrelevant to the claim.  
4. Summarize all verified facts in `new_notes` with an emphasis on clearly correcting or clarifying mistaken or ambiguous claim components.  
5. Compile `titles` to include all and only the context sources that directly substantiate the selected `new_notes`. Use fully disambiguated and domain-specific naming.

Output Expectations:  
- The `new_notes` must present a crisp, unambiguous, and verifiably correct factual baseline to assess the claim, highlighting corrections and confirmations.  
- The `titles` list should facilitate straightforward verification, with clear and specific identifiers linking back to the source context.  
- Avoid unnecessary verbosity, redundancies, or inclusion of facts unrelated to the claim.  
- Preserve domain-specific disambiguations in all entity references and titles for clarity and accuracy.

This task requires meticulous attention to detail, careful resolution of ambiguities, and critical evaluation of source material across multiple distinct factual domains for accurate fact-checking and claim validation.
