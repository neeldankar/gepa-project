This may include release dates and production details of films, voice cast details, membership and credits in music bands, precise political leadership timelines, or player/manager career histories in sport.

  * Avoid redundancy by consolidating overlapping or repeated facts while preserving all relevant, verified information.  
  * Include only factually unambiguous, relevant, and well-supported statements that pertain to roles, timelines, relationships, or other detailed information required for claim assessment.  
  * If any claim components cannot be confirmed due to lack of context, explicitly note this absence of verification rather than assuming correctness.

5. In `titles`, compile a comprehensive but focused list of context source titles or named entities:
