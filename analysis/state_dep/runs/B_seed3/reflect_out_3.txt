Given two fields, `claim` and `notes`, your task is to generate a precise and focused `query` that can be used to verify or investigate the truthfulness of the claim based on the information in the notes.

Detailed task description:

1. **Input Fields:**
   - `claim`: A statement containing one or more factual assertions about people, works (movies, books, etc.), dates, or events.
   - `notes`: A list of supporting or related factual information extracted from various documents, which often includes biographical data, filmographies, authorship details, dates, and contextual clarifications.

2. **Output Field:**
   - `query`: A concise, targeted question or information request that:
     - Directly addresses ambiguous or missing information needed to confirm or refute the claim.
     - Focuses on retrieving specific domain facts (e.g., birth dates of individuals, authorship, nationality, involvement in projects).
     - Mentions the key entities or relevant documents referenced in the `claim` and `notes`.
     - Is formulated as a clear question or query that can guide retrieval or verification in a knowledge base or document corpus.

3. **Key Considerations and Domain-Specific Details:**
   - Identify all relevant entities (people, works, organizations) mentioned in the claim and the notes. Note that sometimes the claim can be false or misleading if an entity mentioned does not exist (e.g., a Ukrainian director of a specific movie that does not exist).
   - The query should explicitly seek verification or details of these entities, including:
     - Biographical data (birthdates, nationality).
     - Professional involvement (e.g., directing, acting, writing).
     - Existence or association with a specific work (movie, book, etc.).
     - Clarifications of contradictions or unsupported facts in the claim.
   - If the notes indicate missing information, the query should focus on the absent or uncertain facts necessary to assess the claim.
   - Ensure the query requests information that would enable retrieval of all relevant gold documents connected to the claim.
   - Avoid vague or overly broad queries; focus on precision and relevance to the claim’s key elements.
   - When multiple persons or works are mentioned, explicitly include them in the query to target the required documents.
   
4. **Generalizable Strategy:**
   - Parse the claim to identify the factual components.
   - Cross-reference the claim components with the notes to identify confirmed facts and missing or contradictory information.
   - Formulate the query to ask for data on the unverified or ambiguous elements that determine the claim’s validity.
   - When a claim hinges on the existence or attributes of a person or entity, the query should explicitly request confirmation of those attributes or existence.
   - When the claim references a work and its creators, the query should include the names of the work(s) and associated persons whose roles or attributes are in question.
   
By following these instructions, the generated `query` will serve as an effective information-seeking question that enables accurate verification or refutation of the claim based on the underlying factual notes and facilitates retrieval of all relevant supporting documents.
