Task Overview:  
You will be given a `claim`, a set of raw `notes`, and authoritative `context` related to the claim. Your task is to validate and refine the notes based on the context to produce accurate, concise, and non-redundant factual statements (`new_notes`) that either support or refute components of the claim. Additionally, you will identify and list (`titles`) relevant source documents or named entities from the context that clearly substantiate the refined notes.

Input Format:  
- `claim`: A statement containing complex factual assertions involving entities, their relationships, roles, timelines, or involvements in events. Claims span multiple domains (film, music, politics, sports) and sometimes contain ambiguous, partially incorrect, or conflated information requiring disambiguation and verification.  
- `notes`: A raw list of factual statements extracted from various sources related to the claim. The notes may be redundant, contradictory, partially accurate, or imprecise. Your job is to filter, verify, consolidate, and refine these notes using the context.  
- `context`: A set of authoritative, detailed excerpts (documents, entity descriptions, timelines) that provide disambiguated, reliable information about the entities, events, and relationships referenced in the claim. Contexts often include domain-specific facts such as film release years, country-specific information, actor/role/linkage details, political party leadership timelines, sports team founding dates, or musician membership information.

Output Requirements:  
- `new_notes`:  
  - Include only factually verified statements that are directly relevant to analyzing the claim.  
  - Summarize the key facts clarifying or correcting inaccuracies or misconceptions identified in the claim.  
  - Merge or remove redundant or overlapping notes.  
  - Exclude any unsupported, contradictory, or ambiguous information.  
  - Emphasize precise role, timeline, and relationship information validated by the context.  
  - Address ambiguous names and titles by clarifying identity and domain-specific attributes.  

- `titles`:  
  - List names of relevant source documents, films, named persons (actors, directors, politicians, musicians), organizations, or events explicitly referenced in your `new_notes`.  
  - Disambiguate entities clearly (e.g., include film year, country, full official titles or roles) to avoid confusion with similarly named entities.  
  - Exclude any unrelated or unsupported context titles.  
  - Ensure the list supports direct traceability to the facts in `new_notes`.

Domain-Specific Considerations:  
- Film Domain:  
  - Films with identical or similar names must be distinguished by year and country.  
  - Confirm correct actor/actress roles, director, and composer attributions as per context.  
  - Verify accurate casting details (lead vs supporting roles).  
- Music Domain:  
  - Distinguish band members from collaborators.  
  - Correctly identify who performed/sang on specific songs or albums.  
  - Verify affiliations and role titles such as lead singer or producer.  
- Political Domain:  
  - Differentiate among federal versus provincial political parties.  
  - Verify leadership timelines, birthdates, and affiliations, including youth wings linked to parent parties.  
  - Clarify names and identities of political figures clearly.  
- Sports Domain:  
  - Confirm team founding dates, managerial tenures, player careers, and team name changes accurately.  
  - Verify career history links, especially between playing and later managerial roles.

Recommended Reasoning Strategy:  
1. Parse and identify all entities, assertions, and subtle relationships in the `claim`.  
2. Cross-examine each fact with the given `context` to verify accuracy, roles, timelines, and relationships. Use disambiguating clues (release years, geographic locations, organizational hierarchies) to distinguish similarly named entities.  
3. Evaluate each `note` for factual correctness, relevance, and direct support or refutation of the claim considering context. Discard contradictory, unsupported, or irrelevant notes.  
4. Summarize a refined, factually unambiguous set of notes (`new_notes`) that clearly confirms or corrects claim components.  
5. Collect the minimal comprehensive set of titles reflecting the sources/entities underpinning your new notes, clearly disambiguated and domain-appropriate.

Additional Guidance:  
- Avoid repeating the same fact multiple times; consolidate where appropriate.  
- Explicitly address and rectify misattributions or misconceptions in the claim.  
- Be precise in language, especially when clarifying relationships and timelines to avoid ambiguity.  
- When entities share names (films, people, organizations), always attach clarifying details from the context.  
- Use only facts present and verified in the provided context; do not speculate beyond supplied evidence.

Outcome:  
Your output will be a concise, coherent, and accurate factual summary and a corresponding list of source references, enabling clear verification and assessment of the original claim with attention to detail and domain-specific nuances.
