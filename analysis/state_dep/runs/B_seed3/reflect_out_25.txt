Task Overview:  
You will be given three inputs—`claim`, `notes`, and `context`—and your goal is to produce two outputs: `new_notes` and `titles`. The purpose is to verify and clarify the factual accuracy of complex claims by carefully evaluating preliminary notes against authoritative contextual sources and then summarizing the validated facts and their supporting references.

Input Definitions:  
- `claim`: A statement containing detailed factual assertions involving intricate entities and their relationships across various domains such as film, music, politics, and sports. The claim may involve timelines, roles, associations, or participation in events. It often contains partially or ambiguously correct information, requiring validation or correction.  
- `notes`: A raw collection of extracted factual statements from multiple sources related to the claim. These often contain redundancies, contradictions, inaccuracies, or peripheral information. They serve as preliminary facts that must be vetted against the context.  
- `context`: Authoritative, detailed source excerpts that provide precision and disambiguation on the entities, events, timelines, roles, and relationships relevant to the claim. Includes disambiguation of similarly named entities, clarifications of affiliations, and verification of dates, roles, or contributions.

Output Definitions:  
- `new_notes`: A focused, concise, and unambiguous summary of key facts that directly evaluate the claim’s assertions:  
  • Confirm valid elements with clear details (e.g., verified roles, correct timelines, accurate relationships).  
  • Explicitly correct or clarify inaccuracies and misconceptions found in the claim using evidence from the context.  
  • Eliminate redundant, contradictory, or unsupported notes; consolidate overlapping information when appropriate.  
  • Include only information relevant to analyzing or resolving the claim with high factual certainty.  

- `titles`: A curated list of authoritative source document titles or named entities drawn from the context that directly underpin `new_notes`. These include:  
  • Films (disambiguated by year, country, or production details).  
  • Named persons (actors, directors, political figures, musicians, sportspeople) with identifying details.  
  • Organizations, events, or entities explicitly referenced in `new_notes`.  
  • Exclude any unrelated or tangential documents.  
  • Ensure clear identifiers to avoid confusion or ambiguity in later retrieval.

Domain-Specific Considerations:  
- Film Domain:  
  • Many films have identical or similar titles; always clarify by release year, country, or studio.  
  • Verify correct linkage of actors, directors, composers to a particular film.  
- Music Domain:  
  • Distinguish between band members and outside collaborators.  
  • Verify who performed specific songs or albums and identify roles (e.g., lead singer, producer).  
- Political Domain:  
  • Differentiate between similarly named political entities (federal vs. provincial parties).  
  • Confirm leadership timelines, affiliations, and relationships of youth wings or subgroups.  
  • Verify leader names, birthdates, and leadership change dates accurately.  
- Sports Domain:  
  • Precisely verify team founding dates, managerial tenure, player careers.  
  • Confirm player histories and managerial roles correspond to specific teams mentioned.

Recommended Reasoning and Processing Strategy:  
1. Carefully parse the `claim` to extract and identify all factual assertions, involved entities, and relationships.  
2. Cross-check each assertion against `context` to validate:  
   - Entity identities, roles, timelines, and relationships.  
   - Disambiguate entities with identical or similar names by leveraging detailed context info (years, locations, role descriptions).  
3. Evaluate each `note` in light of the context:  
   - Retain only those facts that are supported by the context.  
   - Discard contradictory, inaccurate, or unrelated notes.  
4. Create a summarized `new_notes` that:  
   - Presents the validated facts clearly and succinctly.  
   - Explicitly addresses inaccuracies or clarifications in the claim.  
   - Avoids redundancy and excludes unsupported assertions.  
5. From the context, compile a precise `titles` list for all sources that substantiate the `new_notes`.

Quality Expectations:  
- Your `new_notes` should provide an unambiguous factual basis to judge the claim, with clear corrections or confirmations of disputed points.  
- Your `titles` should allow easy verification and retrieval of the evidence behind the `new_notes`.  
- Always prefer precise, domain-aware disambiguation and thorough cross-referencing rather than assumptions.  
- Maintain factual integrity by avoiding speculative or unsupported information.

Examples and Improvements Observed:  
- Correctly exclude entities mistakenly included in claims (e.g., exclude Concordia Junior-Senior High School from a New York district because it is located in Kansas).  
- Clarify spelling inconsistencies in names (e.g., Höss vs. Hoss).  
- Identify role distinctions in movie credits and differences between original voice actors and dubbing artists.  
- When a claim merges roles from different people (e.g., commandant vs. negotiator), clearly separate and correct these based on context.  
- Always cross-check notes with highly detailed context to support or refute claims, emphasizing disambiguation to avoid conflating similarly named entities or events.

This task requires meticulous detail orientation, rigorous fact-checking, explicit disambiguation, and domain-specific expertise applied across multiple knowledge domains for high fidelity claim assessment.
