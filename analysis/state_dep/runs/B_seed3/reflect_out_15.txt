Your task is to generate a precise, focused, and entity-rich query that facilitates verification or investigation of the truthfulness of a factual claim, by leveraging contextual notes about related entities. The inputs you receive are:

- **claim**: A factual statement or assertions involving entities such as people, works (films, books, bands, etc.), places, events, dates, or their interrelations.
- **notes**: A list of factual details or contextual snippets extracted from various documents related to the claim’s entities. These notes may include biographical facts, geographic data, career roles, affiliations, clarifications, or contradictions relevant to the claim.

Your output must be:

- **query**: A clear, direct question or information request precisely targeting the verification of specific, critical facts supporting or refuting the claim. This query should enable retrieval of missing, ambiguous, or contradictory details from knowledge bases or documents about the named entities.

---

### Detailed Task Description and Domain-Specific Considerations:

1. **Entity Recognition and Explicit Referencing**  
   - Identify and mention all relevant entities from the claim and the notes in your query. Entities include:  
     - Persons (full names or aliases)  
     - Works (movies, books, albums, bands)  
     - Locations (cities, counties, natural landmarks)  
     - Organizations (councils, record labels, etc.)  
   - This comprehensive listing ensures retrieval of all pertinent documents and evidence related to the claim.

2. **Focus on Core Fact Categories**  
   The query must seek verification of key factual information types connected to the claim’s entities:  
   - **Biographical details:** birth/death dates, nationality, education, mentorship, etc.  
   - **Professional or artistic involvement:** roles (actor, director, member), affiliations with works or organizations, origin or base location of groups or works.  
   - **Geographical facts:** administrative jurisdictions, locations relative to other landmarks or regions.  
   - **Existence and linkage:** confirmation whether entities and asserted relationships exist or are documented.

3. **Target Ambiguities, Contradictions, or Missing Facts**  
   - If the claim mentions a fact absent or contradicted in the notes, your query should explicitly address that gap or contradiction.  
   - If any entity or relationship is asserted in the claim but missing from the notes, ask specifically for verification of its existence or connection.  
   - Avoid vague or overly broad queries; instead, target discrete factual points critical to confirming or refuting the claim.

4. **Guidance on Query Construction**  
   - Queries must be clearly posed, direct questions, easily interpreted as precise requests for factual information.  
   - When multiple entities or works appear, explicitly mention all to maximize retrieval coverage.  
   - The query should enable retrieval of **all** key missing or disputed facts needed to fully assess the claim—not just partial confirmation.  
   - Avoid generic questions that do not help resolve core uncertainties about the claim.

---

### Generalizable Strategy for Crafting the Query:

1. **Parse the Claim:**  
   - Identify all factual assertions and involved key entities.  
   - Break down complex claims into subcomponents if appropriate (e.g., separate questions for biographical and geographic claims).

2. **Cross-Reference the Notes:**  
   - Determine which facts are confirmed, contradicted, or not mentioned.  
   - Identify missing entities or ambiguous relationships necessitating further investigation.

3. **Formulate the Query:**  
   - Focus tightly on unresolved factual elements essential to assess the claim’s truthfulness.  
   - Mention explicitly all relevant entities (persons, works, places, organizations).  
   - Phrase as a direct, focused question requesting specific facts to clarify or verify the claim.

---

### Examples of Effective Queries (for reference):

- **Education and mentorship relationships:**  
  “What is the identity of Ariel Ramírez’s science teacher, and is there documentation that this teacher also taught Marta Lambertini?”

- **Membership and geographic base of bands:**  
  “Are the bands God Seed, Sahg, Ov Hell, and Gorgoroth all based in Bergen or the Norwegian county of Hordaland, and what is King ov Hell’s membership and association with each?”

- **Geographic jurisdiction and proximity:**  
  “Which town is administered by the Eurobodalla Shire council and located near Higgin’s Creek and Deua National Park?”

Your final output is a specific, entity-rich, and fact-targeted question designed to uncover all missing or ambiguous facts critical for verifying or refuting the claim, informed by cross-referencing the provided notes.
