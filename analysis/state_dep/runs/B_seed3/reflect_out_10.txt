You are given two fields, `claim` and `notes`. Your task is to produce a concise, precise, and highly focused `query` designed to verify or investigate the truthfulness of the `claim` by leveraging the information (or lack thereof) in the `notes`.

---

### Input Format

- **claim**: A factual assertion about entities such as people, works (films, books, bands, organizations, etc.), places, dates, events, or their interrelations.
- **notes**: A list of factual statements or contextual snippets from various documents. These detail biographical data, affiliations, geographic info, career involvements, dates, or other relevant facts related to entities mentioned or linked to the claim.

---

### Task Objective

Generate a targeted and entity-rich **query** that:

1. Explicitly focuses on verifying, clarifying, or investigating the core factual elements—especially ambiguous, missing, contradictory, or unsupported parts—of the claim based on the notes.
2. References key entities from the claim and notes (persons, works, places, organizations) to drive retrieval of pertinent supporting documents.
3. Enables precise fact-checking by asking narrowly scoped, direct questions rather than vague or general ones.

---

### Key Domain-Specific Considerations

1. **Entity Identification & Coverage**
   - Extract and explicitly mention all relevant entities (full names or aliases):
     - Individuals (e.g., actors, directors, athletes)
     - Creative works (films, books, albums, bands)
     - Organizations (leagues, councils, groups)
     - Locations (cities, counties, landmarks)
   - Ensure entities in both claim and notes are included to maximize comprehensiveness.

2. **Fact Categories to Verify**
   Focus your query on verifying discrete factual relationships, such as:
   - **Biographical data:** birthdates, career roles, affiliations, teachers, mentors.
   - **Professional or artistic involvement:** memberships, roles in works or organizations, formation locations.
   - **Geographic relationships:** administrative jurisdictions, proximities.
   - **Existence and linkage:** validating whether claimed entities or connections (clubs, sponsorships, actors’ roles) actually exist or are documented.

3. **Address Missing or Contradictory Information**
   - If notes contradict the claim or lack information on a key fact, your query must explicitly ask to verify that fact.
   - If an entity or relationship in the claim is absent from the notes, ask for evidence supporting or refuting it.
   - Avoid broad or generic questions; be specific about what must be checked.

4. **Question Style & Focus**
   - Formulate queries as clear, direct, fact-targeted questions.
   - Explicitly list all relevant entities to guide precise document retrieval.
   - Include comparative elements when necessary (e.g., comparing dates, verifying sponsorship between entities).
   - Focus on missing or ambiguous aspects critical to establishing the claim’s truth, not on elements already confirmed.

---

### Generalizable Strategy for Query Construction

1. **Parse the Claim**
   - Identify each factual assertion and involved entities.
   - Break complex claims into fact components if needed (e.g., identity of a teacher + membership in an organization).

2. **Cross-Reference Notes**
   - Determine which facts are confirmed, contradicted, or absent.
   - Note entities mentioned or missing.

3. **Formulate the Query**
   - Target specifically ambiguous, unsupported, or conflicting claim elements.
   - Explicitly mention all key entities to maximize relevant information retrieval.
   - Ask fact-based questions designed to uncover all information necessary to confirm or reject the entire claim.

---

### Examples of Effective Queries

- For education/mentorship claims:  
  “What is the identity of Ariel Ramírez’s science teacher, and is there any documented connection between this teacher and Marta Lambertini?”

- For verifying band memberships and geographic bases:  
  “Are the bands God Seed, Sahg, Ov Hell, and Gorgoroth all based in Bergen or the county of Hordaland, and what are King ov Hell’s roles and membership status in each?”

- For administrative and geographic clarifications:  
  “Which town is administered by the Eurobodalla Shire council and is located near Higgin’s Creek and Deua National Park?”

---

### Summary

Your output is a sharply focused, entity-rich, direct question that addresses key factual gaps or contradictions in the claim, guided by the notes. This precise query structure ensures comprehensive retrieval of all documents necessary to validate or refute the claim effectively.
