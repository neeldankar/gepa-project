Task Description:
You are provided with three input fields: 
- `claim`: a factual statement or assertion that may or may not be true,
- `notes`: a list of sentences containing both supporting and contradicting information extracted from some source(s) about elements of the claim,
- `context`: a list of background documents or excerpts related to the claim that provide detailed, factual data.

Your goal is to produce two output fields:
1. `new_notes`: a concise, coherent set of notes that directly relate to validating or refuting the claim. These should summarize crucial evidence from the existing `notes` and `context`, emphasizing factual correctness and relevance. Remove redundancy and highlight information that supports, contradicts, or clarifies the claim.
2. `titles`: a list of relevant document or topic titles from the `context` that are most pertinent to assessing the claim’s truthfulness or providing background. These titles should reflect key entities, locations, events, or concepts essential to understanding or verifying the claim.

Detailed Guidelines and Domain-Specific Instructions:

- Understand that many claims involve domain-specific knowledge, e.g., airline companies’ headquarters, soft drink flavors by brand and origin, cemetery locations within national parks, or other niche factual details. You must carefully verify claim statements against the context and notes, since these often hinge on subtle details (e.g., geographical locations of airline headquarters, specific park boundaries, brand flavor profiles).

- When analyzing the claim, differentiate between entities with similar or confusing names (for example, “Golden Gate National Cemetery” vs. “San Francisco National Cemetery,” or different airlines headquartered in similarly named locations). Avoid conflating distinct entities just based on partial name or geographic proximity.

- Use the context to disambiguate and confirm or deny parts of the claim, focusing on:
  * Exact headquarters locations of companies or airlines.
  * Flavors and country of origin of soft drinks or beverages.
  * Geographical and administrative boundaries of parks, cemeteries, or historical sites.
  * Biographical and burial details linked to persons mentioned in the claim.
  
- Your reasoning should clearly state which parts of the claim are supported, which parts are contradicted, and whether the evidence is insufficient to fully confirm or deny it.

- Construct `new_notes` by extracting and synthesizing the strongest, clearest pieces of evidence from the original `notes` and `context`:
  * Combine duplicates and related notes into a single concise point.
  * Emphasize factual clarifications over speculative or uncertain information.
  * Include both confirmation and contradiction where applicable.
  
- For `titles`, identify the set of documents or entity pages explicitly mentioned or implied in the context that are essential for verifying the claim. Titles should represent key referenced topics such as:
  * Person names (e.g., “Candi Kubeck”, “George W. Wallace”)
  * Organizations or companies (e.g., “ValuJet Airlines”, “PrivatAir”)
  * Locations or geographic entities (e.g., “Clayton County, Georgia”, “Golden Gate National Recreation Area”)
  * Products or thematic categories (e.g., “Solo (soft drink)”, “Orange-flavored soft drinks”)
  * Specific landmarks or relevant historical sites (e.g., “San Francisco National Cemetery”, “Presidio of San Francisco”)

- When some gold or key documents appear in the original context but were missed, note these as feedback indicates the importance of retrieving all relevant titles. Do not invent titles; only use those present in the provided `context`.

- In your reasoning, explicitly state any critical distinctions or clarifications about geographic, administrative, or organizational relationships discovered in the context to justify your conclusions regarding the claim.

- Your output must be precise, evidence-based, and avoid speculative assertions without support from provided data.

Summary:
Given a claim, detailed but sometimes redundant notes, and relevant contextual passages, distill correct, unambiguous supporting or refuting points into `new_notes`, and identify essential reference titles in `titles`. Your work will aid fact verification by clarifying complex factual relationships especially in niche domains such as corporate headquarters, beverage product details, and geographic-political boundaries of parks and cemeteries.
