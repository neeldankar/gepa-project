Given two fields, `claim` and `notes`, your task is to generate a single, concise, precise, and focused `query` designed to verify, clarify, or investigate the truthfulness of the claim. The query must be constructed specifically to address any ambiguities, contradictions, unsupported assertions, or missing information in the claim relative to the available notes.

---

### Input Fields

- **claim**: A factual statement or a set of factual assertions involving entities such as people (by full name or alias), creative works (films, TV shows, albums, songs, bands), places (counties, towns, national parks), organizations (councils, record labels), dates, and specific relationships among these entities.

- **notes**: A collection of factual details or contextual information extracted from multiple documents. These might include biographical data, career roles, affiliations, production or broadcast details, geographic and administrative information, and clarifications that either support, contradict, or omit parts of the claim.

---

### Output Field

- **query**: A single, entity-rich, explicitly targeted question (or set of tightly linked questions) that:

  - Explicitly references **all key entities** (persons, works, organizations, places) mentioned or implied in the claim and cross-referenced or contradicted in the notes.

  - Zooms in on **the specific points of factual uncertainty**: ambiguous relationships, missing data, contradictory facts, administratively or geographically unclear information, or existence of certain entities or connections.

  - Is structured to elicit **discrete, verifiable domain facts** that will directly confirm or refute the claim in whole or in critical parts.

---

### Domain-Specific and Task-Specific Considerations

1. **Entity Identification**

   - Carefully extract and include in the query:  
     - Individuals by name or notable alias  
     - Works: film titles, TV series, albums, songs, bands  
     - Locations: administrative entities, towns, natural landmarks, counties  
     - Organizations: councils, networks, production companies, record labels

2. **Categories of Facts to Verify**

   The query should target verification or retrieval of:

   - **Biographical and Career Data:** birth/death dates, nationalities, career roles (director, screenwriter, actor, band member), affiliations with works or organizations, mentors and teachers.

   - **Creative and Professional Involvement:** verification of authorship, directorial roles, membership in bands, production or broadcast details (such as programming blocks or episode titles).

   - **Geographic and Administrative Relationships:** location details, jurisdictional administration, proximities to natural landmarks or neighboring places.

   - **Existence and Linkages:** confirming whether claimed entities and their asserted connections exist or are documented.

3. **Handling Missing, Contradictory, or Ambiguous Information**

   - If the claim asserts connections or facts not corroborated or contradicted in the notes, explicitly target those missing or disputed points with your query.

   - If entities mentioned in the claim (such as a specific teacher, a show title, or geographic location) do not appear in the notes, include a direct request for verification or identity of these entities.

   - Avoid overly broad or vague questions; focus narrowly on the key facts needed to establish the claim’s truthfulness.

4. **Query Construction**

   - Phrase the query as a clear, concise, and factual question or tightly linked questions.

   - When multiple related entities or works are involved, list them explicitly to ensure retrieval considers all relevant documents.

   - The query must be sufficient to allow fact-checkers or retrieval systems to gather all missing or disputed facts critical to evaluating the entire claim.

   - Avoid thematic or open-ended questions; prioritize precise factual verification.

---

### Generalizable Step-by-Step Strategy

1. **Parse the Claim**

   - Identify every factual assertion and all involved entities.

   - Break complex claims into discrete verification points if necessary (e.g., verifying education and film involvement separately).

2. **Cross-Reference with the Notes**

   - Determine which facts and entities are supported, which are contradicted, and which are missing.

3. **Formulate the Query**

   - Target only those fact elements that are ambiguous, missing, or contradictory.

   - Explicitly name all involved entities referenced by the claim and notes.

   - Ensure the query asks for specific, verifiable domain facts (dates, identities, associations, titles, locations).

---

### Examples of Effective Queries

- For clarifying education and mentorship claims:  
  "Who was Ariel Ramírez's science teacher, and is there documentation that this teacher also taught Marta Lambertini?"

- For band membership and geographic claims:  
  "Are the bands God Seed, Sahg, Ov Hell, and Gorgoroth all based in Bergen or the Norwegian county of Hordaland, and what is King ov Hell’s membership and association with each?"

- For administrative and geographic verification:  
  "Which town is administered by the Eurobodalla Shire council and is located near Higgin's Creek and Deua National Park?"

- For disambiguating identities and roles in film and TV:  
  "Who co-wrote the TV series 'Yes Minister,' and are any of these co-writers named or associated with the documentary 'Che strano chiamarsi Federico' or Ettore Scola? Additionally, is 'Che strano chiamarsi Federico' a person or solely a documentary film directed by Ettore Scola?"

- For director and nationality claims related to films:  
  "Is there any Albanian individual credited as a director or involved in directing the 1980 Italian film 'Cannibal Holocaust,' and what is the full list of credited directors? Additionally, can we confirm Alejandro Springall's nationality and directorial roles?"

- For TV programming and episode verification:  
  "Did any TV show titled 'La La Land' air on KidsClick's Sunday programming block, and is there an American Dad episode titled 'La La Land' that features songs from the Wax Fang album 'La La Land'?"

### Summary

Your query must be a direct, specific, entity-inclusive question that addresses the precise factual gaps, contradictions, or uncertainties in the claim relative to the notes. The query should enable a focused fact-checking or retrieval effort to obtain all essential documents and data to validate or refute the claim comprehensively.

Avoid vague or broad questioning; your goal is to provide a crystal-clear, narrow fact investigation prompt grounded tightly in the entities and facts presented.

---

### Additional Notes

- The output is a single field named `query`.

- Always rely on and reference entities explicitly mentioned in claim and notes. Do not introduce extraneous entities.

- In cases of compound claims, the query may consist of multiple connected questions but should remain concise.

- Ensure the query is formulated to maximize retrieval of relevant documents by naming all pertinent entities and works.
