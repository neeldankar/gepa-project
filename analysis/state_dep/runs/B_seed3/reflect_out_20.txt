Task: Generate a precise and focused `query` to verify or investigate the truthfulness of a given factual `claim` by leveraging the provided `notes`.

Inputs:
- `claim`: A complex factual statement asserting relationships or attributes concerning people, works (e.g., films, books, episodes), dates, events, or entities.
- `notes`: A collection of factual, domain-specific information that supports, contradicts, or contextualizes elements of the claim. This may include biographical data (birthdates, nationalities, professions), filmographies, authorship, production details, event descriptors, or clarifications relevant to the entities or works mentioned in the claim.

Output:
- `query`: A concise, unambiguous, and domain-targeted question or information request designed to:
  - Directly address ambiguous, missing, or contradictory pieces of factual information critical for verifying the claim.
  - Focus on key entities and relationships referenced in both the claim and notes.
  - Yield retrieval of relevant gold documents necessary for final claim verification.
  - Avoid vagueness or overly broad scope; ensure specificity to the claim’s factual core.

Detailed Instructions and Domain-Specific Guidance:

1. **Entity Identification and Analysis**  
   - Extract and explicitly identify all relevant entities (people, works, organizations, platforms, characters, specific episodes, commercials, etc.) mentioned in the `claim` and `notes`.
   - Note any named entities potentially mismatched or unverified (e.g., an actor’s nationality or involvement in a work, the existence of a film or platform).
   - Recognize domain-specific roles and relationships such as actor-character links, director-work associations, player or band member roles, producer-platform origins.

2. **Factual Component Parsing**  
   - Decompose the claim into its constituent factual assertions (e.g., birth years, nationality, involvement in specific works or roles, comparative ages).
   - Cross-check these components against the `notes` to detect:
     - Confirmed facts.
     - Contradictions or disconfirmations.
     - Missing key details preventing verification.

3. **Domain-Relevant Query Focus Areas**  
   Depending on claim content and notes, the query should seek specific types of information, such as:
   - Biographical details: birthdates, nationalities, ethnicities, ages.
   - Professional involvement: directing, acting, authorship, musical roles.
   - Existence and production details of creative works (films, games, episodes).
   - Platform origins or the country of production for hardware or software.
   - Specific roles within works (e.g., CIA operative in an episode).
   - Comparative or relational facts requiring precise data (e.g., age comparisons).

4. **Formulation of the Query**  
   The query must:  
   - Be posed as a clear, focused question or request for specific verification data.
   - Include the names of key entities or works to direct retrieval effectively.
   - Explicitly address ambiguity or missing facts critical to claim validation.
   - In cases involving multiple entities or works, include them all for comprehensiveness.
   - Avoid general questions; instead, tightly target the factual hinge or contradiction in the claim.

5. **Generalizable Strategy**  
   - Parse the claim to enumerate factual assertions and involved entities.  
   - Align these with provided notes to identify what is confirmed, disputed, or absent.  
   - Formulate a minimal yet sufficient query targeted at the missing or ambiguous facts.  
   - Include multiple entities/works if needed to ensure retrieval completeness.  
   - Always frame the query to enable retrieval of all gold documents necessary for final verification.

6. **Examples of Task-Relevant Factual Details**  
   - The nationality of a platform manufacturer versus a game platform.  
   - Birth dates and nationalities of actors connected to specific TV episodes or commercials.  
   - Confirmation of existence and production countries of hardware or software platforms.  
   - Comparative age relationships relying on birthdates of musicians or public figures.  
   - Verification of the involvement of actors or creators in specific works.

By adhering to these detailed guidelines and domain considerations, the generated `query` will be a precise, effective verification tool that directly supports retrieval and evaluation of the claim’s truthfulness.
