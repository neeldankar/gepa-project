Task Description:

Context may mention multiple related entities or disambiguate similarly named entities (e.g., distinguishing films with similar titles or distinct persons with similar names).  

Outputs:  
- `new_notes`: A concise, relevant, and non-redundant subset of the `notes` that directly supports analyzing and verifying the claim based on the `context`. The selected notes should:  
  - Summarize key factual points that address the claim components (e.g., who starred alongside whom, film release years, composer roles).  
  - Clarify any misconceptions or factual errors in the original claim, if present.  
  - Reference verified and unambiguous facts clearly connected to the entities and relationships under scrutiny.  

- `titles`: A list of document or entity titles from the `context` that directly underpin your analysis and support the new_notes. These include film titles (with year and sometimes language or country for disambiguation), names of actors, directors, composers, or other relevant persons or entities. The list should be comprehensive for the evidence used but should avoid unrelated or tangential documents.

- Films often have similar or identical titles but differ by year, country, or production studio (e.g., "Bombay Talkie" (1970) vs. "Bombay Talkies" studio active in 1930s-40s). Ensure correct disambiguation based on the context information.  
- Actors and directors can be confused when roles are claimed ambiguously (e.g., an actor wrongly identified as a director) — verify roles explicitly stated in context.  
- Relationships such as family (father/son), professional roles (composer, director, actor), and film appearances (co-stars, debut works) are common; factual verification requires precise linking, usually from context entries or notes.  
- Filmographies and timelines: verify whether an actor, director, or composer was involved with a particular film at a stated time.  
- Titles should correspond to official or common titles including disambiguating details (year, country, film type) to avoid confusion.  

Recommended Reasoning Strategy:  
1. Parse the `claim` carefully to identify all embedded factual assertions and entities involved.  
2. Cross-reference these components with the `context` to verify facts:  
   - Identify correct names, film titles, years, roles, and relationships as established by the context.  
   - Disambiguate entities when multiple possibilities exist.  
3. Use the `notes` as supporting evidence, selecting statements that are accurate, relevant, and corroborated by the context; discard contradictory or unsupported notes.  
4. Summarize findings succinctly in `new_notes`, emphasizing corrections or confirmations related to the claim.  
5. Compile a complete but focused list of `titles` that represent the sources primarily used to verify or refute claim components.  

Deliverables:  
- `new_notes`: concise, accurate, and context-supported factual statements addressing the claim.  
- `titles`: relevant document/entity titles extracted from the context that underpin these notes.  

The output should facilitate clear understanding of the factual basis for evaluating the claim, including documenting misinformation where applicable, with fully disambiguated references suitable for later retrieval or verification.
