You will be given three input fields:

- `claim`: a statement or assertion to verify or analyze.
- `notes`: a list of extracted factual observations, evidence, or reasoning points related to the claim. These often clarify details, confirm or refute aspects of the claim, and cite contextual facts.
- `context`: a list of documents or snippets presenting background facts, biographies, historical data, timelines, affiliations, etc., relevant to entities or topics in the claim.

Your task is to use information from `notes` and `context` to produce two output fields:

1. `new_notes`: a distilled, concise, and well-structured summary of key points from `notes` that directly address the truth or accuracy of the claim, highlighting supporting or contradicting evidence. The new notes should:

   - Correctly synthesize relevant information from both `notes` and `context`.
   - Identify any conflations, inaccuracies, or missing connections in the claim.
   - Avoid repetition and prioritize clarity and factual grounding.
   - Emphasize important domain-specific details such as individuals’ identities, roles, dates, affiliations, accomplishments, sponsorships, or institutional founding dates.

2. `titles`: a list of the most relevant document or entity titles (from `context`) needed to comprehensively verify or refute the claim. The titles should cover key people, institutions, organizations, leagues, teams, or events specifically referenced or implicated in the claim and evidence. Include just enough titles to fully encompass the claim's components for further retrieval or review.

Reasoning and methodology to produce these outputs:
- Analyze the claim carefully to understand its factual components and relations.
- Review the notes to understand existing evidence supporting or contradicting the claim.
- Corroborate and enrich this with the context documents that provide detailed domain-specific facts, such as:
   - Biographical and career details of persons (e.g., athlete names, trainer roles, sports teams, academic history).
   - Institutional founding dates and affiliations (universities, clubs, leagues).
   - Specifics on sponsorships, team memberships, accomplishments tied to dates and places.
- Identify any logical inconsistencies or conflations in the claim by cross-referencing the facts in notes and context.
- Summarize the evidence in `new_notes` with clear, precise language.
- Select titles representing the core topics/entities within the verified or falsified claim to facilitate targeted retrieval.

The output should be factual, domain-aware, concise, and clearly linked to evidence, suitable for fact-checking, verification, or retrieval augmentation.
