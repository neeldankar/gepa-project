Given three input fields: `claim` (a statement that asserts certain facts), `notes` (a list of extracted snippets or statements relevant to the claim), and `context` (a list of background articles or documents related to the claim), your task is to produce two output fields: 

1. `new_notes`: a concise, non-redundant set of evidence-based notes extracted or synthesized primarily from the `notes` and supported by `context`, which directly support, clarify, or refute the claim. These notes should be factual, precise, and limited to relevant information that helps validate or invalidate the claim.

2. `titles`: a short list of document or article titles from the `context` (or inferred document titles when applicable) that represent key documents containing the information used in `new_notes`. The titles should correspond to authoritative sources that best cover the facts underlying the claim.

When performing this task, keep the following domain-specific considerations and best practices in mind:

- Claims often require verifying specific factual relationships, such as organizational composition, geographical headquarters, biographical data, album attributions, or historical events.

- Identify and exclude misinformation or incorrect details found in the claim by cross-referencing the notes and context (e.g., excluding "Concordia Junior-Senior High School" as part of a district when it is located elsewhere).

- Focus on disambiguation of entities or facts when multiple similar or related entities exist (e.g., distinguishing the solo albums of Carlos Santana from his band’s albums).

- Use notes that are well-supported by context, ensuring the facts are consistent, and remove duplicate or contradicting entries. 

- Summarize detailed reasoning in your own words succinctly to clarify why certain notes were included or excluded based on factual support.

- Titles should align as closely as possible with the entities or subjects in the notes and claim (e.g., use "ValuJet Airlines" rather than generic aviation topics when the claim concerns that airline).

- When multiple documents pertain to the claim, include all relevant document titles, prioritizing completeness of coverage.

- The accuracy of capturing the correct documents is evaluated; strive to retrieve all key relevant documents from the context.

- Avoid introducing unverified information, personal opinions, or speculative reasoning not grounded in the provided notes or context.

- Each `new_notes` entry should be a standalone factual statement that clarifies or backs the claim or its contradiction.

The overall goal is to produce a factual, evidence-based synthesis from the input notes and context that validates or refutes the claim with appropriate, non-redundant supporting sentences and to identify the minimal but sufficient set of source titles that contain definitive information on the claim’s topic.
