Task Description:
You will be given two fields as input: `claim` and `notes`. Your task is to produce a `query` field that formulates a precise, focused search question or set of questions aimed at verifying or investigating the claim using the information in the notes. The `query` should guide retrieval of relevant evidence to support or refute the claim.

Input Format:
- `claim`: A concise factual or relational statement whose truthfulness or details are to be verified.
- `notes`: A list of text snippets, facts, or excerpts containing factual information related to entities or events mentioned in the claim that can be used as evidence.

Output Format:
- `query`: A natural language question or set of linked questions designed to retrieve relevant documents or information that best support the verification of the claim. The query should explicitly target the key entities and relationships mentioned in the claim, as clarified or contextualized by the notes.

Detailed Instructions and Domain-Specific Considerations:
1. Identify the key entities (e.g., places, people, organizations, events) and relationships (e.g., jurisdiction, starring in a film, membership in a group) mentioned in the claim.
2. Use the notes to understand relevant background or additional context about these entities and relationships, which can help refine and disambiguate the query. For example:
   - Geopolitical context (e.g., administrative divisions of counties and cities; provinces and countries in China).
   - Relationships and roles in films (e.g., familial relations among actors; filmography and production companies with similar names but different time periods).
   - Membership or membership disputes in entertainment industry contexts (e.g., judges on a TV show; producers and singers).
3. Reflect contradictions or confirmations present in the notes by phrasing the query to specifically verify disputed or critical facts, such as:
   - Confirming if a location falls under a particular administrative jurisdiction.
   - Checking if a particular person starred in a specific film in a given year.
   - Clarifying who are the official members of a judging panel and whether certain individuals or roles are included.
4. When multiple entities or facts are involved, structure the query to encompass the relevant combined aspects. For instance, verifying if both cities are in the same country, or if a group of judges includes a particular individual plus another related person.
5. Keep queries clear, concise, and answerable with the available or to-be-retrieved evidence—avoid overly broad or vague phrases.
6. The query should aim to retrieve the ‘gold’ or key documents relevant to the claim’s truthfulness as reflected in the feedback examples (e.g., documents specifically tied to key entities like "Puyang," "Jennifer Kendal," or "Aaina (album)").

Generalizable Strategy:
- Extract key factual components and entity mentions from the claim.
- Analyze notes to gather grounded factual context, verify contradictions, dates, names, or jurisdictions.
- Formulate a focused, natural language question or linked questions that explicitly mention the entities and facts to be verified.
- Incorporate any relevant entity disambiguation or temporal context to narrow retrieval.

Example:
If the claim is about a county’s jurisdiction and same-country status with another city, produce a query that asks if the county’s jurisdiction city lies in the stated province and if both cities belong to the same country.

If the claim involves whether a person acted in a specific film in a certain year, produce a query asking if that person starred in that film in that year and if not, what roles or films are associated with them.

If the claim states a list of judges on a show plus an additional individual, produce a query confirming whether all those individuals are judges on that show, especially clarifying disputed membership and any related persons joined later.

Summary:
Produce well-formed, fact-focused queries for verifying relationships and entity attributes in the claim, informed by the contextual details in the notes, to guide retrieval of the most relevant documents for claim verification.
