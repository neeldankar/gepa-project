You will be given three fields as input:

- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion that needs to be verified or supported by evidence.
- `notes`: A list of extracted evidential snippets or statements (often redundant or overlapping) relevant to the claim.
- `context`: A list of document titles paired with brief summaries or descriptions that may contain additional relevant information.

Your task is to analyze the inputs and produce two output fields:

1. **new_notes**: A concise, distilled list of evidence snippets selected from the input `notes` that directly support or relate to verifying the `claim`. Avoid excessive repetition and retain only the most relevant and clear statements that substantiate the claim.

2. **titles**: A list of document titles from the `context` that are crucial for supporting the claim. These are the primary sources or documents that contain key information relevant to validating the claim.

### Detailed Task Description and Domain-Specific Considerations:

- The claims often involve specific domain entities like songs, albums, films, events, venues, or people, and relationships such as release dates, authorship, recordings, ownership, locations, or collaborations.
- Your reasoning should identify which pieces of evidence among the notes directly affirm or clarify parts of the claim, filtering out duplicates or less relevant statements.
- When selecting titles, prefer those documents whose content descriptions in `context` strongly connect to or provide authoritative backing for the claim.
- Some claims require cross-referencing multiple facts (e.g., song release year AND who first recorded it), so your `new_notes` should encompass all relevant fact snippets that together verify the claim.
- Avoid including notes or titles that do not add unique or necessary information toward supporting the claim.
- In cases where the evidence is not explicitly available for part of the claim, use reasonable inference grounded in the provided notes and context.
- The outputs should be concise but sufficiently informative to justify the veracity of the claim.

### General Strategy:

1. **Understand the claim fully**, including domain-specific nuances (e.g., music chart positions, film production roles, venue ownership).
2. **Review the notes** to identify which statements best confirm or deny the claim.
3. **Cross-check the context titles** to find documents whose summaries indicate authoritative or detailed coverage of claim aspects, and include those titles.
4. **Eliminate redundant notes** and produce a focused set of evidence that covers all claim facets.
5. **Ensure titles represent the main knowledge sources** needed to verify the claim, as per the context information.

By following the above approach, you will produce a well-supported evidential summary and identify key documents underpinning the factual verification of the claim.
