Task: Given a claim about specific factual information, and three inputs — `claim` (a statement asserting particular facts), `notes` (a set of extracted snippets directly or indirectly related to the claim), and `context` (a list of background documents or articles relevant to the claim) — your goal is to produce two outputs:

1. **new_notes**: A concise, factual, and non-redundant synthesis of notes that directly support, clarify, or refute the claim. These notes must be:

   - **Evidence-based**, drawing primarily from the provided `notes` and cross-verified by the `context`.
   - **Focused on verifying or invalidating specific factual relationships**, such as individual or organizational biographical details, geographical locations, event dates, entity attributions (e.g., album authorship), or historical facts.
   - **Disambiguated and precisely stated** where entities or facts may be easily conflated (for example, distinguishing an individual’s solo work from a group’s work).
   - **Clean of misinformation or contradictions**, removing notes or claim elements clearly invalidated by the context.
   - **Free from duplication or contradictory statements**, ensuring clarity and exactness.
   - **Standalone and comprehensible** such that each note independently conveys a factual point relevant to assessing the claim.
   - **Restricted to verified information** — avoid adding any unverified speculation, personal opinions, or assumptions not grounded in the provided notes and context.

2. **titles**: A minimal but complete list of authoritative document or article titles from `context` that best cover the facts underlying the synthesized notes. Titles should:

   - Match explicitly or be inferred accurately from the `context` provided.
   - Represent key documents or sources that directly contain or support the facts in your `new_notes`.
   - Include all relevant sources necessary for completeness without unnecessary additions.
   - Use precise and domain-specific titles related to the claim’s subject rather than generic or tangentially related sources.

**Domain-Specific and Procedural Considerations:**

- Claims often hinge on verifying **specific factual relations** (e.g., membership in an organization during a specific season, geographical distribution of a plant subspecies, casting dates in TV series, differing roles an individual may have across times or series).
- Carefully **disambiguate entities with similar or overlapping names or roles** (e.g., distinguishing whether Kevin Wilson’s roles at different times were head coach or offensive coordinator, or whether an actor joined one show or another).
- **Fact-check and exclude incorrect claim elements** if contradicted within `notes` or `context` (for example, if a plant’s habitat is on a different island or if a series premiere date differs).
- Leverage redundancy in `notes` to confirm facts but remove repeated statements unless subtle clarifications differ.
- When multiple relevant documents exist, include all that contribute uniquely or importantly to supporting/refuting the claim.
- Provide succinct reasoning when necessary to clarify **why certain notes were included or excluded**, especially when correcting misinformation in the claim.
- Avoid including unsubstantiated assertions or details beyond what is verifiable from the `notes` and `context`.
- Maintain **precision and clarity in wording**, ensuring factual correctness and easy verifiability.

**General Strategy:**

- Start by verifying each component of the claim against the provided `notes` and corroborating them with `context`.
- Identify contradictions and flag or exclude unsupported elements.
- Synthesize a short list of precise factual statements (notes) that collectively support or refute the claim.
- Extract the minimal set of document titles covering these facts to guide readers to authoritative sources.
- Always ensure every output note can stand alone as a factual assertion, and every listed title clearly corresponds to a source validating these assertions.

By following these instructions, the assistant will generate a focused, domain-aware, factually grounded synthesis that accurately evaluates claims with supporting evidence and identifies key reference documents.
