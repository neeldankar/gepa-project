Task: Given three inputs: `claim` (a factual statement to verify or assess), `notes` (a list of extracted evidential snippets or statements relevant to the claim), and `context` (a list of full background documents or summaries providing supporting or contradicting information), produce two outputs: `new_notes` and `titles`.

1. `new_notes`: A concise, accurate, and non-redundant list of key evidential statements from the provided `notes` that directly pertain to verifying or refuting the `claim`. These notes should:
   - Summarize or emphasize the critical factual points that support or contradict the claim.
   - Eliminate duplicates and irrelevant information.
   - Make clear any contradictions or confirmations found.
   - Incorporate specific factual details extracted from the context.
   - Avoid vague or unrelated content.
   
2. `titles`: A list of document or entity titles that are most relevant and necessary to understand the factual verification of the claim. These should be drawn primarily from the `context` and are expected to include:
   - Proper names of people, organizations, events, or entities cited in the claim or supporting notes.
   - Titles of significant related documents/pages referenced in the context.
   - All core documents that provide essential background or factual evidence relevant to the claim, especially those identified as gold-standard or critical in verification.
   - Avoid including unrelated or marginally relevant titles.

Reasoning Strategy:
- Carefully cross-reference the claim with both the notes and the context, identifying factual consistencies or contradictions.
- Identify domain-specific knowledge embedded within the context, such as company headquarters location, actor roles and connections, sports team nicknames, player positions and historical seasons, etc.
- Distill lengthy, repetitive, or tangential notes into a concise factual summary.
- Ensure that all elements of the claim are addressed explicitly by the selected notes and titles (partial confirmation should be clarified).
- When a part of the claim is incorrect or unsupported, state it clearly in the notes.
- Include all key entities involved in the claim verification to aid downstream retrieval or validation.

Domain-specific considerations observed from examples:
- Geographic and corporate provenance (e.g., American vs. Swiss companies).
- Filmography and actor-role relationships including nuanced career recognition.
- Sports facts including positional roles, seasons, and club nicknames.
- Historical data verification involving multiple related but distinct entities.
- The importance of matching claims to multiple related documents, sometimes naming more than the minimal apparent documents to fully cover the claim’s assertions.

In sum, the task is a multidocument fact verification and summarization exercise that requires precise extraction, disambiguation, and balancing of the evidence to produce a distilled set of notes and a relevant core set of document titles to support or refute each claim comprehensively and clearly.
