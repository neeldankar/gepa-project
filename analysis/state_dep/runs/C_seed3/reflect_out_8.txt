Task Description:  

You will be given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your goal is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

Input Details:
- `claim`: A factual or knowledge-based statement or assertion that requires verification or clarification.
- `notes`: A list of textual snippets, facts, or statements relevant to the claim, containing supporting or contradicting evidence.
- `context`: A list of background documents or passages with detailed, factual, encyclopedic, or biographical information related to entities, events, or concepts mentioned in the claim and notes.

Your Task:
1. Carefully analyze the `claim` to understand the exact factual assertion being made.
2. Examine the `notes` for relevant evidence related to the claim. These notes may contain repetition, paraphrases, or clarifications that help support or refute the claim.
3. Review the `context` documents for detailed, reliable information that can confirm or contradict the claim, or clarify ambiguities in the notes.
4. Using all input information, generate:
   - `new_notes`: A concise, accurate summary or refined set of notes that combine the key verified facts from the input notes and context. Remove redundant, irrelevant, or incorrect information. The new notes should support correct understanding related to the claim.
   - `titles`: A list of the most relevant document titles or named entities from the context that are crucial to understanding the claim and have been referenced in your reasoning. These titles will typically correspond to Wikipedia-style article titles or recognized entity names.

Important Domain-Specific Guidelines:
- The entities mentioned (such as actors, composers, institutions) may have complex or overlapping associations (e.g., actors with multiple films, musical compositions with variants by different composers). Identify and clearly separate these references.
- Pay attention to dates, biographical details, composition catalog numbers, institutional founding years, and other domain-specific facts that help to validate chronological or identity-based claims.
- When clarifying factual conflicts (e.g., a person appearing in one film but not another, a composition authored by one composer versus another), explicitly disambiguate and summarize such distinctions in the new notes.
- The task involves verifying correctness of claims through synthesis of notes and context, not just copying text.
- Signals for inclusion in `titles` include entities explicitly relevant to the claim’s topic and supported by the finalized reasoning (e.g., a person’s Wikipedia page, a film’s article, a musical piece’s entry, or an institution’s page).
  
Suggested Solution Approach:
- Parse the claim carefully for key entities and assertions.
- Cross-validate facts from notes with richer context.
- Discard or flag contradictory or irrelevant notes.
- Summarize the facts supporting or refuting the claim into `new_notes` clearly and factually.
- Collect the key Wikipedia-style document titles from context that were central to your reasoning.
- Be precise, concise, and unambiguous in new_notes to ensure clarity.
- Avoid extraneous or repeated facts; focus on those that resolve or illuminate the claim.

This detailed, evidence-based synthesis supports downstream fact verification, entity linking, or claim validation tasks which rely on tightly curated factual summaries and accurate identification of relevant knowledge sources.
