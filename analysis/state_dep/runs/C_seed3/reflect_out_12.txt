Task Description:

You will be provided with three input fields:
- `claim`: A factual or verification statement summarizing a relationship or fact that needs to be supported or verified.
- `notes`: A list of sentences extracted from relevant documents or sources that may contain evidence supporting or refuting the claim.
- `context`: A list of longer passages or document excerpts that provide detailed background information on entities, events, or concepts mentioned in the claim and notes.

Your task is to analyze these inputs and produce two output fields:
1. `new_notes`: A concise selection or synthesis of notes that accurately support, explain, or verify the claim. These should be factual, relevant statements directly tied to key elements of the claim. Avoid redundancy and include only the essential information needed to verify or understand the claim.
2. `titles`: A list of document, entity, or topic titles that are the main sources of evidence or are essential for verifying the claim. These titles correspond to the documents or topics in the `context` or `notes` and help identify which sources were critical for reasoning.

Detailed Guidelines:

- Understand the claim thoroughly, identifying its key components such as entities, roles, dates, and events.
- Review the `notes` and `context` carefully to find precise, corroborative evidence that links those key components relevantly.
- Filter the `notes` down to those that directly support the claim; exclude unrelated or contradictory sentences unless needed to clarify or contrast.
- When selecting `titles`, prioritize:
  - Entities (people, organizations, species, events) directly involved in or core to the claim.
  - Specific works (films, series, plays), locations (parks, venues), and awards that are referenced in the claim or evidence.
  - Titles that helped disambiguate or verify nuanced facts (e.g., differentiating parks on different islands, or distinguishing actors and their nationalities).
- Include in reasoning the relationships and logical connections: for example, verifying if the same actor played two roles, or if two locations are on the same or different islands.
- Focus on domain-specific knowledge pertinent to the claim context, e.g.:
  - Film and television actors, their roles, awards (Golden Globe nominations), and works.
  - Botanical information including species, subspecies, endemic locations, and geographical relationships of parks.
  - Theater productions, performance venues (like Dorothy Chandler Pavilion), and actor biographies.
- Your responses must center on factual accuracy, clarity, and relevance to the claim.

Generalizable Approach:

- Perform targeted extraction and synthesis by cross-referencing the claim's key elements with the provided evidence in `notes` and `context`.
- Disambiguate entities appearing in multiple contexts by carefully checking identifying details (nationality, specific roles, location attributes).
- Summarize support succinctly in `new_notes`, preserving crucial facts such as dates, places, and titles.
- Provide `titles` that reflect the main evidence sources, covering people, places, works, or awards central to the claim.

This task helps construct a distilled, evidence-backed summary to verify complex claims by drawing from diverse textual data, focusing on precision in note selection and relevant source identification.
