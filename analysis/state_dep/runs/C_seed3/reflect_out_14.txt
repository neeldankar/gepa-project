Task Description:
You will be given three inputs: `claim` (a factual statement to verify), `notes` (a list of extracted evidential sentences or observations related to the claim), and `context` (a list of relevant documents or knowledge snippets containing detailed information about entities, events, or concepts mentioned in the claim and notes).

Your task is to produce two outputs:

1. `new_notes`: A refined, concise set of factually accurate statements drawn from the input `notes` and supported by `context`. These should clarify the truthfulness of the claim, correct any inaccuracies, or confirm parts of the claim with proper evidence. Exclude redundant, contradictory, or unsupported notes. The new notes must be coherent and directly address key elements of the claim.

2. `titles`: A list of relevant document or resource titles (from the `context`) that best support your reasoning about the claim’s veracity. The titles should:
   - Include key entities, events, or concepts central to validating or refuting the claim.
   - Properly represent the documents providing evidence for your refined notes.
   - Be precise and informative, capturing the most pertinent sources available.

Reasoning and Approach:
- Carefully analyze the claim and identify all factual assertions it makes, including entities (people, organizations, places), events (such as film releases, sports finals), and specific facts (cast memberships, award records).
- Use the `notes` and `context` to validate each factual assertion. 
- Resolve contradictions in the notes by referring to corroborated details in the context documents.
- Address common domain specifics, such as:
  * Films: Confirm cast, production companies, release years, and genre.
  * Sports events: Confirm venue locations, teams, and event specifics.
  * Literary and award claims: Confirm laureates, nominations, nationalities, and award categories.
- Recognize subtle distinctions, for example, someone being nominated vs. winning a Nobel Prize, or a company producing a film vs. being only loosely associated.
- Summarize factual conclusions succinctly and clearly in `new_notes`.
- Select `titles` by identifying documents whose content directly supports the verified facts.
- Avoid including unrelated or non-supportive documents in `titles`.
- Ensure completeness by covering all critical facets mentioned in the claim.
- Remember that some claims may be partially true; indicate which parts are supported and which are refuted or unverified.

Additional Notes:
- The `context` often includes multiple documents with overlapping or complementary information; cross-reference them where applicable.
- Some notes may be repetitive; consolidate them when preparing `new_notes`.
- Precision and clarity in your outputs are critical to assist downstream fact verification or knowledge retrieval tasks.
- The goal is to produce outputs that allow a reader or system to understand the claim’s accuracy and reference authoritative sources straightaway.

Format of Your Output:
- Provide `new_notes` as a list of clear, accurate statements.
- Provide `titles` as a list of the exact document or article titles from the `context`.
