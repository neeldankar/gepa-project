Task Description:

You will be given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your goal is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

Input Fields:
- `claim`: A factual or knowledge-based assertion involving named entities (people, places, artworks, films, compositions, institutions, etc.) often involving biographical, geographic, or domain-specific details. The claim requires verification, clarification, or synthesis.
- `notes`: A list of textual snippets, facts, or statements relevant to the claim. These contain supporting or contradicting evidence, but may include redundancies, contradictions, paraphrases, or ambiguous information.
- `context`: A list of detailed background documents or passages providing encyclopedic, biographical, geographical, cultural, or domain-specific information. This is the authoritative source material for verifying, clarifying, or refining the claim and notes.

Your Objective:
1. Carefully analyze and parse the `claim`, identifying its key factual assertions and involved entities. Understand each element the claim asserts or implies.
2. Review the `notes` for evidence related to the claim, paying attention to:
   - Supporting or contradicting facts
   - Redundancies or repetitions
   - Possible inaccuracies or contradictions within the notes
   - Domain-specific details (dates, places, catalog or reference numbers, institutional histories) critical to factual validation
3. Cross-reference the claim and notes with the detailed `context` documents:
   - Use the context to confirm or refute facts mentioned
   - Resolve ambiguities or contradictions found in the notes 
   - Extract authoritative factual details that clarify or correct the claim
4. Produce two outputs:
   - `new_notes`: A concise, accurate, and refined summary synthesizing key verified facts from both the notes and context.
       * Remove redundant, irrelevant, or unsupported assertions
       * Clearly disambiguate any overlapping or potentially confusing entities (e.g., actors appearing in multiple films, composers with similar works, institutions with complex histories)
       * Explicitly clarify or summarize any conflicts arising among input information, especially around identities, associations, dates, and geographic details
       * Preserve only information directly relevant to the claim’s factual verification or clarification
   - `titles`: A list of the most relevant Wikipedia-style document titles or named entities from the `context` crucial to understanding and validating the claim.
       * Titles correspond to named entities such as persons' full names, film or artwork titles, institutions, or geographic locations referenced and used in reasoning.
       * Include entities that your reasoning explicitly references or that are central to the factual understanding of the claim.
