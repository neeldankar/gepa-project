Task Description:  
You are given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your task is to analyze these inputs to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

Input Details:  
- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion that needs to be verified or supported through the information given.  
- `notes`: A list of textual pieces of information, often extracted or summarized facts relating to entities, events, or claims relevant to the claim. These may contain redundancies, partial overlaps, or slight variations of facts.  
- `context`: A list of longer textual documents or passages providing detailed background, definitions, or encyclopedic data on entities, institutions, events, or other concepts pertinent to the claim and notes.

Generalizable Approach:  
1. **Comprehend the claim fully** — understand what entities, relationships, and temporal or causal assertions it contains.  
2. **Identify relevant facts from `notes`** that support or refute the claim. This involves recognizing key entities, dates, roles, institutions, and events cited. Prefer facts that directly connect to the claim’s elements without redundancy.  
3. **Corroborate and expand using `context`** — Use longer passages to:  
   - Confirm the foundational facts in the `notes`.  
   - Extract authoritative titles (entities, institutions, roles, events) related to the claim and notes.  
   - Identify and distinguish related but different entities or facts precisely (e.g., to clarify whether one actor is involved or not).  
4. **Generate `new_notes`** as a consolidated, de-duplicated subset of `notes` that directly support, clarify, or contextualize the claim. The goal is to enhance clarity and factual basis while removing irrelevant or redundant notes.  
5. **Generate `titles`** as a list of unique key entities and documents drawn from `context` and `notes`. Ideally these are exact proper names or titles of persons, institutions, films, awards, or other notable concepts underpinning the claim. Titles should reflect all three components of the claim: the primary subject(s), institutions or entities compared or referred to, and contextual background relevant to the claim’s reasoning.

Domain Specific Information & Nuance:  
- The task often involves verifying timelines (establishment years for institutions, release years for films, nomination years for awards).  
- It requires distinguishing between related but different entities with similar or overlapping names (e.g., different actors involved in a performance, or different institutions with related histories).  
- When claims mix information spanning multiple domains (such as an actor’s roles in different productions and awards for them), clarity must be maintained about which fact belongs to which entity or role.  
- Recognize and resolve certain types of implicit equivalences (e.g., "The King of Cocaine" refers covertly to Pablo Escobar).  
- Focus on providing supporting evidence to either validate or refute the claim clearly, while maintaining factual and terminological precision.

Output Requirements:  
- `new_notes`: A concise, relevant, and non-redundant list of textual notes from `notes` that directly support or contextualize the claim.  
- `titles`: A comprehensive yet precise list of unique, clearly identified named entities and documents derived primarily from `context` but also from `notes` as needed, covering all components necessary for claim validation.

Additional Recommendations:  
- Aim to retrieve all gold-standard documents/entities relevant to the claim; missing any key title diminishes the response quality.  
- Avoid including irrelevant or marginally related facts to maintain focus on claim support.  
- When two or more entities share names or attributes, explicitly clarify differences if possible in notes or reasoning.
