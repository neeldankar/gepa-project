Task Description:

You will be given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your goal is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

Input Details:
- `claim`: A statement or assertion making a factual or knowledge-based claim that needs to be verified, clarified, or supported.
- `notes`: An unordered list of textual snippets, facts, or statements that may support, contradict, or add nuance to the claim. These notes can contain redundancy, paraphrases, or incomplete/conflicting information.
- `context`: A list of detailed, reliable background documents or passages. These include encyclopedic, biographical, historical, or domain-specific information about the entities, works, events, or concepts mentioned in the `claim` and `notes`. The context is expected to be the richest authoritative source for verifying and clarifying ambiguities found in notes.

Your Task:
1. Analyze the `claim` carefully to understand precisely what factual assertion is being made. Identify all key entities (people, groups, works, institutions, events) and temporal or biographical details relevant to the claim.
2. Examine `notes` for relevant evidence supporting or refuting the claim, noting repeated information, paraphrases, or contradictions.
3. Rigorously consult the `context` documents to verify, clarify, or correct facts in the notes. Pay special attention to domain-specific details such as:
   - Correct identities and differentiations of similarly named or related entities (e.g., distinguishing multiple composers or actors working on related but distinct works).
   - Chronological facts, e.g., years of activity, birthdates, film release years, unit founding years, composition catalog numbers.
   - Relationship and overlap among groups, units, or works (e.g., military units that merged, collaborations in films or documentaries).
   - Specific contributions or roles within entities or works (e.g., a person’s involvement as director, actor, or technician).
4. Synthesize the information from both notes and context into:
   - `new_notes`: A concise and refined set of notes that accurately summarize the verified facts related to the claim. Remove irrelevant, redundant, or false information. Where contradictions or ambiguities exist, explicitly clarify by distinguishing between entities or versions and citing precise domain facts. The goal is to produce a factual evidence summary that fully supports an accurate understanding of the claim.
   - `titles`: A list of the most relevant encyclopedic or authoritative document titles or named entities from the `context` that are essential to understanding the claim and justify your synthesis. These will typically be Wikipedia-style article titles for biographies, films, musical works, military units, institutions, or related concepts explicitly referenced or crucial to your reasoning.

Domain-Specific Guidelines and Considerations:
- Always distinguish between similarly named entities or overlapping references, e.g., multiple individuals with similar names, different military units, films with overlapping casts or subject matter, or musical compositions with variants by separate composers.
- Dates and chronological orderings are crucial: verify birth and death years, release years, founding or merger dates—all may influence claim accuracy.
- For military units and groups, verify distinctions between component and combined units, their periods of activity, and relevant combat service years.
- For artistic works (films, compositions, novels), identify exact roles of entities involved (director, cinematographer, composer, author, etc.), and production or release years to determine correctness about simultaneity or precedence.
- When no explicit evidence is found for part of the claim (e.g., no data on whether a particular individual enjoyed an activity), clearly note the information gap and do not assume.
- Synthesis involves judgement about possible contradictions, clarifying ambiguities by separating references, and precise terminology to avoid error propagation.

Generalizable Strategy:
- Identify and extract key factual components from the claim.
- Cross-check all supporting and contradicting pieces from notes against the in-depth, reliable context passages.
- Filter out repeated or extraneous notes unless they provide additional factual clarification.
- Use context to correct mistaken or ambiguous information in notes, especially focusing on facts critical to the claim.
- Produce a tightly focused note summary that supports an evidence-based verification of the claim.
- Collect the minimal set of context document titles which are referenced during reasoning, essential for downstream linkage or validation.

Output Format:
- `new_notes`: List of verified, concise factual statements relevant to verifying the claim.
- `titles`: List of Wikipedia-style article or entity titles from context that were instrumental in your factual synthesis.

This approach ensures precise, domain-informed, and well-verified summaries that support rigorous fact verification or knowledge extraction downstream.
