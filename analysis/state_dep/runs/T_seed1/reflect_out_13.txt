Your task is to generate a precise, focused, and factually relevant *verification query* from a given *claim* (a factual or historical statement) and a corresponding set of detailed *notes* containing factual excerpts, which may confirm, contradict, or leave gaps regarding the claim.

**Input Format:**  
- `claim`: A factual or historical statement containing key factual elements such as specific entities (people, organizations, teams, military units, film titles), temporal facts (formation dates, event years, release years), events (battles, matches, tournaments, film releases), or relationships (roles, affiliations, geographic composition).  
- `notes`: A collection of factual sentences or excerpts summarizing information relevant to the claim. Notes may provide confirmations, contradictions, clarifications, or highlight missing or ambiguous information about the claim components.

---

### Objective:  
To compose a single, clear, neutral, and explicit *verification query* that:  
- Identifies and references key entities in their full, official, or domain-appropriate forms (e.g., full school and district names, official military unit designations, exact film titles including year and directors).  
- Incorporates precise temporal details (event dates, formation years, release years, seasons, or episode numbers) for time-bound accuracy.  
- Clearly addresses roles, relationships, or affiliations relevant to the claim, such as who played a character in a specified film version, official membership of schools in districts, correct team participation or player roles in events, unit participation in battles, or actor roles in different film versions.  
- Captures relevant geographic specifics (cities, census-designated places, countries) to disambiguate entities.  
- Explicitly surfaces any ambiguities, contradictions, disputed points, or missing information evident from comparing the claim and notes, phrased as a neutral question rather than assuming truth or falsehood.

---

### Domain-Specific Considerations:
- **Education:** Confirm official school membership in districts; verify school names and geographic areas served, addressing contradictions or gaps about district composition.  
- **Sports:** Identify players, teams, match details linked to events and dates; verify championship status, player-team associations (including nicknames), and scoring or participation details.  
- **Military Units:** Verify official unit names, formation dates, and historical battle participation; clarify if units existed by specific dates or earlier.  
- **Film and Media:** Verify voice actor roles tied to exact film versions (year, director); confirm film genres, release years, connections between films and real-life subjects, and ordering of remakes or versions.  
- **General:** Distinguish clearly similarly named entities or works (animated vs live-action films, official vs colloquial names); use authoritative, complete naming conventions.

---

### Methodology to Generate the Verification Query:
1. **Parse the claim** into its factual components: entities, relationships, temporal facts, events, and geographic details.  
2. **Cross-reference all components** against the notes, noting:  
   - Explicit confirmations or refutations, including dates and affiliations.  
   - Ambiguities, conflicts, or missing relevant information.  
   - Nuances suggesting partial truth or need for further clarification.  
3. **Formulate one single, clear, neutral question** that:  
   - Explicitly references full entities, temporal contexts, and relationships aligned with the claim.  
   - Highlights any contradictions, ambiguities, or gaps (by contrasting alternatives, e.g. “Did X occur in year Y or earlier?”, “Is school A an official member of district B?”, “Who scored in specific event C?”, “Was actor X the voice in film Y (year, director)?”).  
   - Is open-ended—designed to confirm or refute key facts without presuming correctness.  
4. **Avoid any assumptions or unwarranted inferences** not supported by claim or notes.

---

### Style and Format:
- Use full official or domain-specific names and precise dates whenever possible.  
- Be concise but explicit, avoiding vague or compound questions that confuse the fact-checking focus.  
- Use neutral language that invites verification or refutation rather than asserting facts.  
- When the claim contains conflated or ambiguous facts (e.g., conflating premiere dates of different series or actor roles across series), your query must explicitly separate and highlight these points for verification.  

---

### Examples of Verification Queries (adaptable templates):  
- “Which high schools are official members of [Full District Name] in [Location]? Is [Full School Name] included?”  
- “What census-designated places are served by each school in [District Name], specifically with regard to [School Name]?”  
- “Was [Person] the head coach of [Team Name] during the [Year] [Event Name] football season, and did he become offensive coordinator at [University Name] only after that season?”  
- “Did the [Military Unit Full Name] exist starting from year [Year] or earlier?”  
- “Did [Actor Name] provide the voice of [Character Name] in the [Year] film '[Exact Movie Title]' directed by [Director’s Full Name], or was there an earlier version?”  
- “Which players scored in the [Year] [Tournament/Match Name] between [Team A] and [Team B], and which professional team, colloquially known as [Nickname], did the scorer represent?”

---

By strictly following these instructions, you ensure that the generated verification query is factually precise, domain-appropriate, and focused on the exact points necessary for fact verification, including duly noting any conflicts or incomplete information between the claim and notes.
