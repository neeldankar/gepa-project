Task Description:
You are given three input fields: `claim` (a factual statement or assertion), `notes` (a collection of extracted evidence fragments relevant to the claim), and `context` (background documents or descriptions related to entities involved in the claim). Your objective is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

Definitions:
- `claim`: The statement whose truth or validity is to be established or explained.
- `notes`: A list of textual excerpts or facts extracted from sources related to the claim, possibly redundant or overlapping.
- `context`: A list of background documents or entries, each associated with a specific entity (e.g., a place, person, concept, or topic) that provides factual information relevant to the claim and notes.
- `new_notes`: A distilled, concise, and factually accurate subset of the `notes` extracted or synthesized based on evidence from both `notes` and `context`, supporting or clarifying the claim.
- `titles`: A list of relevant document or entity titles from `context` that best support the claim, including any critical entities or key concepts mentioned in either the claim or the `new_notes`. Ideally, these are the documents one would consult to verify or understand the claim fully.

Task Requirements and Best Practices:
1. **Reasoning & Evidence Integration**:  
   Carefully analyze the `claim` against the `notes` and `context`. Use both to reason about the veracity, details, or intricacies of the claim. Cross-check overlapping information and resolve contradictions or clarify distinctions (e.g., different subspecies inhabiting different islands). Base your conclusions exclusively on the given inputs.

2. **Precision and Conciseness in `new_notes`**:  
   Filter and synthesize the provided `notes` to include only essential, unique, and non-redundant facts that directly support or clarify the claim. Remove repetition and irrelevant statements. If the context reveals key factual clarifications, ensure these are reflected in `new_notes`.

3. **Comprehensive but Focused `titles` Selection**:  
   Select the minimal but sufficient set of document titles from `context` that are necessary to understand or verify the claim based on the evidence. Titles should correspond to entity names or concepts directly mentioned or implied by the `claim` and supported by `new_notes`.  
   - Include entities specifically relevant to persons, places, or topics central to the claim’s fact pattern.  
   - Include titles for distinct but related entities that help establish nuances (e.g., distinguishing two subspecies on different islands or different roles played by an actor).  
   - Avoid irrelevant or marginally related titles.

4. **Domain-Specific Details to Consider**:  
   - Geographic specificity: Be attentive to locations, especially when claims involve places or species endemic to specific areas (e.g., islands, parks, towns). Clarify any distinctions or exclusivities.  
   - Named entities and roles: Identify persons with specific roles (e.g., actor roles, award nominations) and their relationships to titles, productions, or events mentioned.  
   - Popularity or recognition claims: If the claim involves comparisons (e.g., popularity of chess vs. a designer’s games), incorporate knowledge of relative scale and scope, drawing on context and notes.  
   - Scientific or taxonomic distinctions: When subspecies or variants are involved, be precise about their habitats and differences as per the context.  
   - Award and nomination facts: Confirm nomination or award claims carefully and distinguish between related but distinct honors or roles.

5. **Example of the Reasoning Strategy**:  
   - Start by parsing the claim to identify key entities and assertions.  
   - Compare relevant `notes` for repeated or conflicting facts.  
   - Refer to `context` documents that describe these entities in detail to verify or refute ambiguous points.  
   - Synthesize a clear summary in `new_notes` and select the most relevant titles from `context` to aid further validation.

6. **Output Format**:  
   Produce:
   - `new_notes`: A list of concise, unique, and relevant notes supporting the claim.  
   - `titles`: A list of relevant document/entity titles from the `context`.

By following these instructions, you will ensure that the outputs are factually sound, traceable to the given information, and useful for verifying or understanding the claim.
