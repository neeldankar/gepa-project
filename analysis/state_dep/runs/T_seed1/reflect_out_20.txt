Your task is to generate a single, precise, focused, and clearly structured *verification query* that factually checks the core assertions of a given factual or historical `claim` by explicitly cross-referencing them with the corresponding set of detailed `notes`.

---

### Input Format:
- `claim`: A statement containing specific entities (persons, organizations, schools, military units, films, teams, events), temporal information (dates, years, episode numbers), event details (battles, matches, tournaments, movie releases), or relationships (roles, affiliations, geographic composition).
- `notes`: A set of detailed factual sentences or excerpts that summarize, confirm, contradict, or nuance the information in the claim. These include domain-specific data such as official names, exact dates, geographic clarifications, correct roles, and precise relationship or event identifications.

---

### Core Objective:
Develop a well-formed **verification query** that:
- Explicitly names all relevant entities with official and domain-appropriate full names or designations.
- Includes key temporal markers (e.g., formation dates, release years, event years or episode numbers) to give the question critical time-bound specificity.
- Clearly addresses the crucial relationships, roles, or affiliations presented in the claim (e.g., who played or voiced whom, which schools belong to which districts, which team a player represented during a tournament).
- Incorporates geographic specifics such as towns, census-designated places, counties, or countries to remove ambiguity.
- Explicitly raises and focuses on contradicting, unclear, or missing elements found between the claim and the notes — does not assume truth but formulates open questions to confirm or refute those disputed points.
  
---

### Domain-Specific Considerations:

- **Education Domain:**
  - Verify official membership of named schools in school districts by their full names.
  - Confirm precise official school names and the census-designated places or geographic areas they serve.
  - Highlight any contradictions about district composition or school membership.

- **Sports Domain:**
  - Confirm player names, team designations (including colloquial nicknames), and participation in matches or tournaments with explicit event names and years.
  - Verify championship statuses or scoring details tied to specific matches or events.

- **Military Units:**
  - Confirm official military unit titles and formation dates.
  - Verify engagements or participation in historical battles, ensuring chronological accuracy.

- **Film and Media:**
  - Confirm actor roles tied to a specific film by exact title, release year, and director.
  - Distinguish between original and remake films or adaptations, including their temporal order.
  - Confirm genres, release dates, and direct links between films, real-life subjects, or characters.

- **General:**
  - Clearly differentiate among similarly named entities or events.
  - Use full official names over informal or partial references.
  
---

### Methodology / Approach:

1. **Parse and extract components** from the claim:
   - Identify named entities, temporal information, events, roles, affiliations, and geographic data.

2. **Cross-reference with notes**:
   - Detect contradictions, missing or partial confirmations.
   - Identify nuances or gaps in either claim or notes.
   - Detect ambiguity or competing factual possibilities.

3. **Construct a question** that is:
   - Factually neutral and clearly framed.
   - Explicit about all relevant entities, dates, and relationships.
   - Designed to directly confirm or refute the core factual elements in the claim.
   - Open-ended with respect to verification, avoiding assumptions beyond the data provided.
   - Inclusive of ambiguity or conflicts, asking precisely about disputed or unclear facts.

4. **Avoid**:
   - Presumptive phrasing that assumes the claim is true or false.
   - Vague or broad questions that do not focus on specific disputed facts.
   - Omitting key entities or details referenced in the claim that appear in the notes.

---

### Examples of effective verification queries: 

- "Which high schools are official members of the Sewanhaka Central High School District in Nassau County, New York? Specifically, is Concordia Junior-Senior High School included, or is H. Frank Carey Junior-Senior High School the fifth school? Which census-designated places do these schools serve?"

- "Who was the Cuban President who first served in 1940 and claimed that Fidel Castro was killed during the 26th of July Movement landing? Who disproved this claim, and what was the nature of their relationship or disagreement with New York Times correspondent Ruby Hart Phillips?"

- "Did the Disney Channel series 'Even Stevens', starring Shia LaBeouf as Louis Stevens, premiere before 2004, and did Christopher B. Landon co-write the 2007 film 'Disturbia', which starred Shia LaBeouf?"

---

### Summary:

Your output must be a **single, well-structured verification question** that explicitly and neutrally challenges or confirms the main factual elements in the claim, by precisely referencing entities, dates, events, relationships, and geographic elements as found in or contradicted by the notes. The question should ideally enable retrieval or expert verification of key disputed facts, helping to resolve any contradictions or knowledge gaps illuminated by the notes.
