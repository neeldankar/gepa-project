Your task is to generate a precise, neutral, and explicit *verification query* based on a provided factual or historical `claim` and accompanying detailed `notes`. This query is to be used for fact-checking purposes and should be designed to confirm or refute the factual elements in the claim (e.g., entities, dates, events, roles, affiliations) by cross-referencing against the notes.

---

### Input Format:
- `claim`: A factual or historical statement containing specific entities like people, organizations, film titles, military units, or geographic locations; temporal facts such as formation or release years; events such as battles, film releases, or sports matches; and relationships such as roles (e.g., voice actors), affiliations, or geographic memberships (e.g., school district compositions).
  
- `notes`: A set of factual sentences or excerpts containing domain-specific details, which may confirm, contradict, nuance, or lack information about the claim’s components. These notes commonly specify official names, dates, roles, geographic clarifications, or precise historical data.

---

### Task Objective & Key Requirements:

Generate **a single, well-structured fact-checking query** that:

1. **Explicitly references key official entities and precise domain terminology:**  
   - Use full official names (e.g., exact full school names, official military unit designations, complete film titles with release years and directors, official tournament names).  
   - Reference full temporal information (e.g., event years, release years, formation dates).  
   - Specify geographic details if relevant (e.g., cities, census-designated places, countries).

2. **Targets core factual elements to verify:**  
   - Entities (actors, directors, players, military units, schools).  
   - Temporal facts (when an event or formation occurred, release years).  
   - Events and their details (battles, film releases, sports matches, tournament outcomes).  
   - Roles and relationships (e.g., who voiced which character in what film, who starred alongside whom, school district memberships, military unit participation in battles).

3. **Highlights any ambiguities, contradictions, or missing information between the claim and the notes:**  
   - Frame the question to clarify disputed or unclear facts.  
   - Explicitly compare alternatives if the data is contradictory or incomplete (e.g., “Was X formed in year Y or earlier?” “Does school A belong to district B or not?” “Did actor X voice character Y in film Z?”).

4. **Keeps a neutral and open-ended tone:**  
   - Avoid assumptions or presuppositions not explicitly supported by the claim or notes.  
   - The query should enable either confirmation or refutation.

---

### Domain-Specific Considerations:

- **Education domain:**  
  - Verify official member schools of school districts by full, correct school names.  
  - Confirm geographic areas served by each school (e.g., specific census-designated places).  
  - Identify and clarify any contradictions about district composition or school naming.

- **Sports domain:**  
  - Identify players and their teams in specific matches or tournaments with dates.  
  - Confirm championship status and player participation details.  
  - Verify colloquial team nicknames linking back to official team names.  
  - Clarify goal scorers or participants in key sports events.

- **Military units:**  
  - Verify official unit names and formation dates.  
  - Confirm historical engagements such as battles or wars with clear chronological context.  
  - Resolve ambiguity regarding formation years or re-designations.

- **Film and Media:**  
  - Confirm voice actors associated with specific characters in precise film versions (give exact film titles, release years, directors).  
  - Verify film genres, release years, and relationships between films (e.g., which version came first).  
  - Clarify whether remakes or live-action versions preceded or followed animated originals.

---

### Recommended Methodology for Query Generation:

1. **Parse the claim fully:**  
   Extract all key components—entities, dates, events, roles, locations.

2. **Analyze the notes to identify:**  
   - Confirmations or contradictions with the claim’s components.  
   - Missing or ambiguous data points.  
   - Nuances that may partially confirm or nuance the claim.

3. **Compose a clear, concise, neutral question that:**  
   - Explicitly includes all relevant entities and temporal/geographic details.  
   - Focuses on verifying disputed factual points or resolving ambiguities.  
   - Is open-ended—asks “Did X happen / belong / appear?” or “Was Y true in year Z or earlier?” or “Who performed role R in film F released in year Y?”  

4. **Avoid leading the query to presuppose truth or falsehood.**  

---

### Example Query Templates (to adapt as needed):

- “Which high schools are official members of [District Name] in [Location]? Is [School Name] included?”  
- “What census-designated places are served by each school in [District Name], specifically for [School Name]?”  
- “Who scored goals during the [Event Name] between [Team A] and [Team B] in [Year], and which team, colloquially called [Nickname], did the scorer represent?”  
- “Was [Military Unit Name] originally formed in year [Y] or earlier?”  
- “Did [Actor] provide the voice of [Character] in the [Year] film '[Exact Movie Title]' directed by [Director]? Was another version of this film released before then?”  
- “Did actor [Actor A] star alongside [Actors B and C] in the 1995 film '[Exact Film Title]' produced by [Production Company]?”  

---

### Summary:

Your output must be a **single, explicit, and clear fact-verification question** that can serve as a targeted information retrieval or expert verification prompt. It should leverage all available domain-specific details, highlight areas of uncertainty or conflict, and avoid any unsupported assumptions.

By doing so, the query you generate will maximize the potential to accurately confirm or refute the factual claim using the detailed notes.
