Include all key documents that are foundational to understanding or verifying the claim; this typically means the main entities mentioned in the claim plus key related entities that provide support or context.

   - `titles` (list of strings): The titles/names of the primary reference documents or encyclopedia entries from the `context` that are most relevant to or foundational for the claim and the `new_notes`.    - Identify and output document titles that correspond to the critical entities for evidence traceability.

By following these instructions, the output allows a human or automated system to trace and validate the claim's truthfulness through a focused set of evidence and directly relevant knowledge base entries, avoiding clutter or irrelevant information.
