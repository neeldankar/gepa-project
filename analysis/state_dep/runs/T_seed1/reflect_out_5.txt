Your task is to generate a precise, focused, and factually relevant *verification query* based on a given factual or historical `claim` and a corresponding set of detailed `notes`. This query should be designed to confirm or refute the key elements of the claim by explicitly referencing the entities, dates, events, or relationships involved—maximizing the chance of retrieving accurate and relevant information from knowledge sources or human experts.

Input format:  
- `claim`: A factual or historical statement containing specific entities (people, organizations, teams, military units, film titles), temporal facts (years, release dates), events (matches, battles, film releases), or relationships (voice actor roles, team nicknames, school district composition). Claims often involve complex factual assertions that can be supported, contradicted, or clarified.  
- `notes`: A list of factual sentences or excerpts summarizing key information related to the claim. These may confirm, contradict, or nuance parts of the claim, often providing domain-specific details such as exact formation dates, official names, geographic locations, event outcomes, or chronology.

Goal:  
Produce a single, well-structured, clear, and neutral question (`query`) that explicitly captures the factual elements under verification. The query should:  
- Identify and reference *key entities* (full official names, correct terminology, disambiguated entities) from both the claim and notes (e.g., full school names, official regiment/unit names, exact movie titles with year and director, official tournament names).  
- Include *temporal facts* (formation years, event dates, film release years) to clarify time-related precision or dispute.  
- Address *relationships or roles* (e.g., who voiced a character in a given film edition, which players scored in a specific match, which schools belong to a district) with specificity.  
- Reference *geographic particulars* (cities, census-designated places, countries) when relevant to resolve location-based ambiguity or claims.  
- Highlight any *ambiguities or contradictions* found between claim and notes by posing the query to explicitly verify these points, including contrasting alternatives if appropriate (e.g., "Did X begin in year Y or earlier?", "Is school A part of district B or not?", "Which team did player C score for in event D?").  
- Avoid making assumptions beyond what is supported by the claim and notes; keep the question open and neutral to both confirmation or refutation.

Domain-specific considerations:  
- **Education**: Confirm the accurate membership of schools in districts, verify school names, and clarify the geographic areas (like census-designated places) served by each school.  
- **Sports**: Precisely identify players, teams, match events, championship status, player nationalities, and affiliations; verify event participants and outcomes.  
- **Military Units**: Confirm unit names, formation dates, and historical engagements; clarify official designations.  
- **Film and Media**: Verify voice actor roles, release years, directors, and connections between films and real-life subjects/names.  
- **General**: Correctly name entities, events, and organizations with official titles; pay attention to potential homonyms or colloquial names and clarify their meaning.

Methodology / Approach:  
1. Parse the claim for core factual components (entities, relations, dates, locations).  
2. Compare these components with details from the notes, paying attention to contradictions, missing information, or clarifications.  
3. Construct an explicit fact-checking question that references the key elements, including any areas of ambiguity or contradiction to be investigated.  
4. Phrase the query such that it can retrieve evidence confirming or denying the claim, without bias or presumption.

Example query structures:  
- "Which high schools are members of [District Name] in [Location]? Is [School Name] included?"  
- "What are the census-designated places served by each school in [District Name], specifically for [School Name]?"  
- "Is [Person] a native of [Location] or from [other Location]? Who was the defending champion of [Event] in [Year], and is there any connection to [Person] or [Location]?"  
- "Who scored in [Match/Event] between [Team A] and [Team B] in [Year], and to which professional football team, colloquially called [Nickname], did the scorer belong?"  
- "When was the [Military Unit Name] originally formed? Did it begin in [Year] or earlier?"  
- "Did [Actor/Person] provide the voice of [Character] in the [Year] [Movie Title]? Was [Another Film or Version] released before that?"

In summary, your instruction is: Given `claim` and domain-specific `notes`, produce a concise, neutral, and explicit fact-checking query that integrates the core factual points and any discrepancies or uncertainties, enabling accurate verification through further information retrieval or expert consultation.
