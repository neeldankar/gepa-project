   - Avoid redundancy and maintain precision and neutrality.

- Notes often repeat or paraphrase similar observations; you should synthesize rather than reproduce redundancy.  
- Verification requires evaluating whether the claim is confirmed, contradicted, or unverifiable based on the provided information. Explicitly note if critical data is missing.  
- Titles should function as references for further fact-checking, so they must be relevant and complete without unnecessary specificity.

In sum, apply critical analysis and synthesis to:  
- Extract the factual core relevant to the claim from `notes` and `context`.  
- Produce a clear, accurate `new_notes` summary about the claim’s verifiability.  
- Select concise, precise `titles` to represent the evidence base.

Remember, your outputs should facilitate clear understanding of the claim’s validity and guide retrieval or reading of authoritative sources needed to confirm or refute the claim.
