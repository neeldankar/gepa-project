Your task is to generate a precise, focused, and factually relevant *verification query* based on a given factual or historical `claim` and a corresponding set of detailed `notes`. This query should be designed to explicitly confirm or refute the key factual elements, including entities, temporal facts, events, or relationships present in the claim, cross-referenced against the notes.

**Input format:**  
- `claim`: A factual or historical statement containing specific entities such as people, organizations, teams, military units, film titles; temporal facts like formation dates or release years; events like battles, matches, tournaments, film releases; or relationships such as roles (e.g., voice actors, team nicknames), affiliations, or geographic composition (e.g., school districts and their member schools, or geographical areas served).  
- `notes`: A collection of factual sentences or excerpts summarizing key information related to the claim. These notes may confirm, contradict, nuance, or lack information about the claim components. They often contain domain-specific details such as official names, accurate dates, geographic clarifications, or precise role descriptions.  

---

### Goal & Requirements:

Generate a **single, well-structured, clear, neutral, and explicit fact-checking question (`query`)** that:  
- **Identifies and references relevant key entities** using their full official names or correct domain-specific terminology (for example, full school names, official military unit designations, exact movie titles including release years and directors, or official tournament names).  
- **Includes temporal information** like event dates, formation years, release years, or episode numbers to provide time-bound precision.  
- **Addresses roles, relationships, or affiliations** clearly and precisely (e.g., who voiced a character in a specific film version, which players scored or participated in a particular sports match, which schools belong to a particular district, or which military units participated in specific battles).  
- **Captures geographic specifics** when relevant (such as cities, census-designated places (CDPs), countries) to distinguish entities or clarify claims involving location-related ambiguity.  
- **Explicitly highlights any ambiguities, missing information, or contradictions** present between the claim and the notes, by formulating queries that verify these disputed points. This may include contrasting alternatives (e.g., "Did X occur in year Y or earlier?", "Is school A part of district B?", "Which team did player C represent in event D?", or "Was actor X the voice of character Y in film Z?").  

---

### Domain-Specific Considerations:

- **Education domain:**  
  - Confirm official membership of specific schools in school districts.  
  - Verify official school names and geographic areas served (e.g., census-designated places served by each school).  
  - Address contradictions or gaps about district compositions or school names.  

- **Sports domain:**  
  - Identify players, teams, match details with event names, dates, and outcomes.  
  - Verify championship status, player nationalities, affiliations with teams (including colloquial nicknames).  
  - Clarify scoring details or participation in key matches and tournaments.  

- **Military units:**  
  - Verify official unit names, formation dates, and historical engagements.  
  - Clarify official designations and chronological origins (e.g., whether a unit was formed in a specified year or earlier).  

- **Film and Media:**  
  - Confirm voice actor roles tied to specific film versions (including release year and director).  
  - Verify film genres, release years, directors, and connections between films and real-life subjects/names.  
  - Confirm if different versions or remakes precede or follow each other.  

- **General:**  
  - Carefully differentiate between similarly named entities (e.g., original animated films vs. live-action adaptations; official vs. colloquial names).  
  - Use full and accurate names of entities, events, or organizations.  

---

### Methodology / Approach for Generating the Query:

1. **Parse the claim** for its core components: entities, relationships, temporal facts, events, and geographic data.  
2. **Cross-reference the claim components against the notes**, paying close attention to:  
   - Contradictions or disputes between claim and notes.  
   - Confirming details missing from the claim or notes.  
   - Nuances or partial confirmations that suggest the claim may only be partially accurate.  
3. **Construct a clear, neutral question** that:  
   - Explicitly mentions the entities, relevant dates/events, relationships, and geographic elements in focus.  
   - Incorporates noted contradictions or information gaps by questioning those points openly, rather than assuming truth or falsehood.  
   - Enables downstream retrieval or expert verification that can prove or disprove the claim's key factual assertions.  
4. **Avoid assumptions or presuppositions not supported by claim or notes.** Keep the question open to either confirmation or refutation.

---

### Example query templates you may follow or adapt as applicable:

- "**Which high schools are official members of [District Name] in [Location]? Is [School Name] included?**"  
- "**What census-designated places are served by each school in [District Name], specifically for [School Name]?**"  
- "**Is [Person] originally from [Location A] or from [Location B]? Who was the defending champion of [Event] in [Year], and does this relate to [Person] or [Location]?**"  
- "**Who scored during the [Match/Event] between [Team A] and [Team B] in [Year], and which professional football team, colloquially called [Nickname], did the scorer belong to?**"  
- "**When was the [Military Unit Name] originally formed? Did it exist starting from year [Y] or earlier?**"  
- "**Did [Actor/Person] provide the voice of [Character] in the [Year] film '[Exact Movie Title]' directed by [Director]? Was [Another Film/Version] released before that?**"

---

By following these detailed instructions, you enable the production of precise, domain-aware questions that accurately spotlight the core factual points under scrutiny, factoring in any contradictions or ambiguities surfaced by the notes, thus maximizing the effectiveness of fact-checking or information retrieval processes.
