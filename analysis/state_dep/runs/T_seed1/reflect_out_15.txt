Task Description:
You will receive two inputs: a factual claim (`claim`) and a list of relevant factual sentences or fragments (`notes`). Your objective is to produce a concise, specific, and well-formed `query` designed to verify, explore, or fact-check the given claim by referencing and integrating key elements from both the claim and the notes.

Input Format:
- `claim`: A statement containing named entities (people, institutions, films, events, dates, etc.), their relationships, attributes, or temporal sequences.
- `notes`: A list of sentences/fragments that may support, contradict, or provide background context relevant to the claim’s entities and relationships.

Output:
- `query`: A precise question or information request that explicitly reflects the key entities and relationships found in the claim and the notes, aimed at retrieving evidence or documents to confirm or refute the claim.

Detailed Instructions and Domain-Specific Guidance:

1. **Identify Entities and Relationships from the Claim:**
   - Extract core entities: people (with full names and any disambiguation), organizations, films (with release years), events (with seasons, years), dates, and other relevant subjects.
   - Identify relationships or attributes claimed: roles (e.g., commandant, director, actor), actions (e.g., conducted negotiations, released albums, competed), timelines (e.g., event order, period of participation), and quantitative claims (e.g., twice as many albums).
   - Capture any implied causal or temporal relationships within the claim.

2. **Analyze Notes for Support, Context, or Contradictions:**
   - Look for definitive facts confirming or denying entity attributes or relationships: founding dates, titles, periods of activity, names of relevant individuals related to the claim’s entities.
   - Highlight conflicts or gaps in the notes: missing data, contradictions, or absence of information about key claim elements.
   - Utilize background or clarifying details to frame the query accurately (e.g., associating a year to a season, specifying a film by title and release year).
   - Identify specifically named documents or sources mentioned in the notes that relate to the claim (e.g., Gerstein Report).

3. **Formulate a Precise and Comprehensive Query:**
   - Ensure the query explicitly references all crucial claim entities and their relationships.
   - Incorporate temporal elements (specific years, seasons, periods) or numeric aspects (e.g., album counts) where present or relevant.
   - Address contradictions or missing elements by including those entities or relationships in the query to prompt retrieval of confirming or clarifying evidence.
   - Use domain- and entity-specific disambiguation (e.g., “Wonder Woman (2017 film)” rather than just “Wonder Woman”) for clarity.
   - Structure the query to target all core factual points needed to adjudicate the truth of the claim.
   - Examples of valid query focuses:
     * Temporal confirmation: “Was X founded before Y?”
     * Role verification: “Did actor A appear in film B directed by person C?”
     * Quantitative comparisons: “How many albums have artist A and artist B each released?”
     * Sequence or participation: “Did competitor X participate continuously from year A to B, including season Z?”

4. **Clarity, Specificity, and Completeness:**
   - Make the query unambiguous and focused to guide retrieval towards relevant documents or evidence.
   - Avoid vagueness or overly broad questions.
   - Cover all significant aspects and entities involved in the claim, including those missing or contradicted in the notes, to allow comprehensive fact-checking.
   - Maintain consistent naming and identification of entities as found in both claim and notes.

5. **Utilizing the Reasoning Approach Exemplified:**
   - Synthesize a brief internal reasoning (not part of the output) referencing how the notes confirm or contradict parts of the claim.
   - Use the reasoning to identify what exact missing information or confirmation the query should seek.
   - The final query should be the distilled targeted question emerging from the reasoning.

Summary:
Your task is to generate focused, factual, and entity-rich queries that integrate all relevant information from both the claim and notes. The queries should explicitly incorporate key entities, their relationships, timelines, roles, and numeric comparisons as needed. If the notes reveal contradictions or missing facts, the query must seek clarification including those elements to enable comprehensive retrieval or verification. The goal is to support precise adjudication of the claim’s accuracy by facilitating retrieval of all critical relevant documents or evidence.
