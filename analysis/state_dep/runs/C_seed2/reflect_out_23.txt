You will be provided with three inputs:

1. `claim` - a factual statement or assertion that requires verification, clarification, or detailed explanation.
2. `notes` - a list of sentences extracted from various sources that contain relevant factual details potentially supporting, refuting, or contextualizing the claim.
3. `context` - a list of background documents or summaries related to entities, events, or topics referenced in the claim or notes. These provide additional encyclopedic or domain-specific information to aid verification or understanding.

Your task is to generate two outputs: `new_notes` and `titles`.

---

### Output 1: new_notes

- Select the most relevant, concise, and factually accurate notes from the input `notes` that directly relate to the claim.
- Only include notes that provide explicit evidence or essential context necessary to verify or explain the claim.
- Remove redundancies, irrelevant details, or contradictory data that do not directly help verify or clarify the claim.
- When appropriate, summarize or slightly rephrase notes for clarity and coherence, but preserve original factual content.
- The `new_notes` collectively should form a factual foundation enabling a reader to assess the accuracy of the claim fully.

### Output 2: titles

- From the `context` input, provide a list of key document or entity titles that are most relevant to the claim and selected `new_notes`.
- Titles should correspond to the main entities, persons, organizations, events, works (e.g., songs, films), or topics explicitly mentioned or implied in the claim and notes.
- Prioritize documents that best contextualize or verify the claim's main subjects, including domain-specific items like songwriter names for music claims or regiment names for military history claims.
- Avoid including loosely related or minor background titles that do not contribute directly to understanding or verifying the claim.
- The list of titles should serve as clear references supporting the claim and refined notes.

---

### Domain-Specific and Task-Specific Guidance

- Claims may relate to:
  - Music: songwriting and performance credits, band lineups, song origins, album releases, genre distinctions.
  - Geography: birthplaces, city population ranks, administrative regions.
  - Military history: regiment formation years, regiment origins, historical or media portrayals.
  - Film and acting careers: actor nationalities, film debuts, role prominence, co-star relationships, film genres, soundtrack performers.
  - Sports: club memberships, league participation, sponsorships.

- When notes describe multiple individuals, bands, or organizations, attentively distinguish among their roles and relevance to the claim (e.g., who wrote vs. performed a song, who is lead singer vs. backing vocalist, who belongs to which football league).

- Use the combination of `notes` and `context` to resolve ambiguities, such as:
  - Differentiating songwriters from performers.
  - Confirming accurate nationality or birthplace of persons.
  - Verifying league sponsorship or team affiliations.
  - Clarifying band genre and member counts.

- Weed out contradictions and unsupported assertions from the notes; focus on verified, corroborated facts.

- The selected `new_notes` and `titles` should together provide a coherent, factual basis allowing a reader to determine the claim’s validity confidently.

---

### Strategy to Complete The Task

- First, analyze the claim carefully to identify critical entities, events, or assertions requiring verification.
- Review all notes to find those that directly address these points; eliminate duplicates and irrelevant sentences.
- Corroborate or clarify details by cross-referencing with the `context` documents to enrich or confirm the extracted facts.
- Summarize and consolidate the final notes to be clear, concise, and factual.
- Select the most representative and authoritative titles from the context that match the refined notes and claim terms.
- Ignore explanatory reasoning in outputs; only produce `new_notes` and `titles`.

---

By following these instructions, you will provide well-supported, logically distilled notes and reference titles that enable effective verification or understanding of the claim in its domain-specific context.
