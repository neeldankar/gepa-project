You will be given three fields as input: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- `claim` is a factual statement or assertion that needs to be verified or understood in detail.
- `notes` is a list of sentences containing relevant factual details extracted from various sources that can help assess, confirm, or refute the claim.
- `context` is a list of background information documents or short summaries related to entities, events, or topics mentioned in the claim or notes. These can provide additional encyclopedic or domain-specific knowledge to support or clarify facts from the notes.

Your task is to generate two fields as output: `new_notes` and `titles`.

1. `new_notes`:
   - Select and refine the most relevant, concise, and factually accurate notes from the input `notes` related directly to the claim.
   - Include only those notes that provide clear evidence or essential context that support verification or explanation of the claim.
   - Remove redundant or irrelevant information.
   - Summarize or slightly rephrase notes if needed to enhance clarity but preserve the factual content.
   - The `new_notes` should collectively allow a reader to understand and verify the claim effectively.

2. `titles`:
   - Provide a list of key document or entity titles from the `context` input that are highly relevant to the claim and notes.
   - These titles should represent main topics, persons, organizations, songs, films, places, or other entities that contextualize or verify the claim.
   - Prioritize documents that directly correspond to names or subjects explicitly mentioned in the claim or the refined notes.
   - The titles serve as references or sources the claim and notes relate to.

Additional domain-specific considerations:
- Claims may relate to music songwriting and performance credits (lyricists, bands, albums).
- Claims may involve birthplaces, geographical facts (cities, population ranks, administrative regions).
- Claims may involve historical military units (infantry regiments), their formation years, and connections to media portrayals (films).
- When notes mention multiple specific individuals, groups, or organizations, carefully distinguish their specific roles or relevance to the claim.
- When multiple closely related titles or entities exist, include those that best cover the claim’s main subjects (for instance, include the main songwriter’s name if songwriter-related).
- Use contextual knowledge from both the notes and context to resolve ambiguities (e.g., a song’s writer versus performer, birthplaces of individuals, regiment origins).
- The refined notes and titles should collectively facilitate verification or understanding of the claim with well-supported evidence.

Reasoning or explanation of your selection is not required in the output but can guide your decisions during processing.
