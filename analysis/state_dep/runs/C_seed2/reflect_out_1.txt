You will be given a claim (a factual statement that may or may not be accurate) and a set of notes which contain detailed, factual information relevant to verifying or understanding the claim. Your task is to produce a precise and focused query (or set of queries) that can be used to retrieve the necessary documents or verify critical facts needed to assess the claim's truthfulness.

When generating the query, consider the following:

1. **Purpose:** The query should be designed to retrieve missing or uncertain factual information that the notes indicate is necessary to fully verify the claim.

2. **Content focus:** Use key entities, dates, locations, names, and relationships mentioned in the claim and notes. The query should target documents about these core elements.

3. **Clarify ambiguities:** If the claim includes imprecise or potentially incorrect information (e.g., incorrect nationality, ambiguous references), the query should explicitly seek to clarify these aspects. For example, ask about the correct identity, nationality, or attributes of an entity.

4. **Cover all critical points:** Ensure the query addresses all parts of the claim that require verification or further evidence, including comparisons, chronological order, or geographic details.

5. **Use question format:** Formulate the query as questions that can be clearly answered by factual documents, such as:
   - “What is the birth date of [Person A] and [Person B]?”
   - “Who directed [Film Title] and what is their nationality?”
   - “Where is [Company] headquartered and where is [Airport] located?”
   - “Which band recorded [Song/Album] and who is their lead guitarist?”

6. **Avoid redundancy:** Avoid including information that is already clearly established by the notes, unless it is needed for context or confirmation alongside missing facts.

7. **Disambiguation:** If the claim references entities inaccurately, your query should aim to disambiguate or correct that misinformation by focusing on authoritative facts (e.g., if the claim says a director is Ukrainian but notes say the director is American, the query should ask about the actual director’s nationality and identity).

Overall, your goal is to produce a well-structured query (or a minimal set of focused queries) that would allow a system or human to retrieve the precise additional documents required to evaluate the claim accurately. Your queries should be clear, concise, and directly relevant to the entities, dates, or facts involved in the claim’s verification.

Input format:
- `claim`: A factual statement or claim to verify.
- `notes`: A list of factual statements and information pieces relevant to the claim.

Output format:
- `query`: A natural language question or series of questions designed to retrieve missing but necessary information to fully verify or evaluate the claim.

Example pattern:
Given the claim and notes, identify what factual information is uncertain or missing, then create specific queries targeting those gaps, ensuring you reflect any necessary clarifications or disambiguations implied by incorrect or incomplete information within the claim.
