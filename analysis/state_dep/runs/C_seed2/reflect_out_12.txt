You will be given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- `claim`: A statement or assertion that may require verification, clarification, or elaboration.
- `notes`: A list of factual sentences or extracted information relevant to the claim. The notes may contain redundancies, repetitions, or partially overlapping facts.
- `context`: A list of detailed documents, descriptions, or text snippets that provide background information, supporting evidence, or source data related to the claim.

Your task is to produce two output fields, `new_notes` and `titles`, by performing the following steps:

1. **Reasoning and Fact Extraction:**
   - Analyze the claim in conjunction with the given notes and context to understand the factual correctness and scope of the claim.
   - Identify which facts from the notes are relevant, verified, and sufficient to explain or adjudicate the claim.
   - Remove redundant, irrelevant, or contradictory notes in order to distill a concise and accurate set of `new_notes` that clearly support or refute parts of the claim.
   - Summarize complex inferences from multiple notes or context snippets into clearer, consolidated facts suitable for `new_notes`.
   - Avoid including repeated or near-duplicate statements.
   
2. **Document (Title) Selection:**
   - Identify key source documents or entities mentioned in the context that are critical for understanding or verifying the claim.
     - These typically correspond to article or document titles (e.g., names of bands, films, persons, albums, or other named entities directly tied to the claim).
   - The selected `titles` should cover all essential aspects or main subjects referenced or referenced indirectly in the claim and underlying notes.
   - Strive to retrieve all (ideally 3 or more) pertinent titles, aiming for comprehensive coverage of relevant entities.
   
3. **Additional Considerations:**
   - The `context` may include closely related topics or similarly named items; choose those best matching the claim’s main subjects.
   - Domain-specific knowledge: 
     - Proper identification of named entities (persons, bands, films, songs, albums, roles, dates) is crucial.
     - Recognize relationships between entities (e.g., band members and their genres, voice actors and films, film directors and their works).
     - Temporal or factual ordering (e.g., release dates of films or music) may be necessary to verify claims.
   - Preserve factual accuracy and avoid introducing unsupported assertions.
   - When contradictions or errors in the claim are detected, clarify the correct information in `new_notes`.
   - Use concise, factual sentences without unnecessary verbosity.

**Output Format:**
- `new_notes`: A de-duplicated, precise list of informative fact statements synthesizing the relevant notes and validating or refuting the parts of the claim.
- `titles`: A list of important document or entity titles from the context that provide core evidence or background for the claim.

In summary, your task is to carefully verify and clarify the claim by filtering and summarizing the notes, while selecting all key contextual documents (titles) necessary to support your analysis. This ensures the final outputs represent accurate, well-grounded, and focused explanations with their corresponding source references.
