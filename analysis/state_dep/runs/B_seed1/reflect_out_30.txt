Task Description: 

You are given three input fields: 
- `claim`: a factual or semi-factual statement that often references people, organizations, events, or other named entities, sometimes combining multiple facts incorrectly or ambiguously. 
- `notes`: a list of textual evidence or fact statements relevant to verifying or explaining the claim, which includes corrections, expansions, or clarifications about the entities or events mentioned in the claim. The notes may point out inaccuracies, missing information, or key factual connections.
- `context`: a list of detailed encyclopedia-like entries (or document extracts) that provide authoritative background information on relevant entities, teams, seasons, people, or events referenced in the claim and notes.

Your goal is to produce two output fields:

1. `new_notes`: a concise, focused subset or synthesis of notes that:
   - Accurately summarizes the factual corrections and clarifications related to the claim,
   - Highlights key distinctions or corrections regarding people, teams, events, or data,
   - Removes duplicates or overly redundant notes,
   - Captures relevant facts that clearly explain why the claim is true, false, or unsupported,
   - Reflects well-contextualized knowledge from both the notes and the context inputs,
   - Emphasizes domain-specific details, such as:
     * Specific biographical details about individuals (e.g., athlete positions, career timelines, coaching roles),
     * Correct team associations (e.g., which team a person coached vs. played for),
     * Precise historical sports seasons identifiers (e.g., 28th season refers to a specific year, and who won),
     * Ethnic or national identity distinctions (e.g., Native American heritage),
     * Distinctions between different similarly named teams or competitions,
     * Information about the validity or falsity of mixed claims combining unrelated facts.
   
2. `titles`: a list of relevant document or entity titles derived from the context that directly relate to the claim or notes, to indicate the key background sources for the new_notes. These should:
   - Include primary entities (people, teams, seasons) mentioned in the claim or needed to understand contradicting or supporting evidence,
   - Reflect the best evidence sources that clarify or verify parts of the claim,
   - Be focused on core entities rather than broad or unrelated topics,
   - Attempt to retrieve all gold or authoritative documents that unlock the key facts behind the claim whenever possible.

Reasoning Approach:

- Analyze the claim carefully to identify which entity or fact combinations are potentially conflated or incorrect.
- Correlate the notes and context to disambiguate and fact-check the claim's components.
- Identify domain-specific knowledge from the context (e.g., sports seasons, team rosters, athlete nationalities, coaching history).
- Remove redundant or repetitive notes; unify multiple similar notes into more distilled, informative points.
- Select titles representing the authoritative sources backing the summarized notes.
- When information is missing (e.g., no context on album counts), state clearly that data is unavailable and produce empty titles.
- Consider various domains such as sports history (football, racing), music discography, or other domain-specific knowledge areas presented in the examples.

Formatting:

- `new_notes` should be a list of clear, standalone factual statements addressing the claim’s veracity and relevant contextual details.
- `titles` should be a list of document or article titles (strings) extracted from the input sources that underpin the new_notes.

Overall, your output should effectively function as a fact-focused, well-informed correction/explanation of the claim grounded in provided evidence and authoritative context, including niche domain facts critical to accurate verification.
