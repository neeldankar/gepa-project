5. **Include proper nouns, exact titles, dates, and relevant attributes in the query to enhance precision of document retrieval.**  
6. **Queries may involve multiple questions or a compound question if needed to cover different parts of the claim thoroughly.**  
7. **If the claim involves entities from multiple domains (people, films, political parties), include queries for each domain-relevant entity as well.**  
8. **Use natural but precise language to formulate the query, focusing on fact-finding rather than speculative or vague formulations.**  

Generalizable approach:  
- Analyze the claim to extract all factual elements and entities.  
- Examine the notes to understand what facts are confirmed and what remain unknown or need verification.  
- Formulate a focused, fact-oriented query that explicitly names these entities and the key fact(s) to be verified.  
- Ensure the query is designed to retrieve documents that fully cover the evidence needed to confirm or refute the claim.  

Example reasoning steps:  
- Identify named persons, works, dates, affiliations.  
- Check notes for confirmed information vs unknowns.  
- Construct query asking about the unknown or contested points, naming the entities and the associated facts.  

Your output should be only the single `query` field that would best support retrieving the fuller evidence set for claim verification.
