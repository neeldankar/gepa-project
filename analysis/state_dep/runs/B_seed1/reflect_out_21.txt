Task Overview:  
You will be given three inputs: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your goal is to produce two outputs: `new_notes` and `titles`. The purpose is to verify or explain the factual claim by distilling and clarifying key pieces of evidence from the provided notes and grounding them in the relevant documents or contexts.

Input Format:  
- `claim`: A factual statement involving real-world people, places, events, creative works (such as films, operas, songs), companies, or geographic locations. The claim may assert relationships, timelines, authorship, ownership, membership, or other verifiable facts.  
- `notes`: A collection (usually a list) of sentences or facts pertinent to the claim. These often contain overlapping, redundant, or extraneous information and may mix relevant and irrelevant details. Notes specify entities, relationships, dates, compositions, attributions, and other details.  
- `context`: A set of detailed textual excerpts from background articles, biographies, definitions, or descriptions related to the entities or events in the claim and notes. The context provides authoritative clarifications, disambiguations, and detailed facts that can be used to validate or enrich the notes.

Output Requirements:  
1. `new_notes`:  
   - Filter and reformulate the original notes into a concise, non-redundant, and factually accurate set of facts directly relevant to verifying or explaining the claim.   
   - Ensure clarity by precisely stating relationships, roles, dates, and entity attributes.  
   - Remove duplicates and near-duplicates.  
   - Correct or clarify ambiguous or incomplete facts using contextual information.  
   - Include only key facts essential for assessing the claim—exclude unrelated or extraneous details.  
   - When multiple similar entities exist (e.g., operas with identical titles by different composers), clearly distinguish them and maintain logical consistency with the claim.  
   - Use exact terms for entities (e.g., full names, correct roles, exact dates) gleaned from context to enhance accuracy and clarity.

2. `titles`:  
   - Provide a list of document or entity titles corresponding to the key facts in `new_notes`.  
   - Titles serve as references to the contextual sources that best support or explain the facts in `new_notes`.  
   - Select all critical sources necessary for comprehensive factual coverage, aiming for a balance between breadth and relevance.  
   - Avoid including unrelated or loosely connected titles, as they reduce precision.

Domain and Task-Specific Details to Keep in Mind:  
- Entities can be historical persons (often with birth and death years), musical groups (with membership counts), films or operas (with release/composition years, creators, and contributors), companies (with ownership and locations), songs, and geographical locations (connected hierarchically: city > state/province > country).  
- Use lifespan overlap or active dates to confirm if people could have interacted or collaborated.  
- Accurately attribute creative works—know the correct composer, director, author, or producer, especially if works share similar names.  
- When claims mention geographic or organizational ownership, verify hierarchical or corporate relationships (e.g., hotel as part of a casino owned by a parent corporation).  
- Verify temporal claims by confirming correct dates—note situations where a date in the claim might be slightly inaccurate and correct it if the evidence supports that.  
- Recognize correct genres or classifications—e.g., don’t mistake band genres, and clarify if a claim misclassifies an entity.  
- Note if any aspect in the claim is false or inaccurate based on evidence and whether the claim is partially or fully supported.

Reasoning and Strategy for Synthesis:  
- Identify the core factual assertion or relationship claimed.  
- Examine all notes to select directly relevant facts supporting or refuting the claim.  
- Leverage the context to resolve ambiguity, correctness of entity details, role definitions, and chronological consistency.  
- Collapse redundant information into succinct, clear statements.  
- Address any incorrect or misleading portions of the claim by clarifying or correcting facts in your `new_notes`.  
- When multiple documents or entities relate to key facts, include all appropriate titles reflective of those sources.  
- Reject extraneous or loosely relevant facts and titles to maintain high precision and focused evidence.

Generalizable Strategy:  
- First, parse the claim’s key elements: who, what, when, where, and relationships involved.  
- Systematically search notes for relevant supporting or contradictory facts.  
- Cross-check and disambiguate entities using context.  
- Synthesize a minimal but sufficient set of new notes that directly support factual verification or explanation.  
- Map each fact to corroborating documents, producing a concise list of titles serving as evidence sources.

Summary:  
Your main objective is to transform noisy or repetitive input notes into a concise, accurate, and contextually informed set of facts (`new_notes`) that validate or clarify the claim. Simultaneously, select a focused list of supporting document/entity titles (`titles`) from the context to anchor your evidence. This precision ensures that any future verification based on your output is straightforward, well-supported, and unambiguous.
