Task Description:

You will receive three inputs—`claim`, `notes`, and `context`—and your goal is to produce two outputs: `new_notes` and `titles`. Your outputs must form a concise, accurate, and evidence-based synthesis that either verifies or explains the claim using only the provided information.

Inputs:

- `claim`: A factual or verification statement concerning real-world entities or events (e.g., individuals, creative works, sports teams, geographical places). Claims may combine multiple assertions that require careful coordinated verification of relationships, timelines, roles, or attributions.

- `notes`: A collection of potentially relevant sentences or facts, which may include overlaps, redundancies, or extraneous details. Notes often describe biographical data, dates, hierarchical geographic details, roles, events, or production credits.

- `context`: Background information related to entities or concepts appearing in the claim or notes. This includes richly detailed entity descriptions, timelines, biographical data, hierarchical geographic information, domain-specific historical summaries, or authoritative identification of roles and contributions.

Outputs:

- `new_notes`:   
  Produce a distilled, coherent, and rephrased subset of the input notes containing only the essential facts that directly support or refute the claim. These notes should:  
  - Be concise, yet sufficiently comprehensive to cover all key facts underpinning the verification of the claim.  
  - Remove duplicates or near-duplicates.  
  - Clarify temporal relationships (e.g., lifespans, event dates) and geographic hierarchies (city > state > country).  
  - Accurately differentiate roles and contributions—such as composer vs. director, player positions, or organizational affiliations—to avoid misattribution.  
  - Disambiguate similarly named entities using contextual features such as dates, creator names, or roles (e.g., different operas with the same title by different composers).  
  - Integrate relevant clarifications or enhancements drawn from context to improve factual precision and clarity.  
  - Avoid logical inconsistencies, confirming that the facts align with domain norms (e.g., sports positions are plausible for described roles).  
  - Summarize complex facts into clear, singular statements wherever possible.

- `titles`:  
  Provide a list of document or entity titles that correspond to the evidence underlying `new_notes`. These titles should:  
  - Precisely correspond to domain-relevant sources for all facts in `new_notes`.  
  - Be comprehensive but focused, excluding irrelevant or tangential documentation.  
  - Cover all aspects needed for full verification or explanation of the claim.

Core Reasoning & Strategy:

1. **Identify the claim’s factual core:**  
   Extract the main factual assertions needed for verification—these may include temporal sequences (e.g., event dates vs. actor participation), hierarchical relationships (geographical or organizational), or roles (e.g., who composed, who performed, who played what position).

2. **Filter for relevance and precision:**  
   From input notes, extract only those facts directly relevant to the claim’s core entities, relationships, dates, or events. Eliminate extraneous, duplicate, or off-topic information.

3. **Leverage context for disambiguation and verification:**  
   Use the detailed background in context to:  
   - Distinguish between entities with identical or similar names by cross-referencing dates, roles, or other attributes.  
   - Confirm accurate geographic hierarchies.  
   - Validate timelines or lifespan overlaps to establish contemporaneity or event order.  
   - Precisely attribute roles or contributions to correct persons or entities.  
   - Clarify sponsorship, affiliations, or organizational relationships.  
   - Detect and resolve potential logical inconsistencies.

4. **Synthesize a clear, precise summary:**  
   Integrate filtered notes and contextual clarifications into a logically coherent, accurate, and non-redundant summary that directly supports or refutes the claim without verbosity.

5. **Select authoritative titles:**  
   Include all relevant titles from notes or context that support the summarized facts, ensuring coverage of the entire factual basis for the claim’s verification.

Domain-Specific Knowledge & Considerations:

- Entities can include historical persons, artists, musicians, actors, sports players, teams, companies, events, geographic locations, or creative works (operas, albums, TV series).  
- Temporal verification encompasses cross-checking birth/death dates, event or release dates, cast joining dates, etc.  
- Geographic locations should be understood in hierarchical form (e.g., Memphis is in Tennessee, USA).  
- Roles must be accurately identified and attributed (e.g., distinguishing composer from director; forward vs. goalie; frontman vs. supporting musician).  
- When multiple similarly named entities exist (e.g., different operas named "Mediterranean"), rely on dates, creators, or role details for disambiguation.  
- Avoid logical contradictions (e.g., a goalie leading a forward strike force on a football team would be implausible).  
- Recognize domain norms and conventions (e.g., typical instruments or roles associated with band members, or typical sponsorship practices in sports).  
- Acknowledge sequencing or timing information to infer continuities or changes (e.g., member departure dates inferred from last album release and tour dates).  

Formatting Requirements:

- Output `new_notes` as a list of concise, factual statements each capturing a key supporting or refuting fact.  
- Output `titles` as a list of exact document or entity titles that correspond to the evidence sources for facts in the `new_notes`.

Examples based on prior cases:

- Confirming song authorship by identifying the frontman’s name, role, and nationality; confirming different nationality of another named person for a contrastive claim.  
- Confirming casting and premiere dates of TV series played by named actors, validating or refuting specific premiere dates.  
- Identifying the musical genre of two bands related by collaboration or membership, distinguishing metal subgenres with progressive rock elements and classifying accordingly.  

This task requires careful, domain-aware reasoning to synthesize accurate and concise factual verification from overlapping input data and detailed context. Prioritize clarity, precision, and elimination of redundancy to provide a trustworthy factual summary and source citation for the claim.
