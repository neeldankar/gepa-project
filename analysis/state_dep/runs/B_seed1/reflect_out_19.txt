Task Overview:
You are tasked with taking three input fields — `claim`, `notes`, and `context` — and producing two output fields — `new_notes` and `titles`. Your goal is to synthesize a tightly focused, accurate, and evidence-supported subset of notes and associated source titles that verify, support, or refute the given claim based on the detailed factual inputs and background context.

Input Details:
- `claim`: A factual or verification statement involving real-world entities, events, or concepts. Claims typically assert relationships, timelines, participation, or sponsorships involving people, places, organizations, creative works (operas, films), sports teams, or historical facts.
- `notes`: A list of sentences or facts relevant to the claim. Notes often contain overlapping information, redundancies, or irrelevant details. They include discrete facts about entities’ biographical data, event dates, memberships, roles, productions, geographical hierarchies, or sponsorships.
- `context`: Detailed text excerpts drawn from relevant articles, biographies, definitions, and domain-specific sources. Context clarifies and disambiguates entities, relationships, timelines, and roles, enabling precise filtering and fact verification.

Output Requirements:
1. `new_notes`:
   - Produce a concise yet comprehensive subset of the input notes that directly supports or contradicts the claim.
   - Remove duplicates and near-duplicates.
   - Clarify relationships, dates, entity identities, and roles.
   - Use contextual knowledge to disambiguate similar entities (e.g., different operas of the same name by different composers), specify nationality, and ensure factual accuracy.
   - Cover all essential facts needed to verify the claim without including irrelevant or extraneous information.

2. `titles`:
   - Provide a list of relevant document, entity, or source titles corresponding to authoritative context excerpts that best support the facts in `new_notes`.
   - Titles act as references for the evidence supporting or explaining the claim.
   - Include all key documents covering principal entities, events, organizations, or productions referenced in `new_notes`.
   - Avoid unrelated or only tangentially relevant titles to maintain precision.

Reasoning and Domain-Specific Guidance:
- Understand the claim’s core assertion: identify what factual relationships, timelines, or roles are being asserted.
- Identify from the notes all facts that directly bear on verifying or refuting the claim.
- Use the context to verify the accuracy of these facts by checking details such as:
    - Biographical dates and nationality of persons (e.g., birth/death years, nationality).
    - Lifespan or career timeline overlaps to verify claims about historical co-existence or participation.
    - Distinctions between works with similar titles (e.g., differentiate operatic works by composer and premiere date).
    - Accurate attributions of creator roles: director, composer, editor, producer, voice actor.
    - Hierarchical geography: city, county, state, country relationships.
    - Sports team founding dates, player tenures, managerial seasons, and sponsorship arrangements.
- Filter out notes that provide redundant restatements or unrelated facts.
- Clarify ambiguities, for example:
    - State explicitly that a named person is American or French if nationality is key.
    - Confirm the exact name of the league or association relevant to clubs.
    - Correct claims regarding sponsors or affiliations by referencing current accurate data.
- When multiple entities with similar contexts or overlapping data exist (e.g., multiple seasons, teams, or individuals), distinctly identify which entity or timeframe applies.
- Ensure temporal consistency to validate or invalidate claims involving dates.
- Use note reformulation to generate clear, precise, and standalone factual statements that do not rely on unverified assumptions.

Operational Strategy:
- Begin by carefully parsing the claim to understand its key components.
- Scan notes to extract all pertinent facts directly related to those components.
- Cross-reference these with the context to confirm correctness and accuracy.
- Eliminate redundant, overlapping, or extraneous facts.
- Reformulate notes as needed for clarity and completeness.
- Compile a minimal set of notes covering all necessary points to validate or refute the claim.
- Identify titles from the context that contain authoritative elaborations of these key facts.
- Prioritize documents that directly cover major entities or events confirming or denying the claim.

This approach ensures a precise and well-supported response, balancing comprehensiveness and conciseness, tailored to domain-specific factual verification involving historical, biographical, geographical, cultural, and sports-related data.
