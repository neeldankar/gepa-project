Task Description:
You are given three fields as input: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Your task is to produce two fields as output: `new_notes` and `titles`.

- `claim` is a factual statement or assertion that requires verification or support.
- `notes` is a list of extracted observations or facts related to the claim.
- `context` is a list of background information or documents relevant to the entities or topics mentioned.

Your job is to synthesize and refine the evidence from both the `notes` and `context` to produce:

1. `new_notes`: a concise, accurate, and focused summary of relevant facts that:
   - Clearly relate to verifying or refuting the claim.
   - Remove redundancy and irrelevant information.
   - Highlight key points from both `notes` and `context` that are directly pertinent.
   - Address any ambiguous or unsupported elements, clarifying when evidence is lacking or uncertain.
   - Incorporate domain-specific factual information and nuanced details that support or challenge the claim.

2. `titles`: a short list of document or topic titles that represent the most relevant sources or key subjects involved in the claim and the refined notes, useful for retrieval and further research.

Detailed Guidelines and Domain Specific Insights:

- Identify and isolate named entities (people, places, works of art, publications, films, etc.) mentioned in the claim and evidence.
- Distinguish between facts supported by strong evidence and those that are missing or contradicted.
- Clarify relationships such as authorship, publication types (e.g., magazine vs. book), geographic locations, and chronology relevant to claim verification.
- If multiple similarly named entities exist (e.g., multiple cathedrals named St Paul’s), use contextual clues to disambiguate and specify the most relevant entity tied to the claim.
- Address nationality, roles, and other biographical facts accurately, and note discrepancies when claims contradict documented data (e.g., a nationality claim that does not match evidence).
- When uncertain, explicitly mention the insufficiency of evidence to confirm or deny elements of the claim.
- Avoid introducing unsupported assertions beyond what is available in the notes and context.
- Titles should ideally be the names of the most pertinent documents, people, or entities that best summarize the refined new notes; keep them informative and succinct.

Generalizable Strategy:

1. Read the claim carefully to understand all components needing verification.
2. Cross-check all `notes` for relevant information connected to claim components.
3. Review the `context` for corroborating or disconfirming details.
4. Synthesize insights, prune redundancies, and summarize clearly in `new_notes`.
5. Identify and list the most relevant document titles or key entity names in `titles`.
6. Flag any unverifiable or unsupported claim parts explicitly in `new_notes`.

Remember that the goal is to produce a clean, accurate, and informative summary of evidence with clear references to the most relevant source materials, facilitating fact verification and knowledge retrieval.
