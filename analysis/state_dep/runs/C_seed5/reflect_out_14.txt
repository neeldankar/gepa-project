Task Description:  
You will be provided with two inputs:  
- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion containing named entities (people, films, bands, roles, dates, etc.) that may require verification or additional factual evidence.  
- `notes`: A list of textual snippets supplying partial information relevant to verifying the claim. These notes may confirm parts of the claim, provide related information, or highlight contradictions or missing facts.

Your primary goal is to generate the output field `query` — one or more concise, focused, and precise questions aimed at retrieving the exact missing or ambiguous factual information necessary to verify the claim. Your query should be designed to guide retrieval of authoritative documents or data that directly fill knowledge gaps established by comparing the claim's assertions with the notes.

Domain-Specific Knowledge and Key Considerations:  
- Claims involve named entities such as actors, directors, musicians, characters, films, bands, dates of birth, nationalities, professions, and their interrelations.  
- Often, a claim asserts complex relationships (e.g., familial ties, roles in specific works, military service, award achievements, nationalities) or relative facts (e.g., ages, contributions).  
- Notes frequently contain overlapping, confirmatory, or contradictory information but rarely resolve all uncertainties.  
- Your queries must specifically target the unresolved factual elements — such as missing identities, ambiguous roles, unconfirmed dates, or uncertain relationships — rather than broad or generic questions.  
- Queries should consider domain-specific conventions, for example:  
   * Verifying whether a named person actually performed a particular role (e.g., voice actor or musician) in a specified work.  
   * Confirming birthdates or ages when claims depend on relative age or timing.  
   * Checking familial relationships and their involvement in specific films or productions.  
   * Linking awards, festivals, and military service records to relevant individuals or films.  
   * Aligning nationality or origin of entities involved with the claim to ensure accuracy.  

Generalizable Strategy:  
1. Parse and analyze the claim to isolate all factual assertions needing verification (entities, relationships, attributes).  
2. Review the notes to identify which components are fully supported, partially supported, contradicted, or missing.  
3. Identify critical knowledge gaps essential to verifying the claim — these are the specific facts or relationships the notes do not resolve.  
4. Formulate a minimal yet complete set of focused questions, balancing precision and completeness, that will retrieve the missing facts.  
5. Avoid overly broad queries; each question should target a specific unknown or ambiguous element, such as:  
   - "Who is the [role/identity] of [named person/film]?"  
   - "Does [person] have [attribute/profession/relationship]?"  
   - "What is the birthdate or nationality of [person]?"  
   - "Did [individual A], related to [individual B], participate in [work] (year)?"  
   - "Which film won [award] at [festival] and who were the notable actors with [specific background]?"  

Additional Notes on Crafting Queries:  
- If multiple entities or relationships are unknown but related, combine them efficiently into a single query to reduce redundancy while maintaining clarity.  
- Ensure the domain context guides the question wording to retrieve relevant authoritative documents (film databases, biographies, award histories, military service records, etc.).  
- Use precise entity names, film titles, event names, or years to anchor the query to specific facts needed.  

Your output should be the formulated `query` string(s) that, if answered, would enable definitive verification or refutation of the provided claim given the partial information and gaps identified in the notes.

Example Application:  
If the claim states a French star in a film voiced a character in a 1994 animated film, but notes confirm the character was voiced by American actresses and do not connect the French star to the film, the query focuses on verifying the French star's identity and possible voice acting credits in 1994, e.g.:  
"Who is the French star of [film]? Did she perform any voice acting roles in animated films released in 1994, especially voicing Nala or similar characters?"

By following this approach with the stated domain knowledge, your queries will effectively pinpoint the missing information, assisting retrieval systems to gather authoritative evidence for fact verification.
