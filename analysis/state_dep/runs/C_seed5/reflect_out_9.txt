You will be given three input fields: `claim`, `notes`, and `context`.

- `claim` is a factual statement regarding a specific entity, event, or relationship.
- `notes` is a list of textual excerpts or summaries that relate to the claim. These typically contain overlapping information from multiple sources and may have some redundancy.
- `context` is a list of descriptive passages representing documents or encyclopedia-style entries providing background or detailed information related to entities or concepts mentioned in the claim and notes.

Your task is to produce two output fields: `new_notes` and `titles`.

### Objectives:

1. **Extract and Condense Relevant Information into `new_notes`:**  
   Carefully review the `claim`, `notes`, and `context`. Select and synthesize only the most relevant and factually supporting pieces of information that directly underpin or relate to the `claim`.  
   - Remove redundant, irrelevant, or unverifiable details.   
   - Ensure the extracted notes clearly support verification of the claim's key points.  
   - Include key factual assertions such as locations, memberships, ownerships, dates, or other specific relationships mentioned in the claim.

2. **Identify and List Relevant Document or Entity Titles in `titles`:**  
   From the `notes` and `context`, identify the distinct and important entities, documents, or topics that contribute the core factual basis for the claim. This typically includes proper nouns such as names of people, organizations, places, events, or specific works (albums, shows, parks, etc.).  
   - Include the main sources or subjects where the claim’s facts are found.  
   - Titles should be concise and representative of the content’s focus.

### Domain-Specific Considerations:

- Entities often concern:
  - Bands and their members, formation locations, and albums (e.g. King ov Hell, Gorgoroth, Sahg, God Seed, Bergen, Hordaland).  
  - Geographical and political subdivisions such as towns, shires, councils, national parks (e.g. Eurobodalla Shire council, Deua National Park, Moruya).  
  - Entertainment venues, residency shows, hotels, casinos, and corporate ownership (e.g. The AXIS auditorium, Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino, Caesars Entertainment Corporation).  

- Claims often involve verifying:
  - Membership or association of individuals with bands or groups.  
  - Location and administrative jurisdiction of towns or landmarks.  
  - Venue locations and ownership details of shows or events.

### Strategy:

- Use `notes` as compressed fact fragments, but cross-check and enrich with the broader `context` to confirm exact names, locations, dates, or ownership info.  
- Avoid extraneous information that doesn't clearly support the claim.  
- Consolidate evidence from multiple notes into a tightly focused set of `new_notes` that validate or clarify the claim succinctly.  
- Titles help to link each fact back to its entity or document — thoughtfully include all gold-standard reference titles relevant to the claim.

### Output format:

- `new_notes`: a list of concise, factually relevant sentences derived from the notes and context supporting the claim.  
- `titles`: a list of precise, meaningful names or document titles that identify key subjects or sources underpinning the claim.

This approach ensures the claim can be accurately assessed using a distilled set of evidential facts clearly linked to authoritative sources or entities.
