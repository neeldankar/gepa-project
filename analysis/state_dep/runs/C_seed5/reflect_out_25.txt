Task Description:

You are given three input fields:
- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion that potentially requires verification or support.
- `notes`: A list of factual sentences or fragments extracted from various sources relevant to the claim.
- `context`: A list of longer textual documents, snippets, or summaries that provide background information or detailed facts related to entities, events, or topics mentioned in the `claim`.

Your goal is to produce two outputs that help to support, validate, or contextualize the `claim`:

1. `new_notes`:  
   - This should be a concise, relevant, and non-redundant set of factually correct notes synthesized or selected from the given `notes` and, if needed, the `context`.  
   - Extract or summarize key facts that directly support or relate to the claim, avoiding repetition or tangential information.  
   - Include information about people, places, organizations, and events as they relate to the claim’s facts, ideally emphasizing the primary entities and their attributes relevant to the claim.  
   - If the claim contains minor inaccuracies (e.g., small date discrepancies), note the correct fact clearly in the selected `new_notes`.  

2. `titles`:  
   - This is a list of document or article titles derived from the `context` or knowledge domain that are the most relevant to the claim.  
   - Titles should correspond to authoritative or primary sources supporting key aspects of the claim (e.g., biographical pages, event summaries, individual works, or key corporate entities).   
   - Prioritize retrieving titles that can directly verify unique or critical facts stated in the claim.  
   - Titles should be specific and informative, typically reflecting the main topic or entity that supports claim verification.

Additional Detailed Instructions and Domain-Specific Guidelines:

- Claims frequently involve individuals (e.g., coaches, actors, writers), events (sports seasons, film premieres), companies, venues, or products. Focus on identifying and cross-referencing these entities across `notes` and `context`.  
- Verify that roles, titles, affiliations, event participation, and timelines associated with a person or entity in the claim align with the provided data.  
- For sports-related claims, identify specific teams, seasons, divisions, coaching roles, and years. For entertainment-related claims, identify film titles, TV series, actor roles, premiere dates, and production credits. For locations or venues, identify precise names, addresses, and ownership details.  
- When the `notes` list contains repetitive information, distill the key unique facts to avoid redundancy in `new_notes`.  
- When correcting or clarifying minor inaccuracies in the claim, explicitly provide the accurate information in `new_notes` without restating the original erroneous claim.  
- When selecting `titles`, consider multiple granularity levels: person biographies, event summaries, organizational pages, and specific works (e.g., films, shows, teams).  
- Use the `context` information primarily to confirm or verify the details in `notes` or to identify authoritative titles relevant for the claim; the `context` may sometimes include related but off-topic documents that should be filtered out.  
- The task is essentially to fact-check or support the claim using the provided factual notes and contextual domain-specific documents and to identify the most relevant primary sources (titles) that would back up these facts.

By following this approach, produce a concise, accurate set of supporting notes and a precise list of authoritative titles relevant for verifying or contextualizing the claim.
