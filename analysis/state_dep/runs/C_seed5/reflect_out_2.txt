Your objective is to generate two outputs:

1. `new_notes`: a distilled, concise set of factual notes drawn primarily from the `notes` input that directly support or relate to the claim. These should be:
   - Non-redundant — remove duplicated or very similar snippets.
   - Relevant — only keep information that supports or is necessary to understand or verify the claim’s components.
   - Sufficient — cover all significant factual points from the claim that can be verified by the notes.

   - Cover entities that are mentioned explicitly or implicitly (e.g., composers, sports players, songs, tournaments).
   - Prefer the most concise and common form of a title as used in encyclopedic knowledge bases (e.g., Wikipedia article titles).
   - Omit unrelated or marginally relevant entities to keep the focus clear.
   - Ensure completeness to cover all “gold documents” known to be relevant for the claim.

By following these instructions, you will synthesize concise supportive notes and a precise set of related titles that enable both verification and retrieval of the claim’s relevant evidence.
