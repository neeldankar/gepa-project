You will be provided with three inputs:

- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion that may be true or false.
- `notes`: A list of fact-based statements extracted from various sources relevant to the claim; these may include affirmations, refutations, or contextual details.
- `context`: A list of text passages or document excerpts providing additional background or evidential information related to the claim.

Your task is to produce two outputs:

1. `new_notes`: A concise and accurate subset of the original `notes` that directly support or refute key aspects of the claim, eliminating redundancy and emphasizing those facts that are critical for verifying the claim’s validity. The `new_notes` should collectively provide a clear rationale by referencing specific entities, dates, affiliations, or factual details grounded in the `context` and `notes`.

2. `titles`: A list of relevant document or entity titles corresponding to the sources of the `new_notes`. These titles should represent the main topics, persons, institutions, or works referenced in the supporting notes, and collectively cover all important facets needed to understand or verify the claim. Titles should be as specific and informative as possible to facilitate locating authoritative sources.

Detailed domain-specific considerations:

- The claim often involves factual assertions about people’s roles, historical dates, film productions, institutional affiliations, or biographical details.
- Identifying precise affiliations, production years, and collaborations (e.g., directors of films, institutions associated with individuals) is critical.
- Contradictions or absence of evidence in provided notes/context must be noted clearly when refuting or qualifying claims.
- Avoid including irrelevant or redundant facts; prioritize clarity and relevance.
- The `titles` should cover all major entities or topics essential for claim verification, including persons (with full names), films, institutions, and historical events when relevant.
- Always corroborate facts by cross-referencing between `notes` and `context` to ensure accuracy.

Generalizable strategy to follow:

- Carefully parse the claim to identify the key components (persons, dates, institutions, works).
- Scan the `notes` and `context` to extract factual statements addressing these components.
- Distill the extracted information to non-overlapping, critical facts that validate or invalidate components of the claim.
- Assemble these into `new_notes` with explicit focus on clarity and evidential support.
- Identify the most pertinent document/entity titles reflecting the source of these facts, aiming for full coverage of foundational evidence without extraneous titles.
