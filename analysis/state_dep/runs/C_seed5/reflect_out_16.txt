You will be given three fields as input:
- `claim`: A factual statement or assertion that may combine multiple pieces of information.
- `notes`: A list of extracted sentences or facts related to the claim, some directly supporting, partially supporting, or refuting aspects of the claim.
- `context`: A list of longer passages, paragraphs, or articles containing detailed background information potentially relevant to verifying the claim.

Your task is to process these inputs and produce two outputs:
1. `new_notes`: A concise, cleaned-up, and distilled list of relevant factual statements derived from the `notes` and `context` that directly relate to verifying or clarifying the claim. Remove redundant or irrelevant information, and organize the facts to clearly support or refute the claim. Include the most pertinent and authoritative information that best supports the fact-checking or reasoning process.
2. `titles`: A list of the titles or headings of the key documents or sources from the `context` that contain the essential evidence used to justify the selection of the `new_notes`. These should be the most relevant documents that would allow a reader to quickly locate the full source of the supporting facts.

In your reasoning, you must synthesize information from both `notes` and `context` to verify each component of the claim, accounting for:
- Named entities (people, institutions, locations).
- Specific details such as dates, official titles, roles, rankings, or events.
- Distinctions between similar entities (e.g., universities with similar names but different locations or statuses).
- Whether a claim is supported, refuted, or partially supported based on evidence.
- Clarifying any common confusions or misstatements in the claim using factual data.

The task requires domain knowledge across various fields such as education institutions, music chart performance, sports clubs and venues, and other specific domains reflected in the input.  
For example:
- Identifying the official titles and historical facts about universities and their trustees.
- Understanding chart positions and original recording artists in music.
- Correctly locating sports clubs and event venues to their cities and countries, and clarifying any geographical errors in claims.

You should also pay attention to nuances such as:
- Multiple mentions or duplicates in `notes` should be consolidated.
- If a claim involves ranking or chart position, ensure that precise numbers and artist attributions are included.
- Distinguish between similarly named entities (e.g., Dynamo Kyiv in Ukraine vs. Houston Dynamo in the USA) to avoid conflating facts.
- Recognize and highlight if a claim is false or unsupported based on the evidence.

Overall, approach the task as a detailed fact-verification and evidence extraction exercise by:
- Cross-referencing multiple evidence pieces,
- Filtering out contradictions,
- Presenting a clear and well-organized summary (`new_notes`),
- And providing relevant source document names (`titles`) for traceability.

This approach ensures that the outputs serve as robust, concise fact summaries and source pointers for claim verification.
